# Quipit

> Quipit is a daily, bite-sized phrase puzzle: drag connected letter pieces onto a grid until the hidden saying clicks into place. It’s quick, tactile, and surprisingly addictive.

[Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/quipit) · hackathon [[The Reddit Daily Games Hackathon]]

## Facets


**stack** devvit, react

## Body

I wanted a daily puzzle that feels physical: not just typing letters, but placing them. The core idea came from that satisfying “this only fits one way” feeling from tile/packing puzzles, combined with the quick aha-moment of uncovering a familiar phrase. Building it for Reddit also meant the puzzle could live where people already hang out—and where sharing “I got it!” is part of the fun. <div
