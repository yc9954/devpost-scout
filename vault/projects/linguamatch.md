The Tracks Most IQ Points Lost, Most Likely Meme Startup, IJAG (I’m Just A Girl), New Hackers Prize, PearAI (YC24) Prize, UI/UX Most Kawaii, Build a Tool that Distracts You When You're Trying to Be Productive, The Most Cutesy Pink Project. Would Piss People Off On Linkedin Inspiration Just wanted to build something chill, you know? A little fun to spice up the day. What it does A browser extension that slaps Reddit memes onto your screen to cure that boredom that hits when you’re deep in those long, boring articles. Meme therapy, basically. How we built it Used the classics: HTML, CSS, and JS. Simple but effective. Challenges we ran into Tbh, dealing with meme APIs was a bit of a struggle, but we powered through. Also, making sure it didn’t ruin the vibe of reading was a challenge—balance, you know? Accomplishments that we're proud of Got it up and running! Memes are now part of our workflow, and it’s pretty smooth. Also, the code’s clean and it actually works, which is always a win. What we learned Turns out integrating APIs isn’t as hard as I thought... once you figure out the format. Also, the power of a good meme can’t be underestimated—it’s like caffeine for your brain. What's next for Lolify Add more meme sources (Instagram, Twitter, wherever the memes are at). Maybe let users upvote their fave memes for extra personalization.
