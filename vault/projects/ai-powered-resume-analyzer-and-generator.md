## Body

Transforming resumes into actionable career insights Finding the right job is already hard—and building a strong, polished resume makes it even harder. I noticed that most students (including myself) struggle with understanding what’s missing in their resume, how to improve, and what skills they should focus on next. This frustration inspired me to create an AI-powered Resume Analyzer & Generator that not only reads resumes but also: Analyzes strengths & weaknesses Generates short notes Converts content into flashcards Creates quizzes based on your resume Gives personalized improvement suggestions Rewrites resumes with ATS-friendly formatting A complete AI career assistant, built for students and job-seekers. <div
