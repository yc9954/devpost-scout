## Body

VIRTUAL STEM LAB ASSISTANT STEM education often struggles with barriers: limited lab access, costly equipment, and uneven opportunities across schools and communities. The Virtual STEM Lab Assistant is inspired by the idea of democratizing experimentation — bringing the thrill of discovery to anyone, anywhere. Imagine a student in a small town running chemistry simulations, a teacher guiding physics experiments without expensive setups, or a curious learner exploring coding and robotics through interactive labs. This assistant bridges the gap between curiosity and capability, turning laptops and tablets into fully equipped laboratories. It’s not just about replicating experiments — it’s about empowering exploration. With guided steps, real‑time feedback, and AI‑driven insights, learners can test hypotheses, visualize outcomes, and build confidence in STEM subjects.
