## Body

The Finch Files is a grounded science-fiction mystery told through intimate, time-fractured vignettes. When Cambridge physicist Dr. Alastair Finch becomes entangled with Project Chronos, he discovers that his work — and his growing connection to Dorothy, a woman tied to a century-old experiment — is destabilizing reality itself.
