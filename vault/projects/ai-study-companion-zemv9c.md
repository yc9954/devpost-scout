📖 Project Story Project Description AI Study Companion is a browser-based learning platform that turns notes, lectures, and textbooks into summaries, flashcards, quizzes, mind maps, and structured data—all locally for privacy and speed. I built it to solve the real problem of managing large amounts of study material efficiently. Using React 18, Vite, and Tailwind CSS, the app is fast, mobile-friendly, PWA-ready, and modular for future growth.
