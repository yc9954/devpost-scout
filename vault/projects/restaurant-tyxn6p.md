Restaurant - HubSpot CMS Theme Restaurant - Features Restaurant - Responsive in All Devices Restaurant - Multiple Home Pages Restaurant - Multiple Most Required Inner Pages Restaurant - Multiple Most Required Modules Restaurant - Lead Generating Landing Page Inspiration We really feel very much excited to be a participant in this HubSpot hackathon. It is the great opportunity for us to expose our designing and development skills. What it does Restaurant theme have 30+ most advanced & essential modules. It has 25 perfectly pre-designed pages to complete the Restaurant project easily with less time consuming. Restaurant Menu, Blog, Header Banners, Menu and Footer modules are having advanced settings and creative design. All these modules are having multiple number of uniquely styled variations. All the pages including Home Pages, Inner Pages, Blog Pages & System Pages are developed with most essentials sections. Users can update all the elements inside the pages without any coding knowledge. This is how we created this as a most powerful and creative HubSpot CMS theme for Restaurant category. How we built it Initially we start with the study of complete work flow and functionalities involving in restaurant and food industries. The experience we got in our previous restaurant projects helped us in this theme development. we decided to create module elements and pages that are most required for a restaurant theme. Then we started development work. The restaurant food menu will be an important feature in this theme. So we give more attention on the food menu module. Its having multiple stylish and attractive variations. Once we done it, we have developed all other modules. and we have strictly reviewed the code and performance of those modules. After optimizing the modules, we started development of pages by integrating the modules. We did a code review and optimization for each page at once they completed. We optimized all the assets and modules that are involved in the pages. Once all the development done, it was sent to QA for testing and fixed all the performance and UI issues. Restaurant theme is now the most perfect HubSpot CMS Theme! Challenges we ran into The challenge part in module development is to keep all the variations in a single module. But we have achieved this successfully. We made all the modules having multiple variations. In this, Food menu, Blogs, Headers, Menu, Footers are having more variations. In the future, we can just add the new variation to the respective module. Providing all the theme settings without overloading. Accomplishments that we're proud of We have developed our module more simple and effective. Also these modules are designed as it have options to add more variations and enhancement in future. What we learned We have learnt to create more user friendly modules and to include tons of variation in a single module with minimal usage of code.
