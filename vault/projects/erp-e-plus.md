Inspiration A Mexican organization, founded in 2007 in the city of Monterrey, Nuevo Leon. We were born from the needs to implement solutions technology in business and focused on the continuous improvement of its results. link What it does We develop software based entirely on the needs of the client, we are a 360 solution and we give great value to companies by facilitating their management and control of their business operations. How we built it We are a great team of programmers and we have been perfecting our software for more than 15 years based on the challenges that arise in retail and manufacturing companies. Challenges we ran into One of the great challenges when implementing our technological solutions (ERP: e-Plus) is when leading large work groups (Different departments), this is because the implementation time is short and the collaboration of all is needed ( Final Client + Ennovasol). Of course, the experience of the Ennovasol group is getting better and better when it comes to implementing our digital platforms in our clients' companies and our leadership is getting stronger. Accomplishments that we're proud of Have the opportunity that our ERP: e-Plus is used by more than 10,000,000 business transactions. (private clients and public clients) What we learned We have learned that teamwork is the key to success What's next for ERP : e-Plus We want to evolve and give the ERP: e-Plus the characteristic of being a WEB 3.0 so that it can connect with the digital wallets of our clients / companies In this way, accounts payable and accounts receivable have the possibility of being executed through digital wallets and paying with cryptocurrencies.
