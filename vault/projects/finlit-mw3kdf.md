What do we offer? Our logo ☁️Inspiration Faced with the problem personally, we realized how important financial literacy is in our lives. 📓What it does? Finlit - is an application that teaches the user's financial literacy through courses and the development of the habit of financial accounting. 🔨 How we built it The emulator was created in the Figma. 👨🏾‍💻 Challenges we ran into Necessity for new design solutions. Studying the work of PCS, credit history, budget planning. 🎉Accomplishments that we're proud of We are proud of the built-in assistant Mike, who was provided to us by the designer of the BLRDY studio. Styling of our application, made in the Figma program. 📙 What we learned We have learned many about saving schemes, investments, how the banking system works, and how PCS works. Also, the creation of the project contributed to more cohesive, fast and high-quality work within the team. 🔜 What's next for Finlit? Finlit won't stop at this stage of development. We are planing to develop the project and not to stop. The idea can become a reality if the project attracts enough investment. With the help of the team's efforts, the application will be in Beta Test status and will be available in local schools to test its effectiveness. After that, we plan to hire professional developers and bring the application to a working version. <div
