## Body

This film began with a single spark — a story idea I developed through long creative sessions with ChatGPT, shaping a narrative that blended historical weight with cinematic tension. Once the core vision was locked, I moved into visual development, crafting every frame through highly detailed prompts in MidJourney. Each still was sculpted to feel grounded, ancient, and emotionally charged, laying the foundation for the film’s aesthetic. Bringing these stills to life required motion work inside ImagineArt, which became the heart of the animation pipeline. Within ImagineArt, I used a combination of powerful tools — Kling, Hailuo, Seadance, and Veo 3 — each chosen for specific strengths, from natural character movement to environmental realism. These tools allowed the project to transition from static imagery to fully moving cinematic sequences without losing the rough, historical authenticity we wanted. Talking about sound design, eleven labs was our go to! To ensure the visuals reached their absolute highest quality, Topaz was an essential step. It upscaled, stabilized, and refined the footage, pulling out texture and clarity that elevated every shot. After all visuals were complete, everything came together in CapCut, my favourite editing tool. This is where pacing, sound, color, tension, and emotional beats finally fused into a complete film. This project has been a dream in the making. I’ve wanted to create a historical film for a long time, and choosing Ancient Egypt felt perfect — the scale, the culture, the harsh beauty, the dramatic stakes.
