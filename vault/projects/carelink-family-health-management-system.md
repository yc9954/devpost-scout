## Body

Inspiration During the pandemic, we noticed how families struggled to manage multiple medical reports, doctor consultations, and prescriptions across different platforms. The idea for CareLink was born from a desire to simplify healthcare management—bringing all family health data into one secure, connected ecosystem. We wanted to create a system that not only stores data but also provides timely reminders, emergency insights, and AI-driven wellness tracking. What it does CareLink is a digital health platform that helps families monitor, manage, and maintain their medical information with ease. Centralized health records for all family members. Smart reminders for medicines, appointments, and vaccinations. Emergency access card for doctors to view vital data instantly. AI-based health analytics to detect trends or anomalies. Teleconsultation & prescription management for quick medical help. How we built it Frontend: React.js, Tailwind CSS for responsive UI. Backend: Node.js with Express for scalable APIs. Database: MongoDB for dynamic health data storage. Authentication: Firebase Authentication for secure user login. Hosting: Netlify for front-end deployment, Render for backend. AI Module: Python-based Flask microservice integrated for predictive health scoring and risk alerts. Challenges we ran into Integrating multiple APIs for health records and telemedicine without compromising privacy. Ensuring HIPAA-like security compliance while keeping the system lightweight. Optimizing data visualization for both elderly and tech-savvy users. Managing synchronization across devices for multiple family members. Accomplishments that we're proud of Built a functional and intuitive platform in limited hackathon time. Designed an AI-driven health risk predictor for early warnings. Created a responsive UI that simplifies complex health tracking. Successfully connected real-time data updates between users and doctors. What we learned The importance of user-centric design in healthcare applications. Data security and encryption standards for sensitive medical data. How to integrate AI modules into real-time web apps efficiently. The value of teamwork and quick iteration during hackathons. What's next for CareLink: Family Health Management System Integration with wearable IoT devices for real-time health monitoring. Blockchain-based record storage for immutable and decentralized data security. Launching CareLink Mobile App for Android and iOS.
