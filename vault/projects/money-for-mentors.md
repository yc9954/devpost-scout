Our M4M logo! This is a part of our website :) About us! Inspiration Our inspiration was a HTML workshop we went to. Also, we've seen schools that are underfunded and we learned about them, and we all felt bad. We decided to create this to help and bring notice to those schools! _ Fun fact: The US is underfunding 2 years of food for everyone in the US! What it does This website allows you to: Learn more about underfunded schools You can register schools that you think might be underfunded so we can bring notice to them! You can also donate to help schools :) ## How we built it We made this on repl.it in CSS, HTML, and a tiny bit of Javascript using Airtable and gofundme as well. ## Challenges we ran into We ran into some design issues and some team issues that we had to fix! ## Accomplishments that we're proud of We're really proud of our gofundme because we want to take action.
