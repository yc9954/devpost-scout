Inspiration Growing up my family didn't have much money. Because we didn't have much money, I didn't have very many things. In order to make the most of what little I had, I had to be resourceful. I created things out of other things or found other purposes for things meant for other things. Growing up this way has taught me that there are free resources in the world and there is always a more resourceful option. Pastebin is a free site that will persist text blobs, at face-value this may not seem like much. But, as someone with a resourceful mindset, I see this as a way for me to output logs from my cloud containers thus bypassing the annoying login through MFA authentication to view logs on a clunky UI. What it does This workdspace contains a collection that will make it easier to use the Pastebin api, to expedite the use of creating, finding and deleting pastes. What's next? I've started my career as a manual tester and taught myself test automation while on the job. Slowly I gained more experience programming and even took on some development tickets. Soon I landed a job as a quality automation engineer then as a software engineer.
