## Body

Inspiration Our inspiration was to create a straightforward solution to global language barriers, verbally and nonverbally. What it does Our app is a prototype that is designed to represent a teaching process of ASL. How we built it We built our app with React Native, and Python. Challenges we ran into We were unfortunately met with the tight deadline barrier, and couldn't implement full gesture recognition functionality. However, we are grateful for what we achieved in a short time span. Accomplishments that we're proud of We are proud of our binary achievements, and our abilities to collaborate in a short time span. What we learned We learned the importance of time prioritization, and impactful collaboration techniques that we can take with us into our individual careers.
