Landing Page How It Works Platform Feature Farmer Dashboard Mint Tokens Process Waste History Inspiration Walking through rural Kenya, we witnessed farmers burning mountains of coffee husks and crop residues—releasing methane gas \(25 \times\) more potent than CO₂. Meanwhile, these same farmers struggled with poverty despite their critical role in food production. The problem: 2 billion tons of organic waste generated annually 33% improperly managed Farmers have zero incentive to process it properly Carbon credit markets exclude small-scale farmers The opportunity: What if we could turn waste into wealth using blockchain? Connect farmers directly to corporations seeking carbon credits? Make environmental action profitable? That's why we built OrganicWasteGems (OWG) on Polkadot —to create transparent, decentralized waste-to-value infrastructure for Africa and beyond. What it does OWG transforms organic waste processing into profit through blockchain-verified rewards . Farmers Process waste → Earn 1 OWG token per 10kg Generate carbon credits (\(1 \text{ kg waste} = 0.4 \text{ kg CO}_2\) saved) Sell credits directly to corporations at market rates (KES 8,000–15,000/ton) Track impact via real-time dashboard Record worker payments on-chain Corporations Purchase verified carbon credits directly from farmers Meet ESG compliance with transparent records Support local communities while offsetting emissions Instant settlement through smart contracts How we built it Tech Stack Polkadot Asset Hub Testnet Smart Contracts: Solidity 0.8.26 + OpenZeppelin + Hardhat Blockchain: Moonbeam (EVM-compatible Polkadot parachain) Frontend: Next.js 16 + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS Web3: RainbowKit + Wagmi v2 + Viem Development Process Research – Interviewed farmers, studied carbon markets Smart Contracts – Built, tested, deployed to Moonbase Alpha Frontend – Responsive Next.js app with wallet integration Deployment – Live on Polkadot Asset Hub testnet and Moonbase Alpha What we learned Technical Solidity gas optimization patterns Moonbeam’s EVM compatibility advantages RainbowKit + Wagmi best practices Smart contract security fundamentals Business Users care about income , not “blockchain” Mobile-first design is essential Pilot testing validates faster than theory Trust is built through transparency Domain Carbon credit market pricing mechanisms Organic waste contributes 5% of global GHG emissions Circular economy principles in practice What's next for Organic Waste Gems Phase 2 (Q1 2026) Mobile app (React Native) Multi-language support (Swahili, French) Integration with M-Pesa & local payment systems QR code verification for waste collection Phase 3 (Q2 2026) IoT sensors for automated waste measurement AI/ML for waste type classification NFT certificates for carbon credits Cross-chain bridge to other Polkadot parachains <div
