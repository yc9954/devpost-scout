## Body

If there is a thing that can control one's mind its simply his heart itself. To make the heart feel good there's only one way exceptionally for any person : Music. People love to listen to their favorite tracks while travelling, dancing, etc. but where it is more necessary is when our heart feels empty. When one cant find out what's going on with them music finds the way to heal. There's different category of people the text chat loving ones , the talkative ones. People have their own way of making their mind cool. I always needed a platform where I can talk to a person and never judges me. Here at the same time a music is played detecting their emotion from the way they speak and from the way they texts. Feel It is a platform where anyone can talk , share their heart , feel your minds. It's a simple web app built using react.js for front-end and TensorFlow to detect emotions. Currently its a basic model. In future I would like to add more features like adding more music and all.
