## Body

Inspiration “Ritual” was inspired by the atmosphere of old European cathedrals, medieval myths, and the quiet tension of Catholic mysticism. I wanted to capture the feeling of stepping into a sacred space where history, symbolism, and the supernatural blend together in subtle, unsettling ways. What it does The film creates a mood-driven journey through faith, fear, and the unknown. It invites the viewer into a world of whispered prayers, hidden symbols, and rituals that seem familiar yet impossibly ancient. How we built it The project was crafted using AI-driven cinematic tools, combining stylized visuals, atmospheric sound design, and slow, deliberate pacing. Each scene was shaped to evoke a sense of mystery without relying on explicit exposition. Challenges we ran into Capturing the right balance between religious authenticity and mythic ambiguity was difficult. We also faced challenges ensuring visual consistency across scenes while maintaining the film’s dreamlike tone. Accomplishments that we're proud of We achieved a unique visual style that feels both sacred and otherworldly, and we managed to create a narrative that remains open to interpretation while still emotionally engaging. What we learned We learned how to push AI tools to evoke specific moods, how minimal storytelling can still resonate, and how powerful atmosphere becomes when details remain intentionally unexplained.
