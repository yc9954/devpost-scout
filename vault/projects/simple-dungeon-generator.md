## Body

Inspiration We built this asset for anyone who needs to prototype a game fast and sometimes gets stuck on modeling or on those tricky bits of programming. The spark came from there: as compulsive gamers (metaphorically speaking), we dove into procedural instancing and, as often happens, you end up playing where you feel most at home. For us, that place is the dungeon . You see it across role-playing games, in countless contexts, even escape rooms. At some point we asked ourselves: why not build a generator specifically for MetaHorizon? That’s how the idea was born. What we built We’re two people — one small team — and we shaped this with a clear goal: remove friction, so you can focus on the experience rather than technical hurdles. We genuinely had fun building it, even with a few bumps along the way: The script, while simple in its design, took time to become solid and clean. A couple of assets acted up. Performance at one stage spiked up to 400% — but we didn’t back down. Now it’s super-optimized. In the end, we packaged everything you need to take it in hand and make it yours: clear info to create it, recreate it, manipulate it, use it, and rebuild it to your taste — a true REMIX . Challenges we ran into Keeping the script simple while still powerful. Balancing performance vs. flexibility . Making sure that anyone could pick it up and use it with minimal learning curve. Accomplishments that we’re proud of A clean, optimized script. Modular assets ready for fast prototyping . A framework that can be easily extended and remixed . What’s next Our intent is exactly this: ignite your imagination. We want this to be a reliable starting point for anyone who wants to: build quickly explore ideas lock a layout when it feels right push beyond If this resonates, know that we poured both head and heart into making sure you feel free to generate, experiment, and grow on top of this foundation.
