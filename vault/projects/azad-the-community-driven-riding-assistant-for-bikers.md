The trip was incredible: 🛣️ Scenic roads that took my breath away 🤝 Amazing people who became friends 🌅 Epic sunsets I'll never forget ☕ Hidden cafes and breakfast joints 🏍️ Fellow riders with incredible stories But here's the problem: I had to squeeze an entire month of memories into one Instagram story. During the ride, I kept thinking— riders need better tools to: 📍 Track the roads we take 🗺️ Remember the underrated places we discover 🤝 Connect with fellow riders we meet along the way I cut my trip short to attend FlutterCon in Bengaluru . There, I met Viktor from Serverpod , who encouraged me to join this hackathon.
