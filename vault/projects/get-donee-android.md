Get Donee – My Hackathon Journey 🚀 Inspiration I started Get Donee with a simple thought: 👉 "What if I had a task manager that felt light, casual, and truly mine?" 📌 What it does It’s a simple and intuitive task management application , built with Flutter, to help you stay organized and productive. 🛠️ How we built it At first, I started with Kiro spec development , which felt magical — specs turned into code like a dream. I vibed with it, let the AI drive, and progress was smooth. ⚡ Challenges we ran into 🎨 UI refinements broke my patience 🐞 Debugging felt like a nightmare without an emulator ⚡ AI sometimes added too much, sometimes too little 🏆 Accomplishments that I'm proud of As this was my first hackathon , I’m proud to have at least reached the end with something good to show as a Flutter developer. That’s enough for now. 🙌 📚 What we learned Slowly, I realized… 💡 The trick was to stay in control : tell the AI exactly what line to change , no more, no less. That’s how Get Donee was born — a task manager made with curiosity, frustration, and a lot of late-night debugging. 🔮 What's next for Get Donee (Android) I’ll try to make it a real working app without bugs 😅 ✨ Thank You! <div
