Classical Music Classical Music Puzzle Preview Inspiration When I see this challenge I think that this is the best form to link my two favorite hobby, music and computing. I started with music when I was 7 years old as the same time as computer. What is the objective of this puzzle? I think that classical music is a fantastic type of music, but younger and teenagers don't like. I design this puzzle to enjoy classical music and improve your listening skills. How to play? Single tap: With a single tap or using the keyboard arrows, you can move one or multiple objects. Drag: When you drag an object you can move it vertically or horizontally. Long Press: When you make a long press in any object, you can listen to part of the music sheet and see an amazing animation. Shake: You can shake your mobile phone or press r key to reset the puzzle.
