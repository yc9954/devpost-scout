Problem and Solution Reference Inspiration Globally, a significant portion of small and medium-sized businesses (SMBs), particularly in emerging markets like India, operate without an online presence, despite the explosive growth of digital commerce. Traditional website development often incurs significant costs (e.g., ₹10,000–₹50,000 in initial target markets like India), a prohibitive investment for microbusinesses and solo entrepreneurs already operating on tight margins. Most small business owners lack the technical expertise and time required to build and maintain websites. Relying solely on social media platforms limits discoverability, professional credibility, and customer trust. What it does Zintro is an AI-powered microsite builder designed for small businesses, freelancers, and local service providers worldwide. While initially focused on the Indian market, our intelligent platform creates personalized, professional microsites that establish instant online credibility and are built for global scalability.
