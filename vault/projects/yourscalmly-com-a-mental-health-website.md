## Body

Inspiration We noticed how so many people around us are suffering silently and are unable to share there feelings to others and must be supported. What it does Our website provides many features that inlude: Valid Information including topics like awareness, stigma around mental health, types of disorders, there causes, symptoms and much more. Therapy Material which will include books, worksheets, guides, videos, interactives, articles and other products. A section where users can chat anonymously to other users. ## How we built it We built the site with the help of HTML5 , CSS3 , JS and some libraries. We used predefined codes from code pen and customized them according to our needs. ## Challenges we ran into We wanted to create a chat box where people can share their feelings but faced a lot of difficulties and code errors . ## Accomplishments that we're proud of Our site as a whole and particularly the Home Page and some fascinating CSS effects ## What we learned Communicating. Distributing work on basis of skills in a Team. Managing time. What's next for yourscalmly.com , a mental health website Features that will be added soon: A platform to interact with counselors, psychologists and psychiatrists.
