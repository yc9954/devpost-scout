## Body

Inspiration Zerocam Mono was inspired by the desire to return to the simplicity and authenticity of traditional black and white photography in the digital age. We wanted to create an app that counters the trend of over-processed, artificially enhanced images prevalent in today's smartphone photography, allowing users to focus on capturing genuine moments rather than chasing technical perfection. What it does Zerocam Mono is an Anti-AI black & white camera app that simplifies the photography process. It captures RAW images and processes them to remove artificial effects, resulting in soft, natural-looking black and white photos. The app features a single-button interface for easy shooting, with no settings or distractions. It also includes a 365 Challenge widget to encourage daily photography practice. How we built it We developed Zerocam Mono with a focus on simplicity and user experience. The app was designed by Dmitry Novikov and developed by Sergio Rodríguez Rama. We implemented RAW image capture and custom processing algorithms to achieve the desired natural black and white look without artificial enhancements. The minimalist interface was carefully crafted to eliminate distractions and streamline the photography process. For the app's animations, we utilized Rive, a powerful real-time interactive design and animation tool. The onboarding experience, shutter button animation, and logo animations were all created using Rive. This allowed us to build interactive, high-performance animations that run smoothly on mobile devices while keeping file sizes small. Rive's versatility and integration capabilities made it an ideal choice for enhancing the user experience without compromising the app's simplicity. Challenges we ran into Creating an extremely simple user interface while still providing a powerful photography tool. Balancing simplicity with functionality required careful consideration and numerous iterations. A significant challenge we encountered was positioning our app in an era dominated by AI-enhanced photography. We were essentially swimming against the current, building an app that deliberately avoids AI processing and complex features. This approach raised concerns about market acceptance and user interest. However, we were pleasantly surprised to find that people are embracing and loving the app despite—or perhaps because of—its departure from current trends. This positive reception validated our vision but also presented the ongoing challenge of maintaining our anti-AI stance while still delivering value in a rapidly evolving technological landscape. Accomplishments that we're proud of We're proud of creating an app that goes against the grain of current photography trends, emphasizing natural aesthetics over artificial enhancements. The one-button simplicity of Zerocam Mono is a significant achievement, making photography accessible to users of all skill levels. We're also pleased with the implementation of the 365 Challenge widget, which encourages users to improve their photography skills daily. What we learned Through the development of Zerocam Mono, we gained valuable insights into the balance between simplicity and functionality in app design. We deepened our understanding of RAW image processing and the nuances of creating natural-looking black and white photographs. The project also reinforced the importance of focusing on user experience and the power of constraint in fostering creativity. What's next for Zerocam Mono Looking ahead, we plan to refine and expand Zerocam Mono based on user feedback. Potential future developments include: Introducing subtle customization options without compromising the app's simplicity Expanding the 365 Challenge with more photography tips and prompts Implementing a community feature for users to share their black and white photography Exploring ways to further optimize the image processing for even more natural-looking results Creating an Android version of the app to reach a wider audience Growing our community not only on X (formerly Twitter) but also on Instagram Developing a dedicated landing page that showcases the best community pictures Our goal is to continue evolving Zerocam Mono while staying true to its core principle of simplifying and enhancing the black and white photography experience.
