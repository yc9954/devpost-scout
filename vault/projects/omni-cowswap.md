## Body

Inspiration Fragmented liquidity, sandwich bots, and arbitrage bots extract value from users. Cow swap offered coincidence of wants but on one chain. We wanted to do better with the recent advances in minimally trustless bridges. What it does Omnicow takes the wants from all different chains and matches them behind the back, bridging assets back and forth. How we built it We are using connext for bridging the assets and Cow's solving algorithm Challenges we ran into Finding a working bridge that services all the chains at the same time Accomplishments that we're proud of We would be able to almost guarantee native 10 million dollar trades with zero slippage. What we learned The omnichain dapp world is ripe for innovation. What's next for Omni Cowswap Advanced solver algorithms and advanced order types.
