Landing page / Dashboard My profile: Add your info & abilities Message people Inspiration Finding skilled people for small jobs or projects can be surprisingly difficult. People often rely on word of mouth, social media posts, or messaging dozens of contacts to find someone with the right expertise. I wanted to create a platform that makes connecting people with skilled professionals fast and straightforward. Whether someone needs a plumber, electrician, tutor, designer, programmer, or any other specialist, SkillBoard helps them find the right person quickly. What it does SkillBoard is a platform where users can create profiles showcasing their skills, specialties, and services. Other users can search for professionals based on the expertise they need and directly contact them through the platform. The goal is to make discovering and connecting with skilled people as simple as possible. How I built it I built SkillBoard using modern web development technologies to create a responsive and user-friendly experience. The platform includes user profiles, skill listings, search functionality, and direct messaging features. Special attention was given to creating a clean interface that allows users to quickly find relevant professionals without unnecessary complexity. Challenges I ran into One of the biggest challenges was designing a system that organizes different types of skills and professions in a way that remains easy to search and browse. Another challenge was creating an intuitive user experience that works for both people offering services and people looking to hire someone. Building reliable communication features between users also required careful planning. Accomplishments that I'm proud of I'm proud of creating a platform that solves a real-world problem. SkillBoard has the potential to help skilled individuals gain visibility while making it easier for others to find the help they need. I'm also proud of successfully combining profile management, search functionality, and user communication into a single cohesive platform. What I learned Through this project, I strengthened my skills in full-stack web development, user experience design, and database organization. I learned how to design systems that connect different types of users while keeping the experience simple and intuitive. I also gained valuable experience thinking about scalability and how online marketplaces function. What's next for SkillBoard Future plans include adding ratings and reviews, advanced search filters, location-based matching, profile verification, portfolio uploads, and improved messaging features. I also hope to implement recommendation algorithms that help users find the most relevant professionals based on their needs. Ultimately, the goal is to grow SkillBoard into a trusted platform where people can easily find skilled professionals for any task or project. <div
