AI Application Inspiration Since from childhood seen a lot of Chronic Diseases cases where diseases identified after it enters a critical section in surroundings so to stop it at the initial stage my hunger for technology with healthcare started growing day by day to make a complete product. In today's world, chronic diseases has become increasingly prevalent, affecting a substantial portion of the population. The alarming fact is that many individuals remain unaware of their condition until it reaches advanced stages, making it a significant public health challenge. What it does the application offers tailored recommendations to the user. These include medication suggestions, dietary guidelines, and lifestyle modifications to manage or prevent the identified health conditions effectively. Furthermore, the app assists users in locating nearby hospitals or healthcare facilities relevant to their needs, providing contact information for further assistance.
