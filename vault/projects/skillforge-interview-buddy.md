Inspiration The inspiration behind SkillForge Interview Buddy was to revolutionize interview preparation using the capabilities of AWS Party Rock. Recognizing the need for an advanced and scalable solution, I aimed to leverage the partyrock.aws platform to enhance the interview game for professionals and job seekers alike. What it does SkillForge Interview Buddy, built on partyrock.aws, empowers users by allowing them to input their job description. The app utilizes AWS Party Rock's features to extract key skills and generates a personalized mock interview. Real-time feedback is seamlessly integrated, providing users with a comprehensive tool for interview excellence. Whether someone is a seasoned professional or a job seeker, SkillForge Interview Buddy on partyrock.aws becomes the essential companion for elevating interview skills.
