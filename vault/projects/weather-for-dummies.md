## Body

Gives the weather information based on gps coordinates Inspiration Normally when I read the weather data from my weather app I don't quite know how to interpret the data when all I'm looking at are numbers it is hard to get a tangible perspective on how hot or cold it will be for the day. I a simple man all I need to know is wheater it will be hot or cold for the day and whether or not it will rain. What it does What weather for dummies does is, is it simplifies weather information by telling you only what you need to know to prepare for the day.ie whether it will be cold or warm and a description of what the weather will be like for the day in your city. so you can prepare for the day in a practical manner. How we built it I built it using flask and python. Challenges we ran into I had to learn to use the flask framework. Accomplishments that we're proud of I can actually use this in my life on a daily basis instead of looking at other apps that makes the wheather more complex than it needs to be.
