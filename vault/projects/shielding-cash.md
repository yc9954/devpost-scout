## Body

Inspiration Inspired by Tornado Cash on Ethereum, we build privacy solution on BTTC. What it does Shielding Cash is a decentralized, non-custodial privacy solution built on BTTC. It improves transaction privacy by breaking the on-chain link between recipient and destination addresses. How we built it Open the shielding.cash app directly or follow the instruction on https://github.com/shieldingcash/shielding-ui-minified . Challenges we ran into Fully understood zero knowledge proofs (zk-SNARKs) to achieve privacy and porting Tornado Cash to BTTC network. Accomplishments that we're proud of Transaction privacy solution on BTTC. What we learned Privacy knowledge especially zero knowledge proofs(zk-SNARKS) Complete skills for web3 development What's next for shielding.cash ERC20 token deployment on BTTC Shielding Cash support on TRON network <div
