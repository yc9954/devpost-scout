## Body

Didi Li was inspired by the question of what is real when everything feels simulated. The film explores the fragile boundary between consciousness and artificial reality through a woman trapped in a mysterious experiment. Combining AI-generated visuals with traditional storytelling, it reflects on isolation, identity, and the human search for meaning. Created using VEO3 with layered sound design and color composition, the project aims to evoke emotion within a digital framework. The main challenge was preserving authenticity and humanity while working with AI tools, but this process taught us how technology can amplify emotion when guided by intent.
