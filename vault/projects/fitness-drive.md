fitness fit fitnessdrive Inspiration: Growing Health Concerns: Obesity, diabetes, and heart diseases are on the rise. Lack of Personalized Guidance: Most available resources are generic and lack expert input. Disconnected Ecosystem: There is a gap between individuals seeking guidance and certified professionals. Solution Aim: To provide a personalized, expert-driven, and easily accessible health support system. What it does What's our solution? "Fitness Drive Project" is a website. Fitness Drive is your ultimate fitness companion, offering exercise guidelines, personalized plans, and progress tracking. Track exercises daily with a pie chart & history!view! Note: Long-Term Health Focus: Providing structured routines that ensure sustainability. How we built it For development, you will need Node.js and a Node global package, Yarn, installed in your environment. Other languages are HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Security Measures: Secure authentication and data privacy policies. Challenges we ran into: Managing expert listings: Keeping an updated and verified database of professionals. Implementation: Implementation of code and hosting the website. Data Privacy & Security: Ensuring user information is protected. Achievements that we're proud of: User-Friendly Interface: Easy navigation and accessibility. Expert Directory: A list of professionals with profiles. Booking System: Allows users to schedule in-person consultations. Personalized Plans: Tailored diet and workout regimens based on user needs. Contact & Support: Direct communication channel with trainers and dietitians. Positive Impact: Helping people make informed health decisions with professional guidance. What's next for FITNESS Drive?
