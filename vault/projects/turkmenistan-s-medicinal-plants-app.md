Elegant splash screen welcoming users to the Turkmen Herbal Encyclopedia. Enjoy reading in standard light mode with a clean, vibrant interface. Explore all volumes in eye-friendly dark mode for extended reading comfort. "User initiates inquiry, poised to engage the AI assistant for expert herbal insights." "AI furnishes a comprehensive, nuanced recommendation derived from the medicinal knowledge base." Dive into detailed plant profiles with scientific classification and therapeutic info. Quickly search for any medicinal plant across 16 volumes with intelligent AI suggestions. Refine results with advanced filters to find exact plants and applications. Seamlessly navigate between home page, settings, and app features using the drawer menu. Switch effortlessly between Turkmen, English, Russian, Japanese, and Spanish. Available on Google Play for easy download and offline access to all volumes. Just search "dermanlyk ösümlikler TM". 200+ real users have been reached. About the Project 🌿 Inspiration 💡 This project originated from my ambition to digitally safeguard the seminal 16-volume compendium of Turkmen medicinal flora 📚—a body of work of profound cultural, historical, and therapeutic significance. Witnessing how frequently individuals relied on these volumes for traditional remedies, I recognized the necessity for a modern, universally accessible, and linguistically versatile platform. My objective was to transform a traditionally analog repository into a sophisticated, intuitive, and fully self-contained mobile encyclopedia capable of operating entirely offline 📱. In doing so, I aimed to preserve an invaluable heritage while elevating its usability for contemporary readers and researchers. What I Learned ⚡ Throughout the development cycle, I encountered numerous opportunities to broaden both my technical expertise and conceptual understanding. This project deepened my experience in: Architecting a high-density offline application while maintaining seamless responsiveness ⚡ Implementing rigorous localization strategies and managing extensive multilingual datasets through strings.xml 🌐 Integrating the Gemini 3 Flash's and Takyk Ai's API to provide context-aware, semantically robust interpretations 🤖 Designing ergonomic, reader-centric interfaces optimized for prolonged scholarly engagement 🖋️ Structuring botanical and phytological data to remain scientifically coherent, scalable, and computationally efficient 🌱 Engineering a consistent, aesthetically refined, and functionally stable dark-mode environment 🌙 To enhance performance, I optimized critical algorithms, achieving approximately: O(n \log n) (for indexing large datasets) and O(k) (for intra-text highlighting) These refinements ensured both scalability and fluid user interaction across the entire dataset. How I Built It 🛠️ The application was developed using a combination of robust and industry-standard technologies, including: Java as the principal programming language ☕ Android Studio as the integrated development environment 🖥️ XML for interface construction and multilingual text localization ✏️ RecyclerView , ViewBinding , and Glide for efficient rendering and resource management ⚙️ * Gemini 3 Flash's and Takyk Ai's API * for semantic inference and intelligent contextual support (Takyk Ai is for less limits and more turkmen skills)🤖 Custom algorithms designed to maximize: search precision 🔍 highlight accuracy ✨ cross-volume data navigation 📖 The overall architecture is modular by design, enabling systematic organization of all 16 volumes , each containing extensive and unique botanical entries 🌿. Challenges I Faced ⚔️ The development process presented numerous structural, technical, and conceptual challenges, such as: Curating and systematizing a large corpus of botanical data while upholding clarity and academic accuracy 📚 Maintaining optimal performance without remote databases or cloud infrastructure ☁️❌ Establishing consistent UI behavior across both light and dark themes 🌞🌙 Mitigating encoding inconsistencies , especially with non-Latin scripts 🔤 Ensuring readability and interface stability across diverse screen dimensions 📱💻 Incorporating advanced AI-driven features while preserving an offline-first architecture 🤖 Designing a logical, intuitive taxonomy for 16 individual books, each with distinct structural characteristics 🗂️ These challenges sharpened my engineering judgment, strengthened my design discipline, and compelled me to adopt a more deliberate and methodical development approach. Summary 🚀 Ultimately, this project evolved far beyond the scope of a conventional mobile app. It stands as a digital preservation effort , an educational instrument , and an AI-enhanced herbal encyclopedia 🌿 that enables users to explore traditional Turkmen natural medicine through a platform that is accessible, intelligent, and culturally respectful 🌏.
