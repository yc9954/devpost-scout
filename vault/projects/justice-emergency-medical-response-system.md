What's next for Justice - Emergency Medical Response System Emergency situations require fast decision-making and quick hospital access, but in many cases ambulances lose valuable time trying to find a hospital with available doctors, beds, or facilities. In many cities, ambulance drivers often contact multiple hospitals manually before finding one that can accept a patient. This inefficiency inspired us to build Justice EMRS, a real-time platform designed to connect ambulances with hospitals instantly. The idea was to create a system where ambulance drivers can see hospital availability in real time, including doctor availability and emergency capacity. Instead of calling multiple hospitals, drivers can simply select an available hospital from the system and notify them instantly.
