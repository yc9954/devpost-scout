A screenshot showing sketch to image with slider and the AI3D generated model Napkinmatic AI3D icon logo ***** After some intense of R+D iterations with multiple SIGGRAPH pubs, indie dev depression and recharge, metamorphosis of sketches and ideas etc, com.ai3d.napkinmatic aka Napkinmatic AI3D is now finally on the App Store and Google Play! ***** Inspiration Everyone doodles on napkins — in cafés, classrooms, airports, and meetings. It’s a universal act of casual creativity. But what if those doodles didn’t stay trapped on paper? What if they could instantly transform into: a masterpiece painting , a 3D model you can rotate, export, or print, a website or an app (coming soon - vibe code on a napkin!), or even a video projected on a Times Square billboard ? Napkinmatic was born from this question: turn everyday sketches into extraordinary creations. It reimagines what AI, AR, and transparent interfaces can do when grounded in the simplest act — drawing on a napkin. What it does Napkinmatic is an iOS + Android app that transforms napkin sketches into magical outputs using AI + 3D + AR. Core Features: Sketch Capture: Take a photo of your napkin doodle or upload from your gallery. Command Plane Overlay: Napkinmatic augments your sketch with an interactive UI directly on top of it. AI Transformations: /image2painting → masterpiece version of your doodle /image2mesh → interactive 3D model (pop-out-of-paper reveal) /image2video → animated billboard-style video Sharing & Virality: Every creation generates a permalink . Users earn Coins for unique views of their permalink, rewarding them for sharing. Viral sharing = organic growth + creative rewards. Monetization via RevenueCat: Buy Napkin Credits , which convert into Coins . Example costs: 5 coins for paintings, 90 coins for 3D. Hybrid model: casual users buy coins, power users subscribe for monthly bundles. Unlock flows use playful design (ticket stubs, origami animations) to make monetization feel delightful. How we built it Frontend: React Native for mobile UI, Unity/Three.js for 3D and AR rendering. Backend: Python services for AI transformations (image-to-image, image-to-mesh, image-to-video). Monetization: RevenueCat SDK powers coin bundles + subscriptions, with cross-platform entitlement syncing. Design System: Napkin textures, origami-style fold transitions, ink ripple effects, paper-crinkle sound design. Growth Engine: Permalink sharing rewards creators with Coins, turning virality into retention. Testing: Soft launch at Maker Faire 2025 — thousands of kids and adults sketched → painted → 3D modeled live. Challenges we ran into Performance optimization: Keeping the 3D “pop-out-of-paper” reveal smooth on mid-range Android devices. Coin economy balance: Deciding fair costs for low-friction vs. high-value transformations. Transparent interface design: Keeping the napkin motif consistent while layering UI commands. Frictionless monetization: Designing RevenueCat unlock flows that felt playful instead of transactional. Deadline push: Shipping v1 live on the Play Store hours before the hackathon deadline. Accomplishments that we're proud of Version 1 live on the Google Play Store before Sept 30 deadline. Maker Faire 2025 soft launch: thousands of people transformed napkin doodles live — proving delight across ages. Playful coin economy: Napkin Credits → Coins powered by RevenueCat, blending microtransactions + subscriptions. Signature design reveal: the 3D model “pops out of the napkin” — consistently wowed users. Growth mechanics: Permalink sharing rewards creators with Coins for unique views, tying virality directly to monetization. Billboard-ready design: Cohesive motif, micro-interactions, and cinematic transformations. What we learned Design matters: Users respond far more emotionally when the transformation happens directly on their napkin sketch — right where they drew — compared to generic form-based or web raw AI outputs. That immediacy makes the magic feel personal. Monetization as play: By turning RevenueCat entitlements into a coin economy, we learned that purchases don’t have to feel transactional. When users spend “Napkin Credits” like game tokens, they see monetization as part of the creative flow. Choice drives retention: Giving users options (5 coins for quick paintings vs. 90 coins for high-value 3D reveals) creates excitement, anticipation, and reasons to return — instead of hitting a flat paywall. Real-world testing beats assumptions: At Maker Faire 2025, thousands of kids and adults sketched and transformed doodles in front of us. Their reactions taught us which animations delighted most, which UI flows confused, and how people of all ages understood the Command Plane. RevenueCat flexibility: We learned how easily RC adapts to hybrid models (coins + subscriptions) and cross-platform syncing, letting us scale from casual users to power creators without rebuilding our backend. Community inspiration: Seeing strangers light up as their napkin doodles became masterpieces reminded us that creativity is universal. The joy of transformation is contagious — and that’s what fuels both design polish and growth. What's next for Napkinmatic AI3D iOS and Amazon App Store App Store launch: Full parity with Android release. - Web app! for those who don't like to download apps Expanded AIMagic commands: /image2avatar , /image2scene , /image2animation . AR gallery mode: Drop multiple sketches into real space as a personal “napkin museum.” Creator marketplace: Let users sell or license their doodle-to-3D assets. Localized expansion: Coins + Credits pricing adapted for global classrooms and creators.
