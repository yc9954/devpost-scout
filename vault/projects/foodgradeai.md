How it works design Food Grade AIs values landing page Report After AI video chat ends Video Chat In Progress Part of the Report Part of the Report Part of the Report 🧠 About the Project: FoodGrade AI 🚀 Inspiration Working in the back of house at Panda Express, I saw firsthand how food safety audits were outdated, stressful, and often rushed. Health inspections are critical—but the current systems rely on paper checklists, human memory, and last-minute prep. I thought: what if a kitchen could run self-audits, guided by AI, before the inspector ever walks in? That’s where FoodGrade AI was born: a real-time, AI-powered food safety assistant that helps restaurant teams detect violations early and improve compliance—with no paperwork, no guessing, and no missed steps. 🛠️ What I Built FoodGrade AI is a mobile-first web app that transforms your phone camera into a live compliance tool. It lets users: Video chat with an AI health inspector avatar Scan the kitchen using the camera to detect food safety violations (e.g. improper food storage, dirty prep areas, unlabeled items) Auto-generate audit reports based on detected issues Guide users step-by-step through their store's compliance walkthrough Blurred-to-clear UI reveal for a futuristic audit launch experience The app is designed with Next.js , Tailwind CSS , and Bolt.new , built to be responsive, intuitive, and immersive. 📚 What I Learned How to rapidly prototype a full-stack AI-driven app with limited tools How to create intuitive UI/UX for high-stakes, real-world use cases Real-world kitchen workflows and the pain points of food safety processes Leveraging camera input + AI avatars to simulate live expert guidance ⚠️ Challenges I Faced Simulating accurate AI vision detection in a short timeframe while maintaining real-time performance Creating a fluid, mobile-first UI that still feels immersive and futuristic Designing around low-latency video + AI avatar interaction without breaking UX Balancing mocked AI logic with realistic kitchen demo flow 💡 What’s Next FoodGrade AI is already being tested in my own store. The next step is integrating real detection models, onboarding new restaurant brands, and scaling to enterprise kitchens and third-party audit firms. This is just the beginning of intelligent food safety. <div
