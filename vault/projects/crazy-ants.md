## Body

Inspiration Most people in developing nations are yet to experience the addictive MMORPG experience that is proven to be both fun and lucrative in Asia. What it does Based on MMORPG game design principles, guided by an internally circular economy, and integrated with DeFi attributes, we aim to create a comprehensive social game. Come for the money, stay for the fun. How we built it Making a long-lasting persisting MMORPG is not a simple feat. Our game product team consists of veteran MMORPG developers， who have experience and know-how in building and running long-lasting MMORPGs. Challenges we ran into In-game chain logic is largely random, and traditional blockchain randomness can be manipulated by miners, leading to unfairness. Complex random logic not only enhances gaming enjoyment but also consumes a significant amount of gas. Accomplishments that we're proud of Embarking on this thrilling journey, our beta testing on the Polygon mainnet has garnered over 20,000 wallet addresses and witnessed the creation of over 300,000 NFTs. Without any ERC20 tokens released, and only a small-scale promotion was done in Brazil during this time, the trading volume on the secondary market has exceeded 1 Million Matic! What we learned MMORPG web3 gaming has enormous market potential.
