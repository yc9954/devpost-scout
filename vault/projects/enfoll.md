## Body

Joined the renewable energy band recently with 5KW of installed solar panels on my roof. Solar panels generate 29% of global renewable energy. The panels are big (and unaesthetic), but also hard to dispose. Recycling technology is still emerging, is costly and not many are doing it. This brings the possibility of reuse, which felt better, with the difference it can make. EnFOLL is an app backed by BaaS options for crowdfunding and financing small to medium scale solar power projects anywhere there is a need. Users who need used solar panels and those who have used panels for sale or donation can raise their requests in EnFOLL. Internally, a price/volume/location mapping is done to find the best combination that fits the approved financial aid.
