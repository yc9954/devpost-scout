IMPORTANT Table number: D18 Primary Track: AI Secondary Track: GameJam Partner challenges: Skype Azure GameJam In-store conversion supercharged Jelastic Inspiration All of us are fans of books. Remember - when you've read your last book what did you thought about stupid mistakes made by main character. Did you thought that you can do it better that him? Now, with Walkie talkie you have a chance to be better space cowboy than Han Solo and better detective than Sherlock Holmes. What it does Walkie Talkie - it's interactive book and text adventure game like MUD, but with extra features like embeded images and videos. You can use it for entertainment and for education.
