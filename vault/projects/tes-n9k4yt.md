LEGO inspiration Inspiration My 4-year-old son loves to build with legos. As an extension of this, together we started trying to replicate his lego builds in voxel art. Using the program MagicaVoxel, we have been slowly building out his "monster" designs. Together we made a Yokai Demon Bear in honor of the Yokaiswap team.
