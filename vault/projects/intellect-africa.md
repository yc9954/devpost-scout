## Body

Inspiration Intellect Africa was built to help high school students and recent high school graduates harness programs and opportunities that are now easily available through the internet. The challenges we faced while submitting international applications to colleges in the US led us to work on this project to help other students who might face similar challenges. What it does Matching students with potential programs and opportunities and the steps required to apply. How we built it We wrote the codes and hosted it on Netlify from GitHub. Challenges we ran into Using Firebase for the contact form backend was not very easy. Accomplishments that we're proud of We're proud of contributing our quota to solving UN SDG 1 and 4. (No poverty and Quality Education) What we learned Every failure leads a step closer to success.
