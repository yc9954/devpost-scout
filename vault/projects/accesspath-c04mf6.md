Homepage of AccessPath Features Sign up Add Place Select Photos Add Accessibility Features Add Custom Accessibility Features Submit Place Search By Your Needs Search By Your Needs With More Filters Click on a place for further detail and reviews Inspiration 💡 Me and my wife live with disabilities. We are always on the lookout for accessible places to visit but finding accessible places are very hard . Several times we have visited a place which says it has a wheelchair ramp access in google maps only to find that they have a step inside. What it does AccessPath has community curated accessible places like shopping center, cafe, museum, recreational places etc. Places can be filtered according to different disabilities like mobility, visual, hearing and cognitive . Advanced filters like wheelchair access♿, braille signage⠃⠗⠁⠊⠇⠇⠑, audio descriptions🎧 etc. can help further to find places to visit for those with special needs. Accessible places can be added easily by searching and each place can be marked for the accessibility features they offer for specific disabilities. How we built it AccessPath was built 100% using ⚡Bolt! Prompt First prompt was based on the comment by my wife on the problem validation platform needgap - Standard websites that give general information about disability e.g. just having a wheelchair sign, are not always useful to the disabled because disability is different for each person and requires customised solutions. For instance, finding hotels with truly disabled friendly bathrooms is a big challenge. Having websites that provide pictures or videos of disabled facilities / other ways of accessing information about the hotel facilities are essential so that people with disabilities can make their own assessment as to whether the facilities are accessible for them.
