## Body

BusinessRadar | Spatial AI Urban Intelligence Transforming "gut feeling" into data-driven precision for the fastest-growing city in Central Asia. Inspiration Tashkent is booming, yet entrepreneurs lose millions on suboptimal locations. BusinessRadar was born to replace intuition with Spatial AI . We created a tool that actively interprets the city’s commercial pulse to eliminate investment risks and maximize urban potential. What it does BusinessRadar is a high-performance Urban Intelligence Platform designed for surgical decision-making: Smart Site Scoring | Instant AI Viability Score (0-10) based on foot traffic and demographics. Opportunity Heatmap | High-contrast data overlay identifying "Gold Zones" vs. saturated markets. Vibe Analytics | AI-driven district classification (e.g., Gen Z Hub vs. Corporate Center ) for brand alignment. AI Consultant Sidebar | Real-time advisor providing deep competitive insights and revenue forecasting. How we built it Engineered for speed and geospatial precision using a premium tech stack: Frontend: Next.js 13 + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion (Glassmorphism UI). Data Engine: OpenStreetMap (OSM) integrated with custom SQL via Bolt . Intelligence: Custom spatial algorithms and Gravity Models for urban flow calculation. Visuals: Leaflet for geo-rendering and Recharts for analytical dashboards. Challenges we ran into Data Fragmentation: Normalized raw, chaotic urban data into a production-ready engine. Latency Optimization: Re-engineered calculation cycles to provide zero-lag feedback on the map. Cultural Tuning: Iteratively adjusted AI weights to accurately reflect Tashkent’s unique district nuances. Accomplishments that we're proud of Live Deployment: Successfully mapped over 30+ live business locations with real-time scoring. Visual Synthesis: Developed a complex Opportunity Heatmap that translates millions of data points into intuitive insights. Efficiency: Built an investor-grade platform in record time using AI-first development methodologies. What's next for BusinessRadar Live Streams: Integration of traffic camera feeds for real-time pedestrian counting. Economic Layers: Real-time real estate pricing and ROI forecasting.
