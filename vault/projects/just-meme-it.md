# Just Meme It

> A daily meme battle where you submit memes, vote, and climb the leaderboard.

[Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/just-meme-it) · hackathon [[The Reddit Daily Games Hackathon]]

**stack** devvit, react, redis, typescript

## Body

Daily Captions Vote on others submissions Daily and Lifetime Leaderboards Streaks Leaderboards User Stats Just Meme It is our take on turning meme culture into a daily competitive game. Every day at 12 AM UTC, a new caption drops. Your job is simple: reply with an image or GIF that fits it. You can add your own text to make it hit harder. Then you swipe through other submissions, vote on the ones that actually make you laugh, and watch the best memes climb to the top. We built it to be fair. Before your submission is confirmed, you have to go through 5 randomly ordered memes first. Everything is randomized after that too, so early posts don’t dominate and no meme gets buried. Everyone gets a real shot. There are two ways to compete: Points leaderboard for overall performance Streak leaderboard for daily consistency You can also track your stats, wins, and streaks over time through a user panel. Reddit already runs on memes. We just turned that into a structured daily battle. <div
