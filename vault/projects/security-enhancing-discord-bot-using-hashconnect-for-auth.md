We sustain the concept of a world based on transparent and decentralized transactions, valuing it as being not only secure but also efficient and energy-friendly. In order to contribute to this revolutionary, but nonetheless practical movement we decided to build a security-enhancing Discord bot that uses Hashconnect and groundbreaking Hedera Strato JS for the identification process. We are looking at the future of a secure and free, decentralized world with enthusiasm and motivation, thus being inspired to contribute to its development. What it does it manages a target server to verify and authorize its users to specific channels depending on what role has been assigned to them. In other words, a verified or authorized role has its own access features to private channels, public ones being available for those with no such roles at all.
