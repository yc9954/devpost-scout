> An AI-powered smart note-taking assistant that converts lectures, PDFs, and voice recordings into concise notes, summaries, flashcards, and quizzes for efficient learning.

## Body

Inspiration Students often struggle to manage large amounts of study material from lectures, PDFs, and handwritten notes. Important concepts get buried in lengthy content, making revision time-consuming and stressful. We created NoteMind-AI to help students learn smarter by automatically converting study materials into concise notes, summaries, flashcards, and quizzes. What it does NoteMind-AI is an AI-powered learning assistant that helps students organize and understand their study materials efficiently. Users can upload PDFs, notes, or lecture content and instantly receive: Smart summaries Key point extraction AI-generated flashcards Practice quizzes Organized study notes Quick revision materials How we built it We developed the frontend using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript to provide a simple and responsive user interface. The backend was built with Python and Flask. AI-powered text processing is used to analyze educational content, generate summaries, create flashcards, and produce quiz questions automatically. Challenges we ran into Processing different types of educational content. Generating accurate summaries without losing important information. Creating meaningful quiz questions automatically. Designing a user-friendly interface within a limited hackathon timeframe. Accomplishments that we're proud of Successfully built an AI-powered study assistant. Automated note summarization and flashcard generation. Created a simple and intuitive user experience. Developed a scalable solution that can help students save study time. What we learned Through this project, we learned about AI-powered text analysis, educational technology solutions, backend integration, and user-centered design. We also improved our teamwork and problem-solving skills during development.
