Cover Gameplay view Lateral perspective with vehicles Lateral perspective with ball Inspiration Inspired by Tron’s fast-paced gameplay and Rocket League’s scoring system, Cannon League brings Horizon Worlds a new way to experience quick, casual games while providing a sense of accomplishment and epic moments. What it does Dive into intense 3v3 arena battles where scoring goals is just the beginning. Outplay your opponents using powerups: blast them with cannonballs, speed past them, or grow in size to dominate the field. Fast, chaotic, and endlessly fun, every match is a thrill ride from start to finish. How we built it Cannon League was built around the idea of quick games you can play as soon as you join the world. No need to wait for a match to end or sit through long loading screens, just jump in with a friend and start playing instantly! Challenges we ran into We created a custom movement system that takes advantage of both Horizon Worlds’ physics and the game’s framerate to offer a smooth, seamless experience. Accomplishments that we're proud of We spent countless hours refining the game feel, making every movement, shot, and goal deeply satisfying, whether you play for five minutes or fifty. We also created unique, neon-inspired visuals that brings the game atmosphere to life, blending fast-paced action with striking color contrasts and dynamic effects. The result is a distinctive, immersive arena where every match feels like a cinematic moment. What we learned We learned how to make vehicle movement as smooth as possible while maintaining an excellent framerate. What's next for Cannon League We’ve built the foundations of a live-service game that feels great and is fun to play.
