Inspiration Inspired to modernize the aluminium industry by creating smarter connections and AI-driven insights for businesses and users. What it does Twin Aluminia is a web platform that connects manufacturers, suppliers, and consumers in the aluminium ecosystem, offering AI-powered predictions, recommendations, and automated solutions. Accomplishments that I'm proud of Successfully launched a functional platform that delivers smart connectivity and AI-driven solutions for the aluminium industry.
