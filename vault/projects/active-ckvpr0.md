## Body

Inspiration The UNSGD on Health and Wellbeing made me question the health of the current generation. We maintain a sedentary lifestyle addicted to our phones and tablets, lets turn that for the better good, instead of wasting time on our devices lets use them to enhance our living experience. What it does Active + has fitbit integrations and provides the better way to display user data keeping screens clutter-free and focuses solely on user health, tools such as mindfulness and health calculators help keep our body pass the fit check. An intuitive UI is baked into the application so that its good on the eye. Challenges I ran into CSS as it is ruined the application and made me sad most of the time, I took a few deep breaths with the mindfulness tool and kept my mind stress free, but most of the part I fixed the CSS and it looks wonderful according to my peers.
