## Body

Cooked This 📖 What It Is Cooked This is like a fitness tracker but for your kitchen. It helps home cooks build consistency, variety, and momentum by logging meals, tracking progress, and celebrating milestones. Cooking stops feeling like a chore and becomes a skill you can track, level up, and feel proud of. This started as a personal challenge: I love cooking, but I kept rotating the same 4–5 meals. I wanted a way to try new recipes, track variety, and feel that same satisfaction you get from closing fitness rings or keeping a streak alive. At its heart, Cooked This asks: 👉 What if cooking could be as trackable and motivating as fitness? 🛠 How It’s Built iOS app made with: SwiftUI → clean, responsive interface SwiftData → first-time use for logging meals, photos, and notes OneSignal SDK → push notifications + in-app messages to: Gently remind you to log meals Keep streaks going Celebrate milestones dynamically 🚧 Challenges & Lessons Learned Choosing the right metrics Striking a balance between cooking frequency, variety, and batch meals to encourage routine and experimentation. Making logging effortless Logging meals takes just a few taps. Notes and photos are optional, keeping friction low so habits stick. Encouraging consistency Smart reminders and milestone celebrations, e.g.: “🎉 2 new dishes this month! Level up!” 📊 Key Insights Tracking progress turns cooking into a fun game Small wins and milestones motivate more than raw numbers Thoughtful reminders boost consistency without feeling annoying 💡 What’s Next More OneSignal engagement campaigns via email Home screen widgets showing streaks and stats Expanded cooking habit metrics Social sharing of achievements 🙏 Thanks! Thanks for checking out Cooked This ! I’d love your feedback, ideas, or stories about your own cooking habits. Natasha - Developer of Cooked This <div
