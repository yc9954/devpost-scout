Inspiration My experience with panic inducing music played during my first CTF hackathon. 🤪 Interested in exploratory use cases in LLM for sentiment analysis including music recommendation. 🤓 What it does Provide relevant music recommendations for a particular cybersecurity role and task/workflow. Note: "Relevant" for our purposes means the music matches the sentiment, general summary, and context of the initial prompt submitted for the cybersecurity workforce user that we have in mind based on what a cybersecurity expert (i.e. subject matter competency in cybersecurity work and related objects/behavior) and a music expert (i.e. subject matter competency in music, how it is classified, etc.🫶🏼 How we built it With our blood, sweat, and tears...And a modern js/typescript React frontend, Python API middleware/backend, and the power of Beethoveen.ai, and Nebius AI Studio enriched with data via the Spotify Web API.
