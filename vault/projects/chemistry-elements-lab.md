## Body

Head of the app Element filling place and reaction Q&A Inspiration My family is engaged in the chemical industry, but I know nothing about chemistry. I remember that in school, I learned chemistry by rote memorization, which was not fun at all. What it does It can make learning chemistry more interesting for users, and they can independently combine any two elements of the periodic table and then observe what chemical reactions will occur. How we built it Build with the no-code PartyRock platform. Challenges we ran into Because chemical elements are relatively abstract, it is difficult to describe whether the images generated by AI are accurate. Accomplishments that we're proud of Any combination of elements improves user autonomy and fun What we learned Although pictures cannot express the elements accurately, the interactive questions set up by the chemistry teacher at the end will help users understand the chemical elements better. What's next for Chemistry Elements Lab It is hoped that the necessary equipment and utensils needed to protect the experimenter can be added when the two chemical elements are reacted.
