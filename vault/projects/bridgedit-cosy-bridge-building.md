Tutorial Video: https://youtu.be/IG4I2xJDwg4 Inspiration Inspired by classics like Poly Bridge, BridgeDit! is a fresh take on the traditional physics simulator / bridge building puzzle genre which fully utilises Reddit Devvit's social features to build a community around this common puzzle genre. It is a showcase of what is possible on the Devvit platform - blending modern 3D graphics with user-generated content to let users build their own levels and contribute new content regularly . What it does Unleash your inner engineer! Negotiate challenging demands with your puzzle-solving skills by building bridges in all shapes and sizes for varying demands! Getting from point A to B has never been this fun. Compete for first place! Compete with other Redditors for lowest cost, shortest time, and more. Compare solutions and be inspired! User Generated Content - Level Builder - create and share your own puzzles! Create and share your own levels with the entire community with a complete puzzle-creation toolkit, right inside the game! Daily Challenges! New levels released regularly means you'll never get tired! Play anywhere - mobile and desktop! Take the bridge-building puzzle fun anywhere you go, with first-class support for both mobile and desktop. Earthquakes Tackle a new dimension in the bridge-building simulation world with unpredictable tremors which threaten to tumble your bridge. Make it your own - graphics themes! Make each bridge truly your own with varied graphics themes. How we built it Reddit Devvit Web, Three.js, Lots of hard work Challenges we ran into Graphics programming - as there were many possible graphical themes to execute, I ended up implementing three different game "skins" before settling on one. Accomplishments that we're proud of Building a whole bridge-building puzzle physics simulator game with modern 3D graphics in just 2 weeks! What we learned Embrace user-generated content - since creating daily puzzles for the game would be more convenient with a level editor anyway, I realised it would be sensible to polish it up for users to use too, enabling full user-generated content and refreshing new puzzles every day!
