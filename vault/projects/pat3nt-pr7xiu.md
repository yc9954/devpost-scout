## Body

Inspiration After taking building multiple cool projects and unique ideas, we were finding it difficult to find a platform that could tale our ideas and projects to the next level. Inspired by this, we came of a project to develop a platform that allows builders to keep motivated in their projects. What it does It is an NFT Based Patent system, in which users can create 3D NFT Cards for their ideas and increase the price of the NFT based on the progress and achievements achieved. Users can make offers to an idea that they would love to build further. For each offer made, the owner of the idea can accept and transfer the ownership or reject the offer and keep the building on the idea. Problems No place to find new innovations happening Hard to track progression Multiple half-developed projects No place for progression-based ranking Solution NFT-based Idea Patent System Variable price based on progression New revenue source Community showcase problem A launchpad for in-development projects Skill exposure to investors How we built it Unity (3D NFT generation) Next.JS (Frontend development) web3.js (Blockchain integration) Polygon Blockchain / Solidity (Smart Contract development) Chainlink Price Feeds (Conversion of Matic to USD) QuickNode (Blockchain Node Provider) IPFS/Filecoin (Metadata) Figma (UI Designing) Challenges we ran into Using the ERC721 token was one of the challenges when building the smart contract. There were not enough resources available, but we were finally able to implement that. Implementing Unity WebGL was one of the most complex tasks as well, but it was quite a great learning experience.
