ChainFlow - Blockchain Made Easy with Chainlink + Flow (ChainlinkSpring2023) 💡 Inspiration Blockchain is complicated. Did you know that although 10% of adult Americans (33 million!) intend to buy their first crypto asset, 36% of US citizens are not familiar with cryptocurrencies, and 29% find it too complicated to understand?* That's why we made ChainFlow! 🤖 ❓ What it does ChainFlow is a platform-agnostic solution that addresses the high barrier of entry to blockchain via an intuitive chatbot, making Web3 easier than ever. Mint Flow NFTs, access Chainlink's Data Feeds, and get answers to Web3-related questions across web, mobile, Discord, Slack, and Telegram! 🚧 How we built it Chainlink: Empowering users to access Chainlink's Data Feeds across web, mobile, Discord, Slack, and Telegram.
