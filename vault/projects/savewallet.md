## Body

Inspiration Tron Energy system allows users to save significant transaction fees amount, but more than 90% of Tron users are not using this features. This is mostly because utilizing Tron Energy's features requires users to go through multiple steps and have a deep understanding of its mechanisms. Upon discovering these opportunities and difficulties for users, we researched and built a solution that enhances user convenience by integrating the cost-saving transaction fee solution natively into the crypto wallet, allowing users to use it effortlessly, while still ensuring full utilization of all features. What it does Multi-Functional Integrated Wallet: Our application allows users easy access to and interaction with every decentralized application (DApp) and function on the Tron network. Minimize Transaction Fees: Leveraging in-depth knowledge of Tron resources to optimize transaction fees for users Enhance User Experience: Save Wallet UI is designed to minimize steps for users and provide them with a convenient experience Diverse Quest System: Diverse Task System for Users to Earn Points and Convert into Usable Energy. How we built it Our team had experience in developing wallet earlier. We recognize the potential of Energy system on Tron Network then we build a wallet that could save transaction fees for every users by renting Energy automatically. Challenges we ran into The most difficult challenge is gaining trust and awareness of Tron users, cause wallet is a sensitive app Accomplishments that we're proud of Our other product Tronsave have won the 1st prize on HackaTron S5 and gained so many supports from the community. We also helped users save their money and gained interest from our system What we learned The most vital thing we have learned is stick with the community, serve them well and continuously update what we build What's next for SaveWallet After applying for the Hackatron, the next step will be massive marketing campaigns to gain user awareness because there are many wallets in the market, onboarding users to a new wallets requires a massive campaign through out Tron Ecosystem <div
