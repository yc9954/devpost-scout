Connecting social profiles like Linkedin & Instagram Relationship analysis through whatsapp chats Ai for improving relationships AI-Twin Mood Tracking Gaming approach to improve cognitive brain crisis support to stop panic attacks Landing Page Mentorship from top physiatrist Dashboard Relationship analyzer Learning EQ Better facial mood detection based content recommendation ar based meditation center ar based nature view for resting and peer interaction Inspiration We started with one simple observation. The top 10% and the top 1% often have the same technical skills, the same education, and similar knowledge. What really separates them is emotional intelligence and cognitive clarity . The top 1% understand themselves better. They know how they react under stress, how they think, how they communicate, and how to regulate emotions. They make better decisions not because they know more — but because they understand themselves and others better . Most platforms today focus on productivity or mental health in isolation. Very few help people build emotional intelligence in a structured, engaging, and practical way . That gap is what inspired us to build MokshAI . What it does MokshAI is an AI-powered emotional intelligence and wellness platform. It helps users in two major ways: 1. Understanding yourself Tracks your emotions, moods, and thought patterns over time Helps you reflect on decisions, habits, and reactions Builds an AI Twin — a rational version of you that understands your goals, fears, and patterns Guides you step-by-step toward your dreams and long-term goals Offers meditation and calming AR environments for self-reflection 2. Understanding others and building better relationships Analyzes communication patterns (like WhatsApp chats) to show how relationships evolve Highlights empathy gaps, communication shifts, and emotional distance Helps you improve how you communicate, listen, and respond Enables peer interaction and mentorship through calm, immersive AR rooms 3. Growing cognitive and emotional skills Game-based challenges to improve awareness, focus, empathy, and response time Community challenges that make emotional growth fun and social Together, MokshAI helps users move from self-awareness → emotional control → stronger relationships → long-term growth . How I built it Built the frontend using Next.js for a smooth and accessible experience Used Supabase for authentication and data storage Designed automated workflows using n8n to connect different services Used AI models for emotion understanding, conversation analysis, and personalized guidance Created multiple AI agents, each focused on a specific role like reflection, guidance, crisis support, and mentorship Ran emotion detection directly on the frontend for better privacy Integrated WhatsApp and voice-based interactions to meet users where they already are The focus was always on user experience and emotional safety , not just technology. Challenges I ran into Understanding emotions is deeply personal — one solution does not fit everyone Making AI feel helpful without sounding generic or robotic Handling sensitive emotional data responsibly and privately Balancing flexibility of conversation while still using structured personal context Designing features that support users without overwhelming them Each challenge pushed us to simplify, listen more, and design with empathy. Accomplishments that I'm proud of Built an AI Twin that feels personal and reflective, not generic Created tools that help people understand their relationships, not just themselves Designed a system that is privacy-first and user-centric Combined emotional wellness, mentorship, collaboration, and games into one ecosystem Focused on real human problems instead of just building features What I learned Emotional intelligence cannot be taught through static questionnaires People open up more when tools feel familiar and non-judgmental AI is most powerful when it listens first, then guides Simplicity builds trust, especially in emotional wellness Growth happens when reflection feels safe and personal What's next for MokshAI Expand mentorship and counselor support Add more cognitive and empathy-based games Improve personalization using long-term emotional patterns Partner with universities and organizations for real-world pilots Continue building MokshAI as a lifelong companion for emotional growth Because the future belongs not just to those who think fast — but to those who understand deeply . <div
