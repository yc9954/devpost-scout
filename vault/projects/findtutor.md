## Body

FindTutor: A Simple and Efficient Tutor-Student Matching Platform FindTutor is an open-source web application designed to help students find tutors based on their location and subjects of interest. The platform allows both students and tutors to register, making it easier to connect learners with educators. The registration process is simple, offering both manual sign-up and Google sign-up options. Unlike some platforms where tutors apply for students, FindTutor operates differently—only students can search for tutors. They can filter their search based on location and subject expertise, ensuring they find the right match for their learning needs. Once a student selects a tutor, they can contact them directly via the phone number provided in the tutor’s profile. This direct communication method eliminates the need for in-app messaging, making the process straightforward. Currently, the platform does not have a tutor verification process, meaning any tutor can sign up and offer their services. FindTutor provides a user-friendly experience, making it an effective tool for students who need academic support. As an open-source project, it also allows developers to contribute to its improvement.
