Daily Outfit Picker Intended purpose for my app: Ever had those mornings where you stand in front of your closet, staring blankly at your clothes, unsure of what to wear? Yeah, I've been there too. That's why I came up with “Daily Outfit Picker” – to take the hassle out of picking your daily look and make mornings a little easier. What’s AWS PartyRock? PartyRock is an Amazon Bedrock Playground. It's a fun, generative AI app-building playground that allows you to create a variety of apps effortlessly. What it does: Daily Outfit Picker simplifies the process of selecting an outfit by leveraging AI technology to generate personalized recommendations. Users input their style preferences into the app, and it delivers outfit suggestions as images that align with their individual tastes and activities. * How to get started with building your AI-powered app using PartyRock: * Start by building an AI-powered app without writing any code. Sign up for PartyRock and explore the PartyRock Console. Describe the type of app you want in plain English, and PartyRock swiftly generates it using Amazon BedRock. Customize your app by editing widgets or adding new ones to your playground. Choose from various widget types like user input, static text, image generator, and chatbot to fit your needs. Select the "User Input" widget to provide your preferences. For example, you can specify that you prefer blouses and pants in bright colors. (Example: "I prefer blouses and pants in bright colors.") Refine prompts within widgets to tailor the app's functionality. Use @<yourinput> to use output from one prompt as input for another. Customize the AI model to suit your preferences. Adjust widget sizes for the desired layout. Use the "Image Generator" widget to automatically generate AI-generated images based on your prompts. Incorporate a feedback prompt to express gratitude for positive feedback and invite suggestions for future recommendations. Share your app with a click, making it public and accessible to others.
