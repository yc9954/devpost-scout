## Body

Inspiration Only 21% of real estate agents in the U.S. work with commercial properties, leaving most people without the knowledge or access they need. We built our modern property management system to change that. Everyone should be able to explore, understand, and manage real estate with confidence, whether you’re an experienced investor or just starting out. Our platform gives people the clarity and control they deserve, helping them make smarter decisions and uncover real opportunities. Real estate should be open, simple, and accessible to everyone. That’s what we’re building. What it does Cura provides forecasting and analytical insights, commercial real estate properties, pros and cons analysis, and market sentiment. It helps users make informed investment decisions through data-driven insights, interactive property comparisons, and real-time market trends. With Cura, users can explore opportunities, evaluate risks, and understand how each property aligns with their financial goals. Whether managing a portfolio or searching for a first commercial space, Cura brings clarity, confidence, and strategy to every decision. How we built it Cura is built around agentic-driven navigation. The system is designed to think and reason through user input, adapting layouts and responses based on context. From reasoning to web search to analytics, Cura connects each step to create a seamless and intelligent user experience.
