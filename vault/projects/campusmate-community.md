Hero Page Empty chat Chat Page Feed Page Inspiration Why limit yourself to the traditional methods of formal education. Why limit your social circle within your college? What’s CampusMate? CampusMate Community is a platform that connects you to like-minded students across cities, states, countries studying in different schools One solution, to solve all problems Reach out to students from different colleges, collaborate and share your opinions. What it does Community Main Features Groups Users can create groups to let other users join and talk. 1 - 1 Chat Direct messages Channels Create different channels for different topics. Feed Beautiful feed to share your learnings, ideas and much more. How we built it Huge thanks to stream , because this is not something that can be achieved in 3 days. Chat and feed are really complex things to build from scratch, stream SDKs made these really simple. Challenges we ran into This is our first time using stream, so we had a struggle integrating and customizing according to our needs. And also got so many issues and bugs as all developers face. Time management. Accomplishments that we're proud of We built a great community platform for students by connecting to students around the world. What we learned Learned how to integrate stream chat and feed SDKs in our web app. As this is our first building a feed feature, we learned so many like types of feeds, parts of a feed post, how to connect all the things and more. What's next for CampusMate - Community We found this idea really interesting, so planning to make this a real community. We are also planning to add other features like challenges and collaboration. <div
