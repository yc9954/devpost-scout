## Body

All image styles generated for a robot with laser wyes The Image Style Tester app, utilizing the Stable Diffusion XL engine, revolutionizes the way users can explore and apply 17 distinct art styles through the Party Rock image generator widget. This tool not only offers an array of presets like 3D model, anime, cinematic, and more, but also integrates a unique widget system for each style. Additionally, it features a special section with three widgets that demonstrate the impact of varying CFG (classifier-free guidance) values, which control the balance between creativity and adherence to the image prompt, with a recommended default CFG value of 10 for optimal image generation fidelity. Serving both developers and users within the Party Rock community, the app facilitates a creative playground for generating custom images while experimenting with different artistic styles and CFG levels. For builders, it's an invaluable resource to understand and select the most fitting styles and settings for their projects, enhancing the development process.
