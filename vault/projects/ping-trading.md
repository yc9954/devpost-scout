## Body

Price charts for trading pairs A simple and multi-functional trading widget Easy transition between trading pairs Inspiration There are currently 300 million cryptocurrency users globally. Not all users currently understand liquidity pools and swaps. This is potential friction to adopt Osmosis Swaps. How many more users would trade on Osmosis and discover Cosmos-based tokens if these frictions disappear? Our goal is for users to buy and sell comfortably on Osmosis without having to understand liquidity swaps. What it does Our project is a classic trading interface as an alternative way for users to swap, just like in other traditional exchanges. It is complete with price charts for all trading pairs, and users can explore different charts without changing views. The price chart is positioned next to the trading widget for ease of analysis. Users can easily deposit into their balance from the trading widget and immediately execute a trade. How we built it We pulled in data from sources and constructed price charts for every trading pair. We redesigned the trading concept from swaps to buy/sell – similar to a CEX.
