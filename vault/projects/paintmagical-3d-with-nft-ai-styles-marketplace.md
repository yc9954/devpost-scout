## Body

Create your masterpiece painting or 3D mesh in seconds in a skeuomorphic mobile app - and share your styles in an NFT marketplace PaintMagical AI3D is an iOS/Android app that lets ANYONE paint to create 3D objects using AI... Begin by describing to the AI what you want to make - and then start sketching it. A rough sketch is perfectly fine. AI can help us refine it. We select a Style from our Style Marketplace. Once we have a preview image we like, we press the holographic AI3D sticker button to begin our image to 3D process. As we are waiting PaintMagical is a platform that lets anyone create their own Styles from submitting either a LORA link or between 5 and 10 good photos representing their Style. Using Real-Time LCM mode: You can actually be really bad at drawing - like do giant finger paint blobs and have it instantly turned into better looking images. And when we are ready, we do image to 3D. And let's view it in AR PaintMagical is democratic AI 3D creation for mobile - designed to work on all 7 billion+ mobile devices with AR being optional.
