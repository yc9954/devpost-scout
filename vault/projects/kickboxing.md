# Kickboxing

> Perfect Timing. Perfect Strike. Ultimate Kickboxing Duel.

[Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/kickboxing) · hackathon [[Play Everywhere- The Build with Snap Games Lensathon]]

## Facets


**stack** blender, javascript, lensstudio

## Body

Inspiration We wanted to build a competitive game that rewards skill, not randomness. Inspired by rhythm games and real kickboxing timing drills, the idea was simple: a perfectly timed strike should feel powerful and satisfying. Adding Bitmoji players made it personal, social, and fun, turning a precision challenge into a friendly duel. What it does Kickboxing is a turn-based kickboxing game where two Bitmoji players compete across 3 rounds. Each turn, a player strikes using a moving meter: • Hitting the perfect center gives the maximum score of 100 • Accuracy directly impacts performance • Players alternate turns, keeping the game fair and skill-focused The winner is decided based on total performance across all rounds. 🛠️ How we built it • Designed a precision-based meter system to calculate accuracy • Implemented turn-based logic for two Bitmoji players • Structured gameplay into 3 clear rounds with score tracking • Used visual and timing feedback to make perfect hits feel rewarding • Optimized interactions for quick, repeatable play sessions Challenges we ran into • Balancing the meter so it feels challenging but fair • Ensuring consistent timing behavior across turns • Managing clean round transitions without breaking game flow • Keeping the game competitive while remaining accessible to casual players Accomplishments that we’re proud of • Built a skill-first competitive game without randomness • Created an engaging two-player Bitmoji experience • Delivered a complete 3-round combat loop • Made the gameplay easy to understand but hard to master • Achieved smooth pacing suitable for short, replayable sessions What we learned • Small timing mechanics can create high emotional impact • Turn-based systems work extremely well for social AR games • Clear visual feedback is crucial for skill-based gameplay • Simplicity in rules leads to deeper competition What’s next for Kickboxing • Power-ups and special moves for perfect streaks • New punch/kick styles and meter variations • Online turn-based multiplayer & leaderboards • More fighters, arenas, and difficulty modes <div
