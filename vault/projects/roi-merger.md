## Body

Inspiration Organizations struggle to connect financial metrics like ROI and capital efficiency with real-time operational performance. We wanted to bridge financial strategy with execution analytics in one intelligent platform. What it does The system analyzes ROI and equity data alongside production and task metrics to provide real-time dashboards, capital allocation insights, and bottleneck predictions for strategic planning. How we built it We built the backend using data analytics and optimization models, integrated financial and operational datasets, and developed a real-time dashboard for visualization and performance tracking. Challenges we ran into Integrating financial models with operational data, defining measurable capital metrics, and ensuring accurate bottleneck prediction across dynamic workloads were key challenges. Accomplishments that we're proud of We learned how financial theory, production functions, and real-time data systems can work together to drive smarter business decisions.
