Quiz page Quiz page Topic list Subjects HomePage Subjects HomePage SubjectsList Edujoy: A Gamified Quiz Platform for Learning Inspiration Edujoy was inspired by the need to make learning more engaging and interactive. Traditional educational methods often fail to capture the interest of students, leading to disengagement and lack of motivation. By incorporating game mechanics into the learning process, Edujoy aims to create a fun and immersive experience that encourages students to actively participate and enjoy their learning journey. What We Learned Building Edujoy taught us the importance of user engagement and the power of gamification in education. We gained valuable insights into designing user-friendly interfaces and creating interactive content that keeps learners motivated.
