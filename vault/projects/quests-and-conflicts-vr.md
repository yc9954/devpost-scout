Inspiration We're all nerds at heart and for the Netflix "Beyond the Binge" challenge we chose to highlight that passion with our story taking inspiration from classic TTRPGs like "Dungeon & Dragons". We wanted to make our own VR D&D campaign that would be charming and lead to many fun paths. How we built it We used Unity, Blender, and Mixamo for our animations, scripting and VR development needs. Challenges we ran into Packing a lot of story and VR interactions within the span of a day. Managing our time was key to completing this demo to an MVP format. Accomplishments that we're proud of What we learned Time management. And even some cool blender and animation tricks.
