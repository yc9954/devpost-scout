## Body

Start of the app Image Gallery Music Gallery Picture opened Inspiration I love the blockchain NEAR, and I love the artists NFT, I wanted to hear my musics NFT on the go, so I decided to do it. What it does Is an amazing Gallery, not just images (although this would be also nice), but 1 (Or 2) steps more, is an a music, videos and docs Gallery. How we built it I built it for iOS/Android mainly, but thanks to Dart/Flutter it can be launched on web too. Challenges we ran into A lot, making too much http request on a mobile app, a little differences between Paras and Mintbase NFTs, and no enought info about specific details for the request to the nodes. Accomplishments that we're proud of I am proud that the app works, I worked in every little detail, even pixel by pixel and request to request, I accomplish the goal that I propose to myself. What we learned I learn a lot of new things, we never stop learning, the blockchain can offer us more than we know and more than we can imaginate, the is no limited to computers, but to every device. What's next for NFT Gallery Be launched and receive thousands of users and be THE APP that the users will be using for see and show their NFTs. <div
