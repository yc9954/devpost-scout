## Body

Inspiration We want to make the life of cancer patients easier. And we like to help research to get to more valuable data in a short period of time. With the app the health of every cancer patient can be monitored more closely and ajustments for their medication can be done faster. What it does Help cancer patients complete an eprom survey in an efficient and time saving way. It should always be a delightful thing to do and never a burden. The user should give data but also receive data and get an instant gratification with that. With the cough counter the patient can monitor their own health. The doctor is only a fingertip away. How we built it On paper, with figma, vue and Javascript Challenges we ran into Not many. We worked fine as a team of two. But the lack of sleep is hard. Accomplishments that we're proud of We love our solution and are proud of all we could accomplish in this short amount of time. Let’s keep our fingers crossed that Roche will like it too and finally the people we did it for can enjoy the app in their everyday life. What we learned Sleep is overrated, Figma plugins do not always help, Hack Zurich rocks! And so much more…. We’re very happy to be part of this! What's next for Hello Peter First of all we would like to test our ideas with real life users. See if we could help them in their daily life. Next ship this app. And after that work on new features like ‘match making in close distant’. If there is a person with cancer in close distance the app could alert and the persons could meet in real life or over the app. Gamification could further improve the usability of the app. <div
