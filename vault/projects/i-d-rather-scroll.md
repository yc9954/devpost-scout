## Body

Inspiration I'm addicted to scrolling. Instead of trying to fix that, I decided to try and make scrolling productive. What it does I'd rather scroll takes your notes and turns them into short "reels" for you to study. How we built it Used Google Gemini to read the file and extract its key points, and to turn it into content. From there, we used AI voice generation as well as pixabay for video backgrounds.
