## Body

Inspiration Cyber aesthetics (branded tech, neon urbanism, augmented bodies) and moral questions to society: spectatorship vs. empathy, consent under sponsorship, identity as performance, and how entertainment normalizes violence. What it does Introduces a stylized near-future where arena shows shape culture & identity; Chapter 1 sets tone, rules, and key characters (world first, decisions later). How we built it Lookdev: FLUX, Krea, Higgsfield (+ Freepik/FAL) Video: Veo, Kling, Higgsfield Audio: ElevenLabs, ThinkSound Upscale: Topaz. Challenges we ran into Readable action in 9:16; consistent character/look across models; fast-cut timing & mix without going graphic. Accomplishments that we're proud of Cohesive vertical action style; stable FLUX/Krea/Higgsfield → Veo/Kling pipeline; sponsor-aligned tool usage. What we learned Hook→reveal→reaction beats boost retention; shorter-lens looks & bold silhouettes aid clarity in 9:16.
