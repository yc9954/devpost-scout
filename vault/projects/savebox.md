Aiders Landing Page About Us Page NFTs Market Page Reward Page Trading Market Page NFTs Collectibles Inspiration We believe that every action deserves recognition and every user deserves to be rewarded. That's why we have developed a unique NFT reward system that allows users to earn valuable digital assets by simply performing tasks and engaging with our platform. Whether it's completing surveys, participating in quizzes, watching videos, or providing feedback, every interaction is an opportunity to unlock exclusive NFTs that hold real-world value. What it does We offer a seamless crypto-to-cash converter. This feature allows users to easily convert their earned NFTs into traditional currency, giving them the freedom to use their rewards in the real world. Additionally, users can also redeem their NFTs for exciting gift cards from popular brands, further enhancing the value and utility of their digital assets. We're not just focused on rewarding our users. We're also dedicated to empowering advertisers and content creators. With Aiders, publishers can onboard and trade their services, connecting with our engaged community and leveraging the power of NFTs to monetize their content. This creates a win-win situation, where advertisers can reach a highly targeted audience and content creators can unlock new revenue streams. We strongly believe in the power of giving back, which is why our platform incorporates a robust charity and donation feature. Through our blockchain technology, users can contribute to various projects, fund causes related to health, disaster relief, emergencies, and support charitable initiatives. By using our platform, you're not only enhancing your digital experience but also making a positive impact on the world. Aiders also serves as a secure and efficient payment gateway for goods and services. Users can easily send and receive funds, making transactions faster, more transparent, and hassle-free. Our platform ensures the utmost security and privacy, utilizing the latest encryption and blockchain technology to protect your digital assets and personal information. How we built it We came up with an idea then we sketched a diagram of the idea and made use of figma to design the interfaces. Then coming to the coding aspect of the project we made use of react, javascript, html 5, and flutter for both the web and mobile application front end, then we used solidity for the smart contract to deploy smart contact on fantom blockchain. Then the backend we used node.js, firebase, and php for the backend. Challenges we ran into One of the challenges we ran into in the course of this project was, technological complexity. Developing a platform that integrates NFT functionality, crypto transactions, and multiple features requires expertise in blockchain technology, smart contracts, and secure payment gateways. Ensuring the seamless integration of these components can be technically challenging. Another one is the user adoption. Building a successful platform heavily relies on user adoption and engagement, encouraging users to actively participate in tasks, redeem NFTs, and engage with advertisers and content creators requires a well-designed user experience, compelling incentives, and effective marketing strategies. Others are: Security and trust, Partnerships and integration, Community management and Market competition. Accomplishments that we're proud of We were able to come up with an idea that provide sustainability for users doing what they love to do best with an access to the crypto market, NFTs, gift cards and explore more opportunities on the blockchain. What we learned We learnt to use new technologies like building on fantom blockchain and be creative with our ideas and we also learnt to conduct research on various aspect of the project. We also learned to work as a team.
