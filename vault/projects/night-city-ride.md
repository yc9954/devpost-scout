Map Drift Cars Race Cars example interior 01 example interior 02 example interior 03 example interior 04 Inspiration Night City Ride started from a simple wish: capture that late-night “one more run” feeling—neon streets, wet asphalt, engine echoes—and turn it into a social, mobile-first playground where anyone can drift with friends in seconds. What it does Night City Ride is a multiplayer night-driving and drifting experience. Players pick from 10 distinct cars (each with unique handling and custom SFX), cruise through a neon city, and chain drifts to build score. Global leaderboards reward clean lines, long combos, and style. Lobbies let you free-drive, race to checkpoints, or join “drift trains” where you earn bonus points for synchronized runs with other players. How we built it We built the world for smooth mobile play: simple touch-friendly controls, readable UI, short-session objectives, and lightweight VFX that still feel cinematic at night. Networking syncs position, scoring, and combo states so drift battles stay fair. Audio is layered (engine, tire, skid, and city ambience) to keep feedback clear even on phone speakers. Challenges we ran into Balancing believable drift physics with accessible controls, keeping performance stable on mobile, and preventing leaderboard exploits were the toughest problems—especially under real-time multiplayer latency. Accomplishments that we're proud of A fast onboarding flow, 10 cars that truly feel different, and competitive leaderboards that make every run meaningful. What we learned Mobile-first readability, instant feedback, and short rewards loops matter more than maximum complexity.
