## Body

Inspiration To create an action-packed game that brings the exhilaration of battle to your phone or any system you're using! What it does Challenges your hand-eye coordination in a battle of speed and wits. How we built it We were able to create such an elaborate gamer by leveraging assets from another game we created, "RC Aircraft WWII v02 edition." Challenges we ran into Making the way you interact with the gun, between Desktop VR and Mobile. Accomplishments that we're proud of The Aircraft flying system is a work of artistry and flexibility. The level of audio and SFX in the game is remarkable. What we learned Get ahead of the interaction on mobile and VR. What's next for Operation Iron Shield Adding more aspects to the game such as additional guns, and weather effects. Other types of war games.
