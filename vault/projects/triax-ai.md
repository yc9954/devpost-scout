## Body

The Problem: A $10 Billion Education Gap 78% of engineering students never touch CAD software before their junior year. Why? Professional CAD licenses cost $2,000-$7,000 per year CAD software requires high-end computers/laptops - most students can't afford the hardware Traditional tutorials are passive and boring No real-time feedback = slow learning curves Students give up before mastering basics Underprivileged students have zero access to professional design tools Result: Companies spend $50K+ per engineer on CAD training. Universities can't afford enough licenses. Underrepresented communities are locked out of engineering careers. Talent pipeline is broken. The Solution: AI-Powered Interactive Learning Triax AI is the browser-based 3D design platform that combines: Real-time AI coaching - Like having a CAD expert beside you Interactive challenges - Learn by doing, not watching Instant model assessment - AI evaluates designs automatically Lightweight & accessible - Runs on basic devices, unlike traditional CAD software that needs top-class computers Zero installation - Works in any browser, any device 100% free - Accessible to underprivileged students worldwide Key Features Professional CAD Tools Full suite of 3D modeling tools accessible in the browser: Primitive shapes: Cubes, spheres, cylinders, cones Transform controls: Move, rotate, and scale with precision Boolean operations: Union, subtract, and intersect objects Measurement tools: Accurate dimensional control Material and color customization Playground Mode Free-form modeling environment where users can: Build anything they imagine without restrictions Experiment with all CAD tools Practice techniques learned from challenges Create personal projects and designs Save and export creations Challenge-Based Learning System Progressive difficulty levels that simulate real-world engineering tasks: Basic Challenges: Primitive shapes, transformations, and measurements Intermediate Challenges: Precision modeling, constraints, and assemblies Advanced Challenges: Complex mechanical parts and engineering drawings Real-time feedback on every design decision Achievement system to track progress and maintain motivation AI-Powered Learning Assistant Step-by-step guidance for every challenge Design feedback and error correction Best practice recommendations Troubleshooting help in natural language Automated Design Assessment AI evaluates designs based on: Dimensional accuracy Design intent Manufacturing feasibility Industry standards Technical Challenges Overcome Lightweight Architecture Most 3D web applications are resource-heavy, requiring high-end devices. Triax AI was engineered to be lightweight and accessible : Optimized rendering pipeline for low-end devices Runs smoothly on basic smartphones and older computers Minimal bandwidth requirements for underprivileged areas No powerful GPU required - works on integrated graphics Geometric Computation Issues Faced complex geometric calculation problems during development: Boolean operations causing mesh corruption Precision errors in 3D transformations Performance bottlenecks in real-time rendering Solution: Implemented optimized algorithms and efficient data structures to ensure accurate, fast 3D operations on any device <div
