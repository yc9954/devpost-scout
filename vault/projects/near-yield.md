Our platform intelligently utilizes the power of AI to match users with the most lucrative yield opportunities, and Near's Chain Abstraction to reach out to countless blockchains, streamlining the entire process of DeFi investment for the masses on over 110 blockchains and more than 3000 active protocols. Key Features ⚡️ Cross-Chain Investment 🌐: Unlock opportunities on multiple blockchains while staying within the NEAR ecosystem. No need for complex bridging or manual asset transfers—Near Yield handles the heavy lifting. AI-Powered Yield Matching 🤖💡: Our integrated AI model analyzes a wide range of protocols and yield farming opportunities across different chains. It suggests the best yield strategies tailored to your risk profile, helping you maximize your returns with minimal effort. Customizable Risk Levels 🎚️: Whether you're a seasoned "degen" looking for high-risk, high-reward strategies, or someone who prefers safer, more reliable yield generation methods, Near Yield has you covered. Select your risk tolerance and let the platform do the rest. Seamless UX 🖥️: Our intuitive interface makes it easy for both beginners and advanced users to explore DeFi without needing in-depth blockchain knowledge. Near Yield abstracts away the technicalities, allowing you to focus on growing your portfolio. Near Yield Advantages 🎯 1. Simple Multi-chain Investments with no Configuration 🔄 2. All within the secure, scalable NEAR ecosystem 🛡️ 7. No Promoted Content or Platforms, Based on Transparent Blockchain Data 🔍 Technical Information 🔧 Near Yield is built on a robust technical foundation designed to streamline multi-chain DeFi investments while ensuring user security and simplicity. self hosted n8n schema to retrieve and vectorize data from defillama every 24h + self hosted LLMs and RAG using Ollama. More details on github AI Data Direct Feed Chain 📡: The platform’s AI model is powered by real-time, on-chain data 📊. This data is directly fed into the model, allowing it to analyze and identify the most high-performing yield combinations across various blockchains. By using accurate, live data, Near Yield offers users up-to-date, optimized yield strategies tailored to their risk profiles. Self-Hosted AI Infrastructure 🤖: Near Yield runs its AI agents locally using n8n for workflow automation and Ollama for model deployment. Our stack includes Llama 3.1 for language understanding 🧠, embedding retrieval for efficient data search 🔍, and Mixtral/OpenAI for strategic analysis 🎯. By self-hosting, we ensure data privacy, minimize latency, and maintain complete control over our AI processes, delivering fast and secure DeFi investment strategies ⚡. EVM Address Derivation 🔗: Near Yield ensures that users maintain full ownership of their investments when interacting with EVM-compatible blockchains. Through EVM address derivation, users' NEAR addresses are securely mapped to EVM addresses, allowing them to interact with DeFi protocols on chains like Ethereum or Binance Smart Chain while retaining ownership and control over their assets 🔒. Chain Abstraction ⚙️: The core of Near Yield is its seamless chain abstraction technology. This allows users to interact with investment protocols across multiple blockchains directly from NEAR, without needing to switch between chains or manage complex bridge processes. The abstraction layer simplifies interactions with different blockchain ecosystems, making multi-chain DeFi investments as easy as a few clicks. Future Roadmap 🛣️ Q4 2024 - Mainnet Launch, Yield Farming Strategies on EVM & Non-EVM Blockchains Near Yield will introduce user-friendly staking strategies for EVM-compatible blockchains like Ethereum, Binance Smart Chain, and Polygon, and for non-EVM chains like Bitcoin or Cosmos. The initial Yield Farming AI Advisor will be launched on mainnet, allowing users to earn yield 📈, with the platform’s AI optimizing strategies for maximum returns based on risk profiles. Q1 - Q2 2025 - Live AI Investment Suggestions Our AI model will start updating Near Yield users about more profitable categories of investment through global platform notifications. This will allow users to explore new investment fields. Live updates will also be sent regarding more profitable protocols to invest into, based on which investment categories the user is more interested in. Through this Near Yield will essentially become a live, interactive and self-updating platform, informing about the best opportunities in Web3, while giving them direct access to them. Q4 2025 - Auto Investment & Management and Agent Marketplace Users will have the opportunity to let our AI model take the lead and update their portfolio live as new opportunities appear, based on their preferences in investment categories, and risk level. There will also be a degen option that can allow the AI model to independently manage platform selection, category selection, and risk assessment, in order to generate the highest yield possible. Finally we will launch an AI Investment Agent Marketplace, that will be used for more specific agents, such as: Lending & Borrowing Functions, Arbitrage Scouting & Execution, Leveraged Trading etc. Users will eventually be able to sell their own Investment Agents on the marketplace, opening the doors for Near Yield to become an ever-evolving platform.
