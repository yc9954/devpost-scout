# Jarvis: An agentic AI operator that does everything for you.

> AI operator with vision that autonomously controls your computer (mouse, keyboard & apps) completely through your voice (or text) input, and uses reasoning to handle complex tasks end-to-end.

[Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/jarvis-an-agentic-voice-assistant-that-does-things-for-you) · hackathon [[Cal Hacks 12.0]]

## Facets


**stack** claude, elevenlabs, gemini, httpx, openai, pillow(pil), pyautogui, pyobjc, pyperclip, pyscreeze, python, sounddevice, soundfile

## Body

For millions of people with mobility impairments, computers remain unusable. Current accessibility tools like dictation or Voice Control are limited to very simple commands. These individuals need an assistant that can truly understand their screen, their context, and their intent, and do the right things for them. This was the inspiration and motivation needed to build Jarvis this weekend. <div
