Output Screen Output Screen 2 Homepage Generating output Inspiration I wanted to create a tool that creative writers and students can learn and play with. I came up with the idea of a creative prompt generator - one that takes seed words as input and generates creative prompts for writing projects. Like a brainstorming session, but you don't need multiple members to submit their own ideas in a hat, just a word and you can get 5 strips of ideas to work on! What it does Creative PromptR helps students and writers generate ideas for writing with single seed words. How we built it Prototyping ideas with PartyRock AWS app builder Challenges we ran into Its out-of-the-box simplicity can be a limitation on what ideas to work on. You almost have to try it out for a day, learn the features and limitations of the environment, take a break from the app before working on more ideas.
