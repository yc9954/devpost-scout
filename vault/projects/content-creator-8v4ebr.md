Inspiration The idea for Quest Craft: The RPG Generator was born out of a shared passion for storytelling and role-playing games (RPGs) among our team members. We were inspired by the limitless creativity and immersive experiences offered by RPGs, but we also recognized the challenges that aspiring game developers face when trying to bring their visions to life.
