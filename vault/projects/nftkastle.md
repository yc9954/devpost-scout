## Body

✨ Inspiration The eagerness to provide solutions to the issue of low-quality NFT projects and games was a major inspiration. This idea came up because a big part of our combined interest was in the NFT utility. When more and more NFT platforms came out, we were researching existing NFT marketplaces and their limitations. We saw incredible chains that lack the NFT utility in scale, which stops them from revealing their true potential. As the crypto space evolved, and the infrastructure got improved, we decided to create an all-in-one solution that will ease the user experience of the NFTverse. We have identified the issue of lack of quality NFT projects and games, despite its advanced technology, and we thought it was because of the lack of robust NFT infrastructure. So our team has decided to develop just that - an all-in-one NFT infrastructure that combines the NFT marketplace, NFT minting platform, NFT Staking, NFT Editor for content creation, NFT Drawing, and minting NFT materialization service, and A bounty to win NFTs. ⚖ What it does NFT MarketPlace List, Buy and Sell your NFTs on the marketplace. NFT Staking Stake your NFTs, and get rewards. Picture Editor for Content creators Edit your picture, mint it, and use it as NFT on Hashnode, Twitter, Youtube, and other social media platforms. Draw, and mint NFT You will be provided with a board and color grids to draw a painting and mint it as NFT. Play Game and win NFTs We have a game and a leaderboard, you can play the game to win NFTs. The highest scorer in the game at the end of every month will be given our customized NFT. ⚙ How we built it 🛠 Technology Stack & Tools Solidity (Writing Smart Contract) Javascript (NextJs & Testing) Ethers (Blockchain Interaction) hardhat (Development Framework) AWS (Hosting and Database) IPFS (File hosting) ⛓ Blockchain Protocol used ERC-721 standard 😫 Challenges we ran into Budget . We are a small company building NFTKastle, so we have limited funds, and things can be quite costly. 🎉 Accomplishments that we're proud of We are glad to have been able to complete half of what we intended for this project. The progression from the birth of this idea to the establishment of an impactful project is something to be proud of. 🎗 What we learned Building this project gave us the opportunity to learn more about web3 and blockchain development as a whole. We also learned how to verify our smart contract on polygonScan using hardhat. 🔭 What's next for NFTkastle Social Features . Enabling users following, NFT likings, commenting, and direct chatting (soon) Enabling social interactions and chat service. NFT Ticket . Enable users to sell their tickets as NFTs on the blockchain. Invitation Cards . Enable users to give out their invitation cards as NFTs on the blockchain. Launch of NFTKastle DAO. NFTKastle Governance Forum is a platform for a higher level of decentralization. Scaling Faster Further and Beyond. We workaholics at nftKastle are building our platform with scaling in mind from day one. As our platform is live on several main nets and more and more users are joining, we are anticipating exponential growth in our partner chains. <div
