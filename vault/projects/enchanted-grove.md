Enchanted Grove Game Interface Project Description: Enchanted Grove Inspiration Enchanted Grove finds its roots in a profound love for text adventure games, driven by the captivating and interactive narratives that have enthralled players for decades. Functionality Enchanted Grove is a text adventure game where players embody Aria, navigating through scenes filled with challenges and encounters with imaginative creatures. The game ensures a unique experience through carefully crafted prompts while maintaining a consistent plot in each playthrough. Beyond being a game, Enchanted Grove serves as an experiential journey aimed at instilling compassion and understanding in players. The narrative unfolds through choices, challenges, and interactions, encouraging players to explore empathy within the magical world presented in the game. Development Process Initially, I crafted a script for the game, delineating the environment and protagonist (main character). Next, I detailed each scene along the journey. Once the adventure's plot was in place, I transitioned to the second stage. Achieving required guidance from Claude LLM through the described scenes involved several iterations and considerable experimentation. It took a comprehensive prompt of approximately 1200 words to ensure the desired player guidance! Challenges Overcome Balancing narrative consistency and dynamic content with Claude LLMs presented challenges. Ensuring players follow the same overarching plot while introducing variations in each scene required tweaking prompts extensively. Proud Accomplishments Effectively communicating creative ideas to Claude LLM marked a pivotal accomplishment. Persistence and experimentation resulted in breakthroughs, ensuring the realization of the envisioned narrative and overcoming challenges posed by Claude LLM's dynamic nature. Key Learnings Mastery of working with Claude LLM involved adapting strategies, refining prompts, and comprehending language model collaboration nuances. Structured prompts proved effective in controlling Claude LLM responses. Future Plans Enchanted Grove is currently a prototype, with plans to expand its plot, adding diversity and depth. The goal is to maintain player engagement for at least 2-3 hours.
