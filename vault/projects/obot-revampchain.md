Project Marketplace Project Page Infographic NFT on Metmask NFT on Opensea Inspiration The Demand for Food Security and an Ecofriendly Enviroment After making few research of the top problems we face in the world, I found out that food security and enviromental pollution is a top threat. I wish I could solve the owners, however, the power is not in my hands. Am seeing a word of possibility where cryptocurrencies will be unstoppable by the financial as it will be a driving factor in making change in the Ecofriendly Industry(Agriculture, Recycling Industry and Ecological industry). However, for every change, there is a challenge. We tried to solve the problem of centralization, but scams decided to give a bad name to the crypto industry. I went to the future to see the problems of my idea. I realized scams will create fake project and lure our web3 folks to loose their and cause harm to us. This is how the ObotRevampChain is born. A transparent, secure and accountable system needs to checkmate every offchain finance related project coming to web3 space. Centralization needs to be reduced to minimum in web3 for Real World Tokenized Assets. This is basis of my project. Let's surf. What it does ObotRevampChain is a versatile marketplace aim to specialize in Green Energy Market Industry coming to web3. However, we focuse on Agriculture, Recycling Industry and Ecological Industry. The aim of ObotRevampChain is to onboard projects, monitor the legitmacy of projects and be accountable to the web3 enthusiast coming to trade in this Industries. This process will be carried out by the different DAO and web3 users. Measures such as security checks of project contract, real world proof of ownership and availability of staked assets and more will be taken before a project is uboarded to web3 market. Our Aim is to make Trading of Real World Assets transparent and secure to the web3 space while we surf for endless innovation in providing analytics and real time data update to traders. ObotRevampChain decided to have a prototype project called Cocoa Land to show the how it tends to revolutionize Trading of assets and stocks in blockchain. How we built it Frontend The frontend of the project is built with Html, Css, Javascript. Backend logic The backend logic is built with nodejs for calling of apis from different data services for user interaction. Contract logic The contract logic is built with solidity, using services such as chainlink CCIP, Automation Function and Data Feed show how a prototype real world project will look like in the ObotRevampChain. Challenges we ran into I ran into trying to implement the CCIP and Automation functions for updating of dynamic infographic NFTs for the cocoa land project and cross chain interaction of the NFTs and token. Accomplishments that we're proud of I am proud to do this without looking at a tutorials that shows me how to build everything. I am happy that I now have the super power of documentation and figuring things to bring my idea to life. What we learned I learnt that Ideas comes with challenges, travel to the future to see the problems of your ideas and start making a solution for that. What's next for ObotRevampChain After this hackerthon, ObotRevampChain will continue being in development. We will create more prototype project for different industries to study more ways we can bring transparency, accountability to trading of real world assets. Once we are done, we will venture into analytics and DAO development to bring web3 securities, lawyers and other skilled requred eople who will contribute, secure and check agreements and verification of projects. Once we are done, the decentralized binance for trading Real World Asset is born. <div
