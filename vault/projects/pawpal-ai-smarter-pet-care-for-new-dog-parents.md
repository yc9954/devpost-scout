## Body

Inspiration Becoming a first-time dog parent is both exciting and overwhelming. Many new owners struggle to understand behavior, recognize symptoms, or find reliable guidance — leading to stress for families and pets alike. We wanted to build an AI friend that helps new dog parents learn, care, and connect with confidence. 💡 What it does PawPal AI is a smart companion app for first-time dog parents, combining health insights, behavior guidance, and emotional connection. Key Features: 🏥 AI Vet Assistant (Luna) : Checks symptoms and provides quick home guidance. 🧠 Behavior Coach (Lumi) : Helps with training and anxiety issues. 📸 Snap My Mood : Detects your dog's emotions through photo analysis. 📊 Trend Analyzer : Tracks health and mood trends over time. 🎥 AI Video Vet (Tavus) : Personalized video vet coaching for premium users. Together, these tools make caring for your dog smarter, easier, and more loving . ⚙️ How we built it Frontend : Flutter (Android) + Tailwind CSS (Web) Backend : Supabase for data and authentication AI Stack : Gemini (symptom & behavior AI), Tavus (video AI), ElevenLabs (voice AI) Payments : RevenueCat for subscriptions Hosting : Firebase + Vercel # Example of AI integration (pseudocode) if dog_mood == "sad" Luna.suggest("Playtime or short walk for emotional balance") end 🧩 Challenges we ran into Making AI outputs easy to understand for new dog parents. Keeping responses empathetic yet accurate. Handling multimodal inputs (text, images, audio) efficiently. Designing a UI that feels kawaii , calm, and educational, not clinical. 🏆 Accomplishments that we're proud of 🚀 Built a live, working app for real first-time dog parents. 🐶 Created an AI platform fully dedicated to pet care education . ❤️ Combined health, behavior, and emotion tracking in one tool . 🌍 Advanced civic impact by promoting responsible pet ownership. 🧠 What we learned Empathy is as important as accuracy in AI wellness tools. Pet owners need trust, not tech jargon . Civic tech can improve animal welfare and human well-being together. 🚀 What's next for PawPal AI 🐾 Add puppy onboarding journeys & care reminders. 🤝 Launch community support with vets and trainers. 🦴 Partner with shelters to onboard newly adopted dogs. <div
