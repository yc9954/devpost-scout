Ocean Fin — Optimizing Polkadot DeFi Earnings About the Project Optimizing yield in DeFi is often overwhelming: fragmented platforms, complex setups, and hidden risks. Ocean Fin fixes that. Our platform puts you in full control of your tokens and strategies — no custodians, no middlemen, no unnecessary complexity. Using smart, data-driven insights and on-chain automation, Ocean Fin optimizes your yields across Polkadot Asset Hub and beyond. Think of it as your personal autonomous yield engine: it scans opportunities, maximizes returns, and keeps everything 100% under your control. With Ocean Fin, DeFi becomes simple, smart, and truly yours — no longer just for experts. Inspiration DeFi today is fragmented and exhausting. Users have to manually hunt for staking, lending, or liquidity opportunities — usually without clear data on real yields, fees, or risks. Ocean Fin was born to solve exactly that: an autonomous, data-aware yield engine that effortlessly navigates Polkadot’s DeFi ecosystem for you. Challenges we faced Implementing cross-chain communication was challenging due to our limited experience with Polkadot’s XCM and architecture. Current Work Despite these challenges, we have successfully implemented Loop strategies for GDOT & VDOT on Hydration and establishing a foundation for automated yield optimizations. What’s Next for Ocean Fin Expand DeFi protocol integrations on Asset Hub, Bifrost, and Moonbeam. Implement withdraw strategies and execution via Agent Wallet (x402 Protocol). Enable full cross-chain XCM operations for seamless asset transfers. Develop and deploy additional yield strategies to maximize returns. Build metrics and monitoring tools for better performance tracking. Explore grant applications to support further development and growth. How to Use (gDOT Looping Strategy Example) Prerequisites Polkadot-compatible wallet (e.g., Talisman, SubWallet, Polkadot.js) Some DOT on Hydration How to Transfer Hydration (H2O) on Polkadot Testnet Tap the Send button (paper plane icon). Select DOT as the token. Choose Hydration as the asset to send. Enter or paste the recipient address. Input the amount. Tap the Transfer button to confirm. Step-by-Step Go to https://oceanfin.net and connect your wallet Select the strategy → choose gDOT Looping If it's your first time, bind your account (skip if already done) Enter the amount you want to deploy Click "Simulate Strategy" → review the exact steps that will be executed Click "Run / Execute Strategy" Click "Start Execution" to run all steps automatically Sign/Approve each transaction in your wallet as prompted Done!
