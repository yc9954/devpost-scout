## Body

TLDR: Theta Agents SDK is a Python library that makes it easy to build autonomous AI agents on EdgeCloud. Agents are the future of AI and are projected to unblock trillions of dollars in value over the next 10 years. But they’re GPU hungry, so as agents become mainstream, Theta’s EdgeCloud will be perfectly positioned to jump on this opportunity. Agents are the 'killer app' for EdgeCloud. Overview: Agents are the next phase of LLMs Most current LLM apps like ChatGPT let a user enter a prompt and get a generated response. Agents , by contrast, leverage LLMs for reasoning and can take actions, observe the results of those actions, and reason about their next steps. All of this can be done autonomously. Theta Agents SDK includes Theta-specific capabilities for agents out of the box Our library lets developers create complex agent workflows like this quickly. It’s deeply integrated with EdgeCloud and includes prebuilt EdgeCloud tools out of the box, with many more on the way. Value to the Theta Community: Exponential increase for GPU compute The Theta Agents SDK sparks an explosive demand for GPU compute , catalyzing a surge in EdgeCloud usage. This not only increases earnings for Theta nodes but also enriches the entire community by supporting a wide range of AI applications. This flywheel effect positions Theta as a cornerstone in the burgeoning field of decentralized AI infrastructure. How Theta Agents SDK works In just five lines of code, developers can create a fully functional agent capable of handling complex tasks. Our SDK abstracts the complexities of reasoning, tool usage, and graph creation, providing a seamless experience for developers. Under the hood, EdgeCloud uses LangGraph to create complex agent workflows including multi-agent collaboration. This simplicity empowers developers to focus on innovation rather than technical hurdles, accelerating the development of agent-based applications. Agents working with other agents The real power of Theta Agents SDK shines in multi-agent workflows , where agents with distinct personas and capabilities collaborate to accomplish complex tasks. For instance, a "social media agent" can work alongside a "blockchain expert agent," each contributing unique expertise and having access to their own actions. Without our library, orchestrating such workflows would be immensely challenging. Our current toolkit includes prebuilt packages for image, video, and smart contract generation, and we're committed to expanding this suite with new tools and features.
