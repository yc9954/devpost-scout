## Body

This is the main game window Inspiration I got this idea from the game i used to play on nokia mobile. It had an apple whose task was to reach the final destination. It had levels, but my game doesn't have any levels. I am grateful to youtube actually for teaching me pygames. I thank the channel @Coding with Russ, to help me create my first eligible shooting game. What it does It is just a source of entertainment. The player has to shoot the enemies and save it's life while also keeping the aim to reach the final destination. How we built it I collected the vector images from the sites as for example, flaticons, icons-8, freepik. First of all i started with the maincode file and switched to the button file, which is also imported into the maincode. Look into the youtube video where i got the idea form- https://youtu.be/DHgj5jhMJKg Challenges we ran into The main problem was to come up with the idea for this game. There were numerous problems like fixing the sound and scrolling the background, learning the path and so on. Accomplishments that we're proud of I am really proud that i was able to successfully scroll the background. It was one of my main accomplishments. What we learned I have to admit that i was able to clear the doubts i had in my mind related to pygames. and it was my first time creating a full-fledged pygame. What's next for Shooting Game My next step will be creating different levels. <div
