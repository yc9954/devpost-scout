It could be defined as a very advanced faucet for tokens, or as an extremely simple staking platform/token distribution method. The logic is simple, everyone is free to deposit an ERC20 token on the contract (We will start with the XRLC token of our own ecosystem, but will later include parner tokens as additional rewards) Once the contract holds any ERC20 token the owner can allow and set the reward % of a stored token, and every user interacting with the contract will exchange a fixed amount of FTM (starting from 0.1 FTM) and be rewarded with the allowed token. This transaction, called DELVE will be used as a distribution method for our XRLC token, and any future internal or partner tokens; it will also be read by the Relic Survival game build and award a higher score and power-ups during the run. It will act as a very fun faucet + in-game bonus, giving a tangible reward and feedback to the user who likes to play again and again on our awesome little platformer. The contract is deployed at https://ftmscan.com/address/0xd7c721bacad18c4a847a8448e793b079e8d7eacf It is currently configured to distribute minute amounts of DAI stablecoin and our own XRLC currency for each 0.1 FTM received. We plan to load it periodically and balance the reward weights to offer meaningful amounts of ours and partnership tokens on each run of Relic Survival! 🏆Leaderboard: compete for weekly prizes!
