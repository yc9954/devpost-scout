## Body

Inspiration We have seen many people who don't know what movie to watch when they are bored, so we got this idea to build an app where users can create posts and have discussions related to tv series and movies, see list of top trending hashtags, search for something to watch, and add to a watchlist. What it does This app can help the user to know what to watch, search for something. How we built it We built it using MongoDB. Challenges we ran into Since we are college freshmen in our third semester, we have no prior experience in this subject. We made a lot of mistakes, and it took us a long time and numerous attempts to try and fix them. Accomplishments that we're proud of Even though we were not aware that these software programmers were utilized to create applications, we are quite proud of what we have accomplished and pleased with the efforts we made. What we learned We never would have imagined making an application this early in college, but I guess we are capable of more than we typically believe, and we are really proud of what we accomplished. What's next for POPCORN We would like to improve the quality of the application while expanding it so that we can spread the interests of the movies. <div
