## Body

Our cozy living room space, with three games available on the TV. You can also rest or play at the beach! Take your friends out on the boat, or ride a jet ski solo! There are so many outfits to try on and unlock! Nothing like hitting the pool with some friends, life guard may or may not be on duty, check your local Slumber Party to find out! And of course there is so much more to discover across this world, water slides are just the tip of the ice berg! Have fun! We'll see you in Slumber Party! Inspiration Inspired by Laex's daughter, we built a fun space for all ages! With the cozy home, you are invited inside by the colors and soft textures to relax and make friends. On the outside, you can explore with dozens of varying activities. Check out the gliders, jet skis, campfire, potions, magic wands, trampoline, water slides, and so much more! How we built it We started with the house, adding a beach, waves, volcano, and slowly but surely turning it into an exciting world you can get lost in. All while allowing you to hide in the heart of the island, the home, which offers a cozy embrace for you and your friends. Our team consists of two scripters, Laex & Lexy, two modelers Galactic & FlexedTie, and our resident tester Osprey. Together we make an incredible team, capable of bringing anything we dream up to life. In addition, we loved using Meta's GenAI features to expedite our build, giving us back time that we could use to improve the world. For instance we leveraged the AudioGen for custom sound effects you'll hear everywhere. We made outfits for users to role play in using Meta's AI clothing generator. We used MeshGen and TextureGen to create playful pets like the elephant and butterfly. And of course we have social AI with our two talking NPCs that welcome you to the world and invite you to discover more. What's next for Slumber Party! We have additional mini games planned, but what really gets us excited is all of the new features that we've yet to incorporate. For instance, the palm trees can now be animated to sway in the wind. We even added animation to the flags in Blender, but haven't finished importing that version yet. We are having so much fun with the Talking AI NPCs, and want to give them more context about the world, and allow special trigger words to be used by players. Our comfy clothing line is amazing, and even features a special holiday outfit series. We plan to expand this role playing activity to include clothing, that can only be unlocked after completing quests and other activities. <div
