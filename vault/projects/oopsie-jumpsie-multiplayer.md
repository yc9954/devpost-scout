## Body

🧠 Inspiration Oopsie Jumpsie began with a simple goal: to get players having fun together within seconds , while exploring whether a complete game could be built entirely from 3D models generated using Horizon Worlds’ built-in GenAI . Our team joined MHCP in 2025 and first met at the Meta Horizon Creator Summit in San Francisco , which quickly led to a collaboration focused on creating something simple, joyful, and social - an experience anyone can pick up instantly. At its heart, the loop is beautifully straightforward: jump → dodge → survive → retry — and try to get further than your friends. 🛠️ How We Built It From load to gameplay in under three seconds , Oopsie Jumpsie is designed for native-portrait mobile play , with intuitive tap + swipe controls. Runs are fast; the speed increases as you progress , adding excitement and mastery. A custom fragment-spawning system generates a fresh layout every time. The world is fully multiplayer , letting players run side-by-side and compare distance live, supported by personal and global rankings . Every 3D model, SFX, and ambience was created using Meta GenAI , including Mesh/Environment generation, Texture generation, Audio generation , plus Creator Assistant .
