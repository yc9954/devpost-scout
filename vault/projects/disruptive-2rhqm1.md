After growing tired of static notebooks and impersonal to-do lists, we envisioned an AI companion that could make productivity feel more natural and engaging while keeping us accountable. What it does Sahwira is your AI assistant that gets things done. It enables natural conversation for task delegation, provides intelligent tracking, and transforms daily challenges into achievements. Through natural language interaction, users can easily manage their tasks while maintaining a productive dialogue with their AI companion. How we built it We developed Sahwira using advanced language models and conversational AI techniques. The system focuses on creating a personalized experience that adapts to each user's working style, ensuring tasks are managed efficiently while maintaining an engaging interaction.
