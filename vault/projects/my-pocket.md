Inspiration One of the reasons I created this project is that I want all students or the upcoming future of the world to learn how they can manage their finance. As because many know how to earn money but most of them don't how to invest, spend and save wisely. What it does It will allow our users to learn finance from the given material more often an expense tracker has been added where they can add their income and subtract expenses. This will help them to keep the track of their monthly income. A ChatBot which is connected with WhatsApp will let users to contact us tell us what to improve and also we will use it to help them with their finances soon. How we built it We have used various technologies like. HTML CSS JavaScript BootStrap Material-Ui React React-Router Context-API Twilio Firebase .XYZ Custom Domain (It will take some time because it is masked with firebase and I have settled down it 2 hours before submission and approx firebase take 5-6 hours) Challenges we ran into The biggest challenge and hurdle was when my teammate left me after letting me wait for two days. He didn't complete his task and didn't even reply to my single messages, which ends up in too much depression for me. But then I get my motivation back because I am an Entrepreneur mindset boy. I gather all the tasks, made a new plan, worked the whole day and night, and that end's up with a diamond product. Accomplishments that we're proud of As I am a beginner developer and also a front end with no connection to DevOps so was very difficult to do many tasks. I am proud of myself that I have made many things work properly. What we learned As a new developer, My react concepts are very improved, and gained a strong knowledge of Twilio, learned more things too but due to some issues can't complete them. What's next for My Pocket If My Pocket Get Success, We are going to add many new features where a community chat will be first also include social media handlings.
