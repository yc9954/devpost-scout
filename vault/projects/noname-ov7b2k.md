Planning page Estimation app Timer app Settings Users Inspiration While backlog grooming , we always have this urge to fix typos or adding extra remarks to the text that our teammates are writing! This project was born as a result of that. Also, during sprint planning, we faced other issues like: Estimating: as working from home is becoming a norm, we needed an easy way of estimating the stories. Time: teams usually go overtime while grooming a specific story, so we added a cool timer. What it does It's for your team, to collaborate live while creating or updating tasks with some mini-apps (widgets) to help you along the way! We extracted the main sections that our team needed for backlog grooming and added a live aspect to it. The story (item) title and updates are a collaborative text area. Teammates can type simultaneously, fix their typos, and highlight texts (think of it as a mini Google Docs). We had so much extra time (bragging!) that we created two magnificent apps that we always use during grooming sessions. Timer app ⏳ The timer keeps your story planning in check (time-wise), so your team won't go over time.
