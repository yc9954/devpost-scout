See Your Performance Stats Conquer & Celebrate Your Wins Exercises Evolve With You Challenge Yourself With Auto Speed Up Finger Placement Made Easy Perfect Your Play with Auto Restart Learn New Chords Easier Inspiration From the beginning, our goal with IMMERROCK has been simple: help people learn guitar and bass in a way that feels easy, fun, and encouraging. After launching, we listened closely to our community. They wanted progression that felt clearer, feedback that felt more personal, and a practice flow that truly responded to how they played. That feedback became the heart of this update. What it does This update completely transforms how learning works inside IMMERROCK. Courses now react to your playing through real-time Note Detection , exercises evolve as you improve, and new modes give you more ways to learn at your own pace. We also added new celebrations, clearer goals, and more rewarding feedback, so every practice session feels like progress you can see and feel. How we built it We rebuilt big parts of the learning system from the ground up. Some of the biggest changes include: Adding Note Detection to supported course exercises Creating 3 exercise types: Wait Mode, Song Tempo, and Song Mastery Redesigning the star system so rewards actually reflect your playing Building new celebration moments: Cheering, Particles, Level-ups, and Result Sequences Adding Auto Speed Up and Auto Restart to help players grow more naturally Improving UI/UX so goals and progress are easier to understand This update is the biggest learning-system improvement we’ve ever shipped. Challenges we ran into Improving a live app always comes with challenges. Integrating Note Detection into older course logic meant touching systems that had been stable for a long time. Balancing difficulty, refining reward pacing, and adding new automation without overwhelming players took a lot of iteration. But every challenge pushed the experience closer to what we and our players wanted. Accomplishments that we're proud of This update makes IMMERROCK feel more fun and supportive than ever: Exercises that evolve with your skills in real time Stars and goals feel meaningful Celebrations make progress satisfying (because learning an instrument should feel exciting) Automation tools help you stay in flow The whole experience is smoother, clearer, and more polished For many players, this feels like a brand-new version of IMMERROCK. What we learned We learned that people practice more, and feel better when the app listens, responds, and cheers them on. Clear goals, adaptive difficulty, and positive feedback make a huge difference, especially for beginners. What's next for IMMERROCK This update is only the start. Next, we’re pushing Mixed Reality interactions even further. Guided by constant community feedback, our goal is simple: every update should make players feel more confident, more capable, and more excited to pick up their instrument. <div
