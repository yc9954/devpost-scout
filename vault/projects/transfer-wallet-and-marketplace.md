## Body

Introduction: The challenge chosen by the team foresees the development of a solution that democratizes access to digital money. In farewell to TradFi. We envisioned, innovated, and built a new decentralized app aimed at mobile devices in Celo, which is Proof of Stake, EVM compliant, and open source. Problem x Solution: We have solved two major current problems: the low uptake of DeFi solutions by the general population due to the level of complexity in the use and language of current platforms, and also the lack of incentives for the return of in-person events, a sector severely affected by the current global crisis. Our solution, extremely intuitive and with a simplified language, appears as a platform that concentrates portfolio and marketplace services in a single place, focused on the sale of tickets and exclusive collectibles for on-site events. In addition to a simplified and similar experience with TradFi apps - with token transfers, buying and selling, as well as expense reports, you'll have a learning center about the DeFi world. About the persona : Initially targeting a young audience of up to 29 years old, the platform will transform their experience into face-to-face events, offering a way to merge the two worlds - digital and physical. What's next for Transfer: With no direct competitors, our solution combines multiple services into one. And with real possibilities of scale in a gigantic market, we will go through the first phase, which will be national expansion in our country, Brazil, and then internationalization. As it is an initially open source platform, many possibilities and new solutions can be implemented little by little. About the team: We developed the platform through a multidisciplinary team, extremely aligned and committed to bringing viable, functional and necessary solutions for today's world.
