Connect Wallet Chest Shop Game Menu Game play Reward Game play Inspiration We were inspired by the whimsical concept of merging chickens to create stronger units, combined with the excitement of strategy games, to craft a fun and engaging blockchain gaming experience. What it does Chicken Merge & War is a blockchain game where players merge chickens to build powerful units and engage in strategic battles against opponents. Players can collect unique chickens, develop their armies, and compete in thrilling farmyard warfare. How we built it We developed the game using Unity for the gameplay and integrated the AURORA blockchain to handle transactions and smart contracts. This allowed us to create a seamless experience for players in merging and battling their chickens. Challenges we ran into We encountered challenges in balancing the game mechanics to ensure fair play while maintaining engaging gameplay. Additionally, optimizing blockchain interactions for speed and cost-effectiveness was crucial. Accomplishments that we're proud of We successfully launched a playable prototype that features vibrant graphics, smooth merging mechanics, and blockchain integration. Players have responded positively during testing, enjoying the merging and battle dynamics. What we learned We learned valuable lessons about game design, especially in merging mechanics and player engagement. The integration of blockchain technology also taught us how to optimize transactions for a better user experience. What's next for Chicken Merge & War Game with AURORA Moving forward, we plan to introduce new chicken types, additional game modes, and community events. We also aim to enhance player engagement through tournaments and rewards, expanding the game's ecosystem. Project Description for Chicken Merge & War Game with AURORA Overview: Chicken Merge & War is a thrilling blockchain game where players merge chickens to build powerful units, battle in strategic combat, and claim unique NFTs. The game combines merging mechanics, strategy, and blockchain technology to deliver an immersive gaming experience on the AURORA network. Key Features: Merging System: Players merge chickens of different strengths to create powerful combat units, adding variety and depth to battles. Strategic Battles: Engage in tactical battles, deploying merged chickens with unique abilities to outwit opponents. Collectible NFTs: Players can claim unique NFT chickens, which represent exclusive abilities and can be traded or showcased, enhancing game value and community engagement. In-Game Purchases: Using ETH, players can buy coins for upgrades and special items, improving gameplay and expanding strategic options. Track: Aurora Consumer We are submitting Chicken Merge & War under the Aurora Consumer track, as it prioritizes smooth gameplay through fast and low-fee transactions. The AURORA blockchain supports efficient in-game purchases and NFT claims, enhancing the player experience. Sponsor Prize Requirements: Our project meets sponsor requirements by using the AURORA blockchain’s high speed and low fees, enabling seamless in-game transactions and NFT claims.
