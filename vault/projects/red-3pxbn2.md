Menstrual Products What to expect guide through your first period and provide information on menstrual products and also helps you guide with any information. Inspiration Our inspiration stems from a shared commitment to empower individuals navigating their first periods. We aim to create a supportive and informative platform to make this transition more comfortable and empowering. What it does RED provides personalized assistance and guidance for individuals experiencing their first period. It offers a comprehensive toolkit, including educational resources, emotional support, and practical advice tailored to each user's unique needs. How we built it Using the PartyRock Accomplishments that we're proud of We take pride in creating a user-friendly platform that addresses the multifaceted aspects of a first-period experience. Our accomplishments include developing a reliable AI system, fostering a supportive community, and delivering valuable resources to empower individuals. What we learned The journey of building RED taught us the importance of empathy in design, the value of interdisciplinary collaboration, and the significance of ongoing education in the realm of menstrual health. We gained insights into user experiences and refined our approach accordingly. What's next for RED Looking ahead, we plan to expand RED's features, enhance its AI capabilities, and continue refining our resources based on user feedback.
