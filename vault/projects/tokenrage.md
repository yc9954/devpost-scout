## Body

Yuri The Demon Death Match Gemcence The Undead Demo Intro Far far away, behind the tall mountains, far from the countries Vokalia and Consonantia, was born TokenRage – a new Play2Earn RPG/Strategy game with raids, loots and PvP. Demons, Vikings, Knights and Undeads meet each other for final deathmatches where the strongest wins. Each of the skills is crucial for the deathmatches. Train your Dexterity to have more successful attacks, Agility to avoid the hits, or Intelligence - to cause critical damage. Inspiration Most NFT games have limited functionality and repetitive mechanics. We wanted to create a game with dynamic and progressive NFTs so players can improve their characters like classical RPGs and fight to death in PvP matches. What it does It starts with minting your hero with random stats. Both metadata and the image are hold on-chain. Then you can train your fighter according to your strategy. Once you think your fighter is ready for adventure you can start exploring dungeons for better gear and put your NFTs life on line in death matches. Bigger the risk, bigger the reward. How we built it We wanted to have an engaging game that players will be able to enjoy multiple times a day with various mechanics. This is why we chose to build on Polygon which made this possible with small transaction fees and quick finalisation. Other main technology we used is Chainlink. We need provable and strong randomisation in our game and Chainlik VRF Oracle was the go-to solution. Challenges we ran into Creating a fair matchmaking and training system was quite challenging. We needed to test different algorithms over and over again to come up with something that works and then we tweaked it some more. Explaining our game in 5 mins was also quite challenging because we have more cool mechanics that we could not fit in that duration :( So please read "How to play" document on the website. Accomplishments that we're proud of We managed to create a fun game with an economy behind. TokenRage will be using blockchain to its limits and we truly believe it has a great potential with upcoming improvements and some polishing. It can be expanded easily with future mechanics. What we learned How oracles work and how we can use them to create a fun, non-repeating and fair game. How to use blockchain to create dynamic NFTs that people can improve according to their strategy. What's next for TokenRage In game token(RAGE) to build a strong game economy. Create dungeon style adventures that you will return with rare loot you can equip to your character. Animated fights and better visuals. Combine other NFTs with your TokenRage in death matches.
