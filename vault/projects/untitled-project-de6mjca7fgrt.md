## Body

Inspiration The inspiration behind Lighting Studio came from the need for an easy-to-use tool that allows photographers, designers, and creators to add professional-grade lighting effects to their images without the need for expensive equipment or complex software. We wanted to empower users to enhance their photos with the perfect lighting, bringing their creative visions to life with just a few clicks. What it does Lighting Studio is a versatile photo editing add-on that enables users to add and customize lighting effects in their images. It offers a range of presets and tools to adjust light color, position, distance, radius, and intensity. Whether you're aiming for subtle enhancements or dramatic lighting changes, Lighting Studio makes it simple to achieve the desired effect, giving your photos a professional touch. How we built it We built Lighting Studio using a combination of advanced AI image processing algorithms and a user-friendly interface. The development process involved extensive research on light behavior and its interaction with different surfaces and textures. By integrating these findings, we created a tool that simulates realistic lighting effects while providing intuitive controls for users to customize each aspect of the light in their images. Challenges we ran into One of the major challenges we faced was ensuring that the lighting effects looked natural across a variety of image types and conditions. Balancing ease of use with the flexibility required by professional users was another hurdle. We also had to optimize the performance to ensure that real-time previews and adjustments could be made without compromising image quality or processing speed. Accomplishments that we're proud of We're proud of creating a tool that simplifies the process of adding professional lighting to photos while still offering powerful customization options. Successfully integrating realistic lighting effects that adapt to image depth and texture was a significant achievement. We’re also proud of the positive feedback from early testers who found the tool intuitive and effective in enhancing their creative projects. What we learned Throughout the development of Lighting Studio, we learned a great deal about the complexities of light simulation in digital imagery. We gained insights into how users interact with lighting tools and the importance of balancing functionality with usability. Additionally, we learned how to create a scalable serverless API endpoint to run AI model inference, which was crucial in delivering real-time lighting adjustments without compromising performance.
