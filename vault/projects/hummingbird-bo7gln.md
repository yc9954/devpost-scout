Inspiration We were inspired by the challenge that Tyomarkkinatori set out. As a team this junction we all wanted to work on a problem that affected our community. What it does Our service's core feature is helping people get to where they want to go in life. Through our service we want to connect education partners with people with aspirations for self improvement or users we've identified in "endangered careers" onto a path that insures employ ability.
