## Body

Logo Inspiration The staggering growth in popularity of NFTs and just as astonishing complications required to buy one. What it does It allows users to buy NFTs using Credit Card. How we built it We used an NFT smart contract and a marketplace smart contract to mint and transfer NFTs. Challenges we ran into Integrating Credit Card Payments, Creating a general mint function, Connecting with Metamask, Unable to figure out how to not make the user pay gas fees. Accomplishments that we're proud of A grid displaying all NFTs, Stripe Integration for Fiat Payments, Better UI/UX. What we learned How to connect Metamask wallets, How to handle bank payments, How to handle transfer of NFTs and pay the seller in crypto.
