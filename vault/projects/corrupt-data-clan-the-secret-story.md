# Corrupt Data Clan - The Secret Story

> Brooklyn, 1993. A radical hip-hop collective emerges, rapping about algorithms and social media. Way ahead of their time, they had one message for the world: "Upload the love, download the peace!"

[Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/corrupt-data-clan-the-secret-story) · hackathon [[Chroma Awards- AI Film- Music Videos- and Games]]

**stack** elevenlabs, hailuo, kling, lumalabs, midjourney, sora, udio

## Body

This mockumentary pays tribute to the hip-hop bands of the ’90s who made our teenage years unforgettable. It imagines a group so far ahead of their time, they were already rapping about social media and algorithms in 1993. This project is also a tribute to what AI makes possible. Alone, I could create several songs, invent a band, write their story, and bring to life every voice in the film. AI opened my imagination like never before. Suddenly, any story could exist. <div
