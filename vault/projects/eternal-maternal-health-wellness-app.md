## Body

🌸 माँ-Eternal — Digital Maternal Health Companion A comforting, privacy-first AI companion designed to support women through every stage of their reproductive journey — with empathy, accessibility, and trust at its core. 🎀 Inspiration Maternal health is one of the most underserved areas globally — especially for young women, students, and women from low-resource communities who often lack access to reliable reproductive healthcare. We noticed many women around us struggling with: Scattered or unverified health information 📄❌ Irregular or limited access to doctors 🏥 Lack of emotional and mental health support 💔 Confusion during menstruation or pregnancy 🩸🤰 Anxiety, mood swings, and emotional stress 😟 Absence of a safe, judgment-free space to ask questions 🔒 These observations inspired us to build माँ-Eternal — a digital maternal health companion that feels comforting, private, and always available. Our goal was simple: To create a tool that helps even one woman feel supported on her health journey. 💖 🌿 What it Does माँ-Eternal supports women across their entire reproductive journey with accessible, empathetic, and privacy-focused features. 🩸 Cycle & Ovulation Tracking Predicts periods, ovulation, and fertile windows Symptom and mood logging Smart cycle reminders 🤰 Pregnancy Week-by-Week Growth Tracker Weekly fetal development insights Nutrition and self-care checklists Doctor visit reminders 💬 AI-Powered Health Assistant Safe and private answers to sensitive questions Simple explanations of symptoms Non-medical self-care guidance 🍽 Nutrition Plans for Each Stage Menstrual phase-wise diet recommendations Trimester-based pregnancy nutrition guidance 🧘 Mental Wellness Dashboard Mood journaling Emotional check-ins Mindfulness and wellness prompts 🛡 Privacy-First, Empathetic Design Optional login No unnecessary storage of sensitive data Soft, calming UI for a safe and comfortable experience ⚙ How We Built It We designed माँ-Eternal to be simple, accessible, and mobile-friendly — especially for women using low-resource devices. Created UX flows, wireframes, and a comfort-first design system 🎨 Built responsive UI using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript 💻 Used Lovable.dev for rapid front-end development and iteration ⚡ Integrated Gemini API for the AI-powered maternal health assistant 🤖 Developed core modules for: Cycle prediction Pregnancy week progression Reminders and alerts Optimized the entire app for a mobile-first experience 📱 The MamaAI assistant uses the Gemini API to provide empathetic explanations, symptom awareness, and wellness guidance for maternal health questions. 🧪 Challenges We Faced Building a maternal health platform required deep responsibility and sensitivity. Reliable Health Information Ensuring AI responses stayed supportive but non-medical required careful prompt engineering. Designing an Empathetic UI Balancing professionalism with warmth meant multiple design iterations using soft colors, spacing, and tone. Predictive Cycle Algorithms Supporting irregular cycles required testing, flexibility, and refinement. Handling Sensitive Topics Respectfully The platform needed to feel safe, private, culturally sensitive, and non-judgmental. Responsive & Mobile-First Design Since most users rely on mobile devices, every component needed perfect responsiveness. 🌱 What We Learned This project helped us grow both technically and emotionally: Building impact-driven solutions for real communities 🌍 Integrating AI responsibly in sensitive health spaces 🤍 Understanding how UI/UX fosters trust in care-based applications Designing with empathy for women’s lived experiences Communicating health information without crossing medical boundaries Developing efficiently using Lovable.dev 🌟 What’s Next for माँ-Eternal Multi-language support for broader accessibility 🌍 Integration with wearable health devices 🩺 Deeper personalization using AI-driven insights 🧠 Community-safe spaces for shared learning and support 💞 Partnerships with healthcare organizations and NGOs 🏥 माँ-Eternal — supporting women with care, dignity, and empathy at every step. 🌸 UN Sustainable Development Goal माँ-Eternal supports SDG 3 – Good Health and Well-Being by improving maternal health awareness and access to supportive wellness tools. The platform helps women monitor cycles, track pregnancy, manage nutrition, and receive AI-powered wellness guidance. Impact By 2030, AI-driven platforms like माँ-Eternal can help millions of women access private and reliable maternal wellness support even in areas where healthcare resources are limited. <div
