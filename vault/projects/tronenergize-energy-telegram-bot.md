## Body

Inspiration While running a energy project on tron we have noticed many users still find it complicated to rent energy . With the development of a Telegram bot we will narrow the gap by a easy to use system which does not need a deep learning process . This way we we also attract more users to the Tron ecosystem and have them enjoy the passive income that comes with it . What it does Our project, TronEnergize Telegram bot, allows users to create a wallet, deposit TRX, and purchase energy directly within the Telegram platform. With intuitive commands and a straightforward easy to use interface, users can set their desired price, choose the duration of their energy orders, and monitor their open orders conveniently. How we built it We have used Python mysql and the telegram API Challenges we ran into The biggest challenge we ran into was to make the usage as simple as possible for energy buyers so people with no experience on the tron network could use it . Accomplishments that we're proud of We are proud to have managed to simplify the things as stated above in the challenges . What we learned User-Centric Design: Prioritizing user experience is essential for adoption and retention. Iterative design based on user feedback leads to more intuitive interfaces. Agile Development: Adopting agile development methodologies allowed us to iterate quickly, respond to changing requirements, and deliver incremental improvements to our bot. What's next for TronEnergize - Energy telegram bot Enhanced Features: We aim to continuously improve the bot by adding new features based on user feedback. This includes refining the user interface, introducing new commands, and optimizing existing functionalities. Expanded Availability: We plan to make the bot available to a broader audience by integrating with additional messaging platforms beyond Telegram. This could include platforms like Discord, or Slack. Partnerships: Exploring partnerships with other projects in the Tron ecosystem and beyond can help us expand our reach and offer more value to users.
