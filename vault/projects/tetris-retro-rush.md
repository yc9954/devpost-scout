Click "Start Game" Live game: p: pause game, spacebar: speed up block, arrow keys: move block in any direction Game Over Screen Inspiration When I think about what inspired me to create this project, I'm filled with emotions and memories of my dad. You see, my dad has always been a passionate video game enthusiast, and one of his favorite games from his childhood was Tetris. He would talk about how he would spend hours on end playing this game, trying to beat his high score and achieve new levels of mastery. But as time went on, it seemed like Tetris became less and less available on modern gaming systems. My dad would express his disappointment, wishing he could relive those cherished moments from his youth. And that's when it hit me - I wanted to do something special for my dad. I wanted to bring Tetris back into his life, so he could experience the joy and excitement of the game once again. So, I poured my heart and soul into this project, working tirelessly to create a version of Tetris that could be played on any system. I wanted to ensure that my dad - and others like him - could play Tetris anytime they wanted, without worrying about compatibility issues or outdated technology. Through this project, I was able to create a special bond with my dad, as we reminisced about his childhood memories and shared the joy of playing Tetris together. And if my dad's smile and excitement were any indications, I knew that this project was truly special and worthwhile. What it does This project is a re-creation of the classic game Tetris using the Python programming language. I designed a new graphical user interface (GUI) to allow users to interact with the game. In other words, the original game of Tetris has been programmed to work on modern systems and has been given a new look and feel. This new version of Tetris can be played on any system that supports Python, and the graphical user interface provides an intuitive way for players to interact with the game. Overall, the project is an effort to modernize and enhance the classic game of Tetris for a new generation of players to enjoy. How we built it As the sole developer of Tetris: Retro Rush, I utilized the Python programming language to create a graphical user interface (GUI) that replicated the classic game. To achieve this, I leveraged the PyQt5 library, which provided me with the tools necessary to design and implement the GUI. Challenges we ran into As I delved into the project, I realized that the challenge was not only in creating the GUI but also in designing the game logic that would accurately follow the rules and mechanics of the original Tetris game. It was no easy feat, but I was determined to create a version of the game that was true to its roots while also introducing new features and elements. One of the most exciting parts of the project was incorporating the visual elements that enhanced the overall player experience. I spent countless hours designing visually appealing graphics that made the game come to life. Through it all, I was driven by my passion for gaming and the desire to make my dad proud. As I put the finishing touches on the replica of Tetris, I knew that I had created something truly special - a tribute to a beloved classic that would live on for generations to come. Accomplishments that we're proud of I am proud of my ability to recreate the classic game of Tetris in a way that stays true to its roots while incorporating new and exciting elements. Through my use of Python and the PyQt5 library, I was able to create an intuitive and visually stunning GUI that captures the essence of the original game. Another accomplishment that I am proud of is my ability to overcome the challenges that arose during the development process. From creating the game's logic to designing the GUI and incorporating audio and visual elements, I encountered several hurdles that tested my problem-solving skills and perseverance. However, through hard work and dedication, I was able to overcome these obstacles and create a polished final product. What we learned One of the key takeaways from this experience was the importance of careful planning and organization. From the outset, I meticulously considered the scope of the project and mapped out a detailed plan of action, breaking down the development process into manageable tasks. This helped me stay focused and ensured that I stayed on track throughout the project. Another critical lesson I learned was the value of collaboration and teamwork. Even though I was the sole developer of the game, I relied heavily on the feedback and input of my friends and family throughout the development process. Their perspectives and insights were invaluable, and working together allowed us to produce a better final product than I could have on my own. What's next for Tetris: Retro Rush One potential avenue for development is the addition of new game modes and features. While the classic version of Tetris remains a beloved and timeless game, there are many ways that it can be expanded and improved upon. By incorporating new challenges and mechanics, I could create a fresh and exciting experience that appeals to both new and seasoned players. Another possibility is the expansion of the game's multiplayer capabilities. While Tetris is traditionally a single-player game, there are many ways that it could be adapted for multiplayer play.
