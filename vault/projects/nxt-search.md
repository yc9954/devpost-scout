Splash Screen- The Home Page- For Searching Screen showing the uploaded contents Deploying a content on Polygon Inspiration We have seen that the current search engine is governed by a few companies. We have also seen that the government and massive corporations have the power to remove our content from the internet. It makes us feel like we have no control over our data. We also see that journalists and researchers face many problems while sharing their content as the government can downgrade it. What it does NXT-SEARCH is an AI Powered decentralized search engine. It provides a platform to upload your content on the internet which is completely decentralized while protecting your privacy when you search. With NXT-SEARCH, one can upload videos, images, documents, or audio on the internet that anyone cannot remove. How we built it We have used the following technologies:- Moralis- For authentication of Wallet. Firebase- For data backup Nxtjs- For Frontend Development NUCLIA DB- For the purpose of Indexing of searching data. DOJIMA NETWORKS:- It helps in exchange of assets and liquidity in different chains. Alchemi- For Deployment of Polygon Nodes. I.P.F.S:- For storage of data. Solidity:-To create smart contracts Polygon:- For Deployment of smart contracts. Challenges we ran into We had challenges in giving equal opportunities to the node providers. With the help of DOJIMA NETWORK, we were able to give all node providers a fair opportunity. Accomplishments that we're proud of We have implemented DOJIMA NETWORKS in the best way through which the node providers can upload contracts through multiple supportive EVMs and NON-EVMs chains. We have used NUCLIA DB API for personalized content-based searching. The NUCLIA Db is still in the development phase and we are proud of successfully executing it. We are providing a new type of search engine which is a revolution in the field of search. What we learned We have learned and implemented DOJIMA NETWORK and created a huge Blockchain project for the first time.
