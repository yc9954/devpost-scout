## Body

Inspiration IQTOPIA was born from a deep desire to make education truly inclusive. We realized that children with hearing and speech disabilities often struggle with traditional learning methods, which limits their ability to engage and grow. Inspired by the power of gamification and adaptive learning, we set out to create an interactive platform that makes learning both accessible and enjoyable for every child. What We Learned Throughout this journey, we gained valuable insights into accessibility in education. We explored how children with disabilities interact with technology and how gamification can enhance cognitive and social skills. We also learned about designing user-friendly interfaces that cater to diverse needs while ensuring engagement and progress tracking. How We Built IQTOPIA We designed IQTOPIA as a mobile-friendly learning platform with: Interactive Games: To develop cognitive and motor skills. Adaptive Learning Paths: Personalized difficulty levels for every child. Inclusive Design: A child-friendly adventure/fantasy theme with an accessible interface. IQ Test Feature: Tailored assessments to understand learning progress. Using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Unity for AR, we built a responsive web and mobile experience. We also focused on non-copyrighted educational materials to ensure accessibility without legal constraints. Challenges We Faced Building IQTOPIA came with its share of challenges: Designing for Accessibility: Ensuring the interface was intuitive and inclusive for children with different disabilities. Balancing Fun & Education: Keeping the games engaging while maintaining an effective learning structure. Technical Constraints: Implementing adaptive difficulty and tracking progress efficiently. Time Management: Juggling multiple aspects of development within a tight timeframe. Despite these challenges, our dedication to creating a meaningful impact kept us motivated.
