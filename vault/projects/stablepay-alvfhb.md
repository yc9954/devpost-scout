StablePay: Hong Kong's Financial Innovation Platform Inspiration Hong Kong SMEs face a critical payment challenge: receiving overseas payments costs 3-5% in fees and takes 3-5 business days via traditional banks. With Hong Kong's position as Asia's financial hub and the growing adoption of digital assets, we saw an opportunity to create a HKMA-compliant stablecoin payment platform that leverages Hong Kong's advanced financial infrastructure - FPS, licensed crypto exchanges, and direct connectivity to China's payment systems. What it does StablePay is Hong Kong's first HKMA-compliant stablecoin-to-FPS settlement platform designed specifically for the Hong Kong market: SFC-Licensed Integration : Planned integration with Hong Kong's licensed crypto exchanges (OSL, HashKey, Bullish) FPS Instant Settlement : Designed to convert stablecoins to HKD and deposit via Hong Kong's Faster Payment System in 15 minutes HKMA Compliance : Built for full regulatory compliance with Hong Kong Monetary Authority requirements Hong Kong Business Focus : Tailored for Hong Kong SMEs with planned local bank account integration
