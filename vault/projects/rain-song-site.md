## Body

Inspiration ! I wanted to have a way to play the rain song item in the game Terraria with friends without having to play the game itself because the item is hard to get and also other factors may disrupt the playing experience. What it does ! It simulates the Rain Song item in the game terraria, a music guitar, and allows you to play it. Challenges I ran into ! The time I had was very limited so time constraints was definitely a challenge. I also have surprisingly little experience with github and also it was the first time i bound an event listener to the document itself which was confusing at first. What we learned !
