# Strongcoin

> Be strong! Tap and earn

[Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/strongcoin) · hackathon [[TRON Grand Hackathon - HackaTRON Season 6]]

## Facets


**stack** golang, postgresql, react, solidity

## Body

Project Goal: Bring millions of new users to Tron ecosystem with viral tap-to-earn meme coin. Project Value: Strongcoin doesn't have any entry requirements. Users access the web app within Telegram and start playing, gradually getting familiar with the Tron blockchain and ecosystem. Coins are earned with every tap, with significantly more earned by engaging with the Tron blockchain world—by connecting wallets, swapping on Sunswap, providing liquidity on Justlend, or purchasing NFTs on Ape marketplace. Strongcoin serves as an excellent entry point into Tron's diverse ecosystem for millions of new users. Project Info: Viral and social Strongcoin implements various mechanics tailored to the virality of the game: A progressive referral system. Six different leagues based on the number of coins earned. Fun notifications in the Telegram bot. A reward system for entering the game every day. And we also plan to add group activities in the future. Token Strongcoin will have its own token, $STRONG. At first, we are planning to conduct an Initial DEX Offering. Players of the game will have the opportunity to exchange game coins for tokens, starting from a certain amount, with priority given to the most active players. Revenue Strongcoin plans to generate revenue by placing partner quests and advertising banners on the Earn page in the game, as well as from primary and secondary sales of NFTs, along with in-game boosts. <div
