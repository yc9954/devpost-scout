## Body

Your Symphony Your Story Inspiration This started before i new about the hackathon, i was just exploring if LLMs could convert my story into a song, and there it was, and as destiny would have it i got to know about partyrock hackathon, and rest is history. What it does Your symphony basically converts your story influenced by an emotion you want to evoke, artist and genre into a song, and also generates cover art based on the song essence and artist you like. How we built it Partyrock platform was amazing in helping building the components of the page, we used the text prompts to generate the song and chat more about the song using claude AI, then stable diffusion model for the cover art generation. Challenges we ran into Build the app from scratch didn't cover the whole window, later we used the prompt to generate the skeleton app and then on top of it added more components and customized the prompts(tweaked some advanced settings for more creative and distinct output). Accomplishments that we're proud of Building a creative outlet for the global audience to express their stories into music, future prospect of maybe this turning into a hub for creatives and music enthusiasts. What we learned Prompt engineering is fun and interacting effectively with LLMs, designing a system for leveraging LLMs for a specific usecase, showcasing a demo for 90s with video editing, and finally the art of sharing your journey with words into a blog. What's next for Your symphony Hopefully it progresses and crystallizes into a platform for music enthusiasts, producers, and graphic designers to collaborate and build a strong global community. <div
