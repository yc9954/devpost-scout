📈 StockSim – Our Hackonomics Journey “Why isn’t learning the stock market as easy and risk-free as playing a game?” That simple question sparked the beginning of StockSim . During the Hackonomics Hackathon , we— Tanmay , Sahil , and Snehal —came together with a shared vision: to make stock market education accessible , interactive , and risk-free for beginners. 🚨 The Problem Many people are curious about investing but: 😟 Fear losing money 🧠 Don’t know where to begin 🏦 Find the market overwhelming and complex So, we built StockSim — A simulator where users are given $10,000 in virtual cash to explore the stock market using mock real-time behavior , with zero financial risk . 👥 Meet the Team Member Role Tanmay Frontend development — crafted a clean and user-friendly UI Sahil Backend logic — built simulated real-world trading mechanics Snehal Data flow, testing, and refining the overall user experience Youtube Demo link: https://youtu.be/QUenLNMEdts 🎯 Our Approach We focused on our target audience: Students , first-time investors , and curious minds . Key priorities: Realistic experience, mirroring live market behaviors Easy-to-understand interface Beginner-focused features like portfolio analysis, stock info, and leaderboard 🛠️ Built in a Few Days Through: ☕ Late nights 🧪 Debugging marathons 🤝 Collaborative problem-solving We transformed a simple idea into a fully functional prototype in just a few days. 💡 Why This Matters This isn’t just a hackathon project. It’s our step toward making financial literacy more approachable. We believe in: Turning learning into an experience, not just theory. <div
