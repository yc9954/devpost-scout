## Body

Inspiration Our project, ReLeafLoop AI, focuses on making eco-friendly and low-cost paper using fallen leaves and agricultural waste. Today, most paper is produced by cutting trees, while large amounts of leaf waste and farm waste are burned or thrown away, causing air pollution and environmental damage. Our solution uses artificial intelligence to convert this waste into useful paper without harming forests. AI helps in selecting the right mix of fibers, controlling moisture during the process, and checking paper quality using computer vision. This ensures consistent quality and reduces material waste. Since the raw materials are easily available and almost free, the overall production cost is much lower than traditional paper. We also produce seed paper that can be planted after use, supporting a circular economy. ReLeafLoop AI aims to reduce pollution, save trees, and provide affordable paper for schools, offices, and government use while creating a sustainable future.
