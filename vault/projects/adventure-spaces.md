## Body

Inspiration Great storytelling that I have experienced and kept with me throughout my life. Reading cartoons, books. Playing videogames. Watching movies. Playing boardgames. Sci-fi and fantasy stories, but also other types of stories. What it does Creates a story setting, a story setting image and then lets the user experience the story via chatting with the LLM. The LLM then gives the user options for how to proceed. The story ends when the user asks for the story to end. The story can also proceed with other options than the ones the LLM gives, this gives the user freedom to experience the story as they prefer. While being gently inspired in how they wish to continue. How we built it Using Claude-Instant to help create the initial prompt. From there on using AWS Partyrocks "create an app" generator, fine tuning the text widgets it created and adding an image widget. Challenges we ran into Had to test a few times to adjust the prompts. Had to figure out how I wanted the initial prompt to be and where to go with the app creation. Accomplishments that we're proud of That I was able to work out a successful and good result with all this. What we learned More about storytelling. More about working together with AI generators. What's next for Twisty Tales I could see myself integrate AWS Bedrock into a phone app or website. Inspired by this. <div
