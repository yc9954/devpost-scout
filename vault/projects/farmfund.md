Inspiration Farmers are the cornerstones of America and American society. But at the same time the farmers are most affected by natural forces and most vulnerable to emerging threats due to climate change. This requires us to build a new finance model that is suited for this emerging change in the farming eco- system. What it does We have a crowdsourcing application that uses drones and other sensors in conjugation with AI to create a robust system to invest and track farms. How we built it We used Azure AI studio , azure functions and azure data services to create our project. Challenges we ran into Accomplishments that we're proud of We were able to build working chat bots in our application.
