# Lava Escape Run

> The floor is lava. The prize is gold.

[Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/lava-escape-run) · hackathon [[Play Everywhere- The Build with Snap Games Lensathon]]

## Facets


**stack** bitmoji, cameracontroller, lens-studio, script

## Body

This lens is inspired by classic high risk adventure games “the floor is lava,” reimagined in a fiery, game like world where every step matters. By combining Bitmoji characters with glowing magma, floating bricks, and gold bars reward, the experience turns a simple moment on Snapchat into an exciting mini quest. It’s designed to spark adrenaline, fun competition, and curiosity encouraging users to test their timing, challenge friends, and share their best runs. At its core, the lens represents risk versus reward: move smart, stay focused, and if you’re brave enough, the gold is yours. 🔥✨ <div
