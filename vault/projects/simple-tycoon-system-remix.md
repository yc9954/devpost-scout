Claim a Tycoon Dropper Tycoon Variant Passive Tycoon Variant Tools Tycoon Variant Inspiration Tycoon games are one of the most popular and creative genres out there. I wanted to make it possible for anyone to design their own tycoon, no scripting required! With this system, all creators need to do is adjust object properties, and they can bring any tycoon idea to life. What it does Building your own tycoon is simple and straightforward: Create your build. Place the objects you want players to buy inside the PurchasedObjects container. Add buttons and assign them to the objects they should purchase. And that’s it, you’ve got a working tycoon system up and running in just a few steps. Challenges I ran into As newcomers to the Partner Program, one of the biggest hurdles was learning the Worlds Desktop Editor and quickly getting comfortable with TypeScript. Mastering these tools while developing the system was both a challenge and a great learning experience. What's next for Simple Tycoon System Remix This is only the starting point. I plan to expand this framework into multiple tycoon game styles, both as playable examples and as inspiration for other creators. The vision is to give developers a flexible foundation they can remix and build upon to create their own unique tycoon experiences. <div
