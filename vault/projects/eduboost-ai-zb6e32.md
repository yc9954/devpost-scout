## Body

Inspiration STEM education should be engaging, collaborative, and accessible to every student. However, many students struggle because learning resources are fragmented across different platforms, and access to personalized academic support is often limited. We wanted to create a single learning ecosystem where students can study together, receive instant AI-powered assistance, and better understand complex STEM concepts. Class5 AI was inspired by the vision of making quality STEM education available anytime, anywhere through the power of artificial intelligence. What it does Class5 AI is an AI-powered collaborative STEM learning platform that helps students learn more effectively through personalized tutoring and real-time collaboration. Students can: Ask STEM-related questions and receive AI-generated explanations. Upload notes and generate concise summaries. Join study groups and collaborate with peers. Participate in voice and video study sessions. Access an AI assistant during study sessions for instant academic support. Track learning progress and stay organized. Class5 AI combines tutoring, collaboration, communication, and knowledge management into one unified learning environment. How we built it We built Class5 AI using modern web technologies and AI services. Frontend Next.js React Tailwind CSS Backend Node.js API-based architecture AI Features OpenAI models for tutoring, explanations, and note summarization. Intelligent prompt engineering for STEM-focused responses. Collaboration Features Real-time messaging and study rooms. Voice and video communication capabilities. Shared learning environments for group study. Data Management Cloud-based storage and user management. Structured learning data and progress tracking. The platform was designed to provide a seamless learning experience while keeping AI assistance available throughout the learning journey. Accomplishments that we're proud of Successfully built an AI-powered STEM learning ecosystem instead of a simple chatbot. Integrated AI tutoring with collaborative learning experiences. Created a platform where students can study individually or as a group. Developed intelligent note summarization to simplify learning materials. Built a scalable foundation that can support future educational tools and features. Designed an intuitive user experience focused on student engagement and accessibility. What we learned Through building Class5 AI, we learned that effective STEM education requires more than just providing answers. Students learn better when they can collaborate, ask questions freely, and receive explanations tailored to their needs. We also gained valuable experience in: AI integration and prompt design. Building collaborative educational systems. Designing user-centered learning experiences. Combining multiple technologies into a cohesive platform. Creating AI solutions that solve real educational challenges. What's next for Class5 AI Our vision is to evolve Class5 AI into a comprehensive AI-powered learning ecosystem for STEM students worldwide. Future plans include: AI-generated quizzes and assessments. Personalized learning paths based on student performance. Interactive STEM simulations and virtual laboratories. Multilingual support for global accessibility. Teacher and classroom management tools. Advanced analytics for student progress tracking. Mobile applications for Android and iOS. AI-powered study recommendations and learning insights. Ultimately, we aim to make STEM education more personalized, collaborative, accessible, and effective for learners everywhere. <div
