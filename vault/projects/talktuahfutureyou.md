## Body

Landing Page Insipiration I often wondered: "What if I could chat with my future self about money?" That question sparked TalkTuahFutureYou! I wanted to transform dull financial planning into something that feels like grabbing coffee with your future, successful self. (Also, I wanted to create another TalkTuah...) :P What it does I've created a way to meet your future self from 2034! You can: Have heart-to-heart chats about your money dreams Watch your financial journey unfold in colorful timelines Test different life choices (like "Should I save more now?") Get advice from someone who knows you best - future you! How I built it I combined chatty AI with eye-catching graphs to make something that's both fun and practical. Think of it as your future self bringing visuals to your financial conversation! Challenges I ran into Creating charts that tell your financial story clearly Making complex money math simple and understandable Accomplishments I turned spreadsheets into stories! The app makes money talk feel like catching up with a friend who happens to be really good with finances. It's personal, it's visual, and most importantly, it's actually fun to use. What I learned Making finance engaging is tricky but so worth it! I found ways to blend casual chat with smart financial planning, and discovered how to make AI feel more like a friend than a calculator.
