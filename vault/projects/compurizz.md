Inspiration Compurizz is an effort to amalgamate the benefits of networking, productivity, and health applications that have been diverging due to the excess number of applications available online. What it does Compurizz helps CS students and professionals in professional and personal development. It incentivises productivity (solving LeetCode problems), networking (making connections on LinkedIn), personal development (bathing, working out, etc), and many other activities that encourage professional and personal growth for CS students and professionals. Users can earn Aura points and redeem an Aura Coin, our own cryptocurrency, for every 1069 Aura points. How we built it Though it is just an idea at this point, Compurizz would be made using Django and MySQL for backend and HTML-CSS and Javascript for frontend.
