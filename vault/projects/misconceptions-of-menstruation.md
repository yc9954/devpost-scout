## Body

Inspiration Although the world is going forward, the fact that menstruation is still majorly considered something that is unusual concerned our thoughts. We also see that women face a lot of trouble regarding their menstruation as society has its own thoughts on her. There are many misconceptions about periods. So, we wanted to provide everyone with the right facts and bring awareness. What it does We created a website that states the facts behind some common misconceptions. How we built it We used HTML, CSS, and JAVA SCRIPT to build the website and we used the replit, a free online IDE as a place where we could develop the code and work on our website. Challenges we ran into It was hard to collect the right information because many sites on the web show different reasons. Accomplishments that we're proud of We were able to stand on our mission to provide people with the right information. What we learned We learned the facts about periods. We also came across organizations that are working on the same motto and we were happy to see people working to destigmatize the topic of menstruation.
