## Body

Amendment Party is a music film that teaches the 27 amendments the only way Americans seem to learn anything anymore. With a glamorous, unhinged diva as the guide. She sparkles through the Constitution like it is a nightclub setlist, turning civic literacy into camp, comedy, and full-body choreography. The goal is simple. Make young people actually remember their rights. Not through lectures, but through a character who makes the information too entertaining to forget. The film reframes the Constitution as a pop spectacle. Freedom of speech becomes a power ballad. Search warrants turn into a dance cue. Due process becomes a wink. Women’s suffrage gets a runway moment. Prohibition is a bad breakup. The whole thing is shot with hyper-stylized VEO and Dreamina visuals to feel like a mix of RuPaul, Schoolhouse Rock, and an international pop star who might be intoxicated by democracy itself. Beneath the humor is a real message. Know your rights before someone who knows them better takes advantage. Civics can be fun. Civics can be ridiculous.
