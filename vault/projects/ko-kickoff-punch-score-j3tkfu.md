## Body

Unlock powerful gloves like the horned Bull Glove and explosive Fire Glove as you level up your player. Track your rise on the new Level Leaderboard and see who’s dominating the arena. Punch the ball and your rivals in fast-paced soccer chaos - now more rewarding than ever. Celebrate your performance with MVP highlights that showcase top scorers and knockout kings. 🧠 Inspiration KO Kickoff is a fast-paced, social sports game where power meets precision . Our update was inspired by the desire to give players a deeper reason to stay, progress, and return . We wanted to go beyond moment-to-moment gameplay and build a sense of mastery and reward that grows over time. Drawing inspiration from classic competitive games and familiar level-based systems , we designed a mobile-friendly progression loop that’s easy to grasp, but satisfying to climb. 🛠️ How We Built It We introduced a full level progression system , rewarding players with XP for scoring goals and winning matches . As they level up, players unlock new boxing gloves , each with unique visuals, VFX, and sound effects - including a horned “bull glove” at level 6 and a fiery glove with explosive punch effects at level 9. We also revamped the post-match screen to highlight player achievements, including MVP , Most Goals , and Most KOs , complete with usernames and statistics. In the lobby, we added a new glove showcase stand and a dynamic leaderboard that ranks players by level. To elevate gameplay feel and reliability, we refined the camera handling for smoother play . Under the hood, we rebuilt key systems in TypeScript V2 , improving logic clarity , performance , and ensuring a more mobile-first experience . 🚧 Challenges One of the core challenges was designing a progression system that felt rewarding without becoming grindy - especially on mobile, where short sessions and quick feedback loops are key. We also had to ensure our new UI elements, like the glove stand and MVP screen , remained clear and intuitive on small screens, while preserving the game’s fast-paced, arcade simplicity at its core. 🏆 Accomplishments ✅ Introduced a full level-up system powered by XP and Persistent Variables 🥊 Designed and implemented unlockable gloves, each with unique visuals, SFX, and VFX 🏅 Added MVP breakdowns with player stats (Most Goals, Most KOs, and overall MVP) 📊 Created a progression-based leaderboard showcasing top-level players in the lobby 🧠 Refined our core loop to focus on reward, expression, and replayability 🎥 Improved camera dynamics using the Camera API to create cinematic, mobile-friendly gameplay 🚀 Next Steps We plan to expand KO Kickoff with: 🧢 Progression rewards – new gloves, hats, and gear to earn or buy and show off in matches 🎯 Skill-based challenges – quests, daily XP goals, and performance-based unlocks 📈 Seasonal XP resets – to keep the competition fresh and reward returning players 🎵 Unlockable music tracks – let players choose their vibe with in-game soundtracks tied to progression 🧩 Modular glove upgrades – mix and match visual effects to create your signature punch <div
