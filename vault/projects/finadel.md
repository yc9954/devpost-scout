Finadel Business Model Inspiration We saw many local vendors struggling for their livelihood so we decided to make a application for them , so that they can sell their product through that application What it does Our application will connect costumers with nearby shops so that they can order anything they want or they can also check the availability of any product in nearby stores How we built it We have Made website using (Html , Css , Javascript and Angular library ) Backend Through Django Database through Sql App - Kotlin Metaverse (Most Unique part of our Project) - Unity 3D Challenges we ran into We Also didi ground work which was really hard to go to shops and talk to the local vendors Accomplishments that we're proud of We are proud that we have got a fund of 2.5cr (Technical Credits) From Xartup Fellowship and we are working with TBI and Xartup on this project What we learned We have learnt Team work , Connecting different techs , Real implementation issues .
