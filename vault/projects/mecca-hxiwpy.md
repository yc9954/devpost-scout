Website Mecca Ecosystem Mecca Explorer Faucet for TFUEL & MCA Documentation Mecca Lightpaper About Mecca is an incentivized smart contract subchain & acts as a layer 2 multichain DeFi hub for Theta Metachain, utilizing PoS with Dapp Staking & Gas Rebates Mecca rewards developers based on the value and impact of their dapp rather than their close association or connections to the capital. Unlike existing versions of smart contract platforms, where tokens are primarily concentrated in the hands of the first few early participants, Mecca is designed to be shared across all contributors, users and stakeholders. Mecca offers a unique value proposition in the market through our focus on interoperability, scalability, cross-chain transfers, and our commitment to building a strong developer community. Our project has the potential to become a top competing smart contract platform. Key Features EVM & WASM Compatible: Mecca is fully EVM-compatible, enabling developers to iterate and innovate much faster using existing tooling and code from the Ethereum/Theta ecosystems. Mecca will also be compatible with WebAssembly in the future, a widely supported, high-performance standard that will likely serve as the foundation of decentralized systems development. Integration with the Theta ecosystem: As a Theta Subchain, Partnering with existing Theta Network projects and validators will ensure Mecca enjoys all the benefits of the Theta ecosystem. Powered by Metachain: Mecca is built on Theta, an open-source blockchain that powers a variety of top projects and is accessed daily around the world by millions of users. Theta Metachain allows developers to easily and efficiently build sidechains (subchains) that can be optimized for any use case. Access to a Multi-chain Ecosystem: As a key connection between the Theta, Subchain, and other Dapp ecosystems, Mecca will also provide developers with unmatched interoperability access to rival scaling solutions (including Algorand, Binance Smart Chain, Polkadot, Cosmos ecosystems, Aptos, and Near), as well as their respective communities. Challenges we ran into Subhchain keeps losing data due to main chain testnet outages, which removes all smart contract data and makes us have to redeploy our eth rpc's.
