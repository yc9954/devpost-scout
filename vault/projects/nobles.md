## Body

Project Goal: We are planning to build the strongest community and liquidity market around luxury in Web3. Our platform offers the opportunity to win high-end luxury items through affordable tickets powered by NFTs. Project Value: Nobles will bring a significant number of users to the Tron blockchain who are not yet familiar with Web3 – these are sellers of luxury goods. And for existing users, the presence of a gamified NFT platform with the opportunity to win cool items will be a huge advantage, leading to growth in on-chain transactions and NFT sales volume. Project Info: The era of bringing real-world assets onto the blockchain has arrived. Welcome to Nobles. We empower merchants to quickly liquidate luxury assets, while making it possible for everyone to win high-end luxury items with budget-friendly tickets on our platform, powered by NFTs.
