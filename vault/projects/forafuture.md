## Body

Inspiration Many students work hard but do not know where their current habits will lead them in the future. We wanted to build a tool that helps students understand the impact of their daily choices. Instead of only showing grades or study plans, we wanted to show possible future outcomes. That idea became ForaFuture What it does ForaFuture is an AI-powered platform that helps students see different versions of their future. Students enter information such as their goals, study hours, strengths, and weak subjects. The AI generates: Future Score Success Chance Future Paths Future Timeline Knowledge Graph Future Letter AI Insights Students can compare their current path, a better path, and their best possible path. How we built it We built ForaFuture using modern web technologies and AI. The platform uses Google Gemini for future analysis and personalized insights. We designed the interface to be simple, fast, and visually engaging. Challenges we ran into One challenge was creating future predictions that felt useful and understandable. Another challenge was designing a system that shows complex information in a simple way. We also spent time improving the user experience and making the platform responsive. What we learned We learned how to combine AI, data visualization, and user experience into one product. We also learned the importance of keeping AI explanations simple and easy to understand.
