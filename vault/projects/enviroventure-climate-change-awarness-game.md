We wanted to create an engaging and interactive way to educate players about environmental challenges while inspiring them to make a difference. Our goal was to blend learning with entertainment, making climate awareness accessible to all through an interactive gameplay. What it does EnviroVenture is an educational game that immerses players in real-world climate scenarios. Players navigate challenges like deforestation, pollution, renewable energy management and more, each in form of different levels while making decisions that impact the environment of the in-game level. Through interactive storytelling and gameplay, the game raises awareness, provides solutions, and encourages sustainable actions in real life. EnviroVenture provides facts related to the actions taken within game by the player, inspiring them what change they can bring if they do could same in the real-world as well and it also asks players simple yet important questions, fulfilling the educational core of EnviroVenture How we built it We combined various technologies and creative elements to bring EnviroVenture to life. EnviroVenture’s prototype is a web based game. We used HTML5 for scripting, Tailwind CSS for styling, Functionality was brought JS, and the 3d environment of in-game levels was developed by using Three.js. We Used “Font Awesome” for icons and for saving player information and in-game stats we used session storage. Challenges we ran into Creating EnviroVenture came with its set of challenges: • Balancing fun and education: Ensuring the game remained enjoyable while effectively conveying climate change knowledge. • Game mechanics complexity: Designing engaging yet simple to understand mechanics. • Technical performance: Optimizing the game for smooth play across multiple devices. • User engagement: Encouraging players to take real-world actions beyond the game. Accomplishments that we're proud of • Successfully integrating educational content without compromising gameplay fun. • Developing an intuitive and visually engaging user interface. • Receiving positive feedback from early testers who found the game both informative and enjoyable (Classmates and Teacher) • Creating a scalable foundation for future expansions and updates. What we learned Throughout the development process, we gained valuable experience in: • Game design principles and how to make learning interactive. • Environmental science and the importance of accurate climate data. • Team collaboration across design, coding, and research. • User experience (UX) design to maximize engagement and retention. What's next for EnviroVenture: Climate Change Awareness Game We plan to expand EnviroVenture with: • New levels and challenges featuring more environmental topics. • Multiplayer and communitydriven features to promote global climate action. • Collaborations with environmental organizations for realworld impact. • Mobile and web versions to reach a wider audience.
