## Body

Inspiration The inspiration stems from a question, will an AI ever win an Oscar? If this same question were asked of an AI the answer would be: While an artificial intelligence has not won an Oscar, the idea is not far-fetched. AI has been making significant strides in various creative fields, and it’s possible that in the future, AI-created works could be recognized by awards like the Oscars, especially in categories that involve technical achievements or animation. So, while an AI has not yet won an Oscar, the increasing involvement of AI in the film industry suggests that it might be a possibility in the future. According to Fei-Fei Li (Co-Director of the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered AI) "Generative AI is like a new type of paintbrush. It allows artists to create something that was previously impossible." About the APP Since their short time, generative AIs have become increasingly common in everyday life. This application is a way to experience the different ways that these models can help us, for example, to make a movie. From writing a scene, calculating the budget according to the actors that the model itself selected, or even giving us advice on the different problems we will have when filming.
