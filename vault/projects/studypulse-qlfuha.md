# StudyPulse

> AI-powered STEM learning diagnostic that analyzes quiz performance and generates personalized study plans

## Body

StudyPulse solves a real problem in STEM education: students don't know where they're weak. Traditional learning platforms show scores. StudyPulse shows WHY. Input: Student quiz results Analysis: Per-topic accuracy scoring, weakness detection, gap analysis Output: Ranked study plan — not just "practice more" but "practice Calculus flashcards for 37 minutes" The engine is the same multi-factor analysis framework used in quantitative trading — detecting patterns, measuring confidence, and generating actionable signals. Applied to education instead of markets. Built for DSH Hacks V1: "Use AI to improve STEM education accessibility and effectiveness." No accounts needed. No logins. Upload JSON → Get plan.
