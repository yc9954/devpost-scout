Logo Get Started List of matches Make prediction Successfully predirected Problem Statement Have you ever felt that watching E-sports matches is like being on the sidelines, cheering for your favorite team, but feel like you're missing something? E-sports is on fire, with millions of passionate fans worldwide. Yet, there's a problem we've all felt but rarely acknowledged: the gap between passive viewing and active engagement . Many fans want more engagement , rather than simply watching . Watching the matches is thrilling, but imagine if you could do more than just watch. What if there is a way to turn your passion for E-sports into an interaction, rewarding experience without the need for betting or high stakes? Solution Introducing GX EsportPredict. A game-changing concept that revolutionize the way we experience E-sports. GX EsportPredict is an Opera GX extension that introduces an engaging gamification feature that allow users make predictions and win exclusive rewards like coupons, vouchers, or free gifts while watching live E-sports matches. It's important to note that this is not a betting system, as incorporating betting would violate Opera browser policies. Here's how it works: First, brands or companies partner with EsportPredict GX to access the backend system by paying a fee. They can then personalize rewards, choose target matches, and establish prediction conditions. Then, users install the extension on Opera GX, create an account, and able participate in the 'predict and win' contests. If they successfully predicted the outcome set by brands, they will receive exclusive rewards. Business Modal GX EsportPredict operates a business model targeting both E-sports enthusiasts and brands/companies. For users, it offers a free, engaging, and interactive platform to make predictions and win exclusive rewards while watching live E-sports matches. Brands and companies, on the other hand, can access the backend system by paying a fee or partnering with GX EsportPredict , allowing them to customize rewards, select target matches, and set prediction conditions, thereby gaining valuable exposure, increasing brand recognition, and acquiring new customers within the E-sports community. Revenue modal GX EsportPredict can generates revenue through brand and company partnerships, offering them access to the platform for a fee. Additionally, advertising and promotional opportunities within the extension are monetized through brands paying for advertising space and sponsored content. Data insights and analytics services are another option for revenue streams, further enhancing the platform's financial sustainability. Market Study & Opportunity Market statistics reveal that users who use coupons regularly have more than 90 percent, with more than 40 percent of the customers purchased a product just because of a coupon code. But here's the twist: some companies find that only less than 5 percent of their customers use their coupons. Why does this happen? It's all about how you giving out the coupons. Studies tell us that when people get a coupon easily without any effort, they often forget about it. It's like you walk down the street and suddenly a promoter gives you a free coupon code. It's easily obtained, but often not using it. However, if you earn a coupon by winning a contest, or making a purchase, or joining an affiliate program, getting the coupon with some effort, you're more likely to appreciate it and use it. That's where GX EsportPredict comes in, offering an innovate marketing strategy to help brands connect with the passionate E-sports community. Market Size The E-sports industry is booming, with 535 millions of fans, billions in revenue and more than 50k of games businesses. GX EsportPredict taps into this immense market, offering a unique and valuable experience that no other platform provides. Impact & Benefit GX EsportPredict transforms the E-sports experience by Enhanced user engagement, increasing brand awareness, and driving user acquisition and retention. Through this system, brands and companies gain valuable exposure within the E-sports community, fostering greater brand recognition and loyalty among users. <div
