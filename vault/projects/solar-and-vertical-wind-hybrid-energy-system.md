## Body

Inspiration The inspiration for this project comes from the need to explore sustainable energy solutions that can efficiently power systems, especially in areas with low and intermittent renewable energy sources like wind and solar. The hybrid solar and vertical wind energy system aims to ensure a continuous power supply by combining these two renewable sources to provide a reliable alternative to traditional power generation. What it does This hybrid system integrates a solar panel and a vertical wind turbine to generate renewable energy. It collects power from both sources, with the system designed to supply electricity to various electronic products, especially in remote areas or where grid power is unavailable. The system ensures that there is always a power supply available by using a 12V battery to store energy during the day for use at night or during periods of low energy generation. How we built it Wind Turbine: The wind turbine was built to harness the power of the wind. It was tested at various times of the day to measure output voltage and wind speed. Solar Panel: A solar panel was tested for open-circuit voltage and short-circuit current at different times of the day, including morning, afternoon, and evening. Energy Management: The power output from both the wind turbine and solar panel was monitored, and a hybrid system controller was designed to manage and balance the energy sources. The total power output was calculated, and the system ensures smooth integration of both power sources. Challenges we ran into Wind Speed Limitations: The wind turbine performed sub-optimally due to the low wind speeds in the region, resulting in a lower voltage output that was insufficient for the system's requirements. Solar Panel Efficiency in Evening: As the sun sets, the solar panel's voltage output dropped below 12 V, making it unable to power the system during evening hours without relying on the battery. Hybrid System Integration: The hybrid system required careful management to ensure smooth switching between energy sources and maintain a consistent supply of power. Accomplishments that we're proud of System Integration: Successfully integrating both renewable energy sources (solar and wind) into one hybrid system, creating a reliable power supply. Energy Management: Developing an efficient energy management system that monitors and balances power generation from both the wind turbine and the solar panel. Data Collection and Analysis: Collecting and analyzing data from wind turbine and solar panel tests, which helped to identify patterns and improve the design. What we learned Intermittency of Renewable Sources: The challenges with relying solely on wind and solar energy, particularly their intermittency, highlighted the importance of backup solutions like battery storage to ensure a continuous power supply. Energy Optimization: The need to optimize energy generation, storage, and management to maximize system efficiency, especially in areas with variable weather conditions. Importance of Testing: Extensive testing was essential to understanding the performance of both energy sources and integrating them effectively into a hybrid system. What's next for Solar and Vertical Wind Hybrid Energy System Enhancing Efficiency: Further research and development to enhance the efficiency of both the wind turbine and solar panel, particularly in regions with low wind speeds. Battery Storage: Improvement in battery technology to store more energy for use during low generation periods. Scalability: Exploring the scalability of the hybrid system for larger applications, such as powering small communities or remote areas with limited access to grid power.
