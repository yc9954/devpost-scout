Inspiration This portfolio is inspired by my passion for games. I wanted to build a portfolio that’s not just a static showcase but a fun, game-like adventure where users can explore my skills and projects while collecting items, unlocking badges, and progressing through levels. What it does The portfolio invites visitors to explore different folders, each representing a section of me (About me, my projects, my skills, etc.). As users interact with the page, they collect items, earn points, and unlock achievements. The goal is to complete all levels and collect all badges to finish the game. How I built it I built this project using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, and integrated Formspree to handle form submissions without a backend.
