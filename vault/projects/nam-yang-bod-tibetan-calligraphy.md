By combining traditional pedagogical methods with the precision of the Logitech MX Ink , the application transforms a complex writing system into a storytelling-driven journey. The Tibetan language is a precious cultural jewel currently classified as endangered by UNESCO . Forever Snowland aims to bring these Tibetan calligraphy lessons to life to learn the Tibetan alphabet (Salje Sumchu) through immersive storytelling, educating global audiences and ensuring that this ancestral identity remains steadfast in the digital age. The Problem & Opportunity Traditional remote learning (think Zoom calls and prerecorded video lessons) lacks the tactile precision and immersiveness required for calligraphic arts. Meanwhile, the Tibetan language faces a critical decline in active practitioners . There is a clear need for a solution that combines the high-fidelity input of professional tools with the accessibility of XR to ensure these ancestral identities remain steadfast. Experience: Storytelling and Precision with Logitech MX Ink Users enter a virtual studio in the Himalayan Plateau guided by cultural characters like Jyoti and Mother Rabbit . The application utilizes: Interactive Storytelling: A narrative-driven curriculum that explains the origins and meaning of the Tibetan alphabet. Step-by-Step Lessons: Curriculum developed in collaboration with the Costa Rican Tibetan Cultural Association and the Faculty of Asian and African Cultures University of Warsaw . Calligraphic Accuracy: Capturing the intricate pen rotation-sensitive "heads" and "tails" of the Tibetan script that standard controllers cannot replicate. Adaptive Feedback: Real-time stroke review ensures calligraphic accuracy. Community Contribution: The XR Handwriting Module To drive the future of linguistic and writing systems preservation in XR, we are committing to: Reusable Codebase: Releasing the core MX Ink integration module as open-source for the developer community. Standardized Framework: Providing pre-configured stroke-smoothing and haptic feedback scripts to accelerate other non-Latin script projects, such as Ranjana lipi. This is in collaboration with the Callijatra Foundation . Viability & Implementation Target Audience: Language students, cultural heritage enthusiasts, and educational institutions. This is why we're part of the Script Keepers Network actively showing our progress with their community of writing systems academics, scholars, and activists. Scalability: The framework is designed for expansion into other writing systems use cases. Quality: Optimized for Meta Quest 3/3S with a focus on seamless UX and high-performance rendering.
