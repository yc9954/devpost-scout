Inspiration We love AR and We love 3D games,so we aimed to combine both of them under this project What it does Hulls escape is an asymmetrical multiplayer game for 2 players where: Player 1 is the escapee Hull who will play the game in 3D mode and has to reach the target point beating obstacles created by ARgod. Player 2 is the mafia boss Malone, who has the ARgod controller and will play the game in AR mode.
