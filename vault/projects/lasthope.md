## Body

Inspiration During my diploma, a friend lost his puppy but luckily found it several days later just two streets away. Similarly, another friend lost his bike and discovered it abandoned in a remote area after several days. These experiences made me realize how difficult it can be to recover lost items, inspiring me to create a solution. What It Does I developed a platform where users can post about lost items, and others who have any information can contact them directly. How I Built It The platform was built using the MERN stack. It integrates the Google Maps API for location tracking, Firebase for authentication, and Socket.io for real-time chat functionality. Challenges I Faced The biggest challenge was the lack of prior knowledge and experience. It took time to learn new technologies, but I managed to overcome it through continuous learning and practice. Accomplishments I'm Proud Of One of the key features is that when a lost item is registered, users in the surrounding area receive a notification, increasing the chances of recovery. What I Learned Throughout this project, I gained hands-on experience with the MERN stack and Socket.io.
