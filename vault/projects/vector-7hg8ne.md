Inspiration As game developers, we’ve constantly faced the challenges of slow and expensive development. After Junaid attended a game jam in Shanghai, we became determined to create a solution that makes game development faster and more affordable for indie developers and small studios. What it does Vector can generate code, assets and modify your game logic, it is a all in one game developement platform made for indie developers and small studios How we built it We made the entire thing in c++ we forked Godot engine (an opensource game engine) and costumized and added an ai editor into it. Challenges we ran into While wrtining the system prompt we had trouble to make it generate high quality code, and we also need to fix the parsing errors. Accomplishments that we're proud of We are prond that we had already 156 waitlist users and the studios such as Glizzy and former developers from unity What we learned We learned the importance of talking to users early and often. Getting real feedback from developers helped us shape the product in a way that directly addresses their pain points and improves their workflow. What's next for Vector We’re preparing for our launch next week and are actively seeking collaborations with small studios around the world.
