## Body

Inspiration I got the idea for LearnGuru because I wanted to help people who are into programming and tech. When I started my journey as a programmer, I didn't have a clear plan, and it was tough to figure things out. I know others face the same challenge of finding a learning path, projects, and career guidance. So, I made this tool to make the learning journey easier and more fun for everyone, just like how I wished for it when I started. What I Learned While working on LearnGuru, I learned a lot about generative AI and creating effective prompts. Using PartyRock's drag-and-drop tools and trying out different models helped me figure out the capabilities of Amazon Bedrock for building AI apps. I also learned how to write effective prompts to get desired result from the model. What it Does Takes inputs : Programming language/Topic Experience level (Beginner, Intermediate, Advance) Goal of Learning Generates outputs : Personalized roadmap based on topic, experience level, and goals Project Ideas on this topic based on experience level Suggested careers on this roadmap Additional learning resources AI chat mentor for guidance on this learning roadmap Future Enhancements Even though Party Rock is great for making AI apps without coding, it has some limits with its drag-and-drop components and integrating with other applications. I want to make LearnGuru even better and more user-friendly, and Party Rock can't do everything I have in mind. I plan to use Amazon Bedrock models to create a more powerful app from scratch. This way, I can make it better for new learners. <div
