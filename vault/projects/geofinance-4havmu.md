Every tool we tried felt like it was made for Wall Street Pros , not for people like us who were just starting out. We juggled between 5 different apps—one for news, another for market data, another for simulations, and yet another for AI insights. But none of them talked to each other. None of them actually taught us how to make smart decisions. And like most beginners, we made mistakes. Expensive ones. We later discovered we weren’t alone: 80% of new investors lose money in their first year. That’s when we realized: 🔍 There’s no single platform that brings together: Real-time market data Safe, hands-on trading simulations Personalized AI guidance that teaches while you trade Meanwhile, fintechs, brokers, and even banks are all struggling to keep new users engaged—because the current platforms weren’t built for learning, they were built for execution . So we decided to build the platform We Wished WE HAD . This isn’t just an app. It’s a chance to reinvent how a new generation learns, trades, and grows wealth. 👉 And it’s a Multi-Billion Dollar Opportunity , because: 📉 Education tools don’t offer real market experiences 📊 Trading apps don’t teach 🤖 AI tools don’t give live, personalized guidance 🔍 What It Does NeuroTicker is an AI-powered investment simulation and learning platform that blends education with trading: You can grab Real-time Market Insights using the Sonar API Safe Paper Trading via Alpaca Live News Mapping on an interactive Mapbox Globe A powerful Stock Screener powered by SONAR API A tool which will do Market Analysis via SONAR of the stock selected!
