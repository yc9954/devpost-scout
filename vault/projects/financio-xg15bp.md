Homepage Microsoft Trained Copilot Chatbot Finance Calculator Financial Literacy Quiz Inspiration As first-generation computer scientists from Hispanic cultural backgrounds, our team has witnessed firsthand how crucial financial literacy is in a person's life. Many in our communities face challenges in navigating the complexities of the American financial system, which can lead to significant barriers in achieving economic stability and success. Our experiences have shown us that understanding financial concepts is not just beneficial—it's essential for making informed decisions that can impact everything from budgeting for daily expenses to planning for future investments. With this application we created, we aim to empower individuals who may feel lost when it comes to their finances, helping them build the knowledge and skills they need to thrive. What it does Our web application is designed to help train our users in common economic situations they might face to get a better grip on their finances. We accomplish this in multiple ways. First, we have a quiz that helps our users understand where they are lacking in financial literacy. Our biggest tool for users is our custom-made chatbot, designed to answer questions and explain how the financial system works, guiding users to understand their money. Finally, we have a built-in budgeting tool that helps users organize their spending by showing them how much they spend in key areas of their finances. How we built it For our front-end application, we used NextJS to build our web app interface. For our AI chatbot, we integrated a custom-trained Microsoft Copilot AI model. Challenges we ran into We wanted to add Spanish support to our chatbot and create a Spanish version of our page. Unfortunately, due to time limitations, we were unable to train our AI model to accept Spanish inputs and respond consistently. This is one of the areas we hope to improve upon in our next update. Accomplishments that we're proud of Without a doubt, we believe we have created a tool that can make a difference in financial literacy. We accomplished this by creating a chatbot capable of explaining key terms and ideas behind maintaining good financial health. We are also proud of the fact that we launched our product fully within the short amount of time given—a feat that was challenging to achieve. What we learned We learned how to integrate AI components with a React front end, knowledge that will be useful for future projects. Our team also improved our concept-building skills, allowing us to connect ideas to code much more easily going forward. What's next for financio We plan to add full Spanish-speaking support to ensure that this tool can be used in our Miami community, where many immigrants struggle with the complicated terminology and loopholes of the American financial system. <div
