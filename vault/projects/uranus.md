Handcrafted modules to stand out This is the one of Uranus about pages This is the one of Uranus homepages See how your team will look with Uranus Minimal and impressive Hero part Inspiration We are web lovers, Envato elite authors, we live on the web, so it's easy to get inspired. In general, as web designers and developers, we always try to provide modern and creative websites for both simple and advanced users. Uranus is a premium, all-in-one solution for any HubSpot user. What it does Uranus is a HubSpot CMS theme, super user-friendly and speed optimised, that takes advantage of all the amazing HubSpot features. The plethora of options offered permit to design, any kind of website, whether it is a general or a specific purpose, corporations, agencies, freelancers, designers, bloggers or whichever is your need. How we built it We always design everything using Sketch, all theme assets and elements before the development process. Afterwards, we work on website development which can grow traffic and ultimately convert visitors to loyal customers. Always with users in mind, we've developed 50 reusable page sections to save time for the content creators, and encourage brand consistency. Challenges we ran into We are perfectionists, so participating in a challenge with a strict deadline wasn't easy for us. It was the main difficulty and the hardest part of the contest. However, we always love any new challenges in our work. Our main challenge was to build a HubSpot theme that will not be just another theme, something that everyone will be amazed by the fresh look and feel. We feel that we did it in the Uranus theme. Accomplishments that we're proud of You have to be creative to survive. We're proud of Uranus design and layouts and, definitely, for its clean code. User experience is one of our highest priorities, and the clean and contemporary Uranus interface is the icing on the premium cake. What we learned We've learned a lot about HubSpot. We've used HubSpot as CRM for quite a few years, but working for the CMS is different. We found HubSpot CMS and saw so much potential, not just for earning but to carve out a job which we would be satisfied with; creative freedom as it were. Uranus is our second HubSpot theme, and we now feel confident to say that we are HubSpot providers. What's next for Uranus First of all, free and premium support for all Uranus users. It is Greatives philosophy. And, of course, after receiving the feedback from Uranus users, we'll make all the needed improvements. Also, a new free, lite version of Uranus will be published, giving HubSpot users the chance to test drive what we offer. Last but not least, users will decide about the new features of Uranus.
