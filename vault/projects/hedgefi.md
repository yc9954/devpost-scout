## Body

Inspiration In a world where meme coins can skyrocket or vanish overnight, we saw an opportunity to create a smarter, AI-powered platform for meme coin traders and creators. HedgeFi is designed to be the ultimate MemeCoin factory, marketplace, and prediction market on NEAR & Aurora. Whether you want to analyze trends, launch tokens, swap seamlessly, or bet on market movements, HedgeFi brings it all together in one decentralized and AI-enhanced experience. How It Works Seamless Wallet Integration Dashboard & Portfolio Tracking Betting Marketplace Create a bet over Twitter : "I bet $100 that $DOGE will be up 10% by next Friday! @HedgeFi" HedgeFi’s smart contract locks the bet amount securely. The system matches your bet with another user or a market maker. After the bet period ends, the contract verifies the outcome and pays out instantly. Easy to navigate the whole bet process. ## Meme Coin Launch Pad: Create a token on the website. Early contributors receive rewards, allowing them to mint additional tokens on Aurora. Users can create, buy, and trade all launched meme tokens on the market place. ## AI Research & Trading Tools How We Built It Blockchain: Smart contracts deployed on Aurora Testnet, integrating secure transactions and on-chain verification. AI & Market Analytics: ElizaOS AI-powered agents track real-time trends, identify profitable trades, and monitor social sentiment. Frontend & Authentication: Built with Next.js and RainbowKit for seamless wallet connections and session management. Smart Contract Integration: The Factory Contract automates token launches, while the Betting Marketplace uses smart contracts for fair and transparent wagers. What We Learned Throughout this journey, we gained deep insights into crypto market behaviors, AI-powered trading strategies, and blockchain integration. Key takeaways include: Seamless UX is critical Traders benefit greatly from real-time alerts and predictive analytics. Smart contract security is a must Building a strong community With HedgeFi, we aim to redefine the meme coin trading experience, merging AI-powered analytics, automated promotions, and decentralized finance tools into one powerful ecosystem.
