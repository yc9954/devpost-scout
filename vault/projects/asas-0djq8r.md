## Body

Open issues page profile/chats profile/your-issues profile/create-issue profile/courses Buy courses page Inspiration💭 I was inspired by GitHub's feature to raise issues. I thought why don't we try and do something similar to create a platform where people can ask help in the community by raising issues for environmental problems. What it does💻 People can raise issues for environmental problems and can ask for help from other people in the community who can collaborate with them by starting a chat session. Eg: Someone is willing to make a device that can detect plastic floating in rivers and can collect on its own. In that case this person can raise an issue for the same and can collaborate with people who have good technical knowledge. Also this website allows users to buy courses which are supported by cool AR models provided by echo3D. To make payment, I have used Stripe API which will allow us to do some test transactions. These courses can be a medium to teach people about different flora and fauna in a fun way with cool 3D models and people will get motivated to buy such courses when they will know that the amount paid for these courses is used for helping non profit organizations. How we built it👩‍💻 I have built it using React, Node, Socket.io, Firebase and echo3D. Challenges we ran into😣 I faced issues while using socket.io but finally worked my way around it. Accomplishments that we're proud of🏆 I am overall happy with the innovativeness in this project. But definitely needs more improvements. What we learned📖 I learned about the cool world of AR with the help of echo3D while working on this project. I also used Firestore database for the first time.
