Cyb3rPunK2022 - Art Attack! Inspiration This artwork is inspired by the game CyberPunk2077, featuring an uplinked human entity walking out of a dataline. Datalines are used to transfer data or messages between entities. The artist has used datalines and VR headsets to express the concept of future uplinking of human memory into datastream and storage in electronic systems. Why Nervos($CKB) Network? Nervos Network - A community-driven blockchain network for enterprise-level business applications. This project focus on B2B use case, such as payment, trade finance and supply chain. Nervos Network will run on the Godwoken Chain. It has inbuilt support to EVM and ERC20. The block time is expected to be 10 seconds or less as compared to 15 second block time of ethereum. The Nervos network delivers on the promise of blockchain-enabled enterprise systems, with high throughput and low latency network that is capable of reaching thousands of transactions per second in an entirely permissioned environment. Motivation: One of the most revolutionary applications of cryptocurrency is the idea of ​​owning an entire piece of art, starting with the world's first NFT Art, to NFTs more complex features such as the ability to have a piece of land in a digital world. The Paradoxical Arts aspires to explore possibilities of getting notable record by using the available funds through selling NFTs which are considered as a new form of Blockchain Token and partnering with credible organizations to reduce the risks involved.
