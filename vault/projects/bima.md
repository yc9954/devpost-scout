## Body

The name "BIMA", derived from the Swahili word for land or property, reflects our mission: empowering individuals to own and trade land with confidence, speed, and transparency. land ownership is one of the most valuable assets in Africa, yet also one of the least trusted systems. Paper-based titles, missing records, corruption, and disputes have prevented millions of people from accessing secure property rights.
