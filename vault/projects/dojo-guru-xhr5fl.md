Inspiration for dojo.guru dojo.guru is a place by devs for devs sharpen your skills and accelerate your production educate your mind body and spirit What dojo.guru does The number one Web3 framework focused on optimisation, scalability and ease-of-use. dojo.guru combines high end NFT infrastructure that allows creation, sale & trade of dynamic collectibles with a decentralised user management & social toolbox integrated into a simple web frontend. It allows you to publish projects on any evm compatible chain and even deploy projects on multiple chains in parallel even cross chain template projects are in our future roadmap ! We have put lots of effort into our Project and didn't hesitate to make it as complete and flexible as possible! This Tool Supports the DIAS dynamic NFT Standard and uses no react or upscaled frameworks that cost unnecessary server space and limit your creativity. This Framework is Vanilla JS based, builds go through Webpack, web3 connection is connected by truffle, vrf random numbers & oracle sources by chainlink, decentralised data storage on IPFS and the concept is limited only by your creativity and skill level. How we built dojo.guru Tech stack used: NodeJS Truffle Webpack JavaScript Sass HTML5 Solidity ChainlinkVRF ChainlinkFeeds IPFS Filestorage Challenges we ran into dojo.guru is a template framework i have set up and improved from hackathon to hackathon over the years it has become a reliable tool but has caused lots of headache. but the greatest headache was to tackle all the complexity and combining all these tech stacks into one open framework. Accomplishments that we're proud of That i dared the next step of publishing the dojo.guru project and opening up to the world ! What we learned Literally everything i know about web3 What's next for dojo.guru We still have lots of tools to integrate and polish for the best possible user experience. But our future focus will shift on educating devs and newcomers with an edutainment channel how to build dynamic collectibles and decentralised communities. <div
