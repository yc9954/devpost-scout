## Body

Inspiration Nature simulation and city-building games such as spore or sim city What it does You are a spider that likes gardening. Your job is to populate a tiny planet with plants based on the issues in your project. You can use trees, flowers, bushes, and even a mushroom. You can move around and jump using your tiny legs. Make sure you use the mouse wheel to zoom in and out in order to fully admire your creation. How we built it Using three.js for the graphics, typescript for the UI, and forge for interacting with the Atlassian cloud. Challenges we ran into Managing the game elements on a spherical surface was the biggest challenge Getting the camera to follow the player without throwing up Accomplishments that we're proud of Being able to move continuously on a planet without hitting walls Seeing the garden grow and admiring it from all angles It's fun to see content from Jira in the game! <div
