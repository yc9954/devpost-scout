## Body

Inspiration 🌟 Previous healthcare background - Ex-Machine learning intern at Kaiser Permanente (one of America's largest healthcare providers) and volunteer student at Vanderbilt Medical & Williamson County Hospital, TN Through my experiences there, I noticed a significant gap in healthcare: many people, especially those from minority communities, aren't included in clinical trials. For example: African American women have high rates of breast cancer deaths, but make up only 6% of breast cancer trial participants 🎗️ African American men get prostate cancer twice as often as Asian men, but are only 9% of trial participants ⚕️ When medicines aren't tested on diverse groups, they might not work well for everyone. We created ClinicalConnect to help solve this problem by making clinical trials easier to find for all people. 💪 What it does 🚀 ClinicalConnect is an AI-powered web application that: Easy to Use : Patients can enter their health information through a simple form Smart Matching : Our system finds trials that match their health conditions 🧩 Clear Explanations : We show why someone might or might not qualify for each trial ✅ Doctor Friendly : Healthcare providers can use it to recommend trials to patients 🏥 How I built it 🛠️ MeldRx Platform : Registered and published our app on app.meldrx.com OpenAI LLMs : Used LLM models to understand complex trial requirements Next.js & React : For a fast, responsive website Tailwind CSS : For a clean, easy-to-use design FHIR R4 Integration : We connect to FHIR resources for patient data SMART on FHIR : Implemented standard launch protocols for EHR integration Challenges we ran into 🏃‍♀️ Complex Eligibility Criteria : Clinical trials have complicated requirements that are hard to understand 📝 FHIR Data Mapping : Making patient information from FHIR resources match with trial requirements was tricky 🔄 Making it Simple : Creating a website that's easy for everyone to use took a lot of work Performance Optimization : Making sure the website stays quick even when looking through many trials Accomplishments that we're proud of 🏆 User-Friendly Design : Our website is easy for anyone to use FHIR Integration : Successfully connected to FHIR resources for patient data Clear Matching : We explain in simple terms why someone matches or doesn't match a trial 🔍 Works Everywhere : Our website works well on phones, tablets, and computers What I learned 📚 Health Data is Complex : Medical information has many details and special terms 🧬 FHIR Standards : We learned how to work with FHIR R4 resources effectively MeldRx Platform : We gained experience with the MeldRx ecosystem 🔌 Making Websites Fast : We found ways to keep our website quick even when doing complicated tasks What's next for ClinicalConnect 🔮 Better Diversity Tracking : Showing how different groups are included in trials 📊 Tools for Doctors : Making it easier for healthcare providers to use our system 👩‍⚕️ Community Partners : Working with healthcare groups to reach more people 🤝 ClinicalConnect is our way of helping make healthcare fairer. We want to make sure new treatments work for everyone, no matter who they are or where they come from. Together, we can help more people join clinical trials and improve health for all communities. 💙 <div
