## Body

Inspiration After using Wit.ai in one of my python projects, I was impressed by how it made NLP easy and accessible. But when I decided to add a feature to one of my Android Apps using it, I had a hard time finding guides or tutorial talking about how to integrate Wit.ai in Android Apps. As the android-sdk was no longer actively maintained and the recommended way now was to use the HTTP API on Android, I realised how hard it would be for new Android developers to use Wit.ai in their apps. What it does To help new Android Developers out, I wrote a step by step project based tutorial which explained the basics of Wit.ai and showed them how to integrate it into their Android apps. Accomplishments that I'm proud of This was the first time I have written a tutorial and I am really happy with the way it turned out. What I learned The research I did for writing this tutorial made my concepts of Wit.ai clearer and also made me realise the flexibility it offers.
