📸 StripCam – Korean-Style 4-Frame Photobooth for Adobe Express Hi, this is project StripCam, brought to you by our team. StripCam is a lightweight plugin that brings the hottest Korean-style four-frame photobooth experience right into Adobe Express. No more lining up at trendy selfie studios—just open your laptop, strike a pose, and let StripCam do the rest. 🎭 Not Just a Filter—It’s a Vibe StripCam helps you capture four quick snaps, each full of attitude, charm, and personality. Think mood swings in four acts: cute, cool, cringe, and confident. 🎞️ Built-in Filters & Custom Styling 🕶️ Classic Retro and 🎬 Cinematic Black & White filters included 🎨 Want more? Use Adobe Express adjustment tools to fine-tune: Colors Contrast Tones (All in your unique style) 🖼 Seamless Integration with Adobe Express Once your strip is captured, it’s automatically added to your Adobe Express canvas, ready to be transformed: ✨ Add expressive elements using: Content & Media Panel Text Effects AI-generated visuals ✂️ Resize & crop into a mini sticker to print and decorate: Journals Notebooks Phone cases 💌 Or simply keep it as a memory with your best friend or loved one—a tiny emotional snapshot you’ll want to look back on again and again. 💡 Why StripCam? Whether you’re creating for yourself or sharing with someone special, StripCam makes it easy, fun, and deeply personal. Because we’re not just taking pictures—we’re preserving feelings.
