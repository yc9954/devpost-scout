## Body

Inspiration In the last 2 years over 200k employees at tech companies got laid off. It is hard to navigate a layoff in a hard market with the current economic situation. What it does This app helps you on three fronts in case of lay off: Helps estimate financial runway by asking questions about savings, severance, expenses etc. Provides reassurance and ways to reduce stress and improve mental health.
