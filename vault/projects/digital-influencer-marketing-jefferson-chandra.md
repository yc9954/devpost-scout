## Body

Inspiration I watch a lot of Tiktok during the lock down and there is a lot of endorsement from influencers that is not suitable for the viewers that follows the influencers. What it does It helps influencers and small business to find small business and influencers, respectively, with similar preferences. How I built it I used Android Studio and firebase, I have a little bit of experience in firebase and I think this project helped me improve my skill in that field. Challenges I ran into Making the preferences was hard since I tried to use an array but resource can only upload children that is a map with a value of an object. Accomplishments that I'm proud of I was able to show an Instagram profile with the profile picture and followers data just from the username.
