# Coffee_Break

> Working in a hybrid company can be very difficult. We present coffee break platform, to help preserving personal connections between people and teammates in the office.

## Body

Corona changed our work lives forever – more and more companies moved to working from home instead of coming to the office. There are lots of advantages of working from home – more time with family, less time spent in traffic and more. But, there are also disadvantages such as losing personal connection between teammates. Imagine being a new worker in a company starting to work from home. So we present you a coffee break platform for creating personal connections between workers in a hybrid world. Why not enjoy the best from both worlds? When working from home, or from the office, you can enter every day the times you want to take a coffee break and our amazing platform will create a coffee break meeting for you. Our algorithm takes into consideration a priority queue - the longer you haven't met with someone, the higher priority it gets. Imagine coming physically to work and finding yourself alone from your team. We don't want it to happen so coffee break presents to you a work day scheduler - you fill the days you plan to come to office and get to see your teammates' plans. Also, what about a group bonding meeting? We present to you the happy hour feature. Each teammate fills his open slots and our algorithm takes into consideration everyone’s slots and gives priority to the team lead and chooses the optimal hour.
