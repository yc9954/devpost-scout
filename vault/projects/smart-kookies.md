## Body

Inspiration Foundation was inspired by our goal of providing the best experience possible for those in need. What it does Our web-app connects individuals in need to services in the St. Louis area. Our app has certain tools that accommodate to many needs in order to provide the most effective outcome. How we built it Through Google's collaborative software, Editey, we used Twilio, HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap to create our end result. Challenges we ran into Twilio was definitely very difficult to comprehend and navigate. We also struggled with creating the most efficient and simple solution to achieve our goal. Accomplishments that we're proud of The QR code and the language options are two features that we are very proud of. What we learned We learned that how great the need is for technology that can improve our society. Also, the importance of teamwork and communication are essential to an amazing result.
