## Body

App Icon Teen Mind Project: Mental Health for High Achievers High-achieving students are often praised for their results, but rarely for their recovery. Teen Mind Project bridges the gap between ambition and burnout. It is a native iOS experience designed to treat mental health as a performance multiplier, not a distraction. Key Features Zero-Friction Tracking : Log your mood, sleep, and stress levels using an intuitive emoji-based system in under 30 seconds. The Focus Engine : Get one—and only one—actionable suggestion based on your current stress levels to help you stay grounded. Acute Stress Resets : Access specialized tools designed for real-world academic moments, such as pre-exam jitter breathing exercises. The Library : Explore "Science-Backed Sprints"—short, digestible reads on how sleep impacts memory and how to manage academic perfectionism. Smart Data Insights : View your "Burnout Risk Score" and mood trends through a visual system that feels like a supportive peer, not a clinical report card. What Makes Us Different Most wellness apps fall into two traps: they either turn self-care into a stressful "hustle" or they don't understand the specific pressures of student life. Teen Mind Project stands out because: Anti-Anxiety Design : We intentionally removed "streaks" and loud notifications that often trigger anxiety in perfectionists. Native iOS Experience : Built with Swift and UIKit, the app features a custom "soft-blue" palette and gentle haptics to feel like a digital deep breath. Performance-Based Wellness : We speak the language of high achievers. We focus on optimizing your brain rather than just "managing feelings." Low Friction : Every interaction is designed to fit into the small gaps of a busy student's schedule, ensuring you get the most insight with the least effort. Technical Excellence The app is powered by a custom DataManager that correlates your lifestyle data with your mental well-being. By focusing on Weekly Patterns instead of "Daily Grades," we help you see the bigger picture of your health without the pressure of perfection. Coming Soon: HealthKit Integration for automatic sleep and activity tracking. Interactive Home Screen Widgets for instant stress resets.
