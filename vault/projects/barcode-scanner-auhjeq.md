## Body

Barcode Scanner Barcode is a visual depiction of information in the form of bars and spaces on a surface. What it does Manual Entry of data is really hard so for easy identification of data we use barcode. Barcodes eliminate the possibility of human error. The occurrence of errors for manually entered data is significantly higher than that of barcodes. A barcode scan is fast and reliable, and takes infinitely less time than entering data by hand. How we built it we built it using softwares like, Eclipse Launch4jar Exectuable .jar files Accomplishments that we're proud of We made the data entry work easier so that everyone can save their time and efforts.
