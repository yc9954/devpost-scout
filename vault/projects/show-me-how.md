## Body

ShowMeHow Making hidden knowledge visible and shareable. The Problem Think about the last time you joined a company. How did you learn where to find things or how to do basic tasks? Usually someone has to show you, right? This "showing" process: Takes up experienced employees' time Has to be repeated for each new hire Often misses important details Doesn't scale well Our Solution ShowMeHow turns this one-to-one teaching into one-to-many: Record Once: An expert records themselves doing a task AI Learns: Our AI understands what's happening in the recording Guide Everyone: The AI can then guide any number of people through that same task, showing them exactly where to click and what to do next It's like having an expert looking over your shoulder, guiding you through each step - but available to everyone, anytime. Why It Matters Saves time for both sides. Ensures everyone gets consistent guidance Makes knowledge easily shareable across the organization Reduces mistakes and confusion ShowMeHow turns "I'll show you how" into "The AI will show you how" - making knowledge sharing effortless and scalable. <div
