## Body

Inspiration Our survey shows that most non-Web3 people are not willing to invest their time into blockchain because of its technical complexity and cost - such as requiring to learn Solidity and paying for high gas fees. Meanwhile, Web3 developers are still rare and expensive to hire. Although some platforms like OpenSea and Mintable.app offer lazy minting with no coding required, it is not actual minting and not decentralized. What it does Our solution is Mintnite , a multi-use-case Web3 minting tool that anyone can mint fungible tokens, NFTs and even SBTs (soulbound tokens) with no programming required. It supports bulk NFT minting and tokens can be set as public or private. Mintnite takes advantage of Fantom's high-speed but low gas fee benefits. No registration is required and you own your smart contract (no custody whatsoever). You can create as many smart contracts as you wish with no limits on the number of tokens minted. Mintnite Features Smart contracts are based on OpenZeppelin templates, hence they are secure and compatible with industry standards. Support major wallets – MetaMask, Rainbow, TrustWallet and more. Tokens can be transferred, burn and more – just like standard contracts. Bulk minting enables the minting of multiple NFT/SBTs in one transaction. NFT/SBT tokens can be public or private. Public tokens have their payload on-chain while private tokens only contain the file hash. Use Cases We're thinking beyond NFT art. Since most minting platforms are narrowly focused on NFT art trade, we're aiming for a bluer ocean with various use cases as below. NFT/SBT Coupons – On-chain coupons to prevent multi-million dollar fraud. SBT coupons can prevent coupon transfers. NFT Deed – Use with real-world assets such as property deed NFT/SBT Club Membership Access – Replace membership cards with NFT/SBT cards. Car Registration Number Trade – Enable the trade of rare car plate numbers by tokenizing numbers in NFTs. This will enable traceability and royalty collection by departments of motor vehicles. Game Achievements – Issue virtual badges to gamers. NFT/SBT Certificates – Issuance of verifiable certificate to individuals such as university degrees. Digital Identities – Using SBT, we can issue authentic and verifiable digital identifications. Governance and Votes - SBT tokens can enable individuals to vote digitally in DAO or even in offline elections. Credit Scoring – Storing history of payments and debts in SBTs. How we built it The entire project was built within the time period of this hackathon. You may verify via the domain name registration date ( https://who.is/whois/mintnite.io ) or GitHub code . Please kindly bear with any unforeseen bugs. Winning Pledge We pledge to use the cash prize to offer free FTM tokens to our users in order to subsidize their minting gas fees. Accomplishments that we're proud of You can bulk mint one-to-many or many-to-many. Able to mint public or private NFT/SBT tokens. Able to insert up to six payloads in one NFT/SBT token.
