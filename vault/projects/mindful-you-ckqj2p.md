## Body

Inspiration University life is fast-paced, overwhelming, and emotionally intense. Many individuals silently battle stress, anxiety, and burnout without access to timely support. Mindful You was born out of this need—a calming, AI-powered companion that responds to how you feel in the moment and delivers instant emotional relief. We wanted to build something personal, empathetic, and frictionless—no questionnaires, no stigma, just breath, voice, and gentle action. What it does Mindful You is a premium AI-driven wellness companion that offers: 🎯 Emotion-based personalization – Users receive guidance based on their current emotional state. 🧘‍♀️ Guided micro-meditations – AI-generated calming scripts with voice narration via ElevenLabs. 🧠 Agentic AI exercises – Interactive simulations combining breath, reflection, and affirmations. 🔒 Secure user authentication – JWT-based login with token-based protected routes. 🎨 Smooth and accessible UI/UX – Designed with mindfulness-first design principles. How we built it Frontend Next.js 14 (App Router, client-side rendering) Tailwind CSS & Framer Motion (animations) Lucide React (icons) Axios (API communication) Backend Node.js + Express Authentication with JWT middleware AI Integration: OpenRouter (Mistral 7B Instruct) HuggingFace (Zephyr fallback) ElevenLabs (Voice generation) Deployment Tools Localhost during development, prepared for future deployment (e.g., Vercel + Render). Challenges we ran into 🧠 Prompt engineering for emotionally intelligent responses 🔄 Ensuring fallback AI models still deliver high-quality content 🎙 Voice output: tuning TTS output to be natural, calming, and emotionally supportive 🧩 State management + emotion handling in real-time UI flows Accomplishments that we're proud of Built a fully functional, beautiful wellness platform Created a real-time, simulation-style breathing and affirmation module Delivered a premium TTS-driven experience using ElevenLabs Combined AI innovation with deeply human user experience What we learned AI can be warm and human when prompt-designed thoughtfully Even simple breathing cues with audio make a major emotional impact The power of design in transforming health tech from useful to delightful What's next for Mindful You 📱 Native mobile app version (React Native) 🗣 Voice emotion detection for passive check-ins 🌐 Multi-language support for inclusive well-being 🫂 Community journaling & peer support network Built with Languages : JavaScript, Node.js Frameworks : Next.js, Express APIs : OpenRouter, HuggingFace, ElevenLabs Tools : Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, Axios, JWT Team Aaditya Salgaonkar "You can’t always control how you feel, but you can guide how you heal."
