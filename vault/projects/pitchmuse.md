## Body

🚀 PitchMuse – AI-Powered Pitch Deck Creator Turn any idea into a stunning, investor-ready deck in seconds. ✨ About the Project PitchMuse solves a critical pain point for early-stage founders: creating a pitch deck is often the most intimidating and time-consuming step in launching a startup. Not everyone is a designer, and not everyone knows how to communicate investor-friendly narratives. I built PitchMuse to take that pressure off. With just a short description of your startup idea, it generates a beautifully designed, 10-slide pitch deck—complete with Problem, Solution, Market, Team, and more. 💡 Inspiration I’ve seen countless founders with great ideas struggle to express them in slides. Either they spend hours on tools like PowerPoint or pay hundreds for help. I wanted to build something that empowered solo entrepreneurs, hackers, and non-technical founders to tell their story clearly—without needing design or fundraising experience. 🔧 How I Built It Frontend : Built in React with TailwindCSS for a fast and responsive experience. AI Integration : Used AI API with best system prompt to generate structured pitch content based on a single user prompt. UI/UX : Animations via Framer Motion and icons from Lucide-react for a modern, smooth design feel. PDF Export : Converts dynamic pitch decks into clean, shareable PDFs. AI-generated Problem Slide clearly framing the user's pain point. Solution Slide: Generated in seconds, visually structured for investor storytelling. ✨ Features 🔹 Full 10-slide pitch deck from one short prompt 🔹 Beautiful, responsive design templates 🔹 One-click PDF download 🔹 Smart structure: startup storytelling built-in Edit individual slides instantly using AI suggestions. 🧠 What I Learned Prompt design is everything – I had to iterate heavily on prompt structures to get investor-style content that’s persuasive yet concise. Design matters even in MVPs – A clean UI boosts user confidence in AI-generated content. People love speed and control – Users want fast results but also want to refine them. Balancing automation with flexibility was key. 🧗 Challenges Faced 🤯 Structuring AI outputs into a consistent, modular slide format 📄 Making the exported PDFs look as clean as the live app ✍️ Designing prompts that adapt across startup types (e.g., SaaS vs. B2C vs. AI) 🧪 Building an intuitive, editable interface while keeping it light for hackathon standards 🛣️ What’s Next 🎯 AI Investor Persona Tuning (VC vs. Angel targeting) 🎙️ Pitch Simulation Mode (voice or avatar presents the deck) 📈 Traction and metric generation (e.g. TAM, CAC, LTV estimations) 🤝 Live collaboration and comment mode 📊 Analytics on shared pitch links (opens, time spent per slide) ✨ “Magic Edit” mode to tweak AI-generated content 🙌 Final Thoughts PitchMuse was built in the spirit of democratizing entrepreneurship. Not everyone has pitch skills—but with AI, anyone can tell a compelling story. I’m excited to keep building on this vision, and thrilled to share it through this hackathon! <div
