# Chess for Miro

> Play chess on your Miro board! Drag-and-drop pieces, auto-validated moves, live move log, and turn indicators — all on the canvas.

## Body

Chess board after initial setup Screen showing elements change with game progress Logo Chess has helped me break away from tunnel-vision when brainstorming ideas, giving much needed mental distraction... so why not bring it where brainstorming happens. Chess for Miro brings a fully playable chess game directly onto your Miro board. Perfect for team breaks, icebreakers, or casual play during brainstorming sessions. Drag pieces to move — moves auto-validate against standard chess rules. Both players see turn indicators and a live move log on the canvas, so no sidebar is needed to follow the game. Supports captures, castling, en passant, pawn promotion, check, and checkmate. Multiple games can run simultaneously on the same board without conflicts. Key app features: • Play chess directly on the Miro canvas with drag-and-drop pieces • Auto-validation — moves confirm automatically in ~4 seconds • Turn indicators — green/grey bars show whose turn it is • On-board Move Log — all moves, captures, and check/checkmate visible to both players • Full chess rules — castling, en passant, pawn promotion, checkmate detection • Multi-instance — multiple games can run on the same board without conflicts • One-click reset with comprehensive cleanup <div
