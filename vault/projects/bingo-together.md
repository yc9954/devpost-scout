## Body

💡 Inspiration Bingo cards are a fun way for communities to engage in events together, and are a popular type of post of Reddit. Whether it be a Nintendo Direct event or your favourite streamer playing a video game, bingo cards are fun to craft and play together. Reddit is the perfect platform to share bingo cards due to the strong sense of community. What if there was a better way to create, share, crowd source ideas and play bingo cards? This was the inspiration for Bingo Together. ✨ Key Features 🎨 Create - create custom bingo cards for any topic, event, or trend. 🎭 Customize - customize your bingo cards with custom themes and logos. 💡 Community - suggest and upvote the best ideas from the community. 🎮 Play - customize your bingo card and play with friends. 🔗 Share - host a game and share an invite and play bingo together! 🔮 What's Next? I plan to keep developing Bingo Together, the community and support moderators looking to add bingo events to their subreddits. Once inline webviews support is added, I plan on replacing Devvit blocks to show a rich preview so every post feels engaging, fun and interactive. 📚 Learn More Visit r/BingoTogether for examples, community discussions, and to follow for the latest updates! <div
