Inspiration The inspiration for "GetFeed" came from a common challenge faced by businesses - collecting, analyzing, and acting on customer feedback. We recognized the need for a solution that seamlessly integrates with Square Online Site and other platforms to make this process more efficient and insightful. The idea was to empower businesses to harness the power of AI for a better understanding of their customers. What it does "GetFeed" is a comprehensive platform designed to streamline the management of customer feedback. It enables sellers to collect feedback seamlessly through customizable widgets integrated with Square Online Site and other websites. The platform's AI capabilities, powered by Google Vertex AI, analyze this feedback to provide sellers with summarized insights, sentiment analysis, and top keywords. Sellers can then act upon this data to communicate and address issues with their team and provide updates to their How we built it The "GetFeed" platform was built through a combination of cutting-edge technologies. We utilized Square's APIs to integrate feedback collection widgets with Square Online Site and other websites. The core of our platform's analytical power comes from Google Vertex AI, which is used to perform sentiment analysis and keyword extraction. We also incorporated a user-friendly interface for sellers to manage feedback. Challenges we ran into Integration Complexity: Integrating seamlessly with Square API is technical challenges, but we overcame them with determination and expertise. Data Processing: Handling and analyzing large volumes of feedback data in real-time required efficient data processing solutions. User Experience: Ensuring a user-friendly and intuitive interface for sellers was crucial, and it took iterations to achieve the desired level of simplicity. Accomplishments that we're proud of We are proud of several accomplishments: Successfully integrating with Square Online Site and other platforms to collect feedback. Harnessing the power of Google Vertex AI for in-depth sentiment analysis and keyword extraction. Creating a feedback management platform that simplifies seller interactions with their customers. What we learned Throughout the development of "GetFeed," we learned several valuable lessons. We gained insights into the intricacies of e-commerce platform integrations, data processing at scale, and the importance of user experience in a feedback management tool. Moreover, we deepened our understanding of AI's role in improving customer relationships and business operations. What's next for GetFeed The journey for "GetFeed" is just beginning. In the future, we plan to: Enhance AI features for even deeper insights, predictive analytics, and trend identification. Improve the user interface and add more collaborative features to help sellers address customer issues more effectively. Collaborate with Square and Google AI to explore new opportunities for innovation and growth.
