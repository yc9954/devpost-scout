## Body

Inspiration AuraUp was inspired by the need for accessible mental health support. It serves as an AI-powered chatbot designed to provide 24/7 emotional support and guidance to users at their finger tips. What it does AuraUp functions as a mental health companion that offers a range of services, including personalized recommendations, productivity tools, fitness guidance, educational support, expert advice, mental wellness exercises and recommendations How we built it AuraUp was built using PartyRock AWS and operates through the PartyRock website. It offers a free plan (for now) with no word limit of per conversation Challenges we ran into One challenge faced was ensuring the bot gave a tailored result of the prompt by implementing checks in the chatbot while providing powerful language processing tools Accomplishments that we're proud of AuraUp 's accomplishment lies in providing accessible mental health support through an AI chatbot that offers 24/7 emotional assistance and guidance to users in need What we learned Through developing AuraUp , valuable insights were gained on creating an AI-powered chatbot for mental health support and the importance of offering personalized assistance tailored to individual needs What's next for AuraUp The future for AuraUp includes expanding its availability to messaging platforms like WhatsApp, Telegram, SMS, etc., as well as supporting multiple languages to reach a broader user base <div
