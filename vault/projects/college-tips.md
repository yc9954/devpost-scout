College Tips Logo Inspiration I wish, I had tips and advice read out to me every day. Now you can. These tips have helped me in College and guided me through. These tips have been passed down to me from other students, teachers, professors, and co-workers. Hopefully, this will help you all too! What it does After you enable College Tips for Alexa, then you just ask for "college tips" and Alexa will respond with one! Example: "Talking is the best skill you can have." There are many more tips, some go into more detail. Challenges I ran into Not knowing Node.js, JavaScript, JSON, and AWS was difficult, but with many tutorials from Amazon, you can overcome anything! Amazon really helps developers with all ranges of experience. They even have weekly office hours! What's next for College Tips More tips and advice will be coming soon.
