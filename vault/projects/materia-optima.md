First this player sees Example game scene Inspiration The premise of Materia optima was inspired by the game Little Alchemy, where the player combines various “ingredients” to create new “ingredients” and so on. What it does In Materia optima you complete slide puzzles of increasing difficulty to create new materia (alchemical ingredients) and get new story extract. Accomplishments that I’m proud of I’m really proud of this app at large. It was really hard to create my first “big” Flutter app that I worked on from the start to finish.
