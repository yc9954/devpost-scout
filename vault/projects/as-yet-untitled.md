## Body

SNOWY TOWNLET AURORA 🌟 1. INSPIRATION SNOWY TOWNLET was inspired by real connection: I met my husband and best friend in VR while learning to build virtual worlds and wanted to recreate that feeling of joy and shared experience by crafting a magical, welcoming space where people can laugh, bond, and play mini games together, and keep coming back to improve their score or discover new things as there's so much to do/see. The mission? Continuing with the Snowy Townlet franchise (Snowy Townlet is a build of at least 4 relatable worlds). To build a world focused on fun, connection, and casual party games — something that feels both handcrafted and social at its core. To continually add features to the game. 🎲 2. WHAT IT DOES SNOWY TOWNLET is a magical snow covered village, filled with a variety of quick-play, multiplayer games, activities and interactions, designed for shared fun and repeat visits. 🔄 Core Gameplay & Interactivity The world experience is loop-based: most activities are designed to be fun within 2–5 minutes, while still rewarding repeat exploration. 20 FUN ACTIVITIES TO DO IN SNOWY TOWNLET ❄️ (just added a smoother more intuitive onboarding experience using an animated AI NPC) (and also added a reward that if they find all 20 coins they get a cool Sword in their Horizon Inventory) Play a timed Gingerbread Scavenger Hunt 🍪🎯 Adventure and explore the 7 islands 🏝️⚔️ Find the Selfie Spots for shareable moments 📸👑 There is a timed leader board to encourage you to come back and beat your score 🏆⏳ Find the secret hidden Golden Key for a shareable moment and huge surprise! 🗝️🎉 A relaxing place to chill and Hangout with friends 🛋️🍷 Complete the many Quests and see your Quest board 🎲📚 Fly the Magic Sleigh with manual Jingle Bells trigger 🛷🔔 Great world to hold role-playing games (like D&D) or events here 🧙‍♂️⚔️🧝‍♀️ Re-useable timed Jump Pad so you can jump on top of almost anything ⬆️🏰 Beautiful running waterfall with spray, and a surprise that comes right out of it 🌊💧🦄 Find the Lost Bear game, where the Teddy re-hides once you let go of him 🧸🔍 Enjoy a Toboggan ride ⛷️🎢 Find and go inside the cosy home and Xmas Shop ❄️⚔️👹 Fight enemies to reach the Gingerbread 🏹💀 Snowman shooting with Leader board to encourage return play 👀🤫 7 Trivia Quizzes to play with or without dice & counters (plus more to come) ☃️💡 Play different versions of Hide n Seek (player led) 🛡️👑🛠️ IWP Fantasy Armour, helmets, sword & Xmas clothing, plus a KUDOS board 📝🗺️ Heads up display for your score on the Scavenger Hunt which disappears once game over🌬️ Chill and listen to hauntingly beautiful music which hangs on the wind 🎶 Running navigating NPC Snowmen give you hints so you don't miss anything Find the "Surprise Golden Key" to unlock a unique, selfie-worthy shareable moment! HOW WE BUILT IT I created the concept, visuals, and made every single mesh in Blender and hand painted them all in Substance Painter, while my husband baked the textures together and imported the models... I used the desktop editor and utilised all these AI FEATURES: Creator Assistant for coding and building advice (incredible tool) Mesh Generation Skybox Generation SFX Audio Generation NPCs Generation 🔧 Technical 🌟All models (apart from AI ones), textures, and meshes are 100% original and handmade using: Blender (modelling) Substance Painter (texturing and ambient occlusion) Photoshop (additional detailing) 🌟Assets are low-poly and performance-optimized, designed specifically for Horizon Worlds mobile, the world is vast yet only using 50% of the recommended mesh allowance 🌟I coded several interactable experiences from mini games, to rides, to particle systems and animations plus with the help of AI some really cool NPC characters 🌟Improved the onboarding experience and added leader boards to encourage return visits 🎮 Functionality & Features Interactive logic, timer systems, score tracking, and Easter egg trigger Integrated leader-boards, music, ambient sound, particle effects, and fog Custom UI elements and game instructions make the world feel polished and intuitive Every system is built with performance and reliability across devices in mind. Lots of utilisations of Horizons AI features! 🚧 4. CHALLENGES WE RAN INTO Time Constraints: Every mesh and texture was created from scratch — which took longer, but ensured a unique style. Normal Mapping Limitations in Horizon led us to develop our own workaround using creative shading and hand-painted effects. 🏆 5. ACCOMPLISHMENTS WE'RE PROUD OF New features Snowy makeover and snow particle systems. Animated Snowman on skis plus other static Snowman. Skybox, waterfall, lighting and ambiance. Theme song composed by myself IWPs both Medieval & Xmas themed Gingerbread hunt and locations made easier to find Ride-able Santa Sleigh with optional sound trigger. Kudos board, and save world board Doors to other worlds Fun animated Toboggan ride with ray tracing Snowman blaster game now has leader board to encourage return & respawnable Snow gun. Nav mesh area to fight enemies to gain a Gingerbread Running NPCs that give you hints Secret door which leads to a secret inside scene with Xmas shop. Animated Fairy particle system.. Re-useable Jump Pad to give the feeling of temporarily flying Improved onboarding using AI NPCs etc ✨ Visual & Technical Craftsmanship Every mesh is hand-modelled and painted by myself, and low poly! — no 3rd party assets. Developed a custom texturing technique to simulate normal maps, adding fake depth and light behaviour to surfaces. 💑 Real-World Connection This is third full Horizon world — a personal and creative milestone We’ve seen people genuinely excited about visiting the world due to it's rich visuals and addictive gameplay. It's a really atmospheric world. 📚 6. WHAT WE LEARNED We gained deep skills in: Optimizing VR assets for performance without sacrificing style Creating immersive, re-playable game loops (especially for Trivia and Treasure Hunt systems) Faking normal mapping using only textures and light to add perceived depth Designing for social interaction and player-led exploration in VR spaces Leveraging Horizon’s UI and sound tools to make a cohesive, responsive experience We also learned that players love secrets, shared moments, and anything that encourages them to bring a friend. I really learned the benefits of using AI and was able to take the project far further thanks to this incredible feature within the desktop editor. 🚀 7.
