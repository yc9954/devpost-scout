## Body

Inspiration I were inspired by classic color-by-number and mystery picture worksheets, combined with our love for pixel art and daily brain games. We wanted to recreate that nostalgic experience in a modern, interactive way that feels fresh every day. What it does Pixel Art is a daily puzzle experience where users solve a grid-based challenge to reveal a hidden pixel artwork. Each day brings a new puzzle, encouraging consistency, curiosity, and problem-solving through simple visual logic. How we built it We built the project using a custom grid system, ASCII-based pixel definitions, and a color-mapping logic to render pixel art dynamically. Each puzzle is generated from predefined patterns and delivered as a daily challenge with lightweight logic and fast rendering. Challenges we ran into One of the biggest challenges was maintaining visual clarity at low resolutions (10×10 grids) while ensuring puzzles remained recognizable and fun. Balancing simplicity, symmetry, and difficulty also required multiple iterations. Accomplishments that we're proud of • Designed a scalable pixel-art puzzle system • Ensured consistent daily content without repetition • Achieved clear, readable pixel art within tight grid constraints • Built a system that is easy to expand with new puzzles and themes What we learned We learned how powerful constraints can be in driving creativity.
