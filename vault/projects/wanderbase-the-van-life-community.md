## Body

Inspiration Most dating apps look and feel the same: users swipe through profile cards that are little more than photos. There’s very little innovation or differentiation. How does another app stand out? Quin’s brief made me want to challenge that by creating something fresh and bold that also solves real problems for the van life community. What it does Instead of swiping a generic headshot stack, users view a countryside road with a customisable, hand illustrated van avatar that represents their potential match's lifestyle, build style, travel vibe, and personality. This creates a unique and memorable brand language to stand out, and to create joy. After all, dating and making friends should feel fun. At it's core, WanderBase is an invite-only community app for van lifers to meet people and help each other on the road. In one place, users can: Connect for dating or friendship with people whose travel plans overlap Chat and coordinate meetups with those they have matched with Ask and answer van build/conversion questions in a builder-help forum Share tips, routes, and road-life advice with a trusted community How we built it I built the app with react native and used Firebase for the backend. SendGrid was used for sending invite emails. I also used Codex to assist with development. Challenges we ran into Time. It's always time and not having enough of it. I especially struggle when it comes to making videos and it can take me hours just to record a 3 minute video because I keep redoing takes. Accomplishments that we're proud of I’m really proud of how the van editor turned out. Uploaded images have their backgrounds removed automatically using on-device SDKs, and the cover image is then automatically positioned and constrained within the van window. Stickers stay within the van’s visible pixels, so customisation feels creative while still having the right guardrails. I didn’t realise I could build so much with just PNGs, code, and coffee. What's next for WanderBase First stop: the App Stores. I think WanderBase has real potential. Next, I want to expand personalisation in the virtual van editor. This is the core brand language of the app and a key viral feature. That means more van types, stickers, and accessories, plus optional purchasable cosmetic items so users can stand out. As the user base grows, I plan to introduce group chats so friends can organise trips, events, and meetups directly in-app. Once trust and traction are established, the next step is a builder marketplace where users can post van-build jobs or offer their skills to take on paid work.
