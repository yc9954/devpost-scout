GIF 🛢 BarrelProof Labs AI Driven Structural Risk Intelligence for Modern Portfolios 🔥 Inspiration Modern investors don’t just hold assets they hold exposure Exposure to custodians, stablecoin issuers, tokenized infrastructure, regulatory jurisdictions, and hidden systemic dependencies Traditional apps answer “How much is my portfolio worth?” BarrelProof answers “Where is systemic fragility quietly building inside my portfolio?” As DeFi, cross-border custody, stablecoin concentration, and tokenized assets grow, risk becomes layered and interconnected BarrelProof was built to make that structure visible and actionable 🚀 What It Does BarrelProof is a portfolio intelligence app that turns holdings into an explainable structural risk map It aggregates assets across live crypto, tokenized products, and manual holdings like real estate or private capital It identifies concentration risk beyond asset percentages by modeling issuer, custodian, and jurisdiction exposure It surfaces hidden counterparty dependencies and correlated clusters It makes uncertainty explicit by including data confidence as part of the model It shifts users from performance vanity metrics to resilience decision-making 🧠 Core Innovation BarrelProof introduces multi-layer structural exposure modeling Asset layer models what you hold Custodian layer models where it sits Issuer layer models who backs it Jurisdiction layer models where it can be impacted Data confidence layer models how reliable the inputs are Layers are weighted and cross-analyzed to detect hidden single points of failure, false diversification, and regulatory clustering The result is deterministic and explainable, not a black box AI score 🛠 How We Built It Frontend built with Expo, React Native, and TypeScript using a modular scalable UI architecture Backend built on Supabase using PostgreSQL, Auth, and Storage with a structured exposure schema optimized for dependency queries Live pricing ingestion with resilient fallback handling Risk engine built with custom concentration algorithms and cross-layer dependency resolution Outputs are transparent and explainable, avoiding hallucinated AI scoring 💳 RevenueCat Monetization BarrelProof ships with a transparent Free vs Pro model Free includes basic tracking and baseline exposure insights Pro includes advanced layer analysis, deeper dependency mapping, and power features for serious portfolio resilience RevenueCat handles subscriptions, entitlements, and paywall logic This keeps feature gating clean, auditable, and scalable across platforms 🧱 Challenges Modeling risk without false precision or alarmism Representing uncertainty as a feature users can act on Designing exposure metrics that scale across assets and dependencies Keeping mobile UX clean despite analytical depth Maintaining real-time performance with live pricing and layered analysis 🌍 Impact Digital finance risk is fragmented and often invisible in standard portfolio trackers BarrelProof reduces blind concentration risk and improves exposure transparency It supports rational decision-making under uncertainty It helps users anticipate systemic failures before they show up on price charts It shifts portfolio tracking from performance obsession to structural resilience intelligence 🔮 Future Direction Time-evolving systemic risk tracking Diversification simulations and rebalancing scenarios Expanded asset class modeling for stocks, ETFs, and funds Institutional grade export and reporting Multi-device secure sync <div
