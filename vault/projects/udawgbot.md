## Body

Inspiration The UpDawg Hodler Community inspired the uDawgDAO Team to build uDawgBot, aiming to provide read methods, updates and notifications about TRC20 smart contract events directly to Telegram group chats. What it does uDawgBot is designed to deliver read methods, updates and notifications about TRC20 smart contract events directly to Telegram group chats. It allows users to access to read methods of any TRC20 smart contract. Additionally, by incorporating bot games, uDawgBot opens up additional monetization opportunities for users, making their experience more interactive and rewarding. Whether you’re a community member, seasoned trader, developer, or curious investor, uDawgBot provides you access to stay informed about every TRC20 smart contract on the TRON ecosystem. How we built it uDawgBot is built using TRON, TronWeb, Telegraph, node.js, npm, tronbot, telegram, and web3. These technologies were leveraged to create a seamless experience for users, allowing them to access read methods, updates and notifications about TRC20 smart contract events directly on Telegram group chats. Challenges we ran into One of the most significant challenges we encounter is gaining trust and awareness among Tronics, as UpDawg is not a standard TRC20 smart contract token. Overcoming this hurdle required extensive outreach and education efforts to demonstrate the value and legitimacy of the uDawg ecosystem. Accomplishments that we're proud of During the HackaTRON period, users can expect the following milestones: Created uDawgBot telegram interface and backend server functionalities. - March 27th, 2024✅ Integrated TRX read methods into uDawgBot. - March 29th, 2024✅ Incorporated UpDawg smart contract logging. - April 1st, 2024✅ Launched uDawgBot for public access.- April 10th, 2024✅ Integrated UpDawg smart contract read methods.- April 15th, 2024✅ Incorporated uDawgBot with TRC20 smart contracts read methods.- May 2nd, 2024✅ What we learned Through the development and deployment of uDawgBot, we learned that the most important aspect of any project is the community it serves. Building and nurturing a strong, engaged community is crucial for the success and sustainability of any endeavor. We gained valuable insights into the needs and preferences of Tronics, emphasizing the significance of community-driven development and support.
