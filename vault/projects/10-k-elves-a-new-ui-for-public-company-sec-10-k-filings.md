## Body

The Problem: 10-K Filings Are Hard To Read Investors Know They Should Be Reading 10-K Filings But These Documents Do Not Have an Investor-Friendly UI: Why Public Company SEC Filings Are So Hard To Read: are 100-200 pages long filled with legal jargon information covering the same topic is spread out across multiple sections duplicate data with no single source of truth the important information is buried in the footnotes The Solution: Using Coda & Coda AI, We Made 10-Ks Easy To Read We Built a New UI for 10-K Filings Using Coda + Coda AI. The DevPost project link will take you to a 10-K Elves report completed for Microsoft's latest 10-K filing. We used Coda AI on 1,416 distinct pieces of data within Microsoft's 10-K. The Coda AI-empowered report converts legal jargon into more investor-friendly text. When you are in the doc you will see a Before and After section that shows what it is like to read 10-K filings before Coda and after Coda.
