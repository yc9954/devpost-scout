Notes Notes Notes Inspiration The genesis of Tesseract AI was rooted in the burgeoning need for a secure, transparent, and user-empowered approach to genetic data management. Inspired by the potential of blockchain technology to revolutionize data privacy and access, Tesseract AI was envisioned as a platform that could bridge the gap between genetic data holders and the medical research community, all while ensuring the highest standards of data integrity and privacy. What it does Tesseract AI creates a decentralized ecosystem for managing genetic data, leveraging the Avalanche blockchain, Chainlink VRF, and Cartesi's ML solutions. It tokenizes genetic data as ERC-1155 tokens, allowing users to maintain ownership while granting controlled access to others. The platform hosts a data marketplace with a reverse bonding curve for token distribution, incentivizing data sharing, and facilitating the development of personalized medicine and advanced medical research. How we built it Tesseract AI was built by integrating several cutting-edge technologies. We utilized Avalanche subnets for scalable and secure data storage, Chainlink's VRF for transparent randomization processes, and Cartesi for off-chain ML computations with on-chain verification. The platform's data marketplace was designed with a reverse bonding curve model, and ERC-1155 tokens were implemented for flexible data representation. Development involved rigorous testing, continuous integration, and collaboration across multiple blockchain technologies. Challenges we ran into One of the most intricate challenges we faced was integrating AI, specifically Stable Diffusion, with our middleware in Tesseract AI. The integration process involved several complex layers, each presenting its unique set of hurdles. Accomplishments that we're proud of We are particularly proud of creating a platform that not only addresses the privacy concerns in genetic data management but also incentivizes the sharing of this data for the greater good. Successfully integrating advanced blockchain technologies to create a user-centric and secure environment for genetic data is an accomplishment that resonates deeply with our mission. What we learned Throughout the development of Tesseract AI, we gained invaluable insights into the complexities of blockchain interoperability, the nuances of data privacy in the healthcare sector, and the intricacies of developing sustainable economic models in a decentralized context. The project also underscored the importance of community and collaboration in the blockchain space. What's next for Tesseract AI The future of Tesseract AI involves further development of the Cognito-Virtual Machines for more sophisticated ML applications, expansion of the data marketplace, and continuous enhancement of the platform's security and user interface. We also plan to extend collaborations with medical research institutions and explore new use cases in personalized medicine. Long-term, Tesseract AI aims to become a leading platform for decentralized genetic data management, driving innovations in healthcare and personalized medicine. <div
