The book generation process involves the following steps: User provides a book title. If a title is provided, Coda AI intelligently generates a descriptive subtitle that complements the main idea of the book. This ensures that the subtitle captures the essence of the content and attracts potential readers. Coda AI generates a comprehensive table of contents based on the book's content. The table of contents serves as a roadmap, outlining the major sections, themes, and topics that will be covered in the book. It provides a high-level overview to give readers an idea of what to expect. Coda AI analyzes the content and organizes it into logical sections and chapters, ensuring a well-structured and coherent flow of information. The chapter titles from the table of contents are converted into a string, with each chapter separated by a semi-colon (;). This formatting choice of using semi-colons allows for easy parsing and manipulation of the chapter titles when processing them programmatically. It also caters to cases where chapter titles themselves may contain commas, ensuring that the structure of the book remains intact. Coda AI's automations, "add chapters", takes charge and generates titles, introductions, and estimates the number of pages for each chapter. Drawing on its intelligence, Coda AI crafts unique and engaging titles for each chapter. These titles capture the essence of the content and pique readers' curiosity. The introductions provide a brief overview of what readers can expect to find in each chapter, setting the stage for the upcoming content. Coda AI estimates the number of pages for each chapter based on factors like the complexity of the content, the density of information. This estimation ensures a consistent reading experience throughout the book. Another Coda automation, "add pages," comes into play and generates the actual content for each page. Leveraging the estimated number of pages calculated by Coda AI, this automation populates the pages with relevant information. The content is intelligently crafted to maintain a balance between informative text or any other elements that enhance the reading experience. Coda AI's sophisticated algorithms consider factors such as the chapter's main concepts, supporting examples, storytelling techniques, and even reader engagement metrics to create compelling page content. Once all the chapters' pages are completed, the user has the option to either read the book within the template or export it as a PDF file. Reading within the template allows for a convenient and seamless reading experience, enabling users to navigate through the book's chapters and pages effortlessly. Exporting the book as a PDF file provides versatility and accessibility. It allows users to share their masterpieces with others, distribute it through various platforms, or even print physical copies for personal or professional purposes. By offering these options, Coda AI empowers users to enjoy their books in the way that best suits their needs and preferences.
