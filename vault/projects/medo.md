Add task page Task details Inspiration The idea of MeDo is to ease the difficulties faced by freelancers nowadays such as high competition for jobs, unsurity about payments, and high fees charged by middlemen. Blockchain can help by guaranteeing payments with help of smart contracts, providing security and an integrated payment system that can save time delays, and fees in cases of international payments and its decentralized nature mean there is no middleman. What it does The platform allows users to add paid tasks. Two types of tasks are supported: FCFS - the first user who applied will be set as an assignee, Author Selected - the task author can select an executor e.g. by their ratings. Other users can apply and get paid by executing the tasks. The task author can rate the executor. How we built it The smart contract is built with Solidity. The frontend is built with React. Challenges we ran into The biggest challenge was to connect tronWeb to the frontend correctly. Accomplishments that we're proud of Entire platform.
