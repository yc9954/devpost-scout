Wakaba Prize (IJAG) I’m Just A Girl UI/UX Most Kawaii (LAKOG) Lowkey Actually Kind of Good Inspiration The idea for Thalemo was born after visiting a children’s thalassemia center. Witnessing the challenges faced by parents and doctors inspired the creation of a solution to simplify care and provide timely support. What It Does Thalemo is a comprehensive health companion app designed for both parents and doctors: For Doctors: Predicts blood disorders based on symptoms. Provides accurate diagnoses. Offers streamlined access to patient reports and histories. For Parents: Tracks transfusion schedules. Maintains health logs. Provides instant answers to common questions about treatments or diet restrictions. How I Built It React: For building the user interface. Tailwind CSS: For a visually appealing and responsive design. Dataset of Medical Information: Used to train the app for accurate symptom-based predictions and Q&A functionality. Challenges I Ran Into Designing an intuitive interface catering to both doctors and parents. Ensuring the prediction model’s accuracy despite limited and diverse datasets. Managing time constraints for building and testing features during the hackathon. Accomplishments That I Am Proud Of Delivering a functional application in a short timeframe. Addressing the needs of two distinct user groups effectively. Successfully implementing features like the transfusion tracker and instant Q&A. What I Learned Balancing user experience with technical accuracy in health-related apps. Addressing real-world pain points with impactful yet simple solutions. What’s Next for Thalemo Enhanced Prediction Models: Incorporate machine learning for even more accurate diagnoses.
