## Body

Inspiration When I'm not coding, I tend to run to let my brain relax. I've been doing this for at least two years and enjoying every minute of it. But running the same track for two years can be mundane at times. So I thought of an application that would spice up my running routine. Using AR, why not create a scavenger hunt game for runners? Not only it challenges my VO2 max capacity, it also challenges my ability to learn as I haven't yet tinkered with AR nor Android-Kotlin 😅 What it does Rundom is an immersive scavenger hunt app powered by ARCore Geospatial API. Random objects are scattered around you, and your goal is to collect them all as fast as possible. To start the scavenger hunt, users would be asked to define their meter range. This meter range will determine how far or close the random objects will be scattered. Once meter range is set, objects are accurately placed in random areas. Simply run to the object location as shown in the map, and the collect button would appear to collect such objects. Collect all the objects and you have finished a Rundom track 🎉 How we built it The foundation of the app lies on the geospatial codelab written by Dereck Bridie. With zero knowledge of AR nor Android-Kotlin, the codelab acted as my instructor as well as a blank canvas for my idea.
