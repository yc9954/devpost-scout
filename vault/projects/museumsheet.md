## Body

The Jennings Dog vs @corgi.cam Gift Shop Products auto-magically created from the exhibit! MuseumSheet couch-top share-able museum navigation! A typical large museum that shows about 1000 pieces in its current exhibition typically has 30,000 or more items hidden away in storage! What if one could have access to all of that, on-demand without needing extra space or that much funding? Enter our solution that automagically creates 3D models from photography the museum submits, and displays them in AR right on museum floor plans on our iOS and Android app - and also in the mobile browser via Apple's AR Quick Look... and uses each model as both a social conversation piece that anyone can share and a "pop up shop" connecting the patron with relevant gift shop items... that they can buy with Amazon Pay!
