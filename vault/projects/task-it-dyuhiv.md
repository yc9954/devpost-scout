Her comes the spice. Challenge your group. Add any task and let them bid on its value! Really. Just any task. Get the Facts! Who does all the cleaning? Share your expanses among the group. No more manual typing of expanses. Just add them directly from your bank account. Inspiration You know Bankify ? Shared group expenses? Good for you. It should be more exiting. More convenient. Both at the same time! What it does Taking Bankify to the next level. Challenge your friends, create tasks and bid! Do all the tasks, get discounts on shared expenses and become flat mate of the month! Show around the statistics! Automatically retrieve account history via Nordea API and add them to shared expenses. No manual typing. How we built it A nice Python backend to retrieve live account data from Nordea.
