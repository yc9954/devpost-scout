## Body

Inspiration Inspired by a very popular VR game but it needs hardware setup! So I was inspired to achieve the same using just AR in a Snap Lens! What it does Fun AR Game where you have to slash the beats using your hands, and no additional equipment is needed thanks to the excellent hand-tracking in Lens Studio where I wanted to show its potential in achieving such a game where many popular games in VR do but with a dedicated headset and set of hardware controllers! With this, it all works through just a lens! Additionally, there’s an ability to choose your fav music from Snap’s licensed music of thousands of songs and the game elements will react to any beat of that music! How we built it Using Lens Studio excellent hand-tracking and advanced tech offerings Challenges we ran into Working around the ability to pull user's fav music Accomplishments that we're proud of Every bit of the project from achieving what was possible using VR equipment to the ability to pull music and react to it from the licensed library What we learned A lot, this opens the door to a whole new type of lenses What's next for Rhyme BeatAR multiple difficulty levels and partnerships! <div
