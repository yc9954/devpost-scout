What it does Pixieland empowers gamers with a metaversal play to earn game using dynamic digital assets with a cross chain marketplace How we built it We built our front end using react, react spring, solidity and nodejs. Challenges we ran into Most of the challenges we experienced dealt with integrating the front end with the back end, creating the gaming aspect but are proud to come up with a live application What we learned We learned the value of teamwork and the hard technical skills of solidity as well as how to integrate the front and back end. Because of the diversity of the teams skill set, we were also able to share our experience to come up with a great application.
