## Body

Quizo Homepage Inspiration I wanted to make studying more interactive and efficient by turning educational materials like PDFs, notes, and images into quizzes automatically. What it does Quizo is an AI-powered quiz generator that allows users to upload study materials and instantly generate quizzes, take tests, review mistakes, and track results. How I built it I built Quizo using React, Tailwind CSS, Framer Motion, serverless functions, and AI integration for quiz generation and educational content analysis. Challenges we ran into One of my biggest challenges was production deployment and serverless function issues, especially while working with limited platform credits during development. Accomplishments that we're proud of I'm proud of building a polished upload-to-quiz workflow with a modern UI, educational validation system, quiz setup flow, and interactive analytics dashboard. What I learned I learned a lot about AI-assisted development, prompt engineering, frontend architecture, deployment workflows, and designing reliable AI-powered experiences.
