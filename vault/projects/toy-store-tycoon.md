Thumbnail Vertical Thumbnail Main Inspiration The inspiration behind this Remixable world was to unlock Many platform features for those new to the platform. Packaged in a Simple yet sophisticated game that can be understood and enjoyed by everyone. What it does Toy Store tycoon is a Simulation/Tycoon Game where you have a warehouse to manufacture Different Toys to then Supply your "Inventory". You have ability to level up to 3 Assembly lines and hire 2 NPC Workers. Then the Store Front is where you will attend to NPC customers Selling them Items from your Inventory to make money and level up your score on Leaderboard! How we built it Used Typescript, Desktop Editor and AI Gen Accomplishments that we're proud of Creating automated npc customers and NPC WORKER!
