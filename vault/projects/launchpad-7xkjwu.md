## Body

Inspiration What Inspired me to build launchpad was that there were like a bajillion application portals for different universities and colleges and as a busy high-schooler, it took me 2 years to wrap my head around most of them. Thats why I built Launchpad! it consolidates all that you need to know about college applications around the globe and how to make your application better. What it does Launchpad uses your profile data (user entered) to score your profile out of 10 based on your essays, academics, awards and extracurriculars. It then uses gpt-4o-mini to assess your strengths and weaknesses to present you with a tailor made strategy for college applications. Launchpad also has a (currently small) database of universities/colleges as well as upcoming opportunities (competitions, scholarships). Clicking on a college will presented you with AI generated insights about that college and how your 'fit' matches with what they are looking for and whether you are a competitive applicant or not by rating your fit and chances of admission out of 10 based on SEVERAL FACTORS. This is achieved using a REALLY LONG prompt that goes into in depth detail about admission requirements and nuances for every country/region and university type (e.g: us admissions rely more on overall profile strength where as UK admission tend to be more grade oriented). It also understand and looks for and ONLY considers relevant parameters for assessing application strength (e.g: if you're applying for a uk university and have your essay entered and specified as being a ucas essay, then only that will be taken into account since its the only relevant essay). Launchpad also takes into factor your efc (estimated family contribution) as well as your extra curriculars as well. Besides assessing application strength and chances of admission into a university and detailed feedback about your overall strengths and weaknesses as well as your college/university specific strengths and weaknesses, it has a list of upcoming extracurriculars for you to participate in, and upcoming scholarships all with dates and deadlines displayed too.There are also links too college application portals, extracurricular and scholarship signup pages that are added into the description (first as plain text) and then made into anchor tags/clickable links using some complex regex. In addition to that, launchpad consolidates all your dates and deadlines for scholarships, colleges extracurriculars into one place along with a nifty calendar based view to keep track of them. LaunchPad also has an admin panel used to add new universities, activities and scholarships and handle some basic CRUD regarding them. The profile analysis also employs a caching via localstore since the api response times are quite long How we built it and AI Tools Disclosure It was built using Next.js and Next API routes along with Mongo DB and the open AI API (using gpt 4o mini and Tailwind-CSS for styling and AOS for animations.
