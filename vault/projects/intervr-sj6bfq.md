## Body

Inspiration In 2018, 41% of Canadians suffer from an anxiety disorder. anxieties tend to affect people's performance when they are under pressure and a common example is job interviews. Our team of developers eager to help people overcome their anxieties and breakthrough limitations. What it does Hiring agencies will set you up with a VR headset in a room where your actions and voice will be recorded. A mix-reality experience will launch where you will find yourself in a real 360 interactive experience. First, you going to have to choose your profession Once you select your field you will find yourself sitting across the table with an interviewer who will be asking you questions about yourself and questions related to your chosen topic. You will have the chance to playback your answers. If you are confident with your answers you can submit them or try another attempt by re-doing the interview or select questions. Once the answers are submitted, the experts from the hiring agency will evaluate your answers and guide you to perform better in an interview. How we built it We used an Insta360 Camera to take 360 videos and pictures that we used as assets in developing the immersive experience. Challenges we ran into Due to React 360 being relatively new, there is limited information regarding some of the problems we encountered. Accomplishments that we are proud of Executing the project was very challenging but very rewarding. We are very glad that we managed to complete what we aimed for as well as have lots of fun along the process. What we learned The knowledge that we gain was a great award already. We look looking forward to continuing expanding our knowledge and experience in react 360 in hopes to solve more problems.
