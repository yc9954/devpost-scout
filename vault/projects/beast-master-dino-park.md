## Body

Inspiration About 1 week ago the community got access to animated mesh, and as soon as I got ahold of it I needed to do something with it. What's Better Than Riding Dinos. What it does The Island is full of beast to tame, or buy your own to level up. Explore, complete missions with your tame and fight crazy battles with MASSIVE Beasts! How we built it Using the new animated fbx assets paired with Navmesh i was able to create a living ecosystem, where you have to fight to survive. Challenges we ran into Working with the new animated fbx mesh was difficult to figure out with the limited documentation with it being so new. Being new to navmesh i found it also different and slightly difficult to use but after some time I'm happy with the result. Accomplishments that we're proud of I'm really Happy with being able to get the animations in for the Dino's but above all i love the little eco system that's been created with the animals. Sometimes I just sit there and watch them eat each other. What we learned I learned a lot about animation and even more about navemesh. I'm supper excited to use navemesh more in more worlds What's next for Beast Master -Dino Park- With Beast Master the goal is to add tons of beasts for player to tame and unlock. New ways to unlock tames like Unlockable through egg hatching and iwps. Larger maps, More Missions even Different Attacks. <div
