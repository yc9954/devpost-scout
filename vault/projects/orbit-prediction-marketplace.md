landing Page Create Prediction Page Buy Shares Page Swipe card page 🌍 ORBIT – Electroneum-Powered Prediction Marketplace 🚀 💡 Inspiration Prediction markets have traditionally been complex and intimidating , requiring deep market knowledge and technical understanding. We wanted to change that by making forecasting as simple as a swipe . Inspired by intuitive apps like Tinder , we created Orbit —a gamified, swipe-based prediction platform . Our goal? To make prediction markets accessible, engaging, and fast for everyone. 🔮 What it does Orbit is a Telegram Mini App-powered prediction marketplace built on the Electroneum blockchain . It allows users to: ✅ Swipe Right for Yes, Left for No on market predictions. ✅ Predict real-world events , including crypto trends, sports, and entertainment. ✅ Leverage low-cost, high-speed blockchain transactions for seamless participation. ✅ Instantly access markets within Telegram , eliminating the need for additional app downloads. 🛠️ How we built it To ensure Orbit delivers a seamless user experience, we used the following tech stack : 🖥️ Frontend: Built with React + TypeScript for a modern, responsive UI. ⚡ Performance: Used Vite for fast development and optimized builds. 🔗 Blockchain: Integrated Electroneum Network for secure, low-cost transactions. 📱 Mini App Framework: Leveraged the Telegram Mini Apps SDK for smooth in-app experiences. 🎨 Styling & UX: Designed with Tailwind CSS for a clean, mobile-friendly interface. 🔐 Smart Contracts: Developed and deployed prediction market contracts on Electroneum. 🚧 Challenges we ran into 🎭 Swipe Mechanics: Ensuring a smooth, intuitive swipe experience for predictions. ⏳ Real-Time Transaction Processing: Optimizing Electroneum’s blockchain speed for instant updates. 📏 Mini App Constraints: Adapting UI/UX to function effectively within Telegram’s ecosystem. 🔐 Security & Smart Contract Audits: Implementing secure contracts while keeping gas fees low. 🏆 Accomplishments that we're proud of 🎉 Created a first-of-its-kind swipe-based prediction market. 🚀 Seamlessly integrated blockchain transactions for real-time settlements. 📱 Built a fully functional Mini App within Telegram, removing onboarding barriers. 💰 Optimized micro-predictions , allowing users to participate without high fees. 📚 What we learned 🎯 User Experience is Everything: Simplicity drives adoption. ⚡ Blockchain Efficiency Matters: Low fees and fast confirmations make micro-predictions viable. 🌍 Community Growth is Key: Leveraging Telegram’s network effect accelerates adoption. 🔒 Security & Trust are Non-Negotiable: Transparent, audited smart contracts build confidence. 🔜 What's next for ORBIT – Electroneum-Powered Prediction Marketplace 📊 Expansion Beyond Crypto: Adding markets for sports, entertainment, and real-world events. 🤝 Social Features: Enabling user-generated predictions and leaderboards. 📱 Mobile App Development: Expanding Orbit into a standalone mobile application. 🎙️ Strategic Partnerships: Collaborating with influencers and brands for broader reach. 📈 Advanced Analytics: Providing users with data-driven insights for better predictions. 🚀 Orbit is more than just a prediction platform—it’s a revolution in market forecasting , designed for the next generation! <div
