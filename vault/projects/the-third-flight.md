# The Third Flight

> This AI-made film, in modern war and shot by drone, shows war’s brutality and irreversibility. Without dialogue, it shows ordinary people are innocent but harm and suffer, warning to cherish peace.

## Body

Inspiration: We created this film out of a simple, profound sorrow for the human cost of war. Witnessing the ongoing suffering of innocent civilians, we felt compelled to use our art as a poignant reminder to cherish peace. How We Built It : The entire film was crafted using an AI pipeline, viewed strictly through the lens of a drone. We employed intensive prompt engineering and AI video generation models to achieve the consistent, stark visual style. The absence of dialogue focuses the narrative entirely on the AI-generated imagery and a meticulously designed soundscape, edited to deliver a powerful, immersive experience. Challenges We Faced : Our greatest technical hurdle was achieving visual consistency across AI-generated frames. We spent countless hours curating and refining outputs to find the precise frames that conveyed both the epic scale and the intimate tragedy of war. What We Learned : This process was a revelation in the potential of AI as a directorial partner. It taught us that AI is not a replacement for human vision, but a powerful amplifier of it.
