Inspiration Every morning, one of our team members had to request an Uber to the train station for work. We thought it would be useful if we could schedule ride requests ahead of time and even repeat them! What it does Goobr allows users to request their Uber rides ahead of time as well as set up recurring rides throughout the week. Challenges I ran into Spring framework was hard to figure out.
