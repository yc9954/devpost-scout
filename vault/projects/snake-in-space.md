## Body

About the NFT Dark tree trunks, Wind slipping through leaves, shadows, barely visible black trails snaked through the undergrowth. Deep within the forest were two friends warming themselves in the long night. Inspiration I always wanted to make my own NFT. With the help of Photoshop and pixelart.com have tried to make my First NFT. This NFT is inspired by Minecraft. Minecraft is one such game that almost everyone has played. We all have our own Minecraft memory. Be it building a super cool rollercoaster with friends or just exploring the deep fighting the scary mobs. Minecraft has left an everlasting impression on all of us. That is why I wanted to celebrate and cherish my Minecraft memories by making a Minecraft Pixel Art NFT. The scene depicts two friends (A Creeper and a Skeleton) warming themselves in a forest. Maybe this is what mobs do when we are not around, exploring the never-ending terrain and enjoying each other's company. <div
