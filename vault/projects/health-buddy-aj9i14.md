## Body

The Challenge In the United States, accessing healthcare providers often involves long wait times, which can be frustrating and potentially dangerous for patients seeking timely medical advice. Moreover, conducting thorough online health research can be time-consuming and overwhelming for the average person. Many find themselves lost in a sea of medical jargon and conflicting information, unsure of what to trust. The Solution To address these challenges, I developed Health Buddy, a health information chatbot. This AI-powered assistant aims to bridge the gap between the need for quick, reliable health information and the often slow, complex process of obtaining it. Health Buddy provides users with: Rapid access to accurate, up-to-date health information Assistance in understanding general health topics and symptoms Help in locating nearby healthcare facilities Time savings in initial health information gathering Key Features Reliable Information Source : I source the health information directly from MedlinePlus Connect, a free service of the National Library of Medicine (NLM), National Institutes of Health (NIH), and the Department of Health and Human Services (HHS), ensuring accuracy and credibility. Advanced Search Capabilities : Utilizing TiDB's vector search and Jina Embeddings AI, Health Buddy quickly finds the most relevant information. Natural Interaction : Powered by Google's Gemini Pro language model, Health Buddy engages in human-like conversations, making complex health information more accessible. Location-Based Services : Users can quickly find nearby hospitals when needed. Responsible Health Guidance : Health Buddy is designed to provide general information while encouraging professional medical consultation for specific concerns. Important Limitations While our chatbot is a powerful tool for health information, it's crucial to understand its limitations: No Diagnostic Capabilities : The chatbot does not provide medical diagnoses. Accurate diagnosis requires in-person evaluation by healthcare professionals, as misdiagnosis can lead to serious health consequences. Educational Purpose : The information provided is for educational purposes only and should not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Encourages Professional Consultation : Users are always encouraged to consult with qualified healthcare providers for specific medical concerns. The Impact By providing quick, reliable health information, Health Buddy aims to: Empower users with knowledge about their health concerns Reduce anxiety associated with unknown health issues Help users make informed decisions about when to seek professional medical help Alleviate some of the burden on the healthcare system by providing basic information that doesn't necessarily require a doctor's visit Future Vision As I continue to develop and refine Health Buddy, I envision it becoming an integral part of personal health management. By continuously updating the chatbot knowledge base and improving the AI capabilities, I aim to provide an ever more comprehensive and user-friendly health information resource. The goal is not to replace healthcare providers but to complement their work by ensuring patients are better informed and can make the most of their interactions with medical professionals. Through this project, we are taking a step towards a future where reliable health information is accessible to everyone, promoting better health awareness and decision-making in our communities. <div
