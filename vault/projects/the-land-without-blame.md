## Body

The Land Without Blame Inspiration The story is inspired by the short story of the same name from Yuan Mei's What the Master Does Not Speak Of from the Qing Dynasty. The unique Chinese folk view of good and evil presented in the original text evokes many phenomena in contemporary society, such as cyberbullying and mob mentality. Therefore, this adaptation of the original story presents a fantastical tale centered on a group narrative, seeking to explore and contemplate the "banality of evil" within human nature. <div
