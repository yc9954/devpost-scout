## Body

Logo Inspiration We have many friends who want to learn about cars but they don't know where to start, so we got this idea to build an app where content regarding the car community is present. What it does This app basically stores all of the data in regards to the car community and retrieves it whenever someone willing to learn about it shows up. How we built it We started building this app in Android-Studio using Java. We Sampled models from the internet and edited them in Photoshop and Adobe Lightroom Challenges we ran into We were completely new to this field since we are students from their third semester in college. We had many errors and it took us many tries and much time to try and solve these errors. Accomplishments that we're proud of We are very proud of what we have achieved and are quite happy for the efforts we put in even if we were not aware of these software's used in creating applications. What we learned We have never thought of creating an application this early in our college life but I guess we can achieve things we usually don't think are possible and we are very proud of what we did.. What's next for Blazin' Wheels _ Next thing we would like to do is implement the backend part and also improve the quality of the application while expanding it so that we can spread the interests of the car community throughout the world._ <div
