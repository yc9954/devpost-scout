## Body

Inspiration I've a couple young Gen Z / almost Gen Alpha friends, so, once I saw this Hackathon, I decided to consult the experts on the matter. The best ideas from our brainstorm were a Papa's Pizzeria (and the other games in the series) inspired game but with Lunchly, and a Skibidi Toilet Dating Simulator. Considering the amount of days I had to learn how to program a videogame (as I've never done this before) I chose the Skibidi Toilet Dating Sim. What it does It's like most dating simulators tbh; branches of dialogue and stuff. How I built it I commissioned a friend to draw the assets, wrote the dialogue branches, and programmed everything using Renpy. Challenges I ran into My team dipped two days into the Hackathon, and my artist literally finished the drawings last night; so I had to cut the code and story short to have a satisfactory game considering all these issues. Also I couldn't get some of the videos and music to work lol Accomplishments that I'm proud of This is more personal, but I'm proud of actually having finished this by myself considering that everything that could've gone wrong, went wrong (both in my personal life and in my participation in this Hackathon) Also this landed just during my finals season so that was funny Guess I'm just a chill guy who lowkey doesn't give a fuck What I learned Don't team up with people who you know are flakey just because they're your friends.
