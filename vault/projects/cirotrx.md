Website (intro) Website (Explain) Website (Team) Inspiration We clearly understand our inspiration: to simplify the process for novice users on the Tron network, especially when it comes to sending stables. Resource rental can be a hurdle, and Cirotrx emerges as a solution to streamline this process. By removing technical complexities, we empower users to focus on what truly matters: value transfer. What it does After contract approval (with a cost below 10 TRX), we enable users to perform STABLES transactions on Tron without requiring TRX or energy in their wallets. The default bandwidth suffices for these transactions. In return, a small commission is added to the stable being sent. How we built it We have developed a contract with Tron’s major stables, primarily aimed at covering the cost of these transactions Challenges we ran into Our goal is to streamline Tron payments, enabling efficient transactions of stables at a reduced cost. We aim to foster greater adoption and enhance the usability of Tron stables without requiring users to hold large amounts of staked TRX in their wallets. Additionally, we seek to offer a more economical alternative compared to traditional burn fees. Accomplishments that we're proud of We are incredibly proud to have developed a simple and functional utility that is unique to Tron. This utility contributes to solidifying Tron’s position as the blockchain for stables. It’s not only a testament to our technical skills but also reflects a deep understanding of the blockchain ecosystem and how to address specific challenges. What we learned We are incredibly proud to have developed a simple and functional utility that is unique to Tron. This utility contributes to solidifying Tron’s position as the blockchain for stables. It’s not only a testament to our technical skills but also reflects a deep understanding of the blockchain ecosystem and how to address specific challenges. What's next for CIROTRX Following Team Brutus Finance’s philosophy, the next step is to create a token backed by the commissions generated by the Cirotrx contract. This token will be obtainable exclusively through delegating energy to the contract. This innovative approach aligns with the team’s vision and could enhance the ecosystem by providing a unique utility token.
