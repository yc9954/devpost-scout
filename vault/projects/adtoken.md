AdToken Logo AWS Kendra's Chat support by AdToken AWS Kendra's Chat support by AdToken Product Screenshort Product Screenshort Product Screenshort Product Screenshort Overview AdToken is a decentralized, transparent AI advertising solution utilizing blockchain technology, designed to transform the digital advertising landscape. By leveraging blockchain, AdToken provides a fair, efficient, and transparent platform for both advertisers and affiliates. Our platform is chain-agnostic, currently live on Sui and TON Chain, utilizing advanced object models to enable real-time and fast confirmations for AdToken transactions and campaign management. Additionally, we have integrated AWS Kendra's API to power our support chatbot, enhancing user support with intelligent search and information retrieval capabilities. [Decentralized Google Ads] Vision and Mission Vision: To create a transparent advertising ecosystem that empowers advertisers and rewards affiliates fairly. Mission: To eliminate fraud, reduce costs, and increase transparency in digital advertising through decentralization, utilizing advanced object models for enhanced performance and reliability. Key Features Transparent Escrow-Based On-Chain Campaigns: Ensures secure and fair transactions for advertisers and affiliates. Real-Time, Gas-Free Payments: Affiliates receive instant payments on-chain without any gas fees. Crowdfunded Campaigns: Advertisers can crowdfund their ad campaigns with support from their community. On-Chain Reporting & Fraud Detection: Provides immutable and transparent tracking of campaign performance and prevents fraud. Campaign Dashboard: Real-time tracking and analytics for advertisers and affiliates. Cross-Chain Payments (Coming Soon): Flexible and chain-agnostic payment options. Business Integration (Coming Soon): Enables businesses to integrate and display ads, earning money through allocated ad spaces. AWS Kendra Integration: Enhances our support chatbot with advanced search and information retrieval capabilities. How It Works Partner (Advertiser) Onboarding: Advertisers create an account and connect their wallet. Provide campaign details via the decentralized app (dApp) interface. Mint a Conditional AdToken representing the campaign. Create an escrow account to manage the campaign budget. Monitor campaign performance through the dashboard. Campaign Listing and User Participation: Campaigns are listed on the platform’s portal. Users connect their wallets to browse and promote campaigns. Generate unique referral links and earn rewards for each click. Affiliate System and Reward Distribution: Affiliates generate referral links. Each click through the referral link is tracked on-chain. Instant rewards are settled to the affiliate’s wallet. Technical Overview AdToken Mechanics: Each campaign mints a unique AdToken on the blockchain, storing all campaign details and tracking dynamic fields such as clicks and budget in real-time. Smart Contracts and Escrow Accounts: Automated creation and management of AdTokens and escrow accounts, ensuring secure and efficient transactions. On-Chain Data Management: Immutable records and real-time updates of all campaign and affiliate data, ensuring transparency and preventing tampering. Security Measures Regular Audits: Smart contracts undergo regular audits to ensure security and integrity. Fraud Prevention: Validation mechanisms are in place to ensure only legitimate clicks are rewarded. Conclusion AdToken is set to revolutionize digital advertising by providing a decentralized, transparent, and efficient platform that benefits both advertisers and affiliates. With real-time payments, reduced costs, and enhanced security, AdToken is paving the way for the future of digital advertising. The integration of AWS Kendra's API further strengthens our support system, making it easier for users to find the information they need.
