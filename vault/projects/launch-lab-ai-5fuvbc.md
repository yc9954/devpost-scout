Inspiration Students often struggle with STEM not because they lack ability, but because they lack personalized guidance. Inspired by the startup philosophy of Y Combinator—building solutions to real, urgent problems—we focused on a key issue: students repeatedly search for explanations, yet still fail to truly understand concepts. What it does LaunchLab AI is an AI-powered adaptive STEM learning platform that: Diagnoses a student’s understanding in real time Generates personalized explanations based on learning style Provides adaptive quizzes that evolve with performance Simulates interactive STEM scenarios (e.g., physics experiments, math visualizations) At its core, LaunchLab AI ensures that learning is interactive, personalized, and mastery-driven rather than passive.
