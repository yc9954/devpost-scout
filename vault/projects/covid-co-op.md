## Body

Signup page for brands couldn't be simpler. Homepage of covidcoop.com We've partnered with GiveDirectly.org on this to get traction and make it easy for brands to give. Inspiration We were inspired by Sheets & Giggles, a company that has pledged 20% of its online sales for the next 30 days to COVID–19 relief ($15k donated in 10 days). We thought if ever online consumer brand did the same thing, millions could be raised. SheetsGiggles.com/COVID What it does It allows American consumers to search on covidcoop.com for the categories of goods they need, where they'll find participating companies who have pledged to donate a percentage of sales to COVID–19 relief. How I built it Mostly with ReactJS, CSS, react Animation Libraries and Material UI. Challenges I ran into Search functionality logic, routing challenges, finding participating brands, finding a partner charity. Accomplishments that I'm proud of We're proud to officially partner with GiveDirectly.org's COVID–19 relief fund, which gives brands a fantastic, nationally recognized donation partner to easily send donations. Give Directly was very excited about Covid Co-Op! What I learned Streamlining all desired functionality to deliver a working MVP quickly.
