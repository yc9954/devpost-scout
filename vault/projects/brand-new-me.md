## Body

Brand New Me, Icon Inspiration The inspiration behind "Brand New Me" is the iconic Barbie doll and the desire to bring a touch of fun and creativity to everyday life. What it does "Brand New Me" is a body-tracking and segmentation filter, that transforms you into a doll-like figure How we built it I utilized the body-tracking technology and segmentation to create the "Brand New Me" filter. Challenges we ran into One of the biggest challenges i faced during the development of "Brand New Me" was integrating body tracking and segmentation to create a seamless and accurate user experience. Additionally, presenting a back and front texture of the doll box Accomplishments that we're proud of I proud to have created a filter that transforms users into a doll-like figure without altering their body shape, while avoiding stereotypical representations. "Brand New Me" allows users to express their creativity and individuality in a fun and playful way, while preserving the natural form of their bodies. What we learned Through the development of "Brand New Me", I learned the importance of user experience What's next for Brand New Me I constantly working on improving and updating "Brand New Me" to make it the best it can be. In the future, we plan to introduce new features and options to allow users to personalize their experience even further. (new box texture, garment transfert,..) <div
