the dashboard the room for chatting Inspiration We used Zoom as our inspiration. What it does We used firebase as a way to authenticate the users. Users are able to open and view their webcam and text other users within the room who are also active. How we built it We used React for the Front end, Firebase as a database for authentication, and express and socket.io for the real chat aspect. Challenges we ran into We ran into issues trying to use simplePeer as a way to send the video and audio to other users within the room. Accomplishments that we're proud of We are proud that users can sign up, login and text other users. What we learned We learned alot about Css, React, and backend development, we also learned about Firebase as well.
