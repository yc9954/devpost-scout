# Tableaufy.ai

> AI agent to convert plain English text into Tableau visualizations

[Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/tableaufy-ai) · hackathon [[Tableau DataDev 2023-2024 Hackathon]]

## Facets


**stack** flask, gpt4, openai, tableau, tableau-api

## Body

Tableaufy.ai was born out of the frustration of how complicated creating dashboards in Tableau can feel, especially for beginners. While working on BI dashboards for my university, I realized how much time and effort went into training, tweaking, and endless clicks, and thought - there has to be a simpler way.
