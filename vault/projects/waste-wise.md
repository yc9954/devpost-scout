## Body

main menu gameplay in-game knowledge Inspiration Many countries have a problem about waste. Humans throw away things every day leading to a bigger environment problem. So, what a regular person can do at least. From that question, my answer is "waste management" looking at Japan. if people around the world can take care of their trash follow social rules it's easily leading to a clean and sustainable environment. That's why I want to create something to help people aware and understand that it's not difficult at all to take care of the world. What it does This game allows player to learning and testing their waste management knowledge using a simple rule "put right trash in the right bin". How we built it I built it with Flutter which is not a difficult works since I am a programer but I also drew art in game by myself, it took a while till I satisfied with the result. Challenges we ran into At the beginning when I decide to do a matching card game, I can't decide how to categorize items in game there are so many ways of waste sorting method and it different between country. So, I researched it for a while before came up with a simple and effective method which I use in game. Accomplishments that we're proud of I really love the game it has enjoyable gameplay and cozy cute art style. What we learned I learned about the game design it somehow different from general application it not just need to function, but it needs to be fun. What's next for Waste Wise Making it more international, add various categorization methods from countries around the world. Also, add more items. <div
