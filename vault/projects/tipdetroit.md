## Body

Inspiration Seeing how small businesses and the families they support were quickly impacted due to COVID-19. Their call outs for support were very unique to each business. What it does The site aggregates user submitted small businesses and displays the best way people can support them; including where they need to go and do it. How I built it I built the initial front end and some friends helped develop a stronger data structure for displaying businesses. Challenges I ran into Working to get exposure. I’m donating my own $, but I’m not sure it will be enough. Accomplishments that I'm proud of We built this site in a weekend. What I learned People are willing to move fast when the project has a good heart. What's next for TipDetroit It’s not complex, but I would like to open source what we built so other cities and communities can copy and repeat. <div
