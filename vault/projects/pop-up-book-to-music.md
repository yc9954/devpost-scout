## Body

Head of the app Model, origin and theory Sounds and books Competitions, schools and chatbot. Inspiration When my son played with the pop-up music book, he wasn't that interested in the instruments on the paper book. So I want to make an app that can display 3D models of musical instruments, sounds and other information. What it does It can display instrument models, reveal the history of the instrument, the basics of music theory, and display websites where the sound of the instrument can be heard, related competitions and schools for the instrument. How we built it I used https://partyrock.aws/ to build it Challenges we ran into At first, I hoped to create a widget that directly plays the sound of the instrument next to the instrument model, but it failed. Accomplishments that we're proud of Able to provide relevant knowledge and pictures of musical instruments in a very short period of time. What we learned Although it is not possible to directly play the sound of musical instruments, you can consider using other ways to display it. For example, I changed it to directly displaying websites that can play the sounds of musical instruments. What's next for Instrument Explorer The next step is to consider adding the ability to find concerts of that instrument in the user's city. <div
