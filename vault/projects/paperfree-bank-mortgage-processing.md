## Body

Custom tailored intuitive pre-approval Intuitive visuals to facilitate workflow Paperless signatures via docusign Dashboard views for loan officers Inspiration We were really inspired to create a safe and easy way to complete what is usually a very paper and in-person intensive process. With so many people working remotely (the customer and the loan officer in this case), we felt it was important to highlight how cloud tools such as Quick Base, Dropbox, and Docusign could seamlessly replace that paper heavy process. What it does The application allows non-QB users (i.e. "Customers") to apply for pre-approval of a loan, internal employees of the bank to process the pre-approval, send out a loan application via docusign, have the customer complete it, and then manage the tedious process to process and get the loan closed. Using pipelines and custom code, we incorporated dropbox, docusign, wit.ai, and zoom to revolutionize a process which normally entails many in person meetings, and signing of physical paper. How I built it We split the work by functional areas such as integration, workflow, UI/UX, and reporting. Challenges I ran into More of a self-imposed challenge: We wish we would have started at the beginning of the hackathon vs 2 weeks ago -- really could have added some amazing additional features.
