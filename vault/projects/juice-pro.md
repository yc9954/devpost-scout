# Juice Pro

> A casual tap-based game where the player presses the screen to fill a juice glass and must stop at the exact percentage requested. Precision and timing are the key to making the perfect juice.

## Body

Inspiration Inspired by satisfying precision games and juice-making experiences. What it does Players tap and hold to fill a juice glass and release at the exact required percentage. How we built it We implemented tap controls, a fill-percentage system, and smooth visual feedback. Challenges we ran into Balancing fill speed to make accuracy challenging but enjoyable. Accomplishments that we're proud of Creating a clean, satisfying mechanic with clear progress indication. What we learned Simple input can create engaging skill-based gameplay.
