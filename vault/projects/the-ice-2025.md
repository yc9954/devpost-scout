## Body

Inspiration The Ice (2025) was born from a single image — a world frozen in silence, where love becomes the last source of warmth. Inspired by the question “What remains human when time stops?”, the story explores memory, sacrifice, and the fragile fire of hope in a dying world. What it does The project visualizes a frozen post-apocalyptic world where time has stopped and humanity has turned to ice. Through generative AI video, it tells the story of a father who risks everything to protect the memory of his daughter — a poetic narrative about love enduring beyond extinction. How we built it We used AI models such as Midjourney, Kling, and Veo 3 to recreate the familiar language of 2D animation through generative video. Rather than pursuing realism, our goal was to evoke the warmth, texture, and rhythm of hand-drawn animation within an AI-driven cinematic form. Each frame was refined to preserve the imperfections and emotional pulse that define traditional animation. Challenges we ran into Capturing emotion in an artificial world was the hardest part. Ice, silence, and stillness easily feel lifeless — we had to design subtle movement, breath, and light that make the cold feel human. Balancing technical realism with poetic storytelling was a constant challenge. Accomplishments that we're proud of We created a narrative that feels both intimate and epic — a small human story against the scale of extinction. The combination of AI-generated visuals and handcrafted storytelling achieved a cinematic quality that bridges technology and emotion. What we learned We learned that generative AI is not just a tool for speed — it’s a language for emotion. When guided by strong direction and storytelling, AI can express silence, grief, and love with haunting beauty. What's next for The Ice (2025) While The Ice (2025) remains a short animated film, it marks the foundation for our next chapter — the production of a full-length animated feature with commercial potential. Through this project, we refined our creative and technical pipeline, proving that AI-driven storytelling can achieve both artistic depth and market value. Our next step is to apply this experience to developing an original feature animation that can compete on a global stage. <div
