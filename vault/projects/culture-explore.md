app page output page Inspiration The inspiration behind Culture Explore stems from the desire to foster global understanding and appreciation by bringing cultures closer together. In a world interconnected by technology, Culture Explore aims to bridge cultural gaps, promote cultural exchange, and celebrate the richness of human diversity. It seeks to empower users to explore, learn, and connect with different cultures effortlessly. What it does Culture Explore serves as a cultural encyclopedia, allowing users to delve into the vibrant tapestry of global cultures with ease. Users can input any country and gain access to a wealth of information about its people, traditions, festivals, and architectural marvels. From exploring cultural practices to understanding the significance of festivals, Culture Explore also provides a chat app where user can ask any questions they have related to the country and its culture. <div
