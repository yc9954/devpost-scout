## Body

GIF Vision Everyone agrees that GameFi and cryptocurrencies are a natural fit, but there's a common belief that GameFi has run out of innovations. Let's take a broader view. The crypto market isn’t short of tokens—it lacks liquidity and new users. Greedy World started as a research project on the diversity of crypto ecosystem, where we wanted to redistribute tokens through a fun rule where the game is just a vehicle. Introduction Ecotool, 3rd Generation GameFi, Fighting More Earn, Tokens Battle Royale, you are both the hunter and the hunted. No Pledge, No Mining, No Ponzi. Greedy World is rooted in the ecosystem, we serve the ecosystem, and we contribute liquidity and new users to the ecosystem. Features Token Diversity Interesting asset liquidity Exploring Human Nature Ecological Value During the game, you might unexpectedly gather tokens you've never invested in before, thereby transforming you into a token holder. When Greedy World supports over 100 token assets, you'll discover that it transcends being just a game. It gradually transforms into a multi-token platform, or rather, a tool facilitating token liquidity. Even during market downturns, it can still help tokens stay alive. When it is integrated into the ecosystem, it has the potential to unleash tremendous commercial value. Business model 4% inheritance tax If you are killed in the game, the tokens you were carrying in combat may be picked up by other players and the inheritor will be subject to inheritance tax. Why do We believe that the Web3 world isn't limited to the mine-withdraw-sell model, we are looking for a healthier, more compliant and diverse development path. Greedy World returns to the most primitive game rules, assisting Web2 users in a smoother transition into the Web3 world, thereby benefiting the entire crypto sphere. Let's collectively inspire a brand new track. How to play In the game, you will control a snake and collect treasures in a mysterious world. Arrow keys - Control the direction of the snake's movement. Spacebar - Accelerates movement. Blue halos - to increase the length of your body. Yellow halos - to speed up the speed of movement.
