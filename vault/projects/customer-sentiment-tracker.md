## Body

Generate trends across reviews Auto tag your customer reviews So you sell a product? Physical, digital - it doesn't matter what type of product you sell, listening to your customers can either make or break your product. This template does three amazing things: Provides an organized and centralized place to capture and track customer reviews Generates trends and helps spot patterns across the reviews Generates suggestions for improvements based on the customer reviews Auto-assigns customer sentiment to the review itself for easy filtering This template will help take your product to the next level.
