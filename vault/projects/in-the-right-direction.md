# The Right Direction

> The music video is about escaping your current reality to a new and better one

[Devpost](https://devpost.com/software/in-the-right-direction) · hackathon [[Chroma Awards- AI Film- Music Videos- and Games]]

## Facets


**stack** adobe, capcut, gemni, kling, suno, veo

## Body

I started this project with the song itself. I was feeling really overwhelmed, so I began by prompting themes around breathing, slowing down, and taking things one step at a time. I wanted to make a house track, something you could move to, and after a lot of iterating, I landed on a sound I loved. From there, I moved on to the music video concept. I knew I wanted it to feel like an escape from reality, and I’ve always been obsessed with the visual aesthetic of forest creatures dancing, so that became the heart of it. The video opens in a cramped, run-down apartment where everything feels awful, much like the times. Also, my personally wanted to escape from reality, since I’d just spent weeks recovering from pneumonia.
