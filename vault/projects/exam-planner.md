## Body

Inspiration During exam time I tend to feel a little helpless, so I made Exam Planner to help students like me feel a little less nervous for exams. It is a tool that puts all of your exam needs in one place with I would benefit from as my biggest worry is that I don't know everything about my test. What it does It shows students where and when their exams take place. It can also keep students in contact with their teachers for study tips and tricks as they prepare for their exams. How we built it I used Google Sheets and Sites to bring life to Exam Planner. I used sites as a base to show what my idea would look like when fully completed. Challenges we ran into I struggled to get an idea for a long time at first, though looking at past proejcts andthink about, "What would I like to make school better", help the process and eventually I came up with Exam Planner. Accomplishments that we're proud of I'm proud of finding a way to streamline all of students' exam needs into one simple place. What we learned I learned that there are actually a lot of variables when it comes to school exams and that it is hard to manage them all in one's mind, so I hope my product can help students with that stress.
