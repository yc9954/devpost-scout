The use of smart contracts, a dispute system, money lock feature, and on-chain snapshots all contribute to an environment where both parties can feel protected and assured in their transactions. WorkAgora places a strong emphasis on identity verification using Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures. This precautionary measure helps mitigate potential issues concerning the identities of freelancers and companies, further adding to the platform's security. AnyAPI from Chainlink is employed to determine the average price of assignments, thus ensuring fair and transparent pricing in the marketplace. Key Features of WorkAgora Secure and Transparent Contracts : The platform utilizes blockchain technology to facilitate secure and transparent transactions. Each contract is executed as a smart contract, thereby reducing the risk of disputes and ensuring the reliability of terms agreed upon. Simplified Job Search and Talent Acquisition : WorkAgora makes the process of job hunting and talent acquisition easy and efficient. Freelancers can find a myriad of opportunities while companies can tap into a pool of diverse global talents. Furthermore, the integration of OpenAI technologies accelerates the search process, significantly enhancing matchmaking accuracy and speed. Whether you're a freelancer hunting for the perfect job or a company seeking the right talent, WorkAgora optimizes your search process and ensures you find your best match. KYC Verification : To safeguard the platform and its users, a Know Your Customer (KYC) process is implemented. This strengthens the trust between freelancers and companies by ensuring verified identities. Dispute System : In case of conflicts, WorkAgora has a built-in dispute system that allows for fair resolution. This system serves as an intermediary, ensuring both parties' concerns are addressed effectively. Cryptocurrency Payments : WorkAgora embraces the future of payments by facilitating transactions via cryptocurrency. The use of Oracles and AnyAPI from Chainlink provides a fair and accurate determination of the average price of tasks, ensuring a transparent and balanced payment system. On-chain Reputation : The platform holds a transparent and verifiable on-chain reputation system. This makes the reputation a credible asset for every participant, thus promoting honest and professional conduct within the platform. In a nutshell, WorkAgora is a holistic solution that tackles the hurdles of the traditional freelance market, transforming it into a secure, transparent, and efficient space for freelancers and companies alike. Better payment system Advanced Contract Payment System : WorkAgora has developed an innovative contract payment system to foster trust and ensure financial security for all parties involved: Payment Locking : The platform enables a certain percentage of the payment to be locked when the contract is initiated, providing a security measure for freelancers and assurance of work completion for companies. Initial Deposit : Companies are required to make an initial deposit, defined as a percentage of the total contract value. This acts as a demonstration of commitment and ensures freelancers that they are working with a serious client. Deferred Payment : The system supports deferred payments, with the ability to set milestones and release payments based on their completion. This feature requires a snapshot mechanism for accurate tracking and verification of completed milestones. Multi-Step Payment : WorkAgora's platform allows the breaking down of larger tasks into several milestones, each associated with a specific portion of the total payment. As each milestone is achieved, the corresponding payment is released. This ensures that freelancers are compensated throughout the duration of the project, and companies can monitor progress closely. This comprehensive contract payment system ensures a fair and secure method of transaction, greatly reducing potential payment conflicts and fostering a healthy working environment for both freelancers and companies. How we built it WorkAgora is the product of several innovative technologies coming together to create a platform that's secure, efficient, and user-friendly. For the backbone of our platform, we chose the Avalanche (AVAX) blockchain for its robust decentralization capabilities. It helps us ensure security, reliability, and speed, making our platform more resilient and trustworthy. To handle cryptocurrency prices and job price suggestions, we integrated Chainlink's suite of powerful decentralized services. We used Chainlink Price Feeds for accurate, up-to-date crypto prices and Chainlink's AnyAPI for job price suggestions. This combination allows us to maintain transparency in pricing and ensure fair remuneration for freelancers' work. On the server-side, we adopted the NestJS framework and combined it with AWS Lambda for serverless computing. This combination enables us to handle high loads efficiently and make our backend more scalable and cost-effective. For frontend development, we leveraged the power of NextJS combined with the Chakra UI framework. This combination allows us to create a highly responsive and intuitive user interface, delivering a seamless and enjoyable UX for our users. In the quest to make the job and talent search process more efficient, we incorporated OpenAI technology. OpenAI allows us to offer smart and fast search capabilities, significantly enhancing the matchmaking accuracy and speed on our platform. Finally, to ensure the authenticity of identities on our platform, we partnered with Synaps , a leading KYC provider . Synaps helps us prevent potential identity-related issues and further enhance the security and trustworthiness of WorkAgora. Together, these technologies empower us to deliver a superior platform for freelancers and companies, revolutionizing the way they interact and conduct business.
