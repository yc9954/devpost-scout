## Body

Convex database usage For profile generation DirectReach AI Inspiration Posted a LinkedIn update asking tech students about the problems they face in the hiring and internship process. Received an overwhelming response with 200k+ impressions and 2000+ engagements . Over 500 Google Form responses asking for an application to find email IDs of employers and send emails directly. The LinkedIn post generated 150,000+ impressions and 1500+ engagements in just 15 hours, prompting me to address this need. What it does Personalized Cold Outreach : Finds the email ID of any employer based on their profile. Uses AI to craft personalized emails and referral messages tailored to the candidate's profile. Sends messages directly via email and LinkedIn. Company Database : Builds a database of top, fast-recruiting companies. Includes email IDs and LinkedIn profiles of key hiring managers. How we built it Scraped data using tools like Hunter.io . Used Convex for backend and authentication. Built the production-level application with Next.js and TypeScript . Challenges we ran into Creating a comprehensive database of fast-recruiting companies. Need advice on how to automate this process more effectively. Accomplishments that we're proud of 500k+ impressions on an Instagram reel in just 2 days: Instagram Reel . 200k+ impressions and 3000+ engagements on LinkedIn posts in just 20 hours: LinkedIn Post 1 LinkedIn Post 2 500+ Google Form responses from students interested in the service. What we learned Convex is a highly effective tool for backend and database services. Gained valuable insights on building AI applications for practical, real-world uses.
