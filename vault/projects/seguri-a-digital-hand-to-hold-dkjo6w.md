Country Selector Signup Page Settings Chat FAQs Chat Preview Welcome Screen Tools Manual Scan Assurance Dialogue First Aid Explanation History News Page Home Page Seguri: A Digital Hand to Hold The Silence After the Scam It started on a Tuesday afternoon with a simple text message about a suspended pension. In a moment of panic, our team member’s grandmother, Lola Benita, sent her personal details to a stranger. But the true tragedy was not the financial loss. It was her silence. Consumed by shame, she felt the world was moving forward while she was left standing still. We realized that for the elderly and the vulnerable in developing nations, the internet is not a tool. It is a minefield. While the tech industry races to build tools for the savvy and the connected, the "digitally invisible" are left defenseless against scammers who prey on their fear. What We Built We built Seguri . It is a Hybrid-AI Security Suite designed to be a digital hand to hold. Seguri is not just an antivirus. It is a panic-proof digital companion that works completely offline , serving as a 24/7 bodyguard for those whom technology has forgotten. Seguri provides a 6-point defense system that protects users without invading their privacy: Stops the Threat (Active Guard): Runs silently in the background, instantly intercepting threats from SMS and chat apps (Messenger, WhatsApp) without requiring user intervention. Checks the Unknown (Manual Scanner): A "RAM-Only" tool where users can paste suspicious text. It analyzes content in volatile memory and instantly wipes it, ensuring 100% privacy. Calms the Panic (First Aid Kit): If a user clicks a bad link, this high-contrast interface activates to guide them through emergency steps (like cutting connectivity) using simple, non-technical language. Records the Proof (Secure Evidence Log): Automatically saves a local, read-only history of blocked threats. This allows users to show evidence to family members or authorities without the data ever leaving the device. Educates (Hybrid Chatbot): Answers safety questions instantly, toggling between hardcoded offline logic and Gemini AI depending on internet availability. Anticipates (Cybersecurity News Feed): Uses Google Gemini to fetch real-time updates on local scam trends based on the user's selected country (e.g., "Sim Registration Scams"), keeping users one step ahead of new tactics. The Technical Edge Unlike standard antivirus apps, Seguri utilizes a Dual-Layer Architecture . We trained a Global Layer on universal threats (like tax fraud) and a Local Layer specifically on "Taglish" datasets to catch dialect-based scams. This reduces detection latency to milliseconds while consuming zero mobile data for core protection. The Problem It Solves & Who It Helps We are addressing a massive gap in cybersecurity: The Protection of the Underrepresented. Most AI security models are trained on Western data and English syntax. They fail to detect the nuanced, localized threats affecting the Global South. A scam in the Philippines often uses "Taglish," which is a mix of Tagalog and English, or specific cultural pressure points that standard global antiviruses miss. Seguri helps the elderly, the non-tech-savvy, and low-income users who rely on budget devices with limited data plans. By optimizing our Neural Networks to run directly on the phone with zero internet latency , we ensure that security is a human right and not a subscription privilege. The Mathematics of Empathy To solve a human problem, we had to engineer a solution rooted in empathy. Our biggest challenge was the "False Positive Paradox." Early in development, our AI was so paranoid that it flagged a text from a grandchild saying "I'm coming home, please cook dinner" as a scam because it detected urgency. We had to teach the machine what safety looks like. We retrained our models using a "Safety Net" of heuristics and safe messages that look like system alerts. We ensured that connection is never mistaken for corruption. What's Next We are currently scaling our Regional Customization engine to support languages beyond English and Tagalog. We are prototyping Voice-First interaction powered by Gemini, allowing the blind and illiterate to simply ask, "Is this safe?" Why This Matters We are not just protecting devices. We are protecting dignity. We are ensuring that a grandmother does not have to fear her own phone. By bridging the gap between advanced AI and the people who need it most, Seguri ensures that as the world rushes toward the future, we do not leave the people who built our past behind.
