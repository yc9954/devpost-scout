## Body

helping people to focus, find and learn Inspiration We all are usually procrastinating every now and then. Especially my college friends who were in hostel. We always know what to do but our focus is lost somewhere and we gets distracted. We realized this and found that major cause of distractions were unavailability of resources and over-use of social media/online games, etc. We decided to make a solution for the lost focus and how people can tackle it. What it does Our website serves as a platform where you can find YOUR resources and no distractions. -You wake up daily and write your tasks of the day. That's it. Then it is our work to find the best possible resources be it videos, blogs, images, etc. -We also remind people to work on their task so that they don't procrastinate. How we made it We implemented Natural Language Processing(NLP) powered by the deep learning to look for the most appropriate resources for the user. We used HTML CSS for the front end to give user a wonderful experience Django is used for the backend which made it very convenient to integrate the deep learning models as both are powered by Python mySQL is the database we're using Computer Vision is also used in one on our module which alarms people whenever they are dowsy. <div
