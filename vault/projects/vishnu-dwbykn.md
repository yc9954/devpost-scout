## Body

Home_page Inspiration Vishnu was inspired by the increasing demand for accessible mental health support. Many people face emotional struggles but do not have immediate access to professional resources. We aimed to create a compassionate AI assistant that could offer users a safe space to express themselves and receive guidance. What it does Vishnu is an AI-powered chatbot designed to support mental well-being. It provides empathetic conversations, offers emotional support, and suggests helpful resources and coping mechanisms based on user input. It aims to reduce feelings of isolation and provide users with a sense of relief during tough moments. How we built it I built Vishnu using Python and incorporated NLP models for understanding and analyzing user input. The chatbot is integrated with sentiment analysis tools to detect the emotional state of users and tailor responses accordingly. I used Streamlit for easy interaction, allowing users to have seamless conversations with Vishnu. Challenges we ran into I faced challenges in fine-tuning the emotional intelligence of Vishnu. Ensuring that the chatbot responds empathetically and accurately across different emotional contexts was difficult. Additionally, maintaining user engagement and providing relevant advice in diverse situations required continuous model optimization. Accomplishments that we're proud of I'm proud of creating a chatbot that can effectively identify and respond to a wide range of emotional cues. Vishnu’s ability to provide users with timely and supportive guidance is something I’m particularly proud of, as it has the potential to positively impact users' mental health. What we learned I learned how crucial it is to design AI systems that not only respond correctly but do so in an emotionally aware and sensitive manner. The importance of continuously refining AI models for better understanding of human emotions became evident throughout the development process. What's next for Vishnu Moving forward, I plan to enhance Vishnu by adding more advanced features like personalized mental health tips, integration with mental health professionals for live consultations, and expanding its knowledge base to cover more diverse emotional struggles. I aim to make Vishnu even more supportive and scalable for a broader audience. <div
