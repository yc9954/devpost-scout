## Body

Inspiration As I use Discord alot, I wanted to create a bot that I can control myself, add the commands I need to it and format everything about it as I want, I like the idea of being in control of something I use daily. What it does Burger (The bot name) can -play you music in discord when requested, There's commands and buttons to basic music things like -stop , -pause/resume , -volume up/down , -Queue where it shows songs that are currently in queue, -loop to play queued songs over and over again, -skip , -skipTo that skips to a specific song in queue, -seek , -clear that clears the queue, -remove to remove a queued song, -shuffle to shuffle the queued songs, -lyrics to show the lyrics of a song. It has a lo-fi radio that plays you Lo-Fi music also. With Burger, you can register in Burger's Casino and start playing casino games using Burger coins! Games like -bj to play blackjack, -hr which is a game for horse race betting, -crash , -dice , -trivia to get asked trivia questions, earn 1000 coins every 1 hour by -wage command. -leaderboard to view the top 10 richest people registered in burgers Casino! In addition, it has a -meme , -serverinfo command. Dont forget -burger command to get a free burger! You can see the whole commands by sending -help . How I built it Using Java, Java Discord API, Maven, Postgresql for databases, heroku to host it 24/7. Challenges I ran into While coding the casino games, Ran into challenges coding the games but I dont know the word impossible! Accomplishments that I’m proud of Proud that I could code it solo from A to Z, It is now in 45 servers too!
