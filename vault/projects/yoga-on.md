Initial Style for Home Page Prototyping for Logic Prototyping for Logic Finished Screen after Development Finished Screen after Development Completion Screen Pop-up Inspiration The inspiration behind Yoga-ON stems from a desire to blend modern technology with the ancient practice of yoga which I became familiar with due to my family, making it more accessible and interactive for users of all levels and ages. What it does Yoga-ON is a revolutionary yoga app designed to guide users through their yoga practice with ease and precision. Utilizing the power of pose detection and real-time feedback, users can refine their postures while following along with reference videos. The app offers a seamless experience, from selecting yoga levels to practicing poses, all within the comfort of your own space. How I built it Yoga-ON is built using Python and the KivyMD framework, leveraging its capabilities to create a user-friendly interface with multiple screens. Pose detection technology is integrated to provide invaluable feedback on posture alignment, enhancing the user's yoga journey. The app also incorporates a countdown timer and interactive buttons for smooth navigation, ensuring a holistic experience. Challenges I ran into Developing Yoga-ON presented several challenges, particularly in integrating pose detection seamlessly into the app. Fine-tuning the algorithm to accurately analyze and provide feedback on various yoga poses required extensive testing and iteration. Additionally, ensuring compatibility across different devices and optimizing performance were ongoing challenges throughout the development process. Accomplishments that I'm proud of I'm proud to have created a comprehensive yoga app that caters to users of all levels, offering a blend of traditional yoga practice with modern technology. Overcoming the technical hurdles and achieving a seamless user experience is a significant accomplishment. Moreover, seeing users benefit from improved posture and enhanced yoga practice through Yoga-ON brings immense satisfaction. What I learned Developing Yoga-ON has been a tremendous learning experience, providing insights into Python programming, UI/UX design, and integrating machine learning technologies into applications. Additionally, I gained valuable knowledge in leveraging frameworks like KivyMD to build robust and user-friendly applications. Continuous learning and adaptation were key in overcoming challenges and refining the app. What's next for Yoga-ON The journey for Yoga-ON doesn't end here. Moving forward, I envision expanding the app's features to include personalized yoga routines, progress tracking, and community engagement. Incorporating advanced AI algorithms for enhanced pose detection and customized feedback is also on the horizon.
