## Body

Restore an old photo, then get a receipt for every color: what is real, what is enhanced, and what the AI made up. Pick a famous archival photo or upload your own, cached samples restore instantly, live runs show the whole pipeline working. The restored Einstein, next to a plain-language verdict: 100% structure kept, 15% color inferred, 0% fabricated, all hash-sealed. Faces are what people trust most and AI fakes most, so we never rebuild them, we detect each one and show how sure the color is. Two independent AIs colorized the same photo. Where they agree the color is grounded, where they split it is an honest guess. Every restoration lives as a durable record on Backblaze B2: locked master, restoration, authenticity map, manifest, and C2PA credential. Same honesty for sound. A noisy recording cleaned by subtractive gating that can only remove hiss, never invent audio, 0% fabricated. Drop any image and Trueprint hashes it against the B2 record, proving whether it is a genuine unaltered Trueprint output. AI restoration looks gorgeous but quietly guesses the past. Colorizing, upscaling, and consumer apps all invent detail with no paper trail. One orchestrated run, many specialist models: analyze, repair, detect faces, colorize twice, upscale, and clean audio, each step recorded. Provenance is recorded while we generate, not guessed afterward, so every operation is honestly graded original, enhanced, or fabricated. Every restoration writes a hash-verified manifest stored on Backblaze B2 next to a locked master, so the record cannot be quietly rewritten. Built for archives, families, journalists, and courts, on stack where Backblaze B2 is the system of record and Genblaze orchestrates it all. Plain-language explainers for every idea behind Trueprint, from provenance and manifests to C2PA and the EU AI Act. See exactly what the AI invented. Restore across multiple models and keep a verifiable record of every choice, stored on Backblaze B2. Inspiration Everyone has an old photo they wish they could bring back to life, and AI can do that now in seconds. But when an AI colorizes a 1920s photograph, it is not recovering the real colors. It is making a beautiful, confident guess. Once that version spreads, no one can tell which parts were real and which the AI invented, and the historical record quietly gets corrupted. This is not a hypothetical worry. In 2026 the Library of Congress put out a call for tools that keep AI-affected collections authentic and verifiable. The EU AI Act now requires AI-edited media to carry machine-readable disclosure (Article 50). And the Ansel Adams Trust publicly condemned an undisclosed AI-colorized photo. Archives, museums, journalists, and families all want to restore old media, but they cannot cite what they cannot verify. We built Trueprint for exactly that moment: restoration you can actually trust. What it does Trueprint restores old photos and audio like the best AI tools, and then proves exactly what is real and what the AI invented. Restore. It repairs damage and colorizes old black-and-white photos through a Genblaze pipeline. Never fabricate the photo itself. Colorization is luminance-locked: we keep the original's structure and only borrow the AI's color, so the shapes and detail stay the real photo. Structure is reported as preserved, and color is honestly reported as fully AI-inferred. Show where to trust it. We colorize each photo with two independent AI providers and compare them. Where they agree, the color is grounded. Where they disagree, the AI is guessing, and we show it as a confidence heatmap. What the two AIs disagreed on. A side-by-side view shows both providers' raw colorizations and names the exact contested colors (for example, "necktie: black vs navy") versus the ones they agreed on. This is the clearest way we have seen to draw the line between fact and fabrication. Super-resolution, measured. We recover sharpness and then measure the invented high-frequency detail against an honest upscale, so added detail is marked, not hidden. Face-aware, not face-rebuilding. We detect faces (the region people trust most) and report how confident the AI's color is inside each one. We never rebuild faces, because that would invent identity. Audio too. A noisy archival clip is cleaned with subtractive spectral gating, which can only remove noise and never invent sound, so it is zero percent fabricated by construction, with a real measured signal-to-noise gain and before and after spectrograms. Prove it. Every restoration is signed with a real, embedded C2PA Content Credential (readable in Adobe Content Credentials and any C2PA viewer), declared as the EU AI Act Article 50 machine-readable AI marking, plus a hash-verified provenance manifest stored on Backblaze B2 next to an immutable master. Verify anywhere. A Verify page checks any file two ways: it reads the embedded C2PA credential (trust travels with the file, even off-platform) and re-hashes it against the B2 record to catch tampering. A public certificate. Every restoration gets a shareable certificate page (authenticity map, plain-language disclosure, all three hashes, models used, and a verified-on-B2 badge) that an archive could publish right beside a photo. Honest by design. If an image model declines a photo by content policy, that refusal is recorded in the provenance rather than hidden. How we built it Backend: a FastAPI service with live progress over server-sent events, serving a vanilla JavaScript app plus the landing page from one container. Orchestration: the Genblaze SDK runs every restoration as a pipeline. We wrote two custom Genblaze providers so both colorizers (Google Gemini and OpenAI gpt-image) run through Genblaze's pipeline, manifest, and lineage machinery. Genblaze fallback models handle provider outages, and an LLM-as-judge check scores each result for implausible fabrication. Authenticity engine: OpenCV and NumPy. We recombine the original's luminance with the AI's color in LAB space, measure per-pixel disagreement across the two colorizations to build the confidence map, and classify each region on two honest axes (structure versus color). Super-resolution uses a high-pass difference against an honest upscale, face detection uses YuNet, and audio denoise uses a scipy spectral gate. Storage: Backblaze B2 (S3-compatible) is the system of record. Immutable masters use Object Lock, and derivatives, authenticity and confidence maps, super-resolution and face maps, audio and spectrograms, and a JSON manifest per run all live together in a queryable layout. Genblaze's own hash-verified run manifests are folded into our manifest. Provenance: C2PA via the c2pa-python library, signed with a self-provisioned dev certificate. Deploy: a single Docker container on Railway, with a test suite that runs with no credentials needed. Challenges we ran into Every image model looked dead. Genblaze's GMI Cloud chat and image APIs use different base URLs but shared one environment variable, so pointing it at the chat URL made every image model return 404 and look like an account problem. We root-caused it and filed detailed SDK feedback. Truncated vision output. The vision model is a reasoning model that spends hundreds of tokens thinking, so our JSON kept truncating before the color fields. We raised the token budget and made the parser robust to markdown fences. Provider refusals. gpt-image declines photos with children by policy. Instead of hiding it, we turned it into a feature: refusals are recorded as provenance and the pipeline degrades gracefully. Making provenance real. Our Genblaze manifest capture was returning null because we read the wrong fields. We fixed it to record the real run id, canonical hash, and lineage, and now every result shows genuine Genblaze provenance. Provider flakiness and credits. Image models occasionally return no image, so we added retries plus fallback and precached showcase samples so the demo never depends on live provider luck. Accomplishments that we're proud of The confidence heatmap and the two-AI disagreement view are things no consumer restoration tool shows. "Structure preserved, color fully inferred, zero percent fabricated" is not marketing, it is measured. Our LLM-as-judge caught a real ghosting artifact in our own pipeline during development and drove the fix. And the whole product is honest end to end: figures are measured from live B2-backed runs, refusals are disclosed, and the one stylized mockup on the landing page is clearly labeled as illustrative. It is live, tested, and provable. What we learned Honesty can be a feature, not a limitation. The most compelling moment in the whole product is two AIs openly disagreeing, because it makes uncertainty visible instead of hiding it. We also learned how deep Genblaze can go (multi-provider orchestration, fallback, evaluation, and provenance manifests as first-class outputs) and how naturally Backblaze B2 becomes a real system of record when you treat masters as immutable and store the full lineage next to them. What's next for Trueprint A CA-issued certificate on the C2PA trust list, C2PA embedding for audio, ingesting real archival audio recordings, swapping in a learned super-resolution model behind the existing invented-detail measurement, IIIF and PREMIS metadata export, and a batch mode for institutional archives.
