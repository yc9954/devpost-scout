wildrun gameplay home menu level bonus gameplay Inspiration : Wildrun pays homage to early video games, with its pixel art worlds and captivating gameplay. It draws inspiration from classic adventures like Super Mario and the dinosaur game. With Wildrun, we aim to recreate that magic while also raising awareness about the importance of environmental preservation. The game is a fusion of love for past entertainment and hope for a cleaner future. Gameplay : Wildrun's gameplay is designed to be both intuitive and engaging, turning every interaction into a beneficial action for our planet. Players embark on an endless adventure, where saving animals, collecting fruits, and gathering trash become symbolic gestures. Completing each mission makes environmental protection more enjoyable. Each rescued animal enriches the gameplay experience with special powers, while also reminding players of the magnificent diversity of wildlife. Development: The development of Wildrun has been a technological odyssey, exploring the capabilities offered by Flutter and Flame. The technology of these tools has allowed us to create a visually appealing and technically sophisticated gaming experience. Every sprite and every musical note has been carefully crafted to harmonize with the rhythm and style of our game, providing a consistent and immersive experience. Challenges Faced : The design of Wildrun was a series of challenges, especially in learning and mastering Flutter and Flame. Each obstacle was an opportunity to learn and strengthen teamwork, while also enhancing our technical skills. Accomplishments : Every success in the development of Wildrun has been a victory. It was a path traveled further to achieve our goal: raising awareness of the importance of the environment. Animating each sprite, writing each line of code, and composing each melody was evidence of our creativity and commitment. Learning : The Wildrun project has been a journey of continuous learning, enriching our collective expertise in game development. Each implemented feature and every bug fixed has added to our skill set, an asset for future projects. What's Next : The future of Wildrun is very promising. We are already considering the introduction of new maps such as the desert and the city, as well as the development of a multiplayer mode for a more collaborative gaming experience. <div
