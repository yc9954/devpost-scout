## Body

AsGroup Global App Inspiration Till today, it seem as a dilemma for Tanzania county to archive SDGs by 2030 based to SDG global Agenda, due to the fact that, it's accelerate still going very slow per each goal. AsGroup Global App, is an App were Tanzanian, can share their thoughts, develop challenges, campaigns, projects over SDGs targets, and connect to other social development stakeholders, civil societies, Private sectors, NGOs, Sponsors, International partners, global SDGs pioneers, also this can help the project funded from Sponsor. What it does AsGroup Global App, is there to accelerate Sustainable Development Solutions Archivement in Tanzania. How we built it It was built with flutter, Blockchain, JavaScript. Challenges we ran into Still facing with financial challenges to launch it, and build other missing parts Accomplishments that we're proud of We are proud of that, we managed to study and identity the need of Tanzanian on the acceleration of the SDGs in Tanzania, as well develop an App were they can make their project possible. What we learned We learned how we can do a better App for the SDGs Archivement in Tanzania by connecting Tanzanian projects to International sponsors.
