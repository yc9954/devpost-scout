Screenshot of our website after generating items given the user input budget $pent - The Irresponsible Spending Item Generator 🎯 About $pent is a fun, annoying web application that encourages irresponsible spending by generating random, quirky, and potentially useless items based on your budget. Perfect for those moments when you just want to make some questionable financial decisions! This project was the idea of our wonderful team member Sarah, who is arguably the final boss of consumerism (don't worry she changed). She was inspired after not knowing what to gift her friends for Christmas.
