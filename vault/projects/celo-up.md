COP26 was just blah blah blah says Greta Thunberg Humanity needs to act, we can't wait for governments CELO CAN PROMOTE THIS ACTION Celo Up is a platform that helps the user to neutralize their carbon footprint. Through the use of the application, he will play a knowledge quiz, being able to score his knowledge and learn. It also interacts with your consumption profile and the app gives you information on how your carbon footprint is going. Through a Data Lake with consumption data it mines its use of Earth resources and tokenizes carbon. We have the parameters within the self-implementing platform with the Moss MCO2 Token. You may also receive benefits from improved consumption and choices made. Smarth Contratc will formalize the transaction. Within the application there will be agreements with payments for environmental services, where he can choose to amortize his emissions. In order to scale the roadmap, we chose, in principle, the journeys that will make it easier for you to adhere and retain a faster positive result from your actions. An example is the disposal of waste, which is already carried out and monetized, but needs to be centralized and expanded by the geolocation of service disposal. Each tokenized transaction brings benefit and in case his choice is not positive he receives an alert, according to the LGPD. The shared data that will pass through the Data Lake enter anonymized and consisted via blockchain, ensuring the inviolability of the information. The tests are being done confidentially and soon the platforms will release the first results. Let's clean up the planet and start using it conscientiously and responsibly, eliminating waste and monetizing the entire resource.
