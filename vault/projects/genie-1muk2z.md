## Body

Genie: Type What You Want, Get a Live Website Here's the thing about building websites: it sucks. You know what you want. Maybe it's a landing page for your startup idea. Maybe it's a portfolio to show off your work. Maybe you just need to get something online fast to see if people actually care about your thing. But then you're six hours deep into YouTube tutorials, Stack Overflow threads, and CSS that makes absolutely no sense. Or you're paying someone $2,000 and waiting two weeks. Genie cuts through all of that. You describe what you want in normal words, and it builds you a real website. Like, actually live on the internet. Right now. How it works You type: "I need a landing page for my coffee subscription service. Dark brown colors, big hero image, three pricing tiers, email signup." Genie builds it. You see it live in about a minute. If you hate something, you just say "make the pricing section less ugly" or "add customer testimonials" and it updates. When you're happy with it, you deploy. Done. It's online. You can share the link, connect your domain, whatever. No frameworks to choose. No hosting to figure out. No "npm install" nightmares. Just prompts and websites. Why people actually use this You're not stuck waiting on yourself to learn React or waiting on a developer to have time for you. You can test ten different ideas this week instead of spending three weeks building one. The sites don't look like garbage. They're responsive, they load fast, and they don't immediately scream "I made this with a template from 2015." And if you want to take the code and mess with it yourself later, you can. It's yours. Look, most people don't want to build websites. They want to have websites. Genie just gets you. <div
