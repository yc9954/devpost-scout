AWS Scheduler API Gateway AWS Scheduler Latest Hacks Inspiration In the face of growing cybersecurity issues in the web3 industry and the inefficiencies of manual smart contract audits, we were inspired to develop a solution that could address these problems effectively. The need for a faster, cheaper, and more efficient alternative to traditional methods led us to the creation of GETSecured ContractSentry, an AI sentinel for smart contracts. What it does GETSecured OracleSentry is designed to automate the process of auditing smart contracts and assessing their risk. It continuously crawls through all mainnets, creating an indexed repository of deployed contracts and monitoring their risk scores. But it doesn't stop there. It also serves as an Oracle, providing DeFi, lending, and insurance smart contracts with our risk scores. This enables them to make informed decisions about loans, insurance premiums, and coverages. This real-time, accurate risk assessment for every smart contract on the blockchain is a game-changer, offering unprecedented security and peace of mind for blockchain users. How we built it The development of GETSecured OracleSentry was a journey of technological integration and innovation. We built it using Python, TensorFlow, FastAPI, Chainlink, Flow, and AWS services. These technologies work together seamlessly, allowing our system to crawl mainnets, audit smart contracts, assess risk, and update our indexed repository. The result is a robust and efficient system capable of providing real-time, accurate risk assessments. Challenges we ran into The road to innovation is often paved with challenges, and our journey with GETSecured OracleSentry was no different. We faced hurdles in integrating the different technologies and ensuring the accuracy and efficiency of our AI model. However, these challenges only strengthened our resolve and pushed us to develop creative solutions to overcome them. Accomplishments that we're proud of We take immense pride in GETSecured OracleSentry, not just for its technical complexity and efficiency, but also for its potential to revolutionize blockchain security. We've developed a solution that addresses a significant issue in the web3 industry and stands out for its innovation and practicality. The positive feedback and interest we've received from the blockchain community have been incredibly rewarding. What we learned The development of GETSecured OracleSentry has been a learning journey. We've gained deep insights into the complexities of smart contract auditing and the potential of AI in addressing these challenges. We've also learned about the importance of perseverance and problem-solving in overcoming obstacles and pushing the boundaries of what's possible. What's next for GETSecured OracleSentry The future is bright for GETSecured OracleSentry. We plan to continue improving and expanding our solution, with the aim of making it the gold standard for smart contract auditing and risk assessment.
