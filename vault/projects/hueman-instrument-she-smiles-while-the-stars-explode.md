## Body

About the project This project is a dark, breakcore-inspired track with airy, existential vibes and anime-style vocals, paired with a moody music video. The artist name is Hueman Instrument , and the song is “She Smiles While The Stars Explode.” The title “She Smiles While The Stars Explode” comes from this mix of idealism and quiet apocalypse that I was aiming for. I wanted the track to sit in that space where everything feels like it’s falling apart but is still strangely beautiful. It’s loosely in the emotional vein of artists like SewerSlvt , whose songs hit me really hard and inspired me to try for that same kind of intensity. In a way, this track is also a small step toward a future where AI knows my tastes better than I do and can help me create music that feels like the “ideal version” of what I’m always reaching for — even if I don’t think I’m fully there yet. I created the music using ElevenLabs’ new music tools , experimenting with prompts instead of traditional songwriting. One specific goal was to get anime-style vocalizations that felt like chopped samples, but without actual lyrics. I did this by prompting non-verbal sounds and emotional cues rather than writing out words, so the voice feels expressive but not tied to a specific language. Some of the main challenges: Getting the right blend of breakcore chaos and ambient, airy space Prompting for anime-like vocals that felt expressive but not lyrical Guiding the AI toward a very specific mood and texture on a tight timeline of just a few days Overall, this project was a focused experiment in using AI as a collaborator to chase a very particular feeling: cute on the surface, existential underneath, smiling at the end of the world. <div
