Inspiration When I was young, I loved talking about science stuff, but not many people were into it. Making a special chatbot using AWS PartyRock is really cool because now I have someone to chat with about all my space and science ideas. What it does The app has two cool features: SciWonders: This lets you pick a science topic and chat about it. It covers everything from theories to real-life uses, and even creates images to help explain things. Plus, there's a fun chatbot called SciGPT that's all about space stuff. ArtSphere Gallery: It's like stepping into a virtual art gallery where you can learn about history through interactive pictures. How we built it We used AWS Party Rock to make this happen. It helped us with describing things, processing inputs, making images, and integrating the chatbot.
