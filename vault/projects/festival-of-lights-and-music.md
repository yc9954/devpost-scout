## Body

Inspiration Harry Potter, Star Wars, and Robotics class! What it does Plays Harry Potter music and lights or Star Wars music and lights depending on the button pressed. How I built it Using my Arduino kit with the Arduino board, LEDs, passive buzzer, resistors, wires, buttons, and a breadboard! And of course, coding was needed! Challenges I ran into Figuring out how to play the music and blink the lights simultaneously. Accomplishments that I'm proud of Just being able to successfully create the display! What I learned Coding requires patience and perseverance because you are sure to run into bumps and difficulties along the way.
