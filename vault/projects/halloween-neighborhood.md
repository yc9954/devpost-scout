## Body

Inspiration Whilst Halloween night was bustling, I didn't have any friends or a partner to hang out with or spend time with. So, I wanted to create a rock song that would blow away that boredom, and that's how this piece came about. What it does I wish to mock those who, simply because it's Halloween, let themselves go and revel in excess. How we built it The music was composed first, with visuals and story conceived to complement it. It's a bizarre monster-themed rock band comprising Mira (the white-haired beauty), Rocky (the skeleton), Kenta (the jack-o'-lantern), and Vamos (Dracula). Challenges we ran into The most difficult part was maintaining the consistency of the four characters while arranging them in a band formation or lining them up. Accomplishments that we're proud of The fact that we successfully captured each character's individuality. What we learned I discovered that by skilfully utilising nano banana, it is possible to generate images of all four characters together across various scenes while maintaining consistency in their attire.
