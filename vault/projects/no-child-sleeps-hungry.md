## Body

You can wipe tears of a kid or even help ending world hunger Millions don't get food daily While many have excessive food Know how much you can save Don't be a victim of myths And join community to solve problem There are people working for this, let's join them Solution exists, you have to take action! ✨ Inspiration Every night, millions of children go to bed with an empty stomach, while billions of meals are wasted. This painful reality fuels our mission — no child should sleep hungry when solutions exist. 🌍 What it does Our platform maps hunger hotspots, exposes food surplus patterns, and connects communities to life-saving solutions. It empowers citizens, NGOs, and policymakers to act — fast. 🛠️ How we built it We combined open-source hunger data, AI-powered maps, and interactive visualizations to tell the human story behind the statistics. Built with Next.js, Tailwind, and Framer Motion for an engaging, mobile-first experience. ⚠️ Challenges we ran into Data gaps, mapping inconsistencies, and presenting a global crisis without overwhelming users. Turning numbers into action-ready insights wasn't easy — but essential. 🏆 Accomplishments that we're proud of We transformed cold, complex data into a tool anyone can use — from global citizens to grassroots leaders. Every map click, every shared insight brings us closer to ending child hunger. 📚 What we learned Technology is powerful, but empathy drives impact. When people see the faces behind the facts, they care — and when they care, they act. 🚀 What's next for No Child Sleeps Hungry We’re scaling this from awareness to action — real-time food rescue alerts, localized hunger dashboards, and partnerships with those on the frontlines. Hunger can end — but only if we all step up. <div
