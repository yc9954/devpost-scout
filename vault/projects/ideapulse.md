Idea Pulse Build with AI, Grow with Community, Tokenize with DAO 1. Project Overview 1.1 Project Vision and Core Values Project Vision Creating a Web3 innovation incubation platform based on pre-issuance token crowdfunding and community DAO governance, enabling AI and token holders to jointly support project idea incubation, development, and implementation, effectively realizing the "Product Hunt+DAO" model. The platform is committed to breaking the centralized monopoly of traditional VCs, exploring new paths for innovative projects through community-driven, fully transparent processes. Community Philosophy and Ecosystem Strategy We are committed to building a community where humans and AI coexist and co-create, driving more, better, and cooler ideas to materialize in Web3 through Agents and Tokens. In Idea Pulse: Co-creating an Equal Community : Anyone can participate equally and freely in project building, with trusted Agents distributing rewards based on contributions Blockchain Ecosystem Support : For projects in different blockchain ecosystems, we not only increase public chain transaction volume through project tokens and on-chain Agent interactions but also bring more innovative projects to enrich ecosystem development Customized Agent Services : Setting up different Agents for each public chain, screening ideas that better integrate with the ecosystem and providing best practice solutions based on ecosystem characteristics Work Paradigm Innovation : Creating a DAO that revolutionizes traditional work methods, allowing each participant to maximize their value in projects they are passionate about Core Project Objectives Social Creative Entry Point : Users submit ideas by @AI Agent (Farcaster/Twitter), and AI automatically generates structured proposals (including technical roadmap/budget/milestones) Dynamic Staking Crowdfunding Mechanism : Participants need to stake project tokens to join development (staking ratio correlates with task difficulty), and stakes are unlocked and rewards obtained after task completion and passing AI audit + community voting Project Token Economic Design : AI Agent reserves 15-30% of project tokens for ecosystem building (purposes decided by Agent+DAO voting) Token rights include: proposal weighted voting, task staking participation, staking for returns, governance committee seat elections Reference to a16z's VC token model: early contributor tokens linearly released (12-36 months) Pre-issuance Token Crowdfunding Mechanism : In the no-product stage, crowdfunding through AI Agent-generated creative prototypes, with funds used for: Task reward distribution (development/operations/community contributions) AI Agent and platform service fee maintenance Community councilor salary distribution (budget decided by quarterly DAO voting) AI+Community Co-governance System : Token holders and AI Agents jointly participate in voting decisions (including recruitment, task review, etc.). Anti-VC Centralization Design : Ensuring open and transparent decision-making processes through highly decentralized token governance mechanisms, avoiding traditional VC operations. SocialFi Integration : Building a vibrant and innovative community ecosystem through active interaction with users via social media channels such as Farcaster and Twitter. Core Values and Differentiation Advantages Decentralization and Transparency : Fund flows and decision-making processes are publicly recorded through smart contracts and blockchain, ensuring that every fund and decision is traceable. AI-Powered Decision Making : Using AI Agents to efficiently screen ideas and conduct preliminary proposal reviews, improving governance decision efficiency; also assisting in solving issues such as weight distribution and data preprocessing in the early stages of community governance. Multi-level Token Ecosystem : Designing governance tokens and equity tokens to meet community management and voting decisions, while providing support for future project dividends and revenue sharing. Social Fission Effect : Attracting social media traffic and user attention through the SocialFi model, achieving "wealth effect" and long-term activity after project implementation. Phased Governance System : Initial phase: AI agent + community committee dual governance Mid-term transition to reputation-weighted voting Final achievement of fully decentralized DAO 1.2 Core Problems Solved by Idea Pulse Idea Pulse is a Web3 project incubation platform combining AI assistance, community drive, and token economics, aimed at solving multiple pain points in Web3 project initiation and development. Below are the core problems it addresses: Preventing Post-Token Issuance Stagnation Problem Description: Many Web3 projects stagnate after token issuance due to lack of continuous development momentum or clear roadmaps, leading to token value decline and community confidence loss. Solutions: Dynamic Staking Crowdfunding Mechanism: Participants must stake project tokens to join development, and stakes are unlocked and rewards obtained only after task completion verified by AI audit and community voting. This mechanism incentivizes continuous development, ensuring projects don't stagnate after token issuance. Pre-issuance Token Crowdfunding: In the no-product stage, projects raise funds through AI-generated creative prototypes and pre-issued tokens, with clear fund usage (task rewards, AI maintenance, community salaries), avoiding fund misuse or project stagnation. AI+Community Co-governance System: Token holders and AI agents jointly participate in voting decisions, ensuring project direction and resource allocation align with community interests, maintaining long-term development momentum. Increasing Participation Opportunities for Interested Members Problem Description: In traditional Web3 projects, community member participation is often limited to holding tokens and voting, lacking opportunities for deep involvement in project development. Solutions: Social Creative Entry Point: Anyone can submit ideas through AI agents, with AI automatically generating structured proposals, lowering participation barriers and encouraging more people to contribute ideas. Task Staking Mechanism: Community members can participate in specific development tasks by staking tokens, with task difficulty linked to staking ratio, ensuring capable and interested members can deeply participate. Community Councilor Salary Distribution: Providing economic incentives for members actively participating in governance and construction through DAO quarterly voting on budgets, further enhancing participation. Empowering Communities Through Tokens Problem Description: In many Web3 projects, tokens serve merely as trading tools, failing to truly empower communities, resulting in insufficient member rights and sense of participation. Solutions: Multi-level Token Ecosystem: Designing governance tokens and equity tokens, with holders enjoying proposal voting, task staking, staking returns, and governance committee seat election rights, deeply binding tokens with community governance and project development. AI Agent Token Reservation: AI agents hold 15-30% of project tokens for ecosystem building, with purposes decided by Agent+DAO voting, ensuring tokens serve the long-term interests of the community and project. SocialFi Integration: Building a vibrant community through social media interaction, combining "wealth effect" and long-term activity after project implementation, enhancing the community empowerment effect of tokens. Centralization and Transparency Issues Problem Description: Traditional Web3 project governance may be overly centralized, with fund usage lacking transparency, damaging community trust. Solution: Ensuring fund allocation and governance processes are open and transparent through DAO structures and AI-assisted decision-making, enhancing decentralization. High Entry Barriers for New Projects Problem Description: Traditional projects need products or MVPs to attract investment, making it difficult for new projects to start. Solution: Allowing anyone to submit ideas through AI and fund development through the token staking system, combined with pre-issuance token crowdfunding to lower entry barriers. Insufficient Community Participation Problem Description: Community members in traditional projects have low participation and sense of belonging. Solution: Encouraging deep participation through staking and task completion mechanisms, enhancing community activity and sense of belonging. Low Project Management Efficiency Problem Description: Traditional project management relies on manual work, which is inefficient and subjective. Solution: Using AI for idea generation, proposal structuring, and task auditing, improving efficiency and objectivity. 1.3 Comparison with Different Platforms and Token Issuance Methods The design philosophy and mechanisms of Idea Pulse give it unique advantages in the Web3 project incubation field. Below is a comparison with pumpfun, daosfun, and traditional Web3 project issuance: Comparison with pumpfun pumpfun: Characteristics: A meme coin issuance platform based on the Solana blockchain, emphasizing quick launch and speculation, with projects often lacking practical utility. Limitations: Focus on short-term hype, lacking long-term development and community empowerment mechanisms. Idea Pulse: Advantages: Dedicated to incubating Web3 projects with practical utility and innovation, providing full lifecycle management from idea to launch, focusing on community drive and continuous development. Comparison with daosfun daosfun: Characteristics: Focused on DAO management for meme coin investment, still primarily speculative meme coins. Limitations: Single project type, lacking innovation incubation capability. Idea Pulse: Advantages: Covering a wider range of project types through AI assistance and community co-governance, focusing on innovation and deep community participation, not limited to speculative assets. Comparison with Traditional Web3 Project Issuance Traditional Web3 Projects: Characteristics: Typically using ICO, IDO, and other fundraising methods, requiring products or MVPs, potentially centralized governance, and low community participation. Limitations: High entry barriers, insufficient transparency, and potential project stagnation after token issuance. Idea Pulse: Advantages: Decentralized Governance: Ensuring transparent decision-making through DAO and smart contracts, enhancing community participation. Low Entry Barriers: Pre-issuance token crowdfunding supporting project initiation at the no-product stage. Efficient Management: AI assistance improving the efficiency of idea generation and task management. Continuous Momentum: Dynamic staking and task reward mechanisms preventing project stagnation. Summary Idea Pulse solves core problems in Web3 project initiation through: Preventing post-token issuance stagnation: Dynamic staking crowdfunding and pre-issuance mechanisms ensuring continuous development momentum. Enhancing community participation opportunities: Social creative entry points and task staking mechanisms encouraging deep member participation. Empowering communities: Multi-level token ecosystems binding tokens with governance and development. Enhancing transparency: DAO and AI decision-making enhancing decentralization and openness. Lowering entry barriers: Supporting project initiation at the idea stage. Improving management efficiency: AI assistance optimizing project processes. Compared to the speculative focus of pumpfun and daosfun, and the centralization and high barriers of traditional Web3 projects, Idea Pulse provides a more inclusive, efficient, and innovative incubation platform. Through the combination of AI technology and community co-governance, it is poised to become an important force driving Web3 innovation. 2.
