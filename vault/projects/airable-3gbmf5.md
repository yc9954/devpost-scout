## Body

🌱 Inspiration Air pollution silently affects millions every day — especially in cities 🌆. As athletes 🏃, families 👨‍👩‍👧, and health-conscious individuals 💚, we realized most apps show data, but don’t tell you what to do. We wanted a solution that guides people to make healthier, smarter decisions in real time — and that’s how AirAble was born. 💡🌍 💨 What it does AirAble is your smart air quality companion 🌬️: 📍 Real-time AQI & pollutant breakdown for your location 👶 Personalized health tips for sensitive groups (kids, elderly, athletes, etc.) 🛣️ Pollution-aware routing to suggest cleaner paths for walking/cycling/driving ⚠️ Alerts during high-pollution events + safe activity times 📊 Trends & tracking to understand long-term air exposure 🧩 Challenges we ran into Making complex air data easy to understand and act on Designing clean route-finding that actually works with real AQI input Balancing real-time data with performance ⚡ Crafting personalized health advice for diverse user types 🏆 Accomplishments we're proud of Built a fully functional pollution-aware route finder 🛣️✅ Created a user-friendly experience that makes air quality feel personal Achieved fast, live AQI responses with a professional UI 🧪 Received fantastic feedback from testers — they want to use it daily! 🙌 📚 What we learned Not all AQI data is equal — accuracy matters 🎯 People don’t just want info, they want actionable insight 💡 Great UI is essential when dealing with health 🧘 Personalization = retention ❤️ "✨ Unlike other apps, AirAble makes clean air awareness fun and rewarding 🎉 — with challenges 🏆, badges 🛡️, and incentives 🎁 that help you and your family 👨‍👩‍👧 stay safe from harmful pollution 🌍." 🚀 What’s next for AirAble 🌍 Expand to more countries with deeper AQI granularity 🤧 Add allergy-sensitive modes (pollen, mold alerts) 🏙️ Global air comparison to see how your city stacks up 📈 Add long-term exposure tracking & graphs 💬 Partner with health platforms & smartwatches 📱 Launch on iOS/Android with new features + gamified rewards ✨ <div
