Dental Profesional practising with DentiXR using the MX ink Drilling using MX ink 2x Zoom Drilling Caries Caries Removal Procedure MTA placemenet Inspiration Dental education still relies heavily on plastic typodont models and limited clinical exposure. Students often lack repetitive, feedback-driven practice before treating real patients. We were inspired to bridge this gap by combining immersive VR with clinically meaningful haptic feedback — allowing students to feel, see, and analyze their performance in a safe, repeatable environment. Our goal was simple: Create a scalable, data-driven dental training platform that improves psychomotor skills before students enter the clinic. What it does DentiXR is a VR-based dental training simulator designed for universities and training institutions. It allows students to: Select specific teeth and procedures Practice cavity preparation, drilling, caries removal, composite filling, and MTA placement Train freely in Practice Mode Complete instructor-defined tasks in Assignment Mode Receive instant performance feedback Track accuracy, time, and dexterity metrics View attempt history and progress trends How we built it Developed in Unity using C# Integrated VR hardware with haptic feedback Integrated MX Ink as the primary precision input device for pen-based dental interaction Built custom tooth models with layered material simulation Designed a real-time analytics engine to track: Precision Overcut areas Undercut zones Time efficiency Tool angulation consistency Implemented cloud-based dashboards for instructors Optimized for scalable university deployment Using MX Ink allows students to hold and manipulate tools in a pen-like form factor, closely mimicking real dental handpieces and improving ergonomic realism. Challenges we ran into Simulating realistic material resistance differences between enamel and dentin Balancing high-fidelity feedback with MX Ink Designing fair, objective grading algorithms Ensuring low latency for haptic feedback Making the system intuitive for both tech-savvy and non-technical faculty Accomplishments that we're proud of Built a fully functional dental VR simulator with real-time assessment Successfully integrated MX Ink for natural tool handling Achieved clinically meaningful tactile differentiation What we learned Motor skill acquisition improves dramatically with immediate feedback Objective data increases both student motivation and instructor trust Realism is not only visual — tactile and ergonomic fidelity are critical Universities need scalable solutions, not just impressive demos Iteration with real educators is essential What's next for DentiXR Dental Training Simulator AI-powered personalized feedback Expanded procedure library (endodontics, prosthodontics, surgical basics) International university partnerships Research validation studies and peer-reviewed publications Our vision is to make DentiXR the new standard for pre-clinical dental training worldwid <div
