## Body

What is Stubblex ...? Inspiration Every year, hundreds of millions of tons of agricultural waste are generated worldwide. Much of this crop residue is burned, dumped, or left to decompose inefficiently—releasing greenhouse gases, degrading air quality, and wasting valuable biomass. At the same time, industries across the globe are searching for sustainable raw materials for biofuels, packaging, composting, and circular manufacturing. The disconnect is universal: farmers lack access to fair markets for their waste, and industries lack efficient sourcing mechanisms. StubbleX was inspired by a simple global question: What if AI could transform agricultural waste into a valuable resource—benefiting farmers, industries, and the planet at the same time? What it does StubbleX is an AI-powered sustainability marketplace that connects farmers with industrial buyers to convert agricultural waste into economic and environmental value. The platform enables: AI-driven identification of agricultural waste Smart pricing recommendations for fair trade Direct farmer-to-industry transactions Environmental impact tracking to quantify sustainability benefits StubbleX promotes a circular economy model where waste becomes a resource instead of a liability. How we built it StubbleX was built as a full-stack, scalable web platform with AI integrated into the user workflow: Frontend: Next.js (React), TypeScript, Tailwind CSS Backend: FastAPI microservices with RESTful APIs AI & ML: Image classification using TensorFlow and MobileNetV2 Smart pricing logic combining rule-based heuristics with regression-style prediction System Design: Dual dashboards for farmers and buyers AI embedded directly into listing and discovery workflows Deployment Approach: Low-code AI infrastructure to automate training, inference, and scaling The architecture was designed to support global adoption across regions and crop types. ⚠️ Challenges we ran into Limited labeled datasets for diverse agricultural waste types → Designed a flexible AI pipeline that supports incremental learning and regional dataset expansion. Pricing prediction without global historical market data → Built a dynamic pricing model using waste characteristics, quantity, and regional economic factors. Designing for users with varying digital literacy levels → Simplified AI interactions into one-click workflows (upload → classify → list). Accomplishments that we’re proud of Built a functional AI-powered marketplace, not just a prototype Successfully embedded AI into real user actions rather than isolated demos Designed a solution that supports environmental, economic, and social sustainability Created a platform aligned with global circular economy principles What we learned Applying computer vision models to real-world sustainability problems Designing AI systems that are transparent, explainable, and user-friendly Understanding global biomass reuse and circular economy frameworks Balancing technical sophistication with accessibility and usability Translating environmental impact into measurable, meaningful metrics What’s next for StubbleX Expand the AI model with region-specific agricultural datasets Integrate logistics optimization and supply-chain tracking Support multiple languages for global accessibility Add carbon credit and sustainability reporting features Partner with renewable energy, recycling, and agritech organizations worldwide StubbleX aims to become a global sustainability platform that helps reduce agricultural waste, enable circular economies, and create measurable climate impact. <div
