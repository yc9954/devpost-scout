## Body

We noticed that important documents like contracts, leases, tax forms, and financial agreements are often confusing, time consuming, and risky for students, creators, and international residents. Nonresident aliens especially face barriers with mainstream tax software. We wanted a tool that makes these processes simple, secure, and fast. The Forge is an AI powered platform that instantly analyzes, creates, secures, and prepares legal and financial documents. • Upload any contract and it highlights risks, missing clauses, and suggested fixes. • Generate new contracts on the spot with security checks built in. • Upload W2s or other income documents and the system fills out all required US tax forms, including forms for nonresident aliens that other tools do not support. • Export ready to mail IRS forms or finalized agreements. The goal is to deliver instant clarity, protection, and compliance. We combined LLM based document analysis with a rules based legal and tax validation engine. • OCR pipeline for extracting clean text from PDFs and scans. • Contract analysis model trained on common legal structures and risk patterns. • Tax form filler that maps extracted income data to specific IRS schedules and nonresident forms. • Secure storage and encryption for all uploaded documents. • Frontend where users can upload files, generate new ones, and download finalized documents.
