Logo Application Github 🛡️ PatentGuard AI – AI-Powered Patent Intelligence for Inventors 💡 Inspiration Innovation often begins with a simple idea. However, transforming that idea into a patent is an expensive and uncertain process. Many inventors, students, and startups spend months developing a concept only to later discover that a similar patent already exists. Traditional patent searches require specialized legal expertise and access to complex databases. For early-stage innovators, this barrier often discourages experimentation and slows down innovation. PatentGuard AI was inspired by a simple question: What if inventors could instantly check whether their idea is novel before investing time and money in patent filing? Our goal was to build an AI-powered assistant that helps inventors evaluate the novelty of their ideas, understand the existing patent landscape, and even generate a preliminary patent draft. 🚀 What PatentGuard AI Does PatentGuard AI is an AI-driven patent intelligence platform that allows users to analyze their invention ideas in minutes. The system performs several automated steps: Searches global patents and prior art Computes semantic similarity between the invention and existing patents Generates a novelty score and risk assessment Visualizes the patent landscape Suggests improvements to increase patentability Automatically drafts a patent application outline This transforms the patent discovery process from a manual legal research task into an AI-powered workflow. 🧠 How It Works PatentGuard AI uses a multi-stage AI pipeline: User Invention Idea ↓ Patent & Prior Art Search ↓ Record Extraction & Cleaning ↓ Semantic Similarity Analysis ↓ Novelty Scoring ↓ Patent Landscape Visualization ↓ AI Improvement Suggestions ↓ AI Patent Draft Generation <div
