## Body

Inspiration Have you ever felt like if you had some system , in which you would just dump what you want to do and it will automatically understand and schedule it , without compromising on priority , remembers genuine stuff , like "a class test of additional subject after a 4 weeks" , and actually remembers after 4 weeks , and keep a hard bound slot for preparing 1-2 days before the test , compromising on other stuff like coding practicing or football practice , and also understands your long term , short term goals , and analyzes the activity which you are constantly trying to skip , without you realizing them after 4 months that this part of the goal was untouched? You might not have , but being someone who is obsessed with optimizing and productivity , I almost feel like this everytime , whenever it is a starting of a year , after mid-sem motivation , etc, and finally after experimenting with approx 100s of "productivity apps" and not getting anything "productive" , I said those 5 golden words by Linus Torvalds : "How hard could it be?" And you have (*dramatic music playing) "FlowFocus", A Multi-Agent System based scheduling solution , just dump everything , it remembers everything , it plans everything , checks every night for what you did and what you didn't , understands your "BIG 3 GOALS" , identifies what are the things you must do but are constantly slacking off on , analzyes and revise the scheldule regularly to keep all of your short term & long term goals balanced without making stupid decision ("let's do LEETCODE one hour before Math class test.....") , it understands your external constraints (class tests , project & internship deadlines , and assignment submissions ) , analzyes trends of your progress and slacking off , and with all this information overload still somehow manages to optimize your schedule.
