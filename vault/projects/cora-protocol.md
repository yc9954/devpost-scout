## Body

Cora user interface Inspiration We have seen how liquidations are the major risk for a borrower and how stressful is to maintain your position when the price of the collateral goes down. That's why we created a better way to borrow. What it does It enables borrowers to get a loan without the risk of being liquidated. How we built it We are using a decentralized risk management that uses options strategies to calculate the parameters for the lending pool. By using options strategies we can manage the risk in a very efficient manner. Challenges we ran into The subgraph is not supported yet in the Evmos ecosystem and that was very painful. Accomplishments that we're proud of We manage to deploy a protocol to the Evmos testnet that is fully functional. What we learned We learned more about the Evmos ecosystem and how to develop DApps in Evmos.
