## Body

Friend using Draw with your nose filter UPDATES: added the ability to switch between drawing with your nose and drawing with your eyes with some neat laser beams. for video demo skip the video to 0:40 seconds I created this as a fun way for people to get creative inside an already popular app -> Instagram. Tap the screen to enable/disable the brush. Open your mouth to increase brush size. Move your nose! I built it using Spark AR, published it on Instagram and people loved it.
