This improves the longevity of the equipment inside and ensure our service keeps working even in hot Texas summers! Inspiration As a kid I remember playing the backyard and digging little little holes and tunnels and noticing how just a few feet underground it is much cooler then the surface. For the project we aimed to leverage the geothermal techniques in order to cool the ONT unit. What it does Uses earths natural thermal cooling to manage the temperature of the ONT Redesign, Redesign, Redesign Through out the whole process we redesigned or changed the bases of our system several times through the hackathon every time we ran into issues. Going back to the requirements and checking if they meet them. Challenges we ran into Our largest challenge was creating a solution that doesn't create other problem. In those cases in becomes a "Whac-A-Mole" process that results in a overly complicated unreliable mess. One issues we ran into with the model was it not being to scale requiring the use of estimate values for heat dissipation calculations.
