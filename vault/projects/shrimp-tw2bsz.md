This idea aims to make more mindful consumer decisions! How I built it A super simple design concept in figma! Ideally this would be designed as a chrome extension Challenges I was unsure if i should even submit for this hackathon out of intimidation but i decided on an idea about 2 hours before submision and began to work on it! What I learned Even if you are so low on time just try stuff out anyway just because! you wont get anywhere if you dont at least try and its okay to not get where you wanted to be!
