## Body

Fault Detection and seperation We got inspired with the thought that we can make any thing and code it ourselves to solve the problems ,with the help of arduino. Our project's name is " Fault detection and Seperation" as the name tells our project detects the faulty product and seperates it from the perfeccts ones. We built it from scratch using Arduino Uno , I2C Liquid Crystal Display, Buzzer , A Servo , and a IR sensor for making the assembly of the project .We also made a seperate conveyor belt for passing the products with the help of a motor . We faced many challenges such as interfacing all the components and sensors all together with an arduino. Making of the conveyor was also one of the challanges . We learnt many things while making this project , we learnt how different components are interfaced with arduino , how to check for the faults and resolve the problem. it also helped in flourishing our arduino coding.
