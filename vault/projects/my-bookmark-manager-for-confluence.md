## Body

Inspiration App-exact team always searches for problems and always try to find solutions , we made a research and contacted some confluence users and found that an confluence documents management system is not optimal . So we wanted to create the solution. What it does My bookmark manager for confluence is a way to organize your confluence pages, it saves them as bookmarks , schedule time to read them , sort them in categories created by the user , and the latest feature is a text summarizer that uses the textrank algorithm to extract most relevant sentences from the text to help users go throw large documents . How we built it App-exact team used only the Atlassian forge framework . Challenges we ran into App-exact team faced some challenges while building , for example , Queries can only target the key field so it’s harder for us because we had to refilter the data , considering the limitations and quotas, we had to put most of the logic on the client side . Accomplishments that we're proud of We are proud that we had the chance to work on this project ,we gained a lot of experience specially being students . Also to be one of the first to develop an application that helps manage confluence documents , our approach was unique . The achievement that we are most proud of is that we published My bookmark manager for confluence on the marketplace and being a market place partner . What we learned We had a beautiful experience working on My bookmark manager for confluence . We learned how to code serverless application , work with professional consulting teams , the confluence ideology and the use of Atlassian way to manage projects. What's next for My Bookmark Manager for Confluence My bookmarks manager for confluence future is bright and full of creativity . Brain storming meetings are made frequently , ideas are sheared and developed , testing the current features to fix any issues and solve problems ,and most importantly our customer support we’re trying to improve our responds time and bug fixing , for example we are working on implementing an e-mail notifications system and to improve the text summarizer. <div
