## Body

Post-dated Crypto payments made easy! Inspiration Inspired by post-dated cheques & cheque discounting in traditional finance. What it does PDC Finance provides a decentralized platform for making post-dated cryptocurrency payments using Chainlink Automation. How we built it We built the automation module using Chainlink Automation, Smart contract events synced through Moralis to display in front-end Challenges we ran into Nothing major, thanks to helpful sponsor dev support! Accomplishments that we're proud of We are proud to make a first post-dated cryptocurrency payment dapp for Traditional finance users. What we learned We learned a lot about Smart contract design practices and smart contract integration with the Chainlink automation module. What's next for PDC Finance We will continue to build PDC Finance & we are on a Mission to bring DeFi to Corporate Finance! <div
