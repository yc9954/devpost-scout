Advance Theme based on 2021 HubSpot CMS Features 27+ Advance Custom Modules 22+ Custom Landing Pages for the website Company pages to show case services and Company information 3 Different Style Homepage Template Contact Us, Pricing and Testimonials Page Templates Services, Service Details and Case Study Page Templates Advance Custom Modules 2 Styles About Us and About Me Templates Our Team, Career List and Job Detail Page Templates Event Listing and Event Detail Page Template Hero Banner and CTA Section Modules Inspiration With HubSpot CMS expand and latest features we really got Inspired to work create theme for business so they can use it to create their business website, custom landing pages and blogs for their business. What it does TwentyOne theme is Customised HubSpot theme built for All type of businesses to create their own website without writing any coding. TwentyOne theme will allow Marketers and Website editor to easily create any type of sections, pages and landing pages for their campaign, website and blogs to give professional look for their website. How we built it Following things we have created in our HubSpot theme to give more flexible to our clients and theme buyer to create any type of content, section or pages in their website without writing any single line of the code. 27+ Fully customised Modules 22+ Custom Landing pages to start with your website 20+ System pages with customised look and design Challenges we ran into We wanted to make theme more user friendly so anyone can create their own website with their own custom fonts or colours or branding. Making theme with lots of features and flexibility to change colours, backgrounds, images, and videos were challenging part which we fixed. Accomplishments that we're proud of Making fully customised theme with having all the custom modules with flexible styles and customised options in each modules we have created. What we learned We keep updating ourself to provide best solution to HubSpot Users with HubSpot latest features.
