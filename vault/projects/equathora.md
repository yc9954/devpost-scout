# Equathora - Interactive Math & Gamified Learning Platform

> An interactive math platform combining personalized learning pathways, daily mastery quests, and targeted step-by-step guidance to master complex concepts.

## Body

This page shows some statistics on our platform The tracks page shows the paths that you have started This page shows the problem-solving interface where you solve your problems in LaTeX format (in their correct mathematical notation) This page shows the number of achievements we include to engage users This page shows the dashboard: clean and engaging. Inspiration STEM education often rewards memorization over genuine problem solving. Many students encounter mathematics and logic as a collection of formulas rather than a way of thinking. We wanted to create a platform that makes challenging concepts engaging, interactive, and rewarding while helping students develop confidence in their reasoning abilities. Equathora was inspired by the idea that learning STEM should feel like solving meaningful puzzles rather than completing repetitive worksheets. By combining gamification, mentorship, and structured progression, we aim to make advanced mathematical thinking accessible to more students. What it does Equathora is an interactive STEM learning platform focused on mathematics and logic. Students can: Solve curated problems across different topics. Track their progress through achievements and gamification. Learn through structured challenges instead of passive content. Connect with mentors for additional guidance. Build critical thinking and analytical skills through active problem solving. Our goal is to create an environment where students are motivated to explore, experiment, and improve their reasoning skills. How we built it Equathora was developed as a full-stack web platform with a strong emphasis on user experience and accessibility. The project involved: Designing an interactive frontend for problem solving. Creating a scalable backend architecture. Building a structured system for organizing mathematical and logical challenges. Developing progression and achievement mechanics to encourage long-term engagement. Integrating mentor-focused features to support collaborative learning. Throughout development, we iterated on both the educational and technical aspects of the platform based on feedback and testing. Challenges we ran into Building an educational platform presented several challenges. One challenge was balancing difficulty so that problems remain engaging without becoming discouraging. Another was designing a gamification system that motivates students while keeping the focus on genuine learning. From a technical perspective, creating a responsive and scalable platform while continuously adding new features required careful planning and iteration. Accomplishments that we're proud of We are proud of creating a platform that combines STEM education with interactive learning and gamification. Some of our accomplishments include: Building a complete educational platform from the ground up. Creating a structured ecosystem for mathematical and logical problem solving. Developing mentor and community-oriented features. Designing an engaging user experience that encourages consistent practice. Bringing together education and technology to make advanced STEM topics more approachable. What we learned Building Equathora taught us that effective STEM education goes beyond simply providing answers. We learned the importance of: Designing for engagement as well as correctness. Building systems that encourage persistence and curiosity. Iterating quickly based on user feedback. Balancing technical complexity with a simple user experience. The project also strengthened our skills in full-stack development, product design, and educational technology. What's next for Equathora Equathora is an ongoing project, and we have ambitious plans for its future. Our next steps include: Expanding the library of mathematical and logical challenges. Growing the mentor ecosystem. Adding more interactive learning experiences. Improving personalization and student progress tracking. Building a larger global community of students interested in STEM and problem solving. Our long-term vision is to make Equathora a place where students from around the world can strengthen their mathematical reasoning, develop critical thinking skills, and discover that challenging STEM problems can be both accessible and enjoyable.
