## Body

A scenic canyon view from the lobby with aurora skies and a floating balloon. Fast-paced gameplay with punches, goals, and teleporting across the arena. A campfire spot where players gather to chill before or after matches. Victory screens in KO Kickoff make every win feel legendary. 🧠 Inspiration KO Kickoff (Party Game) was inspired by three things we love: classic soccer mechanics , the chaotic physics of Rocket League , and the intensity of boxing . We asked ourselves: what if players didn’t kick the ball - but punched it? That twist led to a new kind of gameplay. Our vision was to place players in a mysterious canyon , where one day a stadium was discovered - with no explanation for how or why it exists. People began to gather , creating their own rules and organizing matches. Maybe one day, we’ll find out who built it. 🛠️ How We Built It Mikolaj led world design, UX, visuals and gameplay balance. Alessio handled scripting and game systems using Horizon’s TypeScript V2 API . We focused on responsive gameplay, clean UI, a beautiful environment, and an adaptive camera for mobile. To speed up development and iterate faster, we used AI tools like ChatGPT , DeepSeek , and Gemini for code and design support. For 3D assets and visuals, we used Blender and Photoshop . 🚧 Challenges One major challenge was making the punch mechanic feel responsive and natural , especially when syncing interactions between the glove, ball, and players. We developed a system that registers punches, avoiding collision glitches. Another difficulty was handling complex script communication - who scored, team status, goal resets - across networked clients, without sacrificing smooth performance. Working on a mobile sports game presented a challenge in developing a camera system that was both dynamic and easy to use on mobile devices . We successfully achieved this by combining various camera tracking techniques. 🏆 Accomplishments ✅ Built our first complete world in the MHW Desktop Editor 🔁 Created reusable game systems for future projects 🎨 Proud of the consistent visual style and strong aesthetics 🎥 Developed a mobile-friendly camera system that feels smooth and intuitive 🥊 Designed addictive core mechanics that make each match feel satisfying 🚀 Next Steps We plan to expand KO Kickoff with: 🏟️ Expanded lobby - more ways to train, play, and hang out between matches 🧢 Player customization - headgear, glove skins, and visual effects to stand out on the field 🎮 New gameplay modes - beyond teleports and the golden ball, more chaos, more strategy 🛠️ Smoother gameplay - better feel, better flow <div
