## Body

Inspiration Our team's mission is to build tools that help everyone act against climate change. While it's common knowledge that climate change is an urgent problem that we need to address immediately, we sought a solution that puts the problem at the forefront - starting with the habits that almost everyone participates in, online shopping. We designed the Climate Impact Bot to make it easy to figure out how much greenhouse gas you're emitting with every purchase you make. It's difficult to put the impact of every online purchase into perspective. With Climate Impact Bot, we hope to put these insights at the front-of-mind for the user so they can make more informed, considerate decisions about their purchases and the ultimate impact they have on the Earth. While it's difficult in a first world country to live 100% carbon neutral, we built Carbon Impact bot to allow users to easily calculate their carbon footprint, and take immediate action to offset it by donating to our line-up of trusted NGOs dedicated to our mission of tackling the climate change crisis.
