ActiSolveAI Landing Page ActiSolveAI Strategy engine ActiSolveAI Strategy Inspiration and Problem Statement Numerous individuals experience challenges, not due to a lack of ideas, but through a lack of structured execution. It was observed that existing AI tools provide information, but do not demonstrate how to assist the user in moving from the problem to the actionable strategy. Therefore, we built ActiSolve AI — an intelligent system capable of understanding complicated problems and supporting the user to execute effectively. What it does and How it works ActiSolve AI is an AI Strategy Engine that transforms a natural language based problem statement into a structured, prioritized and executable plan. It provides a breakdown of the challenges into tasks, an estimate of dependencies between tasks, guidance during the execution phase with adaptive recommendations, as well as a context sensitive co-pilot that allows the user to interact with their strategic plan. How we built it ActiSolve AI was developed using modern Next.js application development using a modular architecture. The reasoning/planning engine is powered by MegaLLM (deepseek-v3.1). The engine allows the decomposition of tasks, execution guidance, and conversational strategy support. The system includes multi-agent logic, dynamic dashboard, execution tracking and analytics, plus export connectors to emulate what would occur in a real world situation. Issues in the development process It was challenging to create an intuitive vs. technical experience in the architecture of the strategy engine as opposed to creating a chatbot. Factors such as AI Output Consistency from Structured output, managing execution State across Sessions, and creating flows between Strategy Development, Execution Tracking and Measurement that are seamless during User Journey presented challenges of overwhelming the User experience rather than enhancing the User experience. Elements of Success We successfully developed a fully functional (end to end) AI Strategy Lifecycle; Understand the Problem/Strategy/Execute Monitor Problems. Through the use of multi-agent reasoning capabilities within the agent you used to create strategies and through the use of predictive connectors in your action (Execution), we created an end to end experience based on real product thinking rather than traditional AI Interface (Chatbot) thinking. What We Discovered We learned that the core value users seek comes from their desire for clarity and support for execution outside of AI Responses from a Raw Data perspective. When creating an adaptive/explainable AI experience you need to develop an effective user experience model aligned with strong strategic backend reasoning. We also learned about how to effectively structure AI Outputs in order to provide reliability in the execution of AI Output as well as designing Order-Publishing Strategies that create a sense of autonomy while still allowing for behavioural Emotional control in the behaviour of the AI engine. ActiSolve AI’s Next Steps(Future Scope) Next steps for the ActiSolve AI include expanding on more Domain Specific Expert Agents, developing more complex Automations with real Integrations, developing Collaborative Planning Methodologies for Strategy and developing Predictive Scoring capabilities/metrics for Success around Executed strategies. Our long term Vision for ActiSolve AI is to become a Universal Decision Making and Execution Companion across Education Canvas, Startup/Small Business/Corporate organisations.
