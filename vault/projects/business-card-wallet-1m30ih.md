Splash screen Home screen empty wallet Add Contact Edit View List/Home Inspiration Many of the persons using the business card. Getting the business card and adding it to the contact is difficult task. This app helps in adding physical business card to contact. What it does It scans the business card and add it to the wallet. You can make calls or send email from there. Additionally you can export the contact to your phone contacts.
