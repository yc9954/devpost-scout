## Body

Inspiration In a world where self-taught skills often go unrecognized, ShowFarm emerges as a powerful bridge between personal initiative and professional credibility. This project transforms the solitary learning journey into a publicly verifiable record of achievement, leveraging the full power of the Hedera ecosystem. By seamlessly integrating five Hedera services—from minting gas-free NFT badges to creating tamper-proof records via the Consensus Service—ShowFarm doesn't just track progress; it builds trust. We were particularly inspired by its mission to empower beginners navigating the decentralized web, evidenced by an impressive early milestone of nearly 3,000 active users and a 94% satisfaction rate. With its production-grade architecture, AI-driven insights, and a clear roadmap toward global educational impact, ShowFarm stands as a testament to how blockchain technology can create tangible, career-advancing opportunities and set a new standard for lifelong learning credentials. What it does ShowFarm is a decentralized tracker platform for self-taught learning built by Hedera. Record milestones, earn NFT badges, and verify your progress publicly via HashScan. With a modern UI/UX, blockchain-backed entries, and dynamic analytics, ShowFarm transforms your journey into trusted digital credentials.
