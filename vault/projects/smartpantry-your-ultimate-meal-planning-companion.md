## Body

HomePage Leftover food log Food Storage tips page Customize recipes Try out recipes for your preferred food Login Page Inspiration: In today's world, we often overlook the value of our food, leading to unnecessary waste and unhealthy eating habits. SmartPantry addresses these issues head-on, offering a solution that encourages mindful consumption and healthier meal choices at the same time provides global food waste solution. What it does SmartPantry empowers users to discover and save nutritious recipes, manage leftovers efficiently, and learn effective food storage techniques. The platform simplifies tracking leftover meals, including details like preparation dates and consumption status, alongside providing crucial food storage advice to extend the life of ingredients and meals. How we built it Crafted with the MERN stack (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js), SmartPantry leverages MongoDB for data storage, including user profiles, saved recipes, and leftover tracking. The seamless integration of frontend and backend technologies ensures a dynamic and user-friendly experience, while the Edamam API enriches the recipe database with diverse, healthy options. Challenges we ran into We navigated through several challenges like establishing database connectivity and ensuring smooth interaction between the frontend and backend. Utilizing tools like Postman for backend testing and refining data retrieval and formatting methods were key strategies that propelled our progress. We also had issues with API communication which we solved with thorough debugging. Accomplishments that we're proud of We take pride in SmartPantry's comprehensive functionality, from saving recipes to managing leftovers, which marks a significant achievement in usability and design. The learning journey, from mastering React to integrating the MERN stack, has been profoundly rewarding, mirroring the satisfaction of engineering a fully operational creation. What we learned This project has been a rich source of knowledge, offering insights into API communication, frontend-backend integration, and the cross connections of using React within the MERN framework. The hands-on experience has significantly enhanced our understanding of web development. What's next for SmartPantry- Your Ultimate Meal Planning Companion Looking ahead, we envision using AI for personalized meal planning, hosting virtual cooking classes, and expanding community features to foster user interaction. Further enhancements like smart kitchen appliance integration and sustainability tracking promise to elevate SmartPantry's impact on promoting eco-friendly and enjoyable meal preparation. <div
