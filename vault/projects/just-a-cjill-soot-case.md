## Body

The Spark How "JUST A CHILL SOOT CASE" Was Created As a fan of absurd internet humor, I wanted to create a game that wasn’t just fun but hilarious and memorable. "JUST A CHILL SOOT CASE" was born from my love of memes and the simple joy of chaotic gameplay. Inspired by the legendary "chill guy" meme, I set out to combine humor, action, and sheer randomness into a single experience. This project isn’t just about making people laugh; it’s about showcasing how quirky ideas can be turned into something uniquely fun and creative. Powered On and Chill What Does "JUST A CHILL SOOT CASE" Do? "JUST A CHILL SOOT CASE" is an action-comedy game where players become the embodiment of internet chillness. Armed with a hilarious soundbite and unmatched swagger, players face off against waves of mischievous bunnies in a battle of memes versus madness. Let’s talk features : ⭐ Chaotic Gameplay: Players fend off endless waves of bunnies while delivering the iconic "I like ur soot case" soundbite with every hit. ⭐ Meme-Fueled Action: The entire game is designed to feel like an internet meme come to life, offering a fresh, humor-driven take on gaming. ⭐ Whimsical Atmosphere: With vibrant visuals, energetic animations, and hilarious sound effects, the game is as visually fun as it is chaotic. YouTube Tutorial, Google Search, Code, Repeat How I Built "JUST A CHILL SOOT CASE" "JUST A CHILL SOOT CASE" was developed in Unity with the programming done in C#. Our team had limited experience with Unity, so much of the development involved learning on the go. Key Technologies: Unity Engine: For creating the game mechanics, visuals, and animations. Blender: To model and animate the bunnies and create a lively game world. Unity Asset Store: For supplementary assets to save time and enhance gameplay. Custom Sound Design: The iconic soundbite "I like ur soot case" was implemented to add a humorous and memorable touch to the game. The Urge to Break Our Laptops The Challenges We Faced Creating "JUST A CHILL SOOT CASE" wasn’t without its obstacles: Learning Unity: As a beginner, understanding the ins and outs of Unity and C# was an uphill climb. Animations and Interactions: Making the bunnies required hours of tweaking and troubleshooting. Time Pressure: Balancing ambition with our time constraints was a constant challenge during development. However, through perseverance, I overcame these hurdles and brought our vision to life. The Top of the Hill What I'm Proud Of! Humor and Creativity: We created a game that’s genuinely fun to play and guaranteed to make people laugh. -Steep Learning Curve: In just a short time, I learned Unity, built functional mechanics, and created an engaging experience. Sunday Morning What I Learned Embrace the Chaos: Sometimes the quirkiest ideas make for the best projects. Have Fun: Projects are most rewarding when you genuinely enjoy the process. What’s Next for "JUST A CHILL SOOT CASE"? I'm just getting started! Our vision for "JUST A CHILL SOOT CASE" includes : New Levels and Challenges: Expanding gameplay with more enemies and unique environments. Multiplayer Mode: Introducing co-op chaos where players can team up for the ultimate meme battle. Customization: Allowing players to personalize their "chill guy" character and unlock n-ew soundbites.
