## Body

In today’s world, full of pollution-causing activities, there is an alarming situation all around. In such difficult times, one possible solution was a CO2 Emission Calculator. This is highly needed in the modern-day world because humans are causing uncontrollable pollution, which is life-threatening. I had to determine the electricity rates in different states and create a function to calculate the carbon emission by its usage. It records the human and other activities such as driving, eating, cooking, etc., and calculates the result, which is the carbon emission of the household.
