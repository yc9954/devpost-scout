Overview Non-Fungible Factory is a service for Game Developers, it provides an incredible simple way for creating In-App game assets marketplace without knowing smart contracts and blockchain internal kitchen. Features Polygon Blockchain. One of the leading blockchains. High efficiency along with low commissions. ERC-721 and ERC-1155 contracts. Non-Fungible Factory supports Polygon ERC-721 and ERC-1155 contract generation. Suites to all your game items you want to tokenize. Server-Side SDK. Use our SDK for seamless integration of your game with your Polygon-based marketplace. Better user experience for game users who wants to play a game with tokenized assets.
