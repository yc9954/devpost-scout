## Body

Collect and conquer the bubble world—one pearl at a time! Crown Island Quests New PopUp Notifications New Item Page UI New Time Trials 🧠 Inspiration The original version of Bubble Pursuit was a fun semi-idle game about collecting pearls and upgrading multipliers. But after reflecting on how players interacted with the world, we realized there was a major opportunity to improve the sense of long-term purpose and progression. We were inspired by the satisfaction of mastery in idle and tycoon games—combined with the joy of active exploration in platformers. Our goal became clear: transform Bubble Pursuit into an experience that rewards time, skill, and investment, giving players deeper goals to chase and a stronger connection to their in-game progress. 🎮 What it does The Progression Update introduces a full revamp of the game loop and reward systems. Key updates include: 🛒 Reworked Shop System with new collectible items: Pickaxe : Grants access to the Mining Field Battleaxe : Allows pearl farming from trees Pulse Gem : Activates Idle Pillars for passive pearl collection Bubble Crown : Unlocks Crown Island and the Ascension System ⏱️ Time Trials A new 6-tier skill-based mini-game where players race against the clock to collect pearls. 🔁 Ascension System A prestige mechanic that allows players to reset progress in exchange for permanent buffs. 👑 Crown Island A late-game destination that unlocks after earning the Bubble Crown. Designed as an “endgame” reward. ⚡ New Boosts Speed Boost – Run faster Jump Juice – Jump higher Pearl Magnet – Attract pearls from a distance Golden Timer – x10 pearl multiplier for 10 minutes 📜 Quest & Milestone Tracking Adds guided objectives and short-term goals to help players feel a constant sense of achievement. ⛏️ Mining Field A new interactive area unlocked with the Pickaxe that adds depth to exploration and farming mechanics. 🎨 Additional Improvements Revamped Shop UI design Revamped overlay HUD design Updated sound design for a more dynamic and polished experience 🔧 How we built it We started by mapping out a new core gameplay loop—one that blends idle progress with player-driven goals. Using Google Docs and whiteboarding tools, we broke down new ideas into key features like items, zones, boosts, and UI. Design Tools : Photoshop, Figma (UI mockups), Substance Painter 3D Modeling : Blender (low-poly style) Development : TypeScript, Windsurf, and Meta Horizon’s scripting tools We paid close attention to the placement of Time Trial gates , Idle Pillars , and Golden Pearls to ensure every part of the world offered meaningful, rewarding interaction. 🧱 Challenges we ran into One of the biggest challenges was designing a progression system that felt meaningful but not overwhelming. We had to carefully balance long-term goals like Ascension and Crown Island with short-term gameplay loops like boosts and time trials. Another challenge was maintaining performance on mobile. With added systems and interactables, we had to do careful optimization and memory management to ensure smooth gameplay. 🏆 Accomplishments that we're proud of Designing a new progression loop that gives players more agency and a clearer sense of direction Creating custom UI and sound design that feel playful and intuitive on mobile Implementing Crown Island and the Ascension System , our most ambitious mechanics to date Making a fun game feel meaningful and motivating without losing its casual charm 📚 What we learned This update taught us how important it is to design with player motivation in mind. Mechanics need to be more than functional—they should feel earned , intentional , and satisfying . We also saw firsthand how good UX and visual feedback can dramatically improve player retention and clarity. Balancing idle, active, and prestige systems requires iteration—but when done right, it forms a powerful loop that keeps players returning. 🚀 What's next for Bubble Pursuit: Idle Tycoon – Progression Update Here’s what we’re planning post-competition: Social Sharing Features – Share Time Trial runs, Ascension stats, and collection moments Leaderboard System – For Crown Island, Ascension, and Time Trials Seasonal Events & Limited-Time Items – Keeps the game fresh and replayable Expanded Crown Island – With new challenges and endgame rewards Daily Quests – Giving casual players bite-sized goals and rewards We’re committed to growing Bubble Pursuit into a standout world on Meta Horizon Mobile—one that players love to return to. <div
