## Body

AI Shopping Host eliminates language barriers in global e-commerce by automatically generating multilingual short-form videos and real-time AI chat support. Sellers simply enter a product URL, and AI extracts details, creates localized promotional videos, and answers customer questions in multiple languages. This helps businesses expand internationally while giving consumers the confidence to shop in their native language. With the global e-commerce market exceeding $10 trillion, AI Shopping Host bridges the gap between sellers and buyers, making cross-border shopping effortless.
