Header Section Map for city properties Transaction Compling and deploying contract - 1 Compling and deploying contract - 2 Inspiration Real estate is one of the world’s largest asset classes, yet its behavior remains slow, opaque, and paperwork-driven. Ownership verification depends on fragmented records, rental income moves monthly, and sustainability signals rarely influence investment decisions in real time. We explored a different direction: What if real estate behaved like a programmable financial primitive , similar to crypto-native assets, while enabling sustainability-aware capital allocation for smarter urban investment decisions? FractionRealmRise prototypes a functional decision-support interface for real-world assets (RWAs) where property becomes: fractional transparent continuously yielding AI-assisted decision-support driven instead of listing-driven This aligns with Quantum Sprint’s goal of building practical, working innovation for real-world financial systems . What it does FractionRealmRise is a working Web3 + FinTech prototype on Aptos that transforms static property listings into interactive programmable investment workflows . The platform enables users to: Explore properties through immersive 3D city → building → unit navigation Acquire fractional ownership using Aptos Move smart contracts Simulate per-second streaming rental yield visibility Verify ownership through on-chain transaction transparency Compare assets using sustainability-aligned investment indicators Interpret signals through AI-assisted compliance and trust layers Experience diversified exposure via gamified fractional allocation sets Instead of acting as a listing marketplace alone, the system demonstrates how RWAs can function as interactive financial decision-support infrastructure . How we built it FractionRealmRise was implemented as a modular full-stack Web3 prototype combining blockchain ownership logic, spatial visualization, and sustainability-aware scoring workflows. Blockchain Layer We implemented programmable ownership using: Aptos Move smart contracts resource-based fractional ownership structures checkpoint-based streaming yield accounting logic wallet-based ownership verification flows These allow property fractions to behave like secure non-copyable on-chain financial assets . Aptos Move was selected for its resource-oriented programming model , ensuring integrity of fractional allocation tracking. Spatial Discovery Layer To improve usability beyond spreadsheet-style listings: Mapbox Standard Style rendered real-world 3D city environments React Three Fiber enabled interior-level visualization hierarchical City → Building → Unit navigation simplified asset comparison This creates a visual decision-support interface instead of static dashboards . Frontend Architecture We developed the interface using: Next.js 14 modular frontend architecture wallet-aware interaction states real-time transaction visibility layers component-driven financial UI rendering The frontend bridges immersive discovery with blockchain execution workflows. Compliance & Sustainability Layer To simulate real-world financial onboarding pipelines within hackathon constraints, we implemented: identity-aware onboarding logic simulation compliance signal visualization explainable sustainability scoring SDG-aligned investment indicators interpretable trust-layer architecture for RWAs This enables transparent decision-support signals instead of opaque investment ratings. Challenges we ran into Designing a functional RWA investment workflow within hackathon constraints required solving: building registry-independent ownership modeling designing gas-efficient streaming yield accounting simulating ESG indicators without institutional datasets translating compliance workflows into intuitive investor-facing UX optimizing browser-based 3D spatial rendering performance ensuring explainable AI-assisted scoring instead of black-box outputs These constraints shaped a transparency-first system architecture. Accomplishments that we're proud of Within the development timeline, we successfully demonstrated: resource-based fractional ownership logic on Aptos a working prototype of continuous-yield financial interaction a 3D spatial investment navigation interface wallet-visible ownership verification flows integration-ready compliance and sustainability scoring architecture an applied AI + blockchain decision-support workflow for RWAs This shows how emerging technologies can be combined into a functional FinTech experimentation platform , aligning with Quantum Sprint’s applied innovation focus. What we learned Building FractionRealmRise revealed key insights: sustainable finance platforms must evolve into decision-support infrastructures trust in RWAs depends on explainable UX as much as blockchain transparency streaming yield models improve perceived liquidity even without secondary markets sustainability indicators influence allocation only when financially actionable AI agents translate SDG-aligned impact metrics into interpretable investment guidance RWAs require hybrid trust architectures combining on-chain verification with compliance signals These insights guided the system toward practical usability instead of conceptual experimentation. Responsible AI & Ethical Design Direction FractionRealmRise prioritizes fairness, transparency, and interpretability through explainable investment signals. Future iterations introduce: explainable investment scoring dashboards liquidity risk indicators regulatory exposure visibility layers bias-aware allocation signals across emerging markets accessibility-ready interface modes investor-facing rental variability risk meters This supports responsible adoption across diverse investor groups. What's next for FractionRealmRise We plan to extend the prototype into a scalable sustainable finance experimentation platform: deploy AI decision agents combining rental forecasts with SDG indicators introduce dynamic property-level risk meters enable portfolio optimization engines support secondary ownership mobility layers integrate legal-backed compliance onboarding pipelines develop institutional sustainability analytics dashboards expand toward green credit scoring and infrastructure financing workflows Our long-term direction is transforming RWAs into programmable capital aligned with sustainability outcomes . <div
