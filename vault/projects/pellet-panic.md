## Body

Inspiration Snapchat's newest technology, the Spectacles, were incredibly interesting and appealing to us developers. What it does A ghost chases around the player while they try to collect pellets. How we built it We built it in Snapchat's Lens Studio using JavaScript and TypeScript. Challenges we ran into We had challenges finding helpful resources as the technology is currently very new, and we had little to no experience with Lens Studio or AR development. Accomplishments that we're proud of We were able to create our first AR project! What we learned We learned a lot about the challenges and responsibilities of collaborating on an AR project on a strict time frame. What's next for Pellet Panic There is a lot to improve on the features of our application. We would like to add leaderboards, items/power-ups, achievements, UI, and animations for the pellets and ghost. <div
