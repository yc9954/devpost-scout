## Body

Inspiration We have seen a lot of users facing a lot of issues with launching NFTs, they have to go through the following process which takes up to 3-6 months of development time, and cost. 1) Hire a solidity developer 2) Write the contract 2) Audit the contract 3) Launch the contract Via LYNC NFT Deployer it can be done in 4 clicks. What it does LYNC NFT Deployer is a smart contract deployment platform that allows users to create and deploy their 1/1 NFT, Multiple editions, in-game assets as an NFT, and Entire NFT Collection with a claimer page in just a few clicks on the mantle chain.
