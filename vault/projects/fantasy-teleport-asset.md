## Body

Fantasy teleport with the script of teleporting the player to selected location. 3D model is optimised for performance both on VR and mobile devices. Contains modular parts and items as a separate assets which can be deleted in case those are not needed. HOW TO USE: Add a teleport asset to your scene. Place the "PointToTeleportTo" gizmo to the point where you want the user to be teleported to. When player enters the teleport center they will be teleported to that point. Teleport has customizable settings. Script is attached to the Teleport trigger zone, there you can select if you want teleport to have teleport itself, audio effect for teleporting and vfx on start and end of the teleporting. You can also switch audio and vfx to the ones of your choosing. Just make sure to attach corresponding gizmos. <div
