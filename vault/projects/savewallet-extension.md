SaveWallet Extension Inspiration Following the success of SaveWallet at HackaTRON S6, we will continue studying and enhancing the product. Many TRON users have embraced the SaveWallet application, although it is still restricted when used on a PC. SaveWallet Extension was built to meet the demands of the TRON community. What it does SaveWallet Extension reduces steps, offering users quick and easy access to their app. It allows synchronization between devices if you use the same browser account. Onboarding more users on Tron by saving their transaction fees, making Web3 accessible to everyone. How we built it Our team had experience in developing wallet earlier. We recognize the potential of Energy system on Tron Network then we build a wallet that could save transaction fees for every users by renting Energy automatically. Challenges we ran into The most most difficult challenge is to increase SaveWallet's user base and establish a strong presence in the TRON community. Accomplishments that we're proud of Our SaveWallet application have won the prize on HackaTron S6 and gained so many supports from the community. We also helped users save their money and gained interest from our system. What we learned The most vital thing we have learned is stick with the community, serve them well and continuously update what we build.
