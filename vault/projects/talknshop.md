## Body

Inspiration Hewlett-Packard's President and CEO, Meg Whitman, said it very well, "Communication is at the heart of e-commerce and community. This quote keep us moving and gave us inspiration for our app TALKnSHOP. What it does TALKnSHOP provides more transparency than any other app you might know. Here on our platform the customer and the seller would be in direct contact with each other. Locals can find it easy to sell and grow their business. That is why the slogan for our app is “Connecting locals around you for them and you” It will help sellers to sell their products or their talent on an interface that will act as a social media cum e-commerce. For example, if someone has a shoe shop in a small town and wants to expand his business by wanting to sell his product in the whole country then with the help of our app he can create an account just like one we create on Instagram or Facebook where he can post pictures of his shoes or do certain creative things to attract customers and post them on our app so that buyers see the pictures and can order them from there. Not only products but if you have certain talents such as singing, here on our app you could post your videos of singing different songs from where people could hire you to sing songs at their parties. You don’t know singing don’t worry you can sell any skills you have such as sketching coding copywriting and many more. Even services like lunch making will also be provided. For instance, it is very difficult to find homemade food for people living far from their families. On our app, they can search for these services too and can have a home-cooked meal from their local residence. Our application will provide more transparency by providing one on one communication. Here the seller and buyer will be in direct contact. Our app will promote and support local and small-scale businesses. Talents will be acknowledged by a larger audience. One will not need to visit or keep different apps for hiring people or buying different goods. The power of freelancing and e-business will be provided under our app on your fingerprint for security. How we built it As we are beginners in the tech domain but we will try our best to develop it if we get recognition and investors. Challenges we ran into As beginners, we were not aware of the technology involved in building an app. Accomplishments that we're proud of We are thinking and planning to facilitate everyone with ease of all the possible services available on e-commerce and social media at one place at your fingertips.
