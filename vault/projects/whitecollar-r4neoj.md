## Body

WaterCollar icon Save water Design of the product The Inspiration Behind WhiteCollar The idea for WhiteCollar struck me during a quiet, everyday moment—using the bathroom. As I listened to the sound of running water while washing my hands and taking a shower, I realized something unsettling: the water didn’t sound calm or relaxing anymore. Instead, it felt as though it was screaming for help, reflecting the rising bills, the strain on electricity from geysers and dispensers, and, most of all, the growing issue of water wastage. It hit me how passive I had become about water conservation—concerned only occasionally, rather than actively addressing the problem.
