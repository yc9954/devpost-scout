Inspiration I wanted a remixable world that feels like a canvas of exploration , inspired by open-world games like Zelda and Skyrim . My goal was to craft modular biomes—grasslands, deserts, sky, and snowfields—that creators can easily remix, expand, or reskin. Creators can expand on a single biome or work with combinations. What it does Knowhere is a multi-biome remixable world featuring: A central hub that connects to distinct elemental regions. Three complete biomes: Grassland (starter hub), Desert (ruins + oasis), and Snow (cabins + ice caves). Modular assets (trees, ruins, mystical elements, cabins, portals, crystals) that others can drag-and-drop. A consistent painterly, semi-realistic art style optimized for mobile. How I built it 85% of the assets and scripts are built using Meta AI (Gen AI) . Meta AI was the sole reason I was able to build this project as a beginner with no knowledge about 3D modeling and texturing. I generated skyboxes, 3D assets, texture options, ambient and SFX sound. Even the scripts were generated and attached with the help of Meta AI. Challenges I ran into Building terrains and pathways was difficult and required experimenting with asset placement. Maintaining a consistent art direction across grass, desert, and snow biomes. Accomplishments that I'm proud of Built three distinct yet cohesive biomes that feel immersive and welcoming. Created reusable assets that fit many genres (adventure, roleplay, obby). Aligned the project with the hackathon’s open source + remixability spirit. What I learned How to design for remixability : decoupling art from scripts, exposing properties, and documenting usage. How much mobile optimization matters—small texture/geometry tweaks can hugely improve FPS. How to blend atmosphere and gameplay so the world is both beautiful and functional. Judging Criteria Reusability Knowhere is a remixable world that provides creators with a library of assets such as trees, ruins, cabins, icy crystals, and portals—ready to be remixed in new projects. Each biome works as a standalone environment but can also be combined with others, allowing remixers to expand the world in their own direction. Modularity Every component is designed to be independent and adaptable . Assets like trees, ruins, and cabins can be rearranged, reskinned, or replaced without breaking the environment. This modular design means creators can remix a single biome or stitch multiple biomes together for larger adventures. Technical Impact All assets were optimized for smooth mobile performance , ensuring consistent frame rates across biomes. Minimal Scripts are used in this version to focus on remixable scenes and assets. By combining AI-generated models with Horizon Worlds scripting, I showed how even beginners can create a technically solid, large-scale world that others can learn from and remix. Experience Design Each biome has its own unique atmosphere : warm grasslands, harsh deserts with ruins, and icy snowfields with glowing crystals and auroras. The painterly semi-realistic art style ties the different biomes together into a cohesive, immersive experience. What's next for Knowhere Add more biomes (Volcanic, Ocean, Secret Dark Level). Introduce new interactive systems ( teleporters, jump pads, environmental hazards ). Grow a community remix chain where creators link their worlds back into Knowhere. Overall I am very proud to have built this as a beginner and that I could actually complete it and submit what I had in mind.
