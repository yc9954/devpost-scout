## Body

STATION-15 Title Image 💡 Inspiration I’ve always loved puzzle games—constantly seeking new ways to challenge my own wits. STATION-15 is my answer to that craving: a space where logic, patience, and creativity collide. 🧩 What It Does STATION-15 is a collection of unique puzzle challenges designed to test a wide range of skills: Deduction Timing Memory Problem-solving Speed Attention to detail Motor coordination Patience Each room presents a distinct, instruction-free challenge that puts your instincts and intuition to the test. With dynamic puzzle solutions, no two playthroughs feel the same—every visit offers something new to uncover. 🛠 How I Built It This project took a ton of pre-planning, iteration, and creative problem-solving. I’m genuinely proud of the final result. Tools Used: 🧠 Horizon Worlds Desktop & VR Editor 🧊 Blender (3D modeling) 🎵 Ableton Live 12 Suite (original background music) Why Codeblocks over TypeScript? Every script in STATION-15 was built using Codeblocks , and that was a deliberate decision. This world is remixable , and I wanted it to serve as a learning tool for new creators. When I started out, Codeblocks were my gateway into understanding scripting logic. They helped me build confidence and eventually transition into deeper TypeScript development. So in this project, I scripted every mechanic with extreme attention to detail , making it as readable, understandable, and learnable as possible. 🚧 Challenges I Ran Into The hardest part? Designing puzzles that felt truly fresh and unexpected . I pushed myself to avoid recycled puzzle tropes. Instead, I reimagined familiar concepts and memory sequences with new twists—always keeping the player experience at the center. 🏆 Accomplishments I'm Proud Of I'm proud of this project— period. It’s a major milestone for me: I dove into Blender for the first time ever I created custom music from scratch I built all scripting logic modularly so others can remix and reuse it And I did it all under tight competition deadlines This project pushed me in all the right ways—and I thrived under the pressure. 📚 What I Learned I’ve never created a puzzle game before. But like every world I build, this one taught me something new—about mechanics, storytelling, world design, and myself. Ask anyone who knows me, and they’ll tell you: “I have an idea.” This was one of my biggest. 🚀 What’s Next for STATION-15 Since STATION-15 is remixable , I’m excited to see how other creators build on it. You can: Add your own puzzles Remix the scripting Repurpose the entire structure for something entirely different Every mechanic is modular and reusable —including door logic, mini-games, code systems, and more. Whether you’re new to Horizon or a seasoned builder, there’s something in here you can learn from or build on. I can’t wait to see where it goes next. <div
