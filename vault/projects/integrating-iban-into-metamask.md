# Integrating IBAN into Metamask

> IBANs are used to identify bank accounts in 77 countries, including most of Europe. Integrating IBANs into Metamask allows users to seamlessly transfer cash between bank accounts and Metamask.

## Body

Inspiration We were frustrated with using on-ramps and exchanges to move money between bank accounts and blockchains. What it does Links an IBAN with your Metamask address so you receive euros from your bank account into Metamask and send it back to your bank account. How we built it Using SNAP interacting with the Monerium API. Monerium is an authorized issuer of electronic money (ie, a fully regulated and authorized "stablecoin"). Challenges we ran into Were to able to listen to events inside Snap. Accomplishments that we're proud of Learning how to use Snap. What we learned How to use Snap.
