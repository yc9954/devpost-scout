Play to earn game and NFT colllection Inspiration Our vision of the metaverse is not an open 3D world how "Sandbox" or "Decentraland", but a Game hub where players can move through other games with one NFT-character. NFT-characters from other games can be integrated into our project. We will create collections for our partners. Integrate their 3d models or create duplicates, matching our style and vision What it does Clash Arena is a PvP game in which the player can play and earn. Luck, psychology and the skill of the players are important in the Clash Arena How we built it We make the game on the Unity engine and integrate it into our website. Challenges we ran into The main unresolved problem is that our game is completely blockchain-based, and every turn we need to sign a transaction in the Metamask, which interferes with the game itself. But we will solve this problem in the future. Accomplishments that we're proud of We made a working prototype. That means we can see the idea through to the end.
