CalorieCoin Inspiration The image above is a scene from Black Mirror Season 1, [“15 Million Merits”]. In this episode, people have lost their jobs to AI and must exercise to sustain their livelihood because they are rewarded with “CalorieCoin(Merits)” for exercising. It vividly portrays the concept of “Exercise to Earn.” The CalorieCoin team envisions an Exercise To Earn Rewards system for the near future. CalorieCoin (Exercise To Earn Blockchain system) aims to provide continuous motivation to visit the gym for sustained exercise. CalorieCoin's vision is to expand beyond a single gym to over 3000 gyms and integrate various E2E platform projects (e.g., Jump Rope, Treadmill, BodyScale, Mobile App). Our goal is to pioneer the E2E (Exercise to Earn) platform, integrating gyms with devices and services like B2B SaaS fitness apps, pedometers, IoT jump ropes, smart scales, hula hoops, and more. ☑️ Ⅱ.
