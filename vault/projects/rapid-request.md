Rapid Request by GridForce Contractor dispatcher gets notifies and can approve their pick Employee slots are routed to available Leads and Classifications Contractors automated team routing means it's Rapid Easy to setup and manage Contractors and their workers Inspiration Power outages last 12x longer during major events like natural disasters. We can't predict the future, but we can create solutions that increase speed, therefore effectiveness in critical emergencies. The goal was to make one app able to hire numerous specialty contractors for emergency work. What it does Rapid Request provides utility companies and their contractors a secure, easy way to search for, book and manage emergency inspections, repairs and maintenance. Rapid Request provides a simple user interface, capable of requesting and routing hundreds of employees across many contractors, in one single form.
