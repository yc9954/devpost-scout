Inspiration Focusing in creating something that would bring the most value to the community we discover that mobile development is missing. I ask even the community and receive feedback from you guys (very grateful for those who helped me out). As you might know there are a lot of projects web based (some of them supports mobile browsing) but very little to none around mobile. So the best value that I could bring is develop around mobile. What it does We are trying to be the greates mobile developer where you could do all sorts of operations. Operations for example that you can’t do on wallets (example TrustWallet you can’t exchange tokens can’t call smart Contracts… so on) . When develop a great app you could develop either a great wallet either a great app that is doing specific task but not both. So we are the TrustWallet extension that does have: 🔁 exchange aggregator 💸 send payments TRX/ TRC20 📊 displaying data such as history trading (charts, transaction list) 🕵 analyzing smartContracts 🧪 interacting with smartContracts ⚡ rent Energy before any transaction 🌟 do not go through in case an transaction is about to fail How we built it WalletConnect was used for our dApp. We use lightweight charts and plan to add advance charts by TradingView(similar with our website). We use Routers and factories across #TronNetwork to make an aggregation and to query prices in order that after that to make the swap with the best price. Accomplishments that we're proud of this hackathon season 🌐 We develop our website and integrated TradingView Advance Charts( https://www.onchainvision.xyz/spot ) 🤖 We make a Telegram Bot & Dicord Bot that shows charts/price/liquidity/volume images on all pairs on multiple DEXes on Tron What we learned Building a community is a hard job even with a good product/service.
