## Body

We have developed a multi-chain designer of decentralized autonomous organizations ( DAO ) Our project solves the problem of the availability of blockchain solutions for business. With the help of our product, anyone can create and manage a DAO for free and without programming skills. To implement the idea, we have developed smart contracts with solidity and a web application with next + react (UX/UI) and node + nest + mongoose (backend). Voting in our DAO is conducted off-chain using a private key signature. We use Chainlink technologies to verify the achievement of quorum in voting and optimize the load on the network. We intend to continue to develop our project. We believe that the main problem of web3 technologies is the complexity of interaction for users, so we will focus on the development of impressive user-friendly interfaces.
