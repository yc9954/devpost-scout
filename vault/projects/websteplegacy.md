Inspiration My son showed me a picture of a bear on a bike. He asked me if I can draw it for him. He’s 6 years old. I drew a version of it for my son and turned it into my first NFT. What it does Cruisin’ Bear cashed in his CKB and is living his life to the fullest. How we built it I have created a outline of the Cruisin’ Bear originally on MediBang but now use ProCreate. I develop a theme for each Cruisin’ Bear that’s related to him getting rich after cashing in his CKB. Each Cruisin’ Bear has his own theme and rare attributes. All are 1/1. Challenges we ran into After a certain amount of color changes within the outline, the outline will change colors. I have to create a new fresh outline every so often. I’m learning as I go. Accomplishments that we're proud of I’ve sold 9 of the 11 minted Cruisin’ Bears ranging from 5000-8000 CKB a piece. What we learned I sell more Cruisin’ Bears at the floor price of 5000 CKB.
