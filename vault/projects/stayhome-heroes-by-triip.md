## Body

Home screen Upload Proof of Stay Receive reward Inspiration Staying home is the most effective solution to slow down the pandemic yet it also makes people feel lonely. We want to make Staying home fun and rewarding. We want to create as many StayHome Heroes as possible. What it does We ask users to submit a Proof of Stay ( photo of staying home) each day so we can reward the user with our TriipMiles token. Users can use these TriipMiles token to pay for accommodations, tours, and experiences in 229 countries later. The more a user stays, the more he/she earn to travel later. How I built it We have built this platform on a strong and fast blockchain infrastructure. The app has been tested with more than 160,000 users in 131 countries. Challenges I ran into It is hard to spread the message to more users across the world as we don't have a budget for marketing. Accomplishments that I'm proud of Our app on iOS and Android have been welcome rated by our users: 4.7/5. What I learned A simple idea can spread to 131 countries.
