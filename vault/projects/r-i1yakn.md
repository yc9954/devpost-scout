## Body

Real Art in a Small Apartment Maya’s obsessed with the look of Japanese calligraphy, but her reality is a cramped apartment with white carpets and zero extra space. Traditional Shodo is just too much of a hassle, you’ve got the heavy ink stones, felt mats, and a constant need for space to let wet paper dry. On top of the clutter, she’s terrified of spilling permanent black ink.** ZenStrokeXR** was built for people like Maya who want the art without the physical mess or the "apartment-friendly" limitations. How It Actually Works ZenStrokeXR isn't just a drawing app; it’s a focused educational tool that uses the Logitech MX Ink and Meta Quest to make digital calligraphy feel real. Real Brush Haptics: We use the MX Ink’s pressure sensors so that when you press down, the digital stroke gets thicker, just like a real brush hitting paper. The "Pathfinder" System: Beginners often struggle with stroke order, so the app shows a "ghost" stroke that you follow to learn the correct flow of the Kanji. Live AI Grading: We'll build an AI engine that tracks how fast you move and how closely you follow the path, giving you an "Accuracy Score" so you know exactly where you messed up. Unlockable Gear: To keep things fun, you earn "Zen Coins" for good practice sessions, which you can spend in the shop to get custom brush skins like the "Dragon" or "Cyber" styles. VR Atmosphere: Instead of a messy desk, you’re sitting in a quiet Japanese garden, which helps with the meditative side of the practice. Technical Complexity We bypass standard VR controllers to focus on the specialized haptics and ergonomics of the Logitech MX Ink, making highly tangible.
