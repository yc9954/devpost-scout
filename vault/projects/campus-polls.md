## Body

CampusPolls Inspiration I've always enjoyed seeing results from twitter polls and I wanted to see if I could replicate that in a way that helps students. What it does It allows students to create or take polls that other students have made specifically for their school. How I built it I built it in Node.js using Firebase as my database and Alexa Presentation Language to build the view. Challenges I ran into Firebase was difficult to work with on lambda and I should have used DynamoDB instead. What I am proud of I am proud of being able to pick up new technologies and make some cool things for students both with Alexa and mobile development What I learned How to use APL to build dynamic user interfaces.
