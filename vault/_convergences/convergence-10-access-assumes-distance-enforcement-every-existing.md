# 6 projects, 6 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** access, assumes, distance, enforcement, every, existing, fall, financial, groups, informal, institution, loss, rotating, savings

### [[perceiv-io]] — PERCEIV/IO
> combined sensory loss defeats every existing accommodation, because sign language assumes sight and transcription assumes hearing, so the people with both losses fall through the gap between two solved problems

*Hack the North 2024* · problem: accommodations are designed per impairment and their intersection is served by neither

### [[farm-sutra]] — Farm Sutra
> the loss is caused by lack of access rather than lack of effort, and every existing tool assumes a smartphone, English and bandwidth the user does not have

*Amazon Nova AI Hackathon* · problem: the intervention is designed for a user profile the affected population does not match

### [[bloinx-opyq7d]] — Bloinx
> over 60 percent of the world's employed are in the informal economy and already run rotating savings groups, so the financial primitive exists and lacks only enforcement

*Celo's Make Crypto Mobile Hackathon* · problem: tandas and chit funds depend on trust that sometimes fails

### [[ikubchain-decentralized-investment-clubs-on-polkadot]] — IkubChain - Decentralized Investment Clubs on Polkadot
> rotating savings groups exist independently across Ethiopia, Latin America and West Africa under different names, so the institution is a human universal and the missing part is enforcement at a distance

*Build Resilient Apps with Polkadot Cloud* · problem: community investment clubs depend on proximity and personal trust

### [[allbank]] — AllBank: Inclusive, Intelligent, Impactful
> banking accessibility remains the barrier while banking technology advances, so the unbanked fall further behind with each improvement that assumes existing access

*HackUTD 2024: Ripple Effect* · problem: the financial system evolves and accessibility for the unbanked does not

### [[decentralized-kameti]] — Decentralized Kameti
> a rotating savings group depends on trust and identity that break at a distance, so the informal institution fails to scale for a verification reason rather than a financial one

*Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon* · problem: rotating savings groups rely on personal trust that does not scale
