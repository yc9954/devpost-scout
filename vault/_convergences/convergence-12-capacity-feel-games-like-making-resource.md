# 6 projects, 6 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** capacity, feel, games, like, making, resource, scarce, supply

### [[citadel-life-dungeon]] — Citadel: LIFE//DUNGEON
> scams, job interviews, financial pressure, bureaucracy and medical paperwork all require decision-making under stress, and they feel like games where the rules are unclear and the difficulty changes without warning

*Pixel Forge AI Hackathon ($18,000+ in Prizes)* · problem: the life skills with the highest consequence are the ones nobody is allowed to practise

### [[metropolis-ai]] — Metropolis.ai
> games like Skyrim and Red Dead represent hundreds or thousands of developer hours sunk into making a world feel populated

*UC Berkeley AI Hackathon* · problem: immersion scales with hand-authored NPC depth, which means the cost of a living world is linear in its size

### [[abhyas]] — Abhyas Teacher Assistant
> India's demographic weight is in the under-nineteen bracket at 37.1 percent, so the country's outcome depends on rural school teaching capacity and that is the scarce resource

*Alexa Skills Challenge: Alexa Conversations* · problem: rural government school teachers need support to serve a very large young population

### [[drive-through-isolator-sampling-station]] — Drive-through Isolator Sampling Station
> a protective measure that consumes a scarce resource per use converts a capacity problem into a supply problem

*COVID-19 Global Hackathon 1.0* · problem: expanding testing depletes the PPE that testing staff depend on

### [[chaotiq]] — ChaotIQ
> studying feels like a chore and games feel like an adventure despite both being effortful, so the difference is in the structure of feedback rather than in the work

*Student HackPad 2025* · problem: learning platforms are static while games hold attention

### [[pyon-pyon-studio]] — Pyon Pyon Studio
> fan attachment to characters generates demand faster than any studio can supply, so the scarce resource is production capacity rather than audience interest

*brainrot jia.seed hackathon ($5,772) in prizes * · problem: fans want more content about characters than exists
