# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** button, care, emergency, help, often, reach, speak, unable

### [[safesign-ai-gesture-based-patient-assistance-system]] — SafeSign – AI Gesture Based Patient Assistance System
> patients in hospitals, care homes and recovery rooms are often unable to speak or reach help — elderly, disabled or post-surgical patients may not manage to press the emergency button

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: the call system requires a physical action the patient's condition is exactly what prevents

### [[allsensesai-guardian-silent-emergency-detection-with-ernie]] — AllSensesAI Guardian — Silent Emergency Detection with ERNIE
> most emergency systems rest on a fragile assumption — that a person in danger can speak, tap a button or clearly ask for help — and people may be threatened into silence, restrained, or unable to act

*ERNIE AI Developer Challenge* · problem: the trigger for help requires the capability that danger removes

### [[riis]] — RIIS
> structural collapses kill survivors not because rescue is impossible but because care cannot reach them in time — in the first hour survival drops sharply with every minute, and the patient is often unreachable

*LA Hacks 2026* · problem: the survival curve is steepest in exactly the window where nobody can find or assess the person
