# 2 projects, 2 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** experience, sharing, worth

### [[jawal]] — JAWAL
> sharing an experience requires an audience and platforms allocate audience by follower count, so people with something worth sharing are silenced by a metric unrelated to the content

*MLH Local Hack Day (2018)* · problem: sharing journeys requires many followers to reach anyone

### [[earthlink]] — EarthLink - Geospatial Social Media
> a shared experience is of a place and social media carries images of it, so the medium loses the dimension that made the experience worth sharing

*Google’s Immersive Geospatial Challenge* · problem: text, images and video cannot convey the place an experience happened in
