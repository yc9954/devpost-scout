# 6 projects, 6 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** affects, india, million, pcos, women, year

### [[aegis-hindi-consent-companion]] — Aegis Hindi Consent Companion
> India performs over 50 million surgical procedures a year, most patients in government hospitals are Hindi-speaking with limited English literacy, and almost all pre-operative consent is in English

*Agents Assemble - The Healthcare AI Endgame* · problem: informed consent is a legal and ethical requirement discharged in a language the patient cannot read

### [[neurobin-smart-waste-segregator-vd5j9x]] — NeuroBin — AI Waste Intelligence for Smart Cities
> India generates over 62 million tons of waste a year and more than 70% ends in landfill — the core issue is not the lack of recycling systems but the failure of segregation at the source

*DSH Hacks V1* · problem: the downstream infrastructure exists and is fed a mixed stream it cannot process

### [[skillmap-f8xsij]] — SkillMap : Employment platform for the next billion
> India produces over 11 million new graduates a year and the majority of jobs are in the top five metro cities

*2018 Developer Circles Community Challenge* · problem: the supply of qualified people and the location of demand are geographically disjoint, and no matching mechanism spans the gap

### [[dandi-iczt68]] — Dandi
> PCOS affects around 175 million women, one in two sees three or more doctors before diagnosis, so the diagnostic delay is produced by the absence of continuous measurement rather than by clinical difficulty

*FigBuild 2026* · problem: hormonal conditions are diagnosed from snapshots taken at appointments

### [[pcosense]] — PCOSense
> PCOS affects one in five women in India and remains widely misunderstood, so the management burden falls on patients who cannot get an explanation of their own condition

*LaunchHacks IV* · problem: a common condition is poorly understood by the people living with it

### [[youcare-sjaovm]] — YouCare
> PCOS affects 5 to 10 percent of women and raises other risks, and the pattern is visible in data the woman already has, so the recognition problem is one of assembling her own signals

*TreeHacks 2023* · problem: women with PCOS symptoms do not identify the condition
