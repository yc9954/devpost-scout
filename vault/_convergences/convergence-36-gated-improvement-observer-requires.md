# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** gated, improvement, observer, requires

### [[stealth-startup-e9og7s]] — Swish
> correcting shooting form requires an observer and a personal coach is the usual answer, so improvement is gated on paying someone to watch

*NexHacks* · problem: learning proper shooting form requires a coach to observe the mechanics

### [[mirror-lmd54a]] — Mirror
> reflection requires a record and building the record is itself the work that a busy person cannot do, so self-improvement is gated on capture rather than on insight

*TreeHacks 2025* · problem: staying on top of reflection and self-improvement while busy is impractical

### [[gameguru]] — GameGuru
> a complex multiplayer game generates more simultaneous information than a person can process, so improvement requires an external observer rather than more play

*Junction 2017* · problem: large-scale tactical games exceed what a player can perceive in real time
