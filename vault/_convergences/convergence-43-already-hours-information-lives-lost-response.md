# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** already, hours, information, lives, lost, response

### [[memexllm]] — MemexLLM
> I watched PhD students spend entire weekends on literature reviews that should take hours — hundreds of PDFs, dozens of tabs, and hours lost finding information you already read

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: the reader has already processed the information and cannot retrieve it, so the cost is paid repeatedly

### [[t-qbl0c3]] — SwiftER
> an ambulance can be fast and still arrive at the wrong hospital because dispatch runs on outdated radio, so lives are lost to routing rather than to response time

*NexHacks* · problem: outdated radio systems send ambulances to hospitals that cannot receive the patient

### [[floodguard-okgzty]] — FloodGuard
> flooding is predictable hours ahead and the response is organised after the water arrives, so lives are lost in an interval where the information already existed

*ShellHacks 2025* · problem: rising waters in coastal and inland cities produce late uncoordinated responses
