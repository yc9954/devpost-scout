# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** badly, everyone, news, reader, wants

### [[mobile-throwing-kit]] — Mobile Throwing Kit
> a mechanic that works on one platform is rebuilt badly by everyone who wants it, so publishing the mechanic itself is worth more than publishing another game using it

*Meta Horizon Creator Competition: Open Source Champions* · problem: mobile fling-based interaction is hard to implement well and each developer redoes it

### [[unfold-f09npq]] — UNFOLD
> news is delivered at one depth for everyone, so a reader who wants a headline and a reader who wants the detail are both served badly

*HackZurich 2021* · problem: staying informed means being overwhelmed by volume at a fixed level of depth

### [[briefo]] — Briefo
> news platforms are either noisy or algorithmically curated, so a reader who wants to be informed has to choose between distraction and a filter they cannot see

*Perplexity Hackathon* · problem: news platforms carry noise or opaque curation
