# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** assembled, data, never, object, person's, seen

### [[chimera-tf2ues]] — Chimera
> a person's digital footprint is distributed across services and is never assembled, so nobody has seen their own data as a single object

*Kiroween* · problem: personal data exists in fragments across platforms

### [[pistis-score]] — Pistis Score
> a person's financial trustworthiness is recorded on chain and never assembled into a reputation, so the data for credit exists and the score does not

*Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon* · problem: on-chain financial behaviour is not aggregated into a usable reputation

### [[life-in-weeks]] — Life in Weeks
> a quantity people manage badly is usually one they have never seen rendered as a finite object

*World’s Largest Hackathon presented by Bolt* · problem: time is discussed abstractly and therefore discounted
