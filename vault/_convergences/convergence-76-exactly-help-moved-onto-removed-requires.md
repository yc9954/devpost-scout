# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** exactly, help, moved, onto, removed, requires

### [[accessform]] — AccessForm
> the people who most need help with official paperwork are the ones least able to complete it online, so digitising a form transfers the burden onto exactly the population it was meant to serve

*DevNetwork [API + Cloud + AI] Hackathon 2026* · problem: a blind person with a hospital bill or an elderly person with a form cannot use the online process

### [[corelytics]] — Corelytics
> an AI tool that requires a good prompt has moved the work rather than removed it, and it moves it onto exactly the people who came for help with writing

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: email assistants demand a detailed prompt to produce a usable email

### [[unremarkable-project]] — Unremarkable project IXO wallet

*Cosmos HackAtom VI * · problem: web3 tools run on hardware and operating systems controlled by the platforms they are meant to escape
