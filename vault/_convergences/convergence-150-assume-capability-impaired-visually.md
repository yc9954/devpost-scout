# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** assume, capability, impaired, visually

### [[visionaid-congo]] — VisionAid Congo
> his visually impaired cousin in the DRC cannot walk alone — not because the capability does not exist, but because the tools that would give her independence were never built for her context

*DSH Hacks V1* · problem: assistive technology exists and its assumptions about connectivity, language and cost exclude most of the people who need it

### [[nailprint]] — NailPrint
> visually impaired people use their hands most of all to explore and navigate, and the products designed for hands assume you are looking at them

*ELC Accessible Beauty Hackathon* · problem: the body part a blind person relies on most is served by an industry that is entirely visual

### [[brailleware]] — FifthSense
> smart devices assume sight in their interface, so their features are unreachable to visually impaired users regardless of capability

*PennApps XII* · problem: smart device features are inaccessible to the visually impaired
