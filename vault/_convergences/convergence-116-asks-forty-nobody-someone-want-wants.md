# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** asks, forty, nobody, someone, want, wants

### [[ta-da-intelligent-teaching-assistant]] — TA-DA: Intelligent Teaching Assistant
> the instructor asks any questions and silence follows — not because everyone understands but because nobody wants to interrupt, so students tab out to Google mid-lecture and lose the context

*TreeHacks 2026* · problem: the only channel for confusion costs social capital, so the confusion is resolved by leaving the room

### [[letmeknow-from-let-me-know-to-let-s-go]] — LetMeKnow
> someone asks what do you want to do and the chat goes quiet; forty-five minutes later, idk, what do you want to do — nobody wants to be the one who decides

*Mind the Product presents World Product Day: Everyone Ships Now* · problem: group decisions stall because proposing carries social risk and deferring carries none

### [[mind-therapy-ai-your-mental-health-companion-2fa3m0]] — Mind Therapy AI: Your Mental Health Companion
> someone dies by suicide every forty seconds not for want of strength but for want of a reachable conversation, so availability and cost are the binding constraints rather than treatment efficacy

*Hack for Humanity | 2025* · problem: mental health care is expensive, delayed and stigmatised
