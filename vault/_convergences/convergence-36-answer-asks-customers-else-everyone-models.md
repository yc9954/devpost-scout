# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** answer, asks, customers, else, everyone, models, product

### [[casting]] — CASTING
> a brand photographs its product on two models and its customers are everyone else — nobody in the company can answer who the catalogue never shows; every try-on tool asks whether this suits you and none asks who was never depicted

*YouCam API Skin AI & Apparel VTO Hackathon * · problem: try-on technology was built to sell to the shopper and can equally measure the brand's own coverage, which nobody has asked it to do

### [[seguri-a-digital-hand-to-hold-dkjo6w]] — Seguri - Digital Hand to Hold
> most AI security models are trained on Western data and English syntax, so they fail to detect the nuanced localised threats aimed at everyone else

*DSH Hacks V1* · problem: the protection available is calibrated to one language and culture, which leaves the most targeted populations unprotected

### [[t-insights]] — InsighT

*HackUTD 2025: Lost in the Pages* · problem: feedback is scattered across social media, outage reports and operational data
