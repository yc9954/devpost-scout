# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** cost, environmental, every, prompt, water

### [[drip-6ao9m2]] — Drip
> every AI prompt consumes water, and we engineered a system where every prompt generates it instead

*LA Hacks 2026* · problem: the cooling water cost of inference is an externality invisible to the person issuing the prompt

### [[sustain-uhrgfy]] — sustAIn
> every prompt has an environmental cost most people never see — electricity, cooling water, infrastructure behind every response

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: the externality is per-request, continuous and invisible at the moment of the request

### [[promptgreen]] — PromptGreen
> model inference consumes energy in proportion to what is sent, so the environmental cost of AI is partly decided by prompt construction before any inference happens

*Dev Season of Code * · problem: large-scale AI relies on energy-intensive infrastructure and prompts are not optimised
