# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** constraint, delivery, technology, water

### [[water-reuse-booster-platform-to-facilitate-reuse-in-eu]] — Water Reuse Booster Platform to facilitate reuse in EU
> nearly 200 billion cubic metres of fresh water go to agriculture and industry that could use reused water, so the constraint on reuse is standards and coordination rather than treatment technology

*Hack to the Future 2020* · problem: water reuse requires stakeholders to agree standards and funding that no one coordinates

### [[drone-soundaware]] — Drone SoundAware
> aerial delivery was promised on the basis of speed and emissions and will be blocked by noise, so the constraint on the future is community tolerance rather than technology

*AWS Lambda Hackathon* · problem: drone operations impose noise on the communities they fly over

### [[tarantulax]] — TarantulaX
> clean water technology exists and reaching people without money or infrastructure is the unsolved part, so the delivery mechanism rather than the purification is the constraint

*GNEC Hackathon 2024 - Compete For UN - Affiliated / NGO Internships and prizes* · problem: low-income people cannot buy clean water and cannot reach purification
