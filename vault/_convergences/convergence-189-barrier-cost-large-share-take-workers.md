**Shared vocabulary** barrier, cost, large, share, take, workers

### [[operation-ghost]] — Operation Ghost
> conventional input excludes people with motor disabilities, sterile-environment workers and anyone whose hands are occupied, so three unrelated populations share one interface barrier

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: keyboard and mouse are unusable across several distinct contexts

### [[easy-eats-obl0kz]] — Easy Eats

### [[pandos-l0b6qs]] — Pandos

*Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon* · problem: music platforms retain a large proportion of artist royalties
