# 10 projects, 10 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** around, billion, captions, costs, deaf, dollars, existing, failure, healthcare, hearing, language, loss, sign, year

### [[signify-hya82t]] — Signify
> captions alone often miss the nuance of sign language, so a Deaf viewer gets the words and not the language

*CS Girlies November Hackathon - Make Learning Cool Again! * · problem: the accessibility standard for video is a transcription into a language many Deaf viewers do not use as their first

### [[spellcam-fpvlhm]] — SpellCam
> to a deaf community of 430 million people globally, at least 5% of the world's population, sign language is not just another language

*XHacks 2021* · problem: the language that a twentieth of humanity depends on has almost no learning tooling

### [[navigator-ai]] — Navigator-AI
> nearly half of healthcare referrals never complete, costing over 150 billion dollars a year, so the loss occurs in the handoff between providers rather than in either provider

*The AI Champion Ship * · problem: referral mismanagement causes incomplete care and enormous cost

### [[mediflow]] — MediFlow
> administrative burden costs American healthcare 265.6 billion dollars a year and closes rural hospitals, so paperwork rather than clinical capacity is what removes care from rural communities

*TreeHacks 2024* · problem: rural physicians burn out on administrative work and over 30 percent of rural hospitals face closure

### [[sign-cast]] — Sign-Cast
> captions serve hearing people who lost the audio, not Deaf people whose first language is a sign language, so the existing accommodation addresses a different need than the one claimed

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: over 70 million Deaf people face barriers to spoken content and captions are an imperfect substitute

### [[pulse-hit94n]] — Pulse
> heart failure costs around 30.7 billion dollars a year with over a quarter of patients readmitted within thirty days, so the expensive period is the one after discharge when nobody is watching

*Disrupt SF Hackathon 2018* · problem: patients deteriorate at home after discharge and are readmitted

### [[signbridgev2]] — SignBridgeV2
> existing sign language tools work in one direction, so a two-way conversation still requires the Deaf person to accommodate the hearing one

*DSH Hacks V1* · problem: tools that bridge deaf and hearing communication are limited and one-directional

### [[nft-bingo]] — NFT Bingo
> around 586.5 billion dollars went to advertising in a year and large companies spend up to 46 percent of revenue on marketing, so promotional spend is enormous and almost none of it leaves anything with the customer

*Chainlink Fall Hackathon 2021* · problem: businesses spend heavily on promotions that produce no durable artifact

### [[asl-buddy]] — ASL Buddy
> about 600,000 people in the United States are deaf or hard of hearing and hearing people learn no sign language, so the communication burden falls entirely on one side by default

*Hack the Northeast* · problem: the hearing population has no accessible path to learning sign

### [[reroute-logistics]] — Reroute
> over 35 billion dollars was spent on rejected freight in a year and a carrier has only two poor options, so the loss is a routing failure for goods that are still good

*Quick Base Virtual Hackathon* · problem: rejected freight leaves carriers with costly options
