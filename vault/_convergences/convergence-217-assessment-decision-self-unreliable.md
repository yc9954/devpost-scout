# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** assessment, decision, self, unreliable

### [[intellidrink]] — IntelliDrink
> drunk driving killed over 36,000 people in a year and the decision is made by a person whose judgement is the thing impaired, so self-assessment is structurally unreliable

*HooHacks 2021* · problem: people decide whether they are fit to drive using impaired judgement

### [[symptom-connect]] — Well Wisher
> around a quarter of health cases could be self-treated and people consult search engines instead of evidence, so the demand for self-assessment exists and the supply is unreliable

*The Postman API Hack* · problem: people research symptoms online with no grounding in medical literature

### [[watermelon-pw8k4u]] — WATermelon
> a purchase decision made by knocking on fruit is folk knowledge with a real signal behind it, so formalising the assessment converts an unreliable tradition into a measurement

*Hack the North 2021* · problem: buyers cannot tell a ripe watermelon from an unripe one
