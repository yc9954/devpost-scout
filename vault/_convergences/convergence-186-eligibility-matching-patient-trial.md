# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** eligibility, matching, patient, trial

### [[curis-rlajbk]] — Curis
> trial matching decides who gets access to an experimental treatment and is performed by whoever happens to remember the trial exists, so eligibility is discovered by chance

*HackUTD X* · problem: identifying eligible cancer patients for clinical trials is essential and manual

### [[clinicalconnect-l8ym0b]] — ClinicalConnect
> trial matching requires reading both the patient record and the eligibility criteria, and nobody is paid to do both, so the match fails between two documents that each exist

*Predictive AI In Healthcare with FHIR®* · problem: patients and trials are not connected despite both being documented

### [[clinicconnect]] — ClinicConnect
> a patient with a long-term condition and a trial recruiting nearby never learn of each other, so the matching failure persists outside emergencies as well as inside them

*Hack the North 2020++* · problem: patients cannot find clinical trials at nearby treatment centres
