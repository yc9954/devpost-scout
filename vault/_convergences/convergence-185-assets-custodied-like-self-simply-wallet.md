# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** assets, custodied, like, self, simply, wallet

### [[keeperc20]] — KeepERC20
> a non-custodial wallet gives ownership and removes every service a bank provided, so features like scheduled or recoverable transfers simply do not exist for self-custodied assets

*Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon* · problem: decentralised wallets have no scheduling or recovery

### [[opto]] — Opto
> hedging is available for assets that already have derivative markets, so anyone exposed to a cost with no market, like gas or blob pricing, simply carries the risk

*Block Magic: A Chainlink Hackathon* · problem: no options exist for the specific costs on-chain participants are actually exposed to

### [[autolimitxswap]] — AutoLimitXSwap
> a limit order requires something watching the market and a wallet watches nothing, so strategies that depend on waiting are impossible for self-custodied users

*Chainlink Spring 2023 Hackathon* · problem: there is no way to set a limit order or a scheduled strategy across chains from a wallet
