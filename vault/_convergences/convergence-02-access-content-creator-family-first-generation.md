# 6 projects, 4 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** access, content, creator, family, first, generation, income, party, platform, relationship, sets, students, support

### [[s-o-a-r]] — SOAR: Scholarships Made Easy
> first-generation low-income students miss awards because searching and applying takes time and knowledge their peers get from family and counsellors, so the award selects on support rather than on need

*Empower Hacks 2.0* · problem: scholarship search and application is a barrier for the students most needing the money

### [[prepair]] — PrePair
> a first-generation student in a technical field lacks mentorship that other students receive from family, so the missing input is industry context rather than academic support

*Funathon* · problem: finding quality mentorship as a first-generation STEM student is difficult

### [[holistic-life-mapper-hlm]] — Holistic Life Mapper (HLM)
> first-generation low-income students face financial instability and career uncertainty simultaneously and are offered separate services for each, so the support is fragmented against a compounded problem

*Empower Hacks 2.0* · problem: FGLI students face interacting financial and career difficulties addressed in isolation

### [[notic3]] — Notic3.
> a content platform owns the relationship between creator and audience, so ownership, access and reward are all decided by a party that is neither

*Cal Hacks 11.0* · problem: creators depend on platforms for distribution and payment

### [[d3-music]] — D3 Music
> a streaming platform pays per stream at a rate it sets and holds the relationship with the listener, so the artist's income is determined by a party with no stake in their career

*NEAR MetaBUILD III Hackathon* · problem: artists earn from listeners through an intermediary that sets the terms

### [[elevyn-unlock-premium-content-with-decentralized-funding]] — Elevyn
> a creator monetising exclusive content depends on a platform that sets the terms and can remove them, so the revenue and the access control are held by a third party

*Cal Hacks 11.0* · problem: creators cannot monetise exclusive content or run fundraisers without an intermediary
