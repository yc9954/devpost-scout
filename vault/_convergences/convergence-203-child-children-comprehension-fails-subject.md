# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** child, children, comprehension, fails, subject

### [[don-t-touch-us-uw2l4x]] — Don't touch us
> protecting children requires teaching them a boundary before anyone violates it, and the subject is one adults avoid raising, so the education fails on adult discomfort rather than on child comprehension

*Hack the Northeast: Beyond* · problem: children are not taught to distinguish safe from unsafe touch

### [[mighty-trivia-a-guessing-game-for-kids]] — Mighty Trivia - a guessing game for kids
> a voice game for children fails on interface complexity before it fails on content, so the design target is the number of things a child must remember to say

*Alexa Skills Challenge: Kids* · problem: existing guessing games had complicated voice interfaces

### [[zeural]] — Zeural
> attention on a difficult subject fails before comprehension does, so interleaving something narratively engaging keeps the session alive long enough to learn

*Student HackPad 2025* · problem: learners get bored studying complex topics for long periods
