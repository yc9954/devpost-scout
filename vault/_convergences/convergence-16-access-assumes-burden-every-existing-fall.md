# 6 projects, 6 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** access, assumes, burden, every, existing, fall, falls, family, loss

### [[perceiv-io]] — PERCEIV/IO
> combined sensory loss defeats every existing accommodation, because sign language assumes sight and transcription assumes hearing, so the people with both losses fall through the gap between two solved problems

*Hack the North 2024* · problem: accommodations are designed per impairment and their intersection is served by neither

### [[farm-sutra]] — Farm Sutra
> the loss is caused by lack of access rather than lack of effort, and every existing tool assumes a smartphone, English and bandwidth the user does not have

*Amazon Nova AI Hackathon* · problem: the intervention is designed for a user profile the affected population does not match

### [[alzi-1s6uej]] — Alzi
> dementia cost the United States an estimated 345 billion dollars in 2023 and most of the burden falls on unpaid family carers, so the economic figure describes work nobody is supporting

*DeveloperWeek 2024 Hackathon* · problem: growing prevalence and cost with little support for patients and caregivers

### [[unstuck-xnemto]] — unstuck.
> helping someone through an interface over the phone is nearly impossible because the helper cannot see the screen, so the support burden falls on family and fails anyway

*ElevenLabs x 16z Worldwide Hackathon* · problem: explaining how to do something on the internet to a relative or friend takes hours

### [[allbank]] — AllBank: Inclusive, Intelligent, Impactful
> banking accessibility remains the barrier while banking technology advances, so the unbanked fall further behind with each improvement that assumes existing access

*HackUTD 2024: Ripple Effect* · problem: the financial system evolves and accessibility for the unbanked does not

### [[carelink-family-health-management-system]] — CareLink: Family Health Management System
> a family manages multiple people's reports, consultations and prescriptions across providers, so the coordination burden falls on the family because the records do not connect

*Student HackPad 2025* · problem: families struggle to manage medical reports and consultations across providers
