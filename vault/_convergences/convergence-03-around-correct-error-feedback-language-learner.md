# 6 projects, 5 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** around, correct, error, feedback, language, learner, learning, objects, practice, reinforces, requires, sign, whether, without

### [[stemlens-ai]] — STEMLENS-AI
> STEM concepts are taught abstractly while every one of them is instantiated in objects around the learner, so the connection missing is from the object to the concept rather than the reverse

*DSH Hacks V1* · problem: learners cannot connect physics, mathematics and engineering to the world around them

### [[inkphony]] — Inkphony
> learning composition requires instruments a learner may not own, so generating instruments from the objects around them removes the material precondition

*Meta Horizon Start Developer Competition* · problem: experimenting with instruments requires owning them

### [[immerrock]] — IMMERROCK
> guitar learning fails on the absence of immediate feedback on whether the note was right, so instruction without listening cannot correct anything

*Meta Horizon Start Developer Competition* · problem: learning guitar alone gives no signal about correctness

### [[arsl-vr-asl-learning]] — ARSL - Augmented Reality Sign Language
> learning a sign requires knowing what the sign is for and the objects around the learner are unlabelled, so vocabulary acquisition can be driven by the environment rather than by a list

*ShellHacks 2024* · problem: sign language learning uses word lists disconnected from the learner's surroundings

### [[copychat]] — CopyChat
> a language learner cannot tell whether their pronunciation is correct, so practice without feedback reinforces the error

*Azure AI Hackathon* · problem: reading a book or joining a class gives no correction on spoken words

### [[asl-bridgify]] — ASL Bridgify
> learning a sign language requires feedback on whether the sign is correct and a learner alone has none, so practice without pose feedback reinforces error

*UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2024* · problem: ASL learning lacks real-time feedback on the learner's signing
