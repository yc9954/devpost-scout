# 2 projects, 2 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** advantage, goes, whoever

### [[airship]] — Airship bot
> flight pricing rewards constant watching, which is exactly what a human cannot do, so the advantage goes to whoever automates attention rather than to whoever searches better

*Disrupt SF Hackathon 2018* · problem: finding and buying a flight at the right price requires continuous monitoring

### [[hobbits-of-polygon]] — Hobbits Of Polygon
> a game with generated content needs randomness nobody can predict or the advantage goes to whoever can, so verifiable randomness is a fairness primitive rather than a feature

*Chainlink Fall Hackathon 2021* · problem: algorithmic map and character generation can be gamed if the randomness is predictable
