# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** blind, building, exists, getting, nobody, plan

### [[bluekai-bluesky-on-a-flip-phone]] — BlueKai - BlueSky on a flip phone!
> the next billion mobile users are not getting smartphones, they are getting $20 flip phones — the platform powers over 200 million devices and nobody is building modern social software for its constraints

*Kiroween* · problem: the platforms that claim to be open are unusable on the hardware most new users will own

### [[fireflai]] — Fireflai
> firefighters enter burning buildings blind — no floor plan, no idea where the fire started or which room someone is trapped in — they find out by feeling through black smoke one second at a time, and the information already exists

*UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2026* · problem: the building's own plans and sensor data exist and do not reach the person inside it

### [[spatialize]] — Spatialize
> blind visitors rehearse a route before entering a building because getting it wrong means a wasted trip, and the data they need exists as a flat plan nobody can ask questions of, which then rots because updating it requires a human filling in a form

*Backblaze Generative Media Hackathon: Build with Genblaze on B2* · problem: accessibility information is published in a format that is neither queryable nor self-maintaining
