# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** already, cash, infrastructure, network, peer, proximity

### [[corona-bluetooth-beacon-tracking]] — corona-bluetooth-beacon-tracking
> the technology for proximity was already deployed for retail marketing, and the same mechanism answers a public health question at a granularity network data cannot

*COVID-19 Global Hackathon 1.0* · problem: available location data is too coarse for the distance at which the risk actually operates

### [[muda-cash-agent-network]] — Muda Cash Agent network
> most crypto trade in Africa already happens in cash through peer traders, so the missing infrastructure is a formalised agent point rather than an exchange

*Celo's Make Crypto Mobile Hackathon* · problem: users convert cash and stablecoin through informal peer-to-peer trades

### [[buddy-bank]] — BuddyBank
> a bank's branch network is the expensive way to deliver cash and the people nearby already have it, so peer-to-peer withdrawal replaces infrastructure with proximity

*Hack to the Future* · problem: ATM overheads and cash access with institutions losing social relevance
