# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

*LA Hacks 2026* · problem: failure in a multi-agent system is distributed and silent, and the debugging tools were built for single traces

### [[truthkeeper]] — TruthKeeper

### [[tracks-criyp3]] — Tracks

*Web5: Building the Decentralized Web* · problem: travel memories were lost with SD cards and had no central home
