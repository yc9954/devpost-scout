# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** against, asset, assets, credit, market, price

### [[lan-pfkngy]] — LAN - Literally Any NFT
> lending against an asset requires a price and illiquid assets have none, so the absence of a valuation method forecloses an entire credit market

*EthCC Hack 2022* · problem: NFTs cannot be priced efficiently which blocks peer-to-peer term loans

### [[satspay]] — SatsPay
> the principle of borrowing against an appreciating asset is established in property and equities and absent for newer assets, so the same holder is forced to sell

*Bitcoin 2025 Official Hackathon* · problem: holders of an appreciating asset must sell it to meet short-term needs

### [[solarzu-dapp]] — Solarzu Dapp
> accessibility and usability are the same problem in a market where the asset price is the entry barrier, so credit is what turns a spectator into a participant

*Polygon BUIDL IT : Summer 2022* · problem: NFT purchase requires full payment upfront
