# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** capacity, constraint, footage, response

### [[real-time-disaster-imagery-annotation]] — Real time disaster imagery annotation and search analytics
> the golden hour is spent looking at imagery nobody has indexed, so the constraint on disaster response is search over the footage rather than the footage itself

*Pinecone Hackathon: Jumpstart Real-World AI Applications* · problem: natural disasters average roughly 60,000 deaths a year and responders cannot find what matters in incoming imagery

### [[arcticcare-earth-guardian]] — ArcticCare - Earth Guardian
> environmental damage goes unnoticed because nobody is present where it happens, so the observation capacity is the constraint rather than the response

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: damage to vulnerable ecosystems occurs without being seen or recorded

### [[fire-detaction-in-videos-using-cnn-lstm]] — violence Detection in videos using CNN + LSTM
> violence in video is visible to a camera and reviewed by a person after the fact, so the detection capacity is the constraint on response rather than the footage

*2020 Facebook Developer Circles Community Challenge* · problem: public safety cameras record violence and rely on human review
