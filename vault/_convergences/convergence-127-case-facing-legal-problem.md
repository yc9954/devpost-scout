# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** case, facing, legal, problem

### [[justicemap-2q6vgh]] — JusticeMap
> over 80% of low-income Americans facing a legal problem never get help, not because their case is invalid but because legal guidance is expensive, intimidating and inaccessible

*MEGA Hackathon 2026* · problem: the law that protects a tenant exists, applies to them, and is unreachable for the reasons that made them vulnerable in the first place

### [[justicebot]] — JusticeBot
> most people facing a legal problem do seek advice, and what stops them is not willingness but the cost and opacity of the procedure itself

*2018 Developer Circles Community Challenge* · problem: the barrier is procedural literacy rather than absence of demand

### [[fintech-insurance-claims-analysis]] — Claims ML based Subrogation Recovery Prediction
> an economic downturn increases uninsured claimants and subrogation recovery is decided case by case, so the recovery is a prediction problem over claims data rather than a legal one

*AWS Data Exchange Challenge* · problem: insurers cannot predict which claims allow subrogation recovery
