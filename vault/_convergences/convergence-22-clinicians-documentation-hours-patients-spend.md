# 4 projects, 4 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** clinicians, documentation, hours, patients, spend

### [[wingnote]] — WingNote
> nurses spend over four hours per shift on documentation, and most patients do not understand their own medical notes

*TreeHacks 2025* · problem: the same writing consumes the clinician's time and fails to inform the patient it is about

### [[echo-health]] — Echo Health
> clinicians spend over two hours a day on documentation, so the paperwork is the largest single reduction available in clinical time and it is produced by conversation that is already happening

*NexHacks* · problem: nurses transcribe conversations manually and doctors review mountains of notes

### [[evident-ai]] — Evident AI
> officers spend hours transcribing footage and writing reports, so the constraint on policing time is documentation rather than incidents, and the vendors charge for exactly that

*Hack the North 2025* · problem: evidence processing is the bottleneck and takes officers off the street

### [[ai-tele-triage-system-d40u9x]] — AI Tele-Triage System
> clinicians spend up to half their time on documentation while patients wait for triage, so the two problems are the same time budget viewed from either end

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: clinician burnout from documentation and slow triage for large patient volumes
