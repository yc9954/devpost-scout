# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** discovery, finding, limited, market, personal, specific

### [[lern]] — Lern
> finding a high-quality course on a specific topic costs more time than the topic warrants, so discovery rather than instruction is the expensive part of continuous learning

*Atlas Madness* · problem: locating good material on a narrow subject is slow

### [[latinconnect]] — Latinnect
> small community vendors sell through personal networks and general marketplaces do not serve them, so the market is limited by the absence of a venue that matches how they trade

*ShellHacks 2024* · problem: Latine vendors have no platform connecting them to customers and each other

### [[pink-icing-sew-in-style]] — Pink Icing: Sew In Style
> custom clothing demand exists and finding a tailor for a specific design runs on personal recommendation, so the market is limited by discovery

*Hack It Out* · problem: customers wanting custom attire cannot find boutiques matching their design
