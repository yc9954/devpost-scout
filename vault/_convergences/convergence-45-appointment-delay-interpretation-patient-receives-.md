# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** appointment, delay, interpretation, patient, receives, report

### [[medlens-ai]] — MedLens Ai
> a patient receives a result before they can receive an explanation, so the interval between the report and the appointment is spent in uninformed fear

*ERNIE AI Developer Challenge* · problem: medical reports arrive full of abbreviations and metrics the patient cannot read

### [[auralysis]] — Auralysis
> a scan requires expert review that is not always available and the patient cannot understand the result, so the delay and the incomprehension are both failures of the interpretation step

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: medical imaging is slow to interpret and unclear to patients

### [[diagnocenter]] — Diagnocentre
> a patient receives a report written for a clinician and waits for an appointment to learn what it says, so the delay is in interpretation rather than in testing

*Atlas Madness* · problem: quick interpretation of medical reports and images is unavailable between visits
