# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** amount, cost, creates, splitting

### [[bethechange-tucfl5]] — Be The Change
> the barrier to giving is the deliberation a lump sum demands, so splitting the amount removes the research rather than the cost

*Hack to the Future 2020* · problem: deciding where to donate requires research most people never finish

### [[trello-board-flow]] — Card mirror (by Placker)
> splitting work into pieces creates the coordination cost that the split was supposed to remove, so the same item has to exist in several places without diverging

*Codegeist 2020* · problem: complex work is split across boards and the pieces drift out of sync

### [[solshare-cmxous]] — Solshare
> a shared purchase creates a debt nobody records, so the cost of splitting is social rather than financial and the amount usually goes uncollected

*Hack the North 2025* · problem: receipts get lost, people forget to pay and balances are untracked
