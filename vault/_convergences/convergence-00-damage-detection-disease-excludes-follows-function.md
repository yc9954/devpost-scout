# 6 projects, 6 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** damage, detection, disease, excludes, follows, function, late, loss, navigation, objective, optimise, optimising, route, routing, safety, together, visible

### [[saferoute-pune-women-s-safety-route-mapping-system]] — SafeRoute Pune – Women's Safety Route Mapping System
> navigation optimises for time and the person walking is optimising for safety, so the routing objective is wrong for a large fraction of pedestrians

*Google Maps Platform Awards* · problem: women navigating unfamiliar streets face harassment and poor lighting with no route information

### [[mirai-fusion-model-for-alzheimer-s]] — MirAI 未来: Protecting Futures with Intelligence
> Alzheimer's progresses invisibly until significant damage is done, so any detection based on symptoms is by definition late and imaging plus cognition must be read together

*AI 4 Alzheimer's* · problem: the disease remains hidden until substantial neurological damage has occurred

### [[accessbot-ngpfja]] — AccessBot
> navigation systems optimise for time and distance and never for whether the route is traversable by this person, so the objective function excludes the users who most need routing

*Dev Season of Code * · problem: navigation ignores accessibility for blind and deaf users

### [[safewalk-t7f1ze]] — SafeWalk
> navigation apps optimise speed and efficiency and the pedestrian in an unfamiliar area is optimising safety, so the objective function excludes the concern that motivated the route

*AmpliCode Hackathon 2025* · problem: navigating unfamiliar neighbourhoods with only speed-optimised routing

### [[green-health-monitor-a-plant-disease-detection-system]] — "Green Health Monitor ☘️- A Plant Disease Detection System "
> a plant disease is acted on after visible damage spreads, so the loss follows from late detection rather than from the disease

*LaunchHacks III* · problem: crops die because disease is noticed too late

### [[agroguard-1ylp8j]] — AGROGUARD
> crop disease is detected after visible damage and expert advice is not always available, so loss follows from late detection and absent expertise together

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: farmers detect disease late and cannot always reach an expert
