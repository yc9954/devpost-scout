# 6 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** connectivity, destroys, disaster, documents, fails, first, happens, infrastructure, insurance, internet, lost, moment, needed

### [[disaster-brain]] — Disaster Brain
> what happens when a field medic needs AI help and there is no internet — the answer is nothing, because every AI tool requires a cloud connection and connectivity is the first thing a disaster takes

*DSH Hacks V1* · problem: the tools are most needed exactly where their dependency has failed

### [[blackout-4ergbc]] — Blackout
> what happens when AI is needed most and the internet is unavailable — during disasters, infrastructure failures, remote travel and emergencies, connectivity is exactly what is gone

*DSH Hacks V1* · problem: the capability is designed for the conditions in which it is least needed

### [[resilink]] — Resililink
> the infrastructure people need most during a disaster is the infrastructure the disaster destroys first, so any plan routed through the internet fails at the moment it is needed

*Cal Hacks 11.0* · problem: hurricane communication outages cut people off from resources

### [[jansaathi-mw2ctg]] — JanSaathi
> welfare entitlements are lost at the moment of disaster because they are proven by documents that the disaster destroys, so the proof mechanism fails under the conditions it exists for

*DSH Hacks V1* · problem: citizens lose access to benefits after losing documents, with no connectivity and low digital literacy

### [[autoclaim-q8who1]] — CLaiM
> an insurance claim after a disaster requires documentation produced by a person who has just lost their house, so the process demands most from the claimant at their least capable moment

*Cal Hacks 11.0* · problem: filing a home insurance claim after a hurricane is a manual burden on the victim

### [[equilex]] — EquiLex
> a disaster is followed by insurance and legal documents the affected people cannot interpret, so the recovery is gated on comprehension at the worst possible moment

*Tableau DataDev 2024-2025 Hackathon * · problem: communities affected by wildfires struggle to read their legal and insurance documents
