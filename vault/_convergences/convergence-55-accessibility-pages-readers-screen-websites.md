# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** accessibility, pages, readers, screen, websites

### [[visionos-5euxo7]] — VisionOS
> screen readers have not changed in fifteen years — they read pages linearly and break on anything inaccessible

*TreeHacks 2026* · problem: the assistive technology for the profession that builds all technology has itself been frozen for a decade and a half

### [[based_smiles]] — Better Web
> 15% of the global population lives with a disability and 70% of websites fail basic accessibility standards — students with dyslexia avoid online learning, professionals with ADHD cannot read long pages

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: accessibility is the author's obligation and the author overwhelmingly does not meet it, so the burden lands on the reader

### [[wiiwork-8u1x62]] — WiiWork
> websites were built for human eyes and agents parse them anyway, so the accessibility pattern that helped screen readers is the precedent for making pages machine-usable

*TreeHacks 2025* · problem: AI agents interact with sites that were never designed for them
