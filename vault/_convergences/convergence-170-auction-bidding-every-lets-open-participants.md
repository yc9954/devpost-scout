# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** auction, bidding, every, lets, open, participants, price, react, reflects, reveals, valuations

### [[acai-gup0bm]] — Acai
> an open mempool lets anyone replace a pending transaction, so a first-come mechanism becomes a fee auction and the design intent is defeated by the transport layer

*Bitcoin 2025 Official Hackathon* · problem: minting systems suffer frontrunning and replace-by-fee attacks in the public mempool

### [[bestbid]] — BestBid
> an open auction reveals every bid and lets participants react to each other, so the price reflects the bidding dynamics rather than the valuations

*Polygon BUIDL IT : Summer 2022* · problem: NFT auctions are fully transparent and gameable

### [[dauction]] — Dauction
> an open auction reveals every bid and lets participants react, so the price reflects the bidding rather than the valuations and a real sale suffers for it

*Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon* · problem: a friend selling a laptop to several interested buyers had no fair mechanism
