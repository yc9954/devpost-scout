# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** against, asset, borrowing, holder, sell

### [[satspay]] — SatsPay
> the principle of borrowing against an appreciating asset is established in property and equities and absent for newer assets, so the same holder is forced to sell

*Bitcoin 2025 Official Hackathon* · problem: holders of an appreciating asset must sell it to meet short-term needs

### [[nifty-wheel]] — Nifty Wheel
> a token holder with an illiquid asset has no way to sell it fairly, so verified randomness turns an unsellable item into a distributable one

*Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon* · problem: users cannot sell tokens on secondary markets without a buyer for that exact item

### [[monnex-tm-accessing-finance-the-confident-way-tm]] — Monnex(TM) - Accessing Finance the Confident way(TM)
> accessing liquidity against an asset means selling or borrowing at terms set by an institution, so the holder pays either tax or interest to use value they already own

*Hack to the Future 4* · problem: traditional routes to liquidity require personal loans, equity loans, cards or retirement withdrawals
