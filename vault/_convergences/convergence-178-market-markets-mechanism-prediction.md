# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** market, markets, mechanism, prediction

### [[nexhacks-now93q]] — PolyYield
> fear of losing capital rather than lack of interest keeps people out of prediction markets, so removing the loss rather than explaining the mechanism is what onboards them

*NexHacks* · problem: new Polymarket users hesitate because they can lose money

### [[tpmarket-prediction-market-on-tron]] — tpMarket - Prediction Market on Tron
> public bets between individuals already happen on social media with no settlement mechanism, so the demand for prediction markets is demonstrated before any market exists

*TRON Grand Hackathon 2022* · problem: high-profile wagers are made on Twitter and settled by honour

### [[orbit-prediction-marketplace]] — Orbit - Prediction Marketplace
> prediction markets require market knowledge and technical understanding, so the mechanism that aggregates dispersed knowledge excludes most of the people who hold it

*Electroneum Hackathon 2025* · problem: prediction markets are complex and intimidating
