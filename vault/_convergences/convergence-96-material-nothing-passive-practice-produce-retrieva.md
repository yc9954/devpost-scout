# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** material, nothing, passive, practice, produce, retrieval, studying

### [[clarita]] — ClariTA
> retention is produced by retrieval practice rather than by review, so a study tool that re-presents material is optimising the wrong activity

*ShellHacks 2025* · problem: students study by rereading and it does not produce academic results

### [[swot-up]] — Swot up!
> highlighting and rereading feel like studying and produce nothing, so the conversion from passive material to retrieval practice is the whole value

*Nosu AI Hackathon $11,300+ in prizes* · problem: hours of highlighting and rereading leave the student unprepared

### [[studiolingo]] — Studylingo
> passive browsing and studying produce nothing retained, so the intervention is converting the time into active recall rather than adding a timer

*Hack the North 2024* · problem: staying focused while studying or browsing is hard and passive
