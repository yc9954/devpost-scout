# 6 projects, 6 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** answers, delivery, education, exactly, need, needs, returns, search, whether

### [[fd-7g0sbo]] — Zen
> a search engine returns a list and the person who most needs the benefit cannot parse the rules, does not speak the language well, and has no way to know whether help will actually arrive, so pointing is not helping

*USAII® Global AI Hackathon 2026* · problem: eligibility systems assume the applicant can interpret, apply and follow up alone

### [[washedmcp]] — WashedMCP
> semantic code search returns an isolated snippet, so the assistant answers with a fragment while the developer needs the call graph around it

*NexHacks* · problem: search returns one snippet and requires several follow-up searches to find callers and callees

### [[edspark]] — EdSpark
> special education depends on face-to-face interaction with a paraeducator and online delivery removes exactly that, so remote learning fails hardest for the students with the most support need

*AngelHacks 2.0* · problem: special education students lost the in-person interaction they depend on

### [[insight-ai]] — InSightAI
> innovation happens where deep curiosity meets room to explore, and search returns answers rather than territory, so the tool shapes whether curiosity narrows or widens

*HackMIT 2023* · problem: following a curiosity requires an exploration space that search does not provide

### [[mora-bku34d]] — MORA
> a pregnant person is expected to seek out education actively and the ones who do not are the ones who most need it, so passive delivery reaches a different population than passive availability

*Garuda Hacks 2020* · problem: a healthy pregnancy depends on information the person must go and find

### [[athenshub]] — AthensHub
> education is treated as a universal right and delivered through infrastructure that is not universal, so the principle and the delivery diverge exactly where the need is greatest

*HackSwift 2024* · problem: quality education is inaccessible to underserved communities
