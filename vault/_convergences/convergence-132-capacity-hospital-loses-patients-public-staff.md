# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** capacity, hospital, loses, patients, public, staff

### [[root4]] — AfriGov Sentinel
> a generator fails at a hospital in Lomé and ICU patients are on life support — the governance problem is the gap between when a public service fails and when someone with the power to fix it finds out

*Elasticsearch Agent Builder Hackathon* · problem: the failure is known immediately to the people affected and reaches the responsible official through no channel at all

### [[covid-frontline-backtracer]] — Frontline Covid Backtracer
> a hospital losing staff to infection loses capacity faster than it gains patients, so tracing backwards from one case protects the workforce rather than the public

*Pandemic Response Hackathon* · problem: frontline hospitals faced rising patients while losing staff to exposure

### [[winnie-easy-ordering-in-restaurants]] — Winnie - Easy ordering in restaurants
> a restaurant loses custom to wait-staff shortages rather than to the kitchen, so the constraint on service is order-taking capacity

*Disrupt SF Hackathon 2018* · problem: restaurants cannot meet demand because of wait staff limitations
