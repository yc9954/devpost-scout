# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** data, enterprise, holds, keep

### [[complianceguard]] — ComplianceGuard
> enterprise AI adoption is accelerating faster than compliance teams can keep up, and in 2025 alone GDPR fines exceeded $4 billion — employees share customer data and patient records with tools nobody approved

*Airia AI Agents Hackathon* · problem: the rate of tool adoption exceeded the rate of review, so the exposure is created by ordinary work

### [[fitops]] — FitOps
> a wearable company holds the user's own physiological history and can stop providing it, so the person generating the data is the party least able to keep it

*Elastic Weekend with Code for Cause* · problem: fitness data is owned by the vendor who can withdraw access

### [[data-uploader]] — Data Uploader
> an enterprise system holds the data and cannot show the flow, so people keep both systems and reconcile them by exporting and re-entering

*monday Apps Challenge: Dream it, build it* · problem: an ERP manages the work and presents job flows poorly while the visualisation tool has no import path
