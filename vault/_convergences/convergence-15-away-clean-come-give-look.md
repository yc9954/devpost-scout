# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** away, clean, come, give, look

### [[codetwin]] — CodeTwin
> you give a coding agent a task, look away for a minute, and come back to work you now have to clean up — the tool is impressive and the cost lands on you afterwards

*DSH Hacks V1* · problem: agent autonomy is granted at task level and revoked only after the damage

### [[robo-jab]] — Robo Jab
> most reference motions for humanoids come from clean lab data, which does not look like how a person actually moves

*UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2026* · problem: the training distribution for embodied motion is collected in the one setting that excludes ordinary movement

### [[nowaste-ai]] — NoWaste.ai
> you look in the fridge, see a piece of food, feel it, look it up, give it the sniff test, and throw it away anyway

*TreeHacks 2024* · problem: the edibility judgement has no reliable input, so uncertainty resolves as disposal every time
