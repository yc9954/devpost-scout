# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** missing, payment, primitive, transfer

### [[transfer-safe]] — Transfer Safe
> an irreversible payment to a stranger makes every deposit a leap of faith, so the missing primitive is a transfer the sender can still recall

*Polygon BUIDL IT : Summer 2022* · problem: paying a rental deposit in advance to someone who may not exist

### [[web3-scheduler]] — Web3 Scheduler
> a transfer executes when it is submitted and a gift is meaningful when it arrives on a date, so timing is a missing primitive rather than a convenience

*TRON Grand Hackathon - Season 3* · problem: there is no way to schedule a token transfer for a future moment

### [[flowpay]] — FlowPay
> payment apps succeeded by making a transfer a social act, so the missing element in on-chain payment is the conversation around it rather than the settlement

*Chainlink Spring 2023 Hackathon* · problem: on-chain payments have no social layer
