# 2 projects, 2 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** disease, reach, smallholders

### [[farmer-force]] — Farmer Force
> the tools to catch crop disease and drought early — drones, lab tests, agronomists on retainer — are out of reach for the co-ops and smallholders who grow most of the food

*Slack Agent Builder Challenge* · problem: early detection is a purchased service, so it reaches the farms least likely to fail

### [[cropguard-ai-tvc9fp]] — CropGuard AI
> smallholders lose up to 40 percent of their crops to preventable disease because expert diagnosis does not reach them, so an offline phone-based classifier substitutes for an absent extension service

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: expert agricultural advice is unavailable in many developing regions and internet is unreliable
