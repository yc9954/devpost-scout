# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** market, marketing, performance, selects, verify

### [[ai-x-artisans]] — AI-X-Artisans
> a craftsperson competes in a digital market on product photography, SEO and copy rather than on craft, so the market selects for marketing skill rather than for skill

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: small-scale artisans make exceptional work and cannot survive a digital-first economy

### [[x-di5yjk]] — Traderoid
> trading bots are sold on claimed returns that nobody can verify, so the market selects for marketing rather than for performance

*Constellation: A Chainlink Hackathon* · problem: promises of revolutionary trading bots repeatedly turn out to be unfounded

### [[trupr]] — TruPr
> an advertising agreement is performed by one party and paid by the other with no way to verify the performance, so marketing spend is settled on trust between strangers

*Chainlink Fall Hackathon 2021* · problem: brand mention agreements depend on both sides honouring the deal
