# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** party, platforms, runs, support, third, without

### [[hbp100-hummingbird-privacy-firewall]] — hbp100: Hummingbird Privacy Firewall
> users paste sensitive information into prompts without realising the data goes to a third-party API

*DSH Hacks V1* · problem: the privacy decision is made unconsciously, inside a text box, at the moment of pasting

### [[fibril]] — Fibril
> a creator's income runs through platforms that can suspend them without appeal, so the support relationship is revocable by a third party that is not part of it

*Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon* · problem: centralised creator support platforms have deplatformed creators

### [[dtipify]] — Dtipify
> creator support runs through platforms that decide the rate and the eligibility, so appreciation between two people is mediated by a third with different interests

*Fantom Hackathon Q2 2023* · problem: supporting a creator directly is not possible without a platform in between
