# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** another, asset, bridge, chain

### [[buckle]] — Buckle
> I had 10 DAI on one chain and needed it on another, and I did not want to use a bridge because the fees were high and I was scared of losing it — so a friend and I just swapped directly

*Block Magic: A Chainlink Hackathon* · problem: moving a small amount between chains costs more than the amount and carries risk the holder cannot assess

### [[meson-to-inter-chain-stablecoin-transfer-protocol-for-web3]] — Meson-To : Inter-chain stablecoin transfer protocol for web3
> an application accepting payment on one chain excludes users holding the same asset on another, so the exposure of a product is limited by a chain choice made by the developer

*TRON Grand Hackathon - Season 3* · problem: applications accept a stablecoin on one chain while users hold it across many

### [[bitstakenet]] — BitStakeNet
> moving an asset to another chain to earn on it means trusting the bridge more than the asset, so the risk of participating exceeds the risk of the position

*Starknet Re{Solve} Hackathon* · problem: bringing bitcoin into another chain and back safely for deposits and staking
