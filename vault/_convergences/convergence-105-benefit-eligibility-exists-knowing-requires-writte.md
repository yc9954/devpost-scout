# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** benefit, eligibility, exists, knowing, requires, written

### [[compass-hunt9r]] — Compass
> the help exists and it is split across agencies, buried in eligibility rules, and written for people who are not already panicking, which is the only state in which anyone reads it

*USAII® Global AI Hackathon 2026* · problem: assistance is designed to be discovered by a calm, literate, unhurried applicant

### [[covid-19-benefits-tool]] — COVID-19 California Benefits Finder
> a benefit that requires knowing it exists is unavailable to exactly the people the eligibility change was written for

*COVID-19 Global Hackathon 1.0* · problem: eligibility expanded faster than anyone's ability to learn they now qualify

### [[va-rating-assistant]] — VA Rating Assistant - Lambda
> a veteran's entitlement is buried in their own medical records and the claim requires knowing which conditions to name, so the benefit is gated on the ability to read one's own file

*AWS Lambda Hackathon* · problem: veterans are frustrated by a disability claims process that requires identifying claimable conditions
