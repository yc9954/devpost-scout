# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** describe, patients, record, symptoms

### [[health-nfedwy]] — Emi
> medical booking assumes patients can accurately remember and describe symptoms days after they occurred, which creates a diagnostic blind spot

*NexHacks* · problem: the clinical input is a recollection of an event that happened before the appointment was even made

### [[vaidyasaarathi]] — VaidyaSaarathi
> in a country with twenty-two official languages, patients cannot describe symptoms in the language the record is kept in, and translation consumes up to half of clinical staff time

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: the consultation's information loss happens at the language boundary and is charged to staff capacity

### [[mediconnect-mwh96l]] — MediConnect
> doctors are overwhelmed, patients wait and information is lost in outdated systems, so three symptoms share one cause in the record infrastructure

*Nosu AI Hackathon $11,300+ in prizes* · problem: healthcare suffers simultaneously from clinician overload, waiting and lost information
