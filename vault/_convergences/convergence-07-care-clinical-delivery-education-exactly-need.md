# 6 projects, 6 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** care, clinical, delivery, education, exactly, need, patient, skill

### [[carechain-decentralized-offline-first-patient-management]] — CareChain2.0: Decentralized Offline-First Patient Management
> a patient record that requires connectivity is unavailable exactly where care is hardest to deliver, so continuity of care is gated on infrastructure rather than on clinical skill

*FusionHacks 2* · problem: frontline health workers in rural areas cannot reliably access or update patient data

### [[mediglot-ai]] — MediGlot.ai
> a patient who cannot describe symptoms in the clinician's language receives worse care regardless of the clinician's skill, so language is a clinical variable

*UC Berkeley AI Hackathon* · problem: a family member's ear infection went untreated across a language and access barrier

### [[edspark]] — EdSpark
> special education depends on face-to-face interaction with a paraeducator and online delivery removes exactly that, so remote learning fails hardest for the students with the most support need

*AngelHacks 2.0* · problem: special education students lost the in-person interaction they depend on

### [[mora-bku34d]] — MORA
> a pregnant person is expected to seek out education actively and the ones who do not are the ones who most need it, so passive delivery reaches a different population than passive availability

*Garuda Hacks 2020* · problem: a healthy pregnancy depends on information the person must go and find

### [[medishift-align]] — MediShift-Align
> clinical staffing conflicts are resolved by whoever is doing the rota that week, so patient care capacity is set by a scheduling process nobody owns

*Hack for Humanity | 2025* · problem: hospitals and clinics face scheduling conflicts and misallocated resources

### [[athenshub]] — AthensHub
> education is treated as a universal right and delivered through infrastructure that is not universal, so the principle and the delivery diverge exactly where the need is greatest

*HackSwift 2024* · problem: quality education is inaccessible to underserved communities
