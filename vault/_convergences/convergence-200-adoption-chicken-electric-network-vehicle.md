# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** adoption, chicken, electric, network, vehicle

### [[evm-jty6dl]] — EVm
> electric vehicle adoption is limited by charger availability and chargers are built by companies that wait for demand, so the chicken-and-egg is a coordination failure a network could resolve

*Hack the North 2022* · problem: charging infrastructure lags adoption and each deters the other

### [[chargeway-ev-charging-app]] — ChargeWay-EV Charging App
> range anxiety is a planning problem rather than a battery problem, so the constraint on electric adoption is confidence in the route rather than the vehicle

*Tri Valley Summer Long Hackathon* · problem: EV drivers cannot plan around charger availability

### [[ln-celo-bridge]] — LN - CELO Bridge
> every new network faces a chicken-and-egg onboarding problem, so adoption is blocked by the cost of getting people onto the network rather than by the applications

*Celo's Make Crypto Mobile Hackathon* · problem: onboarding people to a new network is hard and often requires identity checks
