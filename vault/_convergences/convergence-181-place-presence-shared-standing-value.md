# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** place, presence, shared, standing, value

### [[wooorld]] — Wooorld
> shared presence in a place is different from each person looking at the same place, so the value of virtual exploration is standing beside someone rather than seeing the view

*Google Maps Platform Awards* · problem: exploring the world together is impossible for people on opposite sides of it

### [[livekrowd]] — LiveKrowd
> a live cultural performance carries its value through presence, so streaming it to a place that cannot host one is a distribution problem rather than a broadcast one

*Amazon Raise-up Buildathon* · problem: hospitals could not host performances for staff and patients during the pandemic

### [[craven]] — Craven
> a shared space with no record of who took what turns a small loss into a standing grievance, so the value of monitoring is the accountability rather than the prevention

*Hack the North 2024* · problem: roommates take food from designated cupboards and nobody can say who
