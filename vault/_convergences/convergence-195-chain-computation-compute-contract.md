**Shared vocabulary** chain, computation, compute, contract

*Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon* · problem: technical indicators cannot be calculated by contracts directly

### [[functionx]] — Cloudbeam
> a contract cannot compute and cannot pay for computation, so any application needing real work is pushed off-chain onto infrastructure the user must trust

*Chainlink Spring 2023 Hackathon* · problem: bringing computation and external data on chain is expensive and awkward

### [[niftymatic]] — NiftyMatic
> generation is computationally heavy and a contract cannot compute, so image generation on chain requires offloading rather than a smaller model

*Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon* · problem: creating generated assets on chain requires computation contracts cannot perform
