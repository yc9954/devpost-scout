# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** buyer, cost, environmental, purchase

### [[fashion-speedometer]] — Fashion Speedometer
> the environmental cost of a garment is invisible at the only moment the buyer can act on it, so intent never reaches behaviour

*HooHacks 2021* · problem: fast fashion externalizes its cost onto developing countries and the shopper sees only a price

### [[save-the-orangutans]] — Save the Orangutans!
> food's environmental cost is determined at purchase and communicated nowhere, so the buyer's only lever is pulled blind

*The Postman API Hack* · problem: food production drives emissions, water use and habitat loss with no information at the shelf

### [[greencart-j9kio3]] — GreenCart
> every purchase carries an environmental cost the buyer contributes to without noticing, so the harm is produced by inattention rather than by indifference

*Hack for Humanity | 2025* · problem: shoppers contribute to environmental damage with each purchase unknowingly
