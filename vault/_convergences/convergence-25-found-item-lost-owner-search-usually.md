# 4 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** found, item, lost, owner, search, usually

### [[sherlostholmes]] — SherLostHolmes
> a lost and found needs proof of ownership more than it needs matching, so the hard problem is verifying the claimant rather than finding the item

*ConUHacks X* · problem: items accumulate unclaimed and anyone can claim anything

### [[i-lost-it]] — I LOST IT
> a lost and found holds the item and the owner has no way to describe it into the system, so recovery fails on search rather than on custody

*ConUHacks X* · problem: campuses and gyms accumulate unclaimed items while owners cannot search for them

### [[find-earn]] — Find&Earn
> a lost item is usually found by someone with no way to identify the owner, so the failure is in connecting two willing parties rather than in either of them

*Nosu AI Hackathon $11,300+ in prizes* · problem: recovering a lost belonging is difficult even when someone has found it

### [[lost-and-found-iby5qh]] — Lost and Found
> a lost item is usually within a known building and the search has no index, so the item is present and unfindable

*Hack for Humanity | 2025* · problem: losing something in a large building means retracing steps with no system
