# 3 projects, 2 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** collects, data, measurement, online, physical, store

### [[rauta-naytto]] — Rauta Näyttö
> an online store knows what a customer looked at and the physical store knows nothing, so the most valuable behavioural data is discarded at the door

*Junction 2016* · problem: e-commerce and in-store operate as disconnected systems

### [[cashmesh]] — CashMesh
> a physical store collects no data about how people move through it, so the space is optimised by intuition while the online equivalent is optimised by measurement

*Junction 2016* · problem: stores have no analytics on customer movement or space use

### [[opex]] — OPEX
> staying open during a pandemic depends on proximity data that no institution collects, so operating decisions are made without the measurement they require

*Hack to the Future 2020* · problem: financial institutions needed to maintain distancing and contact tracing to keep operating
