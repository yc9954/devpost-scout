# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** chatbots, data, deployed, security, sensitive, threats

### [[seguri-a-digital-hand-to-hold-dkjo6w]] — Seguri - Digital Hand to Hold
> most AI security models are trained on Western data and English syntax, so they fail to detect the nuanced localised threats aimed at everyone else

*DSH Hacks V1* · problem: the protection available is calibrated to one language and culture, which leaves the most targeted populations unprotected

### [[test-xfts7e]] — NOVA: The Prompt Pattern Matching
> generative AI is deployed everywhere, powering chatbots, automating workflows and processing sensitive data, and security teams have no visibility into the threats against it

*SANS AI Cybersecurity Hackathon* · problem: a new attack surface was deployed at scale before any detection tooling existed for it

### [[sehat-guftagu]] — CHAPAL
> companies deployed customer-facing chatbots that could hallucinate medical dosages and leak sensitive data, and the safety infrastructure to catch that was never built alongside

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: the deployment velocity of models outran the monitoring designed for them
