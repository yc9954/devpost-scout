# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** alert, happening, requires, tells

### [[fire-tracker-d9uokc]] — Anchor
> an emergency alert tells people something is happening and not why it requires them to act, so the warning is received and discounted

*UNIHACK 2026, funded by the European Union* · problem: a family lost their house to a natural hazard and warnings did not produce action

### [[procsee]] — PROCSee
> detection tools wait and alert; they never ask what is actually happening, because asking requires holding an investigation open across time

*Gemini 3 Hackathon* · problem: security tooling reacts to events and cannot sustain a line of inquiry

### [[civicconnect-as4chj]] — CivicConnect
> an alert system tells residents what is happening and gives them no way to report, so the communication is one-directional in exactly the direction with least information

*Hacktivism II* · problem: an emergency alert system notified students and accepted nothing back
