# 6 projects, 4 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** access, content, creator, language, model, party, platform, relationship, sets, vision

### [[paddleocr_vl_receipt-a-sft-model-extract-info-from-receipt]] — PaddleOCR_VL_Receipt - A SFT Model Extract Info From Receipt
> an OCR model that is also a vision-language model can be fine-tuned as a VLM rather than as an OCR system, which is a different set of methods entirely

*ERNIE AI Developer Challenge* · problem: a model's capabilities are bounded by the training paradigm applied to it rather than by the model

### [[notic3]] — Notic3.
> a content platform owns the relationship between creator and audience, so ownership, access and reward are all decided by a party that is neither

*Cal Hacks 11.0* · problem: creators depend on platforms for distribution and payment

### [[d3-music]] — D3 Music
> a streaming platform pays per stream at a rate it sets and holds the relationship with the listener, so the artist's income is determined by a party with no stake in their career

*NEAR MetaBUILD III Hackathon* · problem: artists earn from listeners through an intermediary that sets the terms

### [[elevyn-unlock-premium-content-with-decentralized-funding]] — Elevyn
> a creator monetising exclusive content depends on a platform that sets the terms and can remove them, so the revenue and the access control are held by a third party

*Cal Hacks 11.0* · problem: creators cannot monetise exclusive content or run fundraisers without an intermediary

### [[vl-adk]] — VL-ADK
> embodied intelligence is emerging and a full vision-language-action model is heavy, so a fleet of coordinated software agents can approximate one at lower cost

*ShellHacks 2025* · problem: vision-language-action models are heavy to build and run

### [[tachiwin-indigenous-languages-ocr]] — Tachiwin Indigenous Languages OCR
> a vision-language OCR model can be finetuned to read the 68 indigenous languages of Mexico

*ERNIE AI Developer Challenge* · problem: indigenous Mexican languages lack OCR and AI support for their diverse glyph repertoires
