# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** control, data, holds, privacy, record, together

### [[moneysavvy]] — MoneySavvy
> a personal finance tool holds the most sensitive record a person has and is operated by a company, so control of the data and control of the money are separated

*Web5: Building the Decentralized Web* · problem: financial management tools are centralised and hold user data

### [[streamyou]] — StreamYou
> a creator's work lives on infrastructure that owns the distribution and the record, so privacy, security and ownership fail together rather than separately

*dAppAthon * · problem: creators do not control their own content or the data around it

### [[metacare]] — MetaCare
> health systems face inefficiency, privacy problems and lack of control over data together, so the failures share a root in who holds the record

*Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon* · problem: health data is inefficiently processed and the patient has no control
