# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** access, availability, classroom, depends, human

### [[bridge-4euwyt]] — Bridge
> there are an estimated 160 million deafblind people and a deafblind student's access to classroom content depends almost entirely on the availability of a human interpreter

*LA Hacks 2026* · problem: access to education is gated on the presence of one scarce person

### [[braillevision-zlumrq]] — BrailleVision
> a visually impaired student cannot read what is on the board and the accommodation is a person reading it aloud, so classroom access depends on a human relay

*LaunchHacks III* · problem: a visually impaired classmate could not read from the board

### [[aifluence]] — AIFluence
> influencer marketing is expensive and unschedulable because it depends on individual people, so the constraint on the channel is human availability rather than audience

*SpurHacks* · problem: human influencers have inconsistent availability, high cost and limited scalability
