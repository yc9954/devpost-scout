# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** creator's, economics, independent, industry, intermediary, music, rights

### [[nsub-content-subscription-is-near]] — NSub - Content subscription is NEAR
> a creator's subscription revenue passes through a payment company that sets the terms, so the economics of independent publishing are decided by an intermediary

*NEAR MetaBUILD III Hackathon* · problem: creators need transparent automatic payment without third-party processors

### [[music-minted-dej7bv]] — Music Minted
> the traditional music industry takes rights in exchange for distribution, so an independent creator's economics are set by an intermediary they cannot avoid

*Fantom Hackathon Q2 2023* · problem: musicians and audio creators depend on an industry model that takes ownership

### [[music-minted]] — Music Minted
> the music industry underpays artists and exploits their rights, so the economics are set against the creator by the structure rather than by the market

*Chainlink Spring 2023 Hackathon* · problem: artist remuneration and IP rights are exploited in the music industry
