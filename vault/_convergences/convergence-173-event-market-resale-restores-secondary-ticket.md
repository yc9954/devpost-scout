# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** event, market, resale, restores, secondary, ticket

### [[eventsnear-us-community-focused-ticketing-platform]] — EventsNEAR.us - Community-focused ticketing platform
> scalping and box office fees both extract from the same event, so a ticket design has to defeat resale and remove the intermediary at once

*NEAR MetaBUILD Hackathon* · problem: community events lose value to scalpers and box office fees

### [[speculate]] — Speculate
> a derivative represented as a balance cannot be transferred as a position, so representing options as individual tokens restores the secondary market that the format removed

*Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon* · problem: on-chain derivative representations are worse than they need to be

### [[eventonchain-y43ul0]] — EventOnChain
> an event ticket is a bearer instrument whose resale market is captured by scalpers because the issuer loses control at the point of sale, so tokenising the ticket restores the issuer's terms to the secondary market

*EthCC Hack 2022* · problem: event management and ticketing across chains is fragmented and hard to control
