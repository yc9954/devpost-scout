# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** change, offline, reaches, want, wrong

### [[stayed-shipped]] — Stayed Shipped
> engineering organisations can measure AI spend to the cent and cannot measure what it produced — merged is the metric everyone reaches for and it is the wrong one, since a change reverted four days later counted as productivity twice

*GitLab Transcend Hackathon* · problem: the accepted productivity measure counts the act of merging and is blind to whether the change survived

### [[videofs-vfs]] — VideoFS (VFS)
> saving videos offline in good quality now requires a subscription — companies are not wrong to want money, and it shows how a policy change reaches everything you thought you had

*Hedera22: Hello Smart Contracts* · problem: content and the terms of access to it are held by the same party, so the terms can change after you have committed

### [[monsoon-mitr-ai-for-sustainable-farming-07uil2]] — Monsoon Mitr – AI for Sustainable Farming
> over half of Indian agriculture depends on the monsoon and the farmer's forecast is looking at the sky, so one wrong irrigation decision is made daily for want of a prediction in their language and offline

*GNEC Hackathon 2025 Fall - Compete For UN-Affiliated/NGO Internships & Prizes* · problem: farmers guess whether rain will come and lose crops or water on the guess
