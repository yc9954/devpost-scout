# 4 projects, 2 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** clinical, eligibility, enrolment, patients, percent, science, trial, trials

### [[trial-match-ai]] — trialmatch.ai
> trials fail on enrolment rather than on science, and millions of patients who qualify are never matched, because eligibility screening is still manual

*Cal Hacks 12.0* · problem: the matching step between an existing trial and an existing eligible patient is performed by hand at both ends

### [[medibuddy-y7t65f]] — MediBuddy
> thousands of clinical trials go under-enrolled, not because patients are unwilling but because they are unaware, and doctors have no time to search eligibility during a short appointment

*UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2025* · problem: the matching failure is an information one on both sides of a conversation that lasts ten minutes

### [[mify]] — MIFY
> over 80 percent of clinical trials miss enrolment deadlines, so recruitment rather than science is the most common reason a trial fails

*Cal Hacks 12.0* · problem: clinical trial recruitment is inefficient and raises ethical problems

### [[trialflow]] — TrialFlow
> 40 percent of patients drop out of clinical trials and scheduling friction drives much of it, so trial completion is a logistics outcome rather than a clinical one

*Cal Hacks 12.0* · problem: clinicians spend hours coordinating visit windows and patients miss them
