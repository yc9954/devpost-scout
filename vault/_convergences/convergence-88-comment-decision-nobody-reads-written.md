# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** comment, decision, nobody, reads, written

### [[lore-living-organizational-record-engine]] — LORE - Living Organizational Record Engine
> a decision is made, agreed, written in a comment on one merge request, and six months later a new person proposes the exact thing it forbade, because agreement was recorded where nobody reads it

*GitLab AI Hackathon* · problem: organisational learning is stored at the site of the discussion rather than at the site of the future mistake

### [[mnemosyne-ubotlw]] — mnemosyne
> the lesson from an outage exists, is written down, and lives where nobody reads it before the decision that repeats it

*Google Cloud Rapid Agent Hackathon* · problem: organisational memory is stored in documents rather than delivered at the moment of the risky action

### [[ai-comment-assistant]] — AI Comment Assistant
> a long comment thread contains the decision and nobody rereads it, so the reasoning is preserved and inaccessible

*Codegeist Unleashed* · problem: discussion threads accumulate and the outcome is buried
