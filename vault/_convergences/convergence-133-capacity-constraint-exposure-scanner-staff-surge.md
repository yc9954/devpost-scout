# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** capacity, constraint, exposure, scanner, staff, surge

### [[clinician-coronavirus-exposure-tracker]] — Infection Exposure Tracker
> no dataset exists for exposure inside hospitals and the institutions lack capacity to build one, so the tradeoff between quarantining staff and keeping capacity is decided without evidence

*Pandemic Response Hackathon* · problem: a decision with enormous stakes is made in the absence of data that nobody has the bandwidth to collect

### [[coronai]] — CoronAI
> lung monitoring requires a scanner and a technician, so during a surge the staff exposure and the monitoring capacity are the same constraint

*hack:now* · problem: clinicians had to see every patient in person to monitor lung condition

### [[coviseg-segmentacao-de-imagens-de-tc-para-casos-de-covid19]] — COVISEG - Segmentação de imagens de TC para casos de COVID19
> during a surge the diagnostic constraint is reading capacity rather than scanner capacity, so automated segmentation adds throughput where the shortage actually is

*Hackcovid19 * · problem: rising case numbers required detection methods beyond the available testing
