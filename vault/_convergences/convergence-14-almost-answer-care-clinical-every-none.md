# 6 projects, 6 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** almost, answer, care, clinical, every, none, patient, pipeline, skill, team

### [[postmortem]] — PostMortem
> every team runs retrospectives and almost none of them work — someone asks what went wrong and the room gives the polished version, the safe answer that does not name names or implicate leadership

*Mind the Product presents World Product Day: Everyone Ships Now* · problem: the retrospective's value depends on candour and its format guarantees the opposite

### [[graphflow-release-safety-intelligence]] — GraphFlow — Release Safety Intelligence
> every pipeline tool reports that a job failed and almost none report what the failure means for the release, so teams answer the only question that matters by hand

*H0: Hack the Zero Stack with Vercel v0 and AWS Databases* · problem: status is reported per component while the decision is about the whole system

### [[dill-pkl]] — dill.pkl
> every team has data worth predicting from and almost none act on it, because a reliable pipeline is a project in itself rather than a step

* GenAI Genesis 2026* · problem: the distance between having data and having a model is many specialist decisions deep

### [[carechain-decentralized-offline-first-patient-management]] — CareChain2.0: Decentralized Offline-First Patient Management
> a patient record that requires connectivity is unavailable exactly where care is hardest to deliver, so continuity of care is gated on infrastructure rather than on clinical skill

*FusionHacks 2* · problem: frontline health workers in rural areas cannot reliably access or update patient data

### [[mediglot-ai]] — MediGlot.ai
> a patient who cannot describe symptoms in the clinician's language receives worse care regardless of the clinician's skill, so language is a clinical variable

*UC Berkeley AI Hackathon* · problem: a family member's ear infection went untreated across a language and access barrier

### [[medishift-align]] — MediShift-Align
> clinical staffing conflicts are resolved by whoever is doing the rota that week, so patient care capacity is set by a scheduling process nobody owns

*Hack for Humanity | 2025* · problem: hospitals and clinics face scheduling conflicts and misallocated resources
