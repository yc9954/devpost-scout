# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** access, appointment, availability, hours, often

### [[get-together-tl496s]] — Get Together + Square Appointments
> 8 out of 10 consumers prefer to interact with businesses by text and the average consumer spends 50 hours a year on hold, often just to book a simple appointment

*Square Unboxed Hackathon* · problem: the preferred channel and the available channel differ, and the gap is measured in tens of hours per person per year

### [[co-llectif]] — Co-llectif
> people who most need help often will not ask because asking a neighbour costs something they cannot spend, so availability of help is not the same as access to it

*The European Commission's EUvsVirus Hackathon* · problem: the request itself is the barrier, and every matching system assumes it will be made

### [[healthme-cshbay]] — HealthMe
> booking a medical appointment requires a phone call during working hours, so access to care is gated on an availability that the ill person often does not have

*ConUHacks X* · problem: finding a clinic and booking requires calling and waiting on the phone
