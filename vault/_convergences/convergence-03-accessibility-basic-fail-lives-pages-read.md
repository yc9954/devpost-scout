# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** accessibility, basic, fail, lives, pages, read, websites

### [[based_smiles]] — Better Web
> 15% of the global population lives with a disability and 70% of websites fail basic accessibility standards — students with dyslexia avoid online learning, professionals with ADHD cannot read long pages

*Frostbyte Hackathon* · problem: accessibility is the author's obligation and the author overwhelmingly does not meet it, so the burden lands on the reader

### [[vertex-a11y]] — Vertex A11y
> over 216 million people have a visual impairment and a WebAIM study found over 96% of home pages fail basic accessibility checks

*HackGT 12: Midnight at the Museum* · problem: the failure rate is near-universal and the remediation information reaches the developer after the site is built

### [[title-ai]] — Title AI
> a $3.6 trillion market has a fifty-year-old manual bottleneck because the data lives on thousands of county websites that were never meant to be read together

*Amazon Nova AI Hackathon* · problem: the obstacle is not the task's difficulty but the fragmentation of its sources
