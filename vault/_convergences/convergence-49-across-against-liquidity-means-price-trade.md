# 3 projects, 3 hackathons, one claim

**Shared vocabulary** across, against, liquidity, means, price, trade

### [[lynk-swap]] — Lynk Swap
> liquidity fragmented across chains means the best price for a trade is unreachable from where the user is, so aggregation across chains is a price problem rather than a convenience one

*Block Magic: A Chainlink Hackathon* · problem: cross-chain swaps are inflexible and route poorly

### [[monnex-tm-accessing-finance-the-confident-way-tm]] — Monnex(TM) - Accessing Finance the Confident way(TM)
> accessing liquidity against an asset means selling or borrowing at terms set by an institution, so the holder pays either tax or interest to use value they already own

*Hack to the Future 4* · problem: traditional routes to liquidity require personal loans, equity loans, cards or retirement withdrawals

### [[arbiswap]] — ArbiSwap
> liquidity separated across venues means a trade moves the price against the trader, so fragmentation is a cost borne by users rather than by the venues

*Evmos Momentum Hackathon led by Web3Scholarships (Deployment required for prize)* · problem: thin separated liquidity causes large price impact on a swap
