# privacy on a public ledger is available in principle and priced out of reach in practice, so cost rather than cryptography is what keeps it unused

Asserted by [[chainlink-vmt]] — *VMTree*  
<sub>Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** on-chain privacy constructions are too expensive for ordinary use

**Mechanism** verifiable Merkle trees making the construction affordable  
**Beneficiary** applications that need privacy and cannot pay for it

**Recurs in** [[voting]] [[identity]] [[medical records]]
