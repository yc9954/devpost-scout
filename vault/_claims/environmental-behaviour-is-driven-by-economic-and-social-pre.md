# environmental behaviour is driven by economic and social pressures rather than by knowledge, so a simulation has to include those pressures or it teaches the wrong lesson

Asserted by [[sinking-earth-thinking-us]] — *Sinking Earth (Thinking Us)*  
<sub>Global Gamers Challenge</sub>

**Problem** environmental games isolate the ecological variable from the economic and social ones

**Mechanism** a real-time multiplayer simulation combining environmental, economic and social factors  
**Beneficiary** people learning why sustainable choices are hard

**Recurs in** [[policy education]] [[urban planning]] [[public health]]
