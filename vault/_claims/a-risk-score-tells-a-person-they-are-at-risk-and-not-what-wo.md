# a risk score tells a person they are at risk and not what would change it, so the output cannot be acted on by the person it describes

Asserted by [[cardioinsight-ai-interpretable-cardiovascular-risk]] — *CardioInsight AI – Interpretable Cardiovascular Risk*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** people do not understand why they are at cardiovascular risk or what would reduce it

**Mechanism** 10-year risk estimation with contributing factors and the effect of specific changes  
**Beneficiary** people given a risk number with no path from it

**Recurs in** [[diabetes]] [[credit scoring]] [[insurance pricing]]
