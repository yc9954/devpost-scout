# a derivative represented as a balance cannot be transferred as a position, so representing options as individual tokens restores the secondary market that the format removed

Asserted by [[speculate]] — *Speculate*  
<sub>Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** on-chain derivative representations are worse than they need to be

**Mechanism** options as ERC-721 tokens  
**Beneficiary** traders wanting to transfer specific positions

**Recurs in** [[insurance policies]] [[warranties]] [[vouchers]]
