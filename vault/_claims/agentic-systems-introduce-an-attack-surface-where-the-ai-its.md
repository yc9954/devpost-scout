# agentic systems introduce an attack surface where the AI itself is the thing compromised, through injection, poisoning or tool hallucination, which no perimeter defence was designed to see

Asserted by [[warden-ny9uwa]] — *Warden*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** security assumed the attacker is outside the system rather than inside its reasoning

**Mechanism** runtime firewall inspecting agent inputs and actions for hijacking patterns  
**Beneficiary** organisation running agents with real access

**Recurs in** [[industrial control]] [[medical devices]] [[autonomous vehicles]]
