# a merchant's cash flow is delayed by the absence of a way for a customer to pay part of an invoice, so people improvise with donation links

Asserted by [[custom-pay]] — *Custom Payments*  
<sub>Square Unboxed Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** businesses wait to get paid and use donation links for partial payments

**Mechanism** customers viewing pending invoices and paying one or several at once  
**Beneficiary** small merchants waiting on receivables

**Recurs in** [[medical billing]] [[tuition]] [[utilities]]
