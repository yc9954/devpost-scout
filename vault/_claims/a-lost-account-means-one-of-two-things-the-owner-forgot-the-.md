# a lost account means one of two things — the owner forgot the key, in which case the assets are gone, or the owner leaked it, in which case anyone holding it can act as them

Asserted by [[0xdeadlist]] — *0xDeadList*  
<sub>Polygon BUIDL IT : Summer 2022</sub>

**Problem** a compromised address is indistinguishable from a live one to everyone except the thief, and no shared record exists

**Mechanism** a public registry of compromised addresses with an incentive to report  
**Beneficiary** applications and users transacting with a compromised account

**Recurs in** [[stolen cards]] [[compromised credentials]] [[recalled certificates]]
