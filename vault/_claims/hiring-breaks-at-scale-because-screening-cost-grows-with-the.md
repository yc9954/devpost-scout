# hiring breaks at scale because screening cost grows with the number of applicants while the number of hires does not, so more reach makes the process worse

Asserted by [[hiredesk]] — *HireDesk*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** recruiters receive over a thousand applications per role and spend 60 to 70 percent of their time screening

**Mechanism** ML ranking of candidates against the posting  
**Beneficiary** recruiters and applicants stuck in backlogs

**Recurs in** [[grant review]] [[admissions]] [[procurement bids]]
