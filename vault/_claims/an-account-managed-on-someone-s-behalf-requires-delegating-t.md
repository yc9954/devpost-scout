# an account managed on someone's behalf requires delegating the credentials, so agency work is blocked by the absence of scoped permission rather than by demand

Asserted by [[social-maven]] — *Maven*  
<sub>Chainlink Spring 2023 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** there is no safe way for an agency to manage a Lens profile for a client

**Mechanism** trustless delegated posting rights without handing over the account  
**Beneficiary** agencies and the clients who cannot safely delegate

**Recurs in** [[financial power of attorney]] [[cloud access]] [[medical proxies]]
