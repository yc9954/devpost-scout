# we are bad at long-term decision-making — we underestimate risks, overestimate our future motivation, and ignore the second-order effects of our choices

Asserted by [[usaid-nsel9u]] — *Usaid*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** the consequences of a major decision are years away and the decision is made with a model of a future self that does not exist

**Mechanism** let a person experience the projected future before choosing it  
**Beneficiary** person about to quit a job or make an irreversible choice

**Recurs in** [[retirement planning]] [[health behaviour]] [[education choices]]
