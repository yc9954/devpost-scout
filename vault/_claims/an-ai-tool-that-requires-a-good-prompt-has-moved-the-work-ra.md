# an AI tool that requires a good prompt has moved the work rather than removed it, and it moves it onto exactly the people who came for help with writing

Asserted by [[corelytics]] — *Corelytics*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** email assistants demand a detailed prompt to produce a usable email

**Mechanism** a structured intent engine replacing free-text prompting  
**Beneficiary** people who write professional email daily and do not write prompts well
