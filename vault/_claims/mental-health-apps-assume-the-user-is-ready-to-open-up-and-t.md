# mental health apps assume the user is ready to open up and the hardest step is admitting how you feel, so the tools serve the part of the journey after the barrier

Asserted by [[feelspace]] — *Feelspace*  
<sub>DSH Hacks V1</sub>

**Problem** most mental health apps require a comfort with disclosure that the person does not yet have

**Mechanism** anonymous privacy-first venting and reflection with community support  
**Beneficiary** people who cannot yet say how they feel

**Recurs in** [[addiction support]] [[grief]] [[abuse recovery]]
