# an open auction reveals every bid and lets participants react, so the price reflects the bidding rather than the valuations and a real sale suffers for it

Asserted by [[dauction]] — *Dauction*  
<sub>Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** a friend selling a laptop to several interested buyers had no fair mechanism

**Mechanism** a sealed-bid auction where bids are hidden until the end  
**Beneficiary** sellers and honest bidders

**Recurs in** [[procurement]] [[spectrum]] [[art sales]]
