# a protocol that pays rewards from ongoing activity has no buffer when activity falls, so a bond mechanism is a solvency structure rather than a product

Asserted by [[ustx-bond]] — *USTX - BOND*  
<sub>TRON Grand Hackathon - HackaTRON Season 6</sub>

**Problem** an ecosystem generating rewards needs a safety net when returns fall

**Mechanism** a bond issuance and redemption mechanism  
**Beneficiary** users depending on the protocol's continued solvency

**Recurs in** [[pension funds]] [[insurance reserves]] [[municipal finance]]
