# listening is passive because the recording is fixed, so making the parts of a song into manipulable objects converts an audience into participants

Asserted by [[m-orph-mixed-reality-music-experience]] — *(m)ORPH Mixed Reality Music Experience*  
<sub>Meta Horizon Start Developer Competition</sub>

**Problem** music listening offers no interaction with the material

**Mechanism** grabbing, throwing and mixing sound objects in a room with audio-reactive surroundings  
**Beneficiary** listeners and people learning how music is built

**Recurs in** [[music education]] [[therapy]] [[live performance]]
