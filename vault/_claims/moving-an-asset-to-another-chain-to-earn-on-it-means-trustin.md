# moving an asset to another chain to earn on it means trusting the bridge more than the asset, so the risk of participating exceeds the risk of the position

Asserted by [[bitstakenet]] — *BitStakeNet*  
<sub>Starknet Re{Solve} Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** bringing bitcoin into another chain and back safely for deposits and staking

**Mechanism** a bidirectional bridge with staking on the destination chain  
**Beneficiary** holders who want yield without custody risk

**Recurs in** [[cross-border settlement]] [[custody]] [[collateral transfer]]
