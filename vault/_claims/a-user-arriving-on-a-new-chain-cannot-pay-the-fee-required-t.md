Asserted by [[vapor-protocol]] — *Vapor Protocol*

**Problem** obtaining gas on a new chain requires going through a centralised party

**Mechanism** exchanging tokens or fiat for gas across chains without an intermediary  
**Beneficiary** users entering a chain for the first time
