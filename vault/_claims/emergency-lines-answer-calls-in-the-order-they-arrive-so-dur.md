# emergency lines answer calls in the order they arrive, so during a mass event the queue collapses and most calls go unanswered while most of what is answered is duplicate noise

Asserted by [[team-outliers]] — *Team Outliers*  
<sub>USAII® Global AI Hackathon 2026</sub>

**Problem** the ordering discipline that is fair under normal load is catastrophic under surge

**Mechanism** intercept overflow, cluster duplicates and prioritise by threat to life  
**Beneficiary** caller in a life-threatening emergency

**Recurs in** [[hospital triage]] [[disaster hotlines]] [[utility outages]]
