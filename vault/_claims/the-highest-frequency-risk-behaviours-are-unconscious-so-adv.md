# the highest-frequency risk behaviours are unconscious, so advice aimed at intention cannot reach them; only an interrupt at the moment of the movement can

Asserted by [[facespace-applewatch-application]] — *FaceSpace - Apple Watch Application*  
<sub>COVID-19 Global Hackathon 1.0</sub>

**Problem** the behaviour to change happens 23 times an hour without the person noticing any of them

**Mechanism** wrist accelerometer detects the hand-to-face gesture and interrupts it  
**Beneficiary** anyone avoiding transmission
