# play-to-earn economies are unsustainable because rewards are paid from new entrants rather than from activity, so the design question is where the yield actually comes from

Asserted by [[legions-of-oasis]] — *Legions of Oasis*  
<sub>Oasis Bloom Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** popular play-to-earn games are structurally unsustainable

**Mechanism** a game whose liquidity mechanics fund the rewards  
**Beneficiary** players in game economies that survive

**Recurs in** [[loyalty schemes]] [[creator funds]] [[cooperatives]]
