# a digital item sold through a store separates the maker from the buyer, so the relationship that sustains an independent creator is exactly what distribution removes

Asserted by [[decentralized-library]] — *LEBIUM*  
<sub>Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** creators have no direct connection to the people who buy their work

**Mechanism** a private library whose items can be listed publicly and sold as copies  
**Beneficiary** independent creators of digital work
