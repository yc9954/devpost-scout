# time accounting fails because the work happens outside the tool doing the accounting, so tracking has to follow the person into the browser

Asserted by [[time-to-coda]] — *Time to Coda*  
<sub>Coda's AI at Work Challenge</sub>

**Problem** a week ends with no account of where the time went because the tracker only saw part of it

**Mechanism** a tracker and extension capturing activity outside the workspace  
**Beneficiary** people whose work spans many applications

**Recurs in** [[billing]] [[research logging]] [[focus measurement]]
