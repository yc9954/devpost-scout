# the moment a person needs emergency help is the moment they can least operate an interface, so the design constraint is a single action and automatic context

Asserted by [[resqtrack]] — *ResQTrack*  
<sub>MongoDB AI Hackathon: Code for a Cause</sub>

**Problem** being lost and unable to summon help as daylight faded

**Mechanism** one-touch SOS with real-time communication and dispatch  
**Beneficiary** people in situations they cannot describe or navigate

**Recurs in** [[medical emergencies]] [[domestic violence]] [[maritime]]
