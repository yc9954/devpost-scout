# a shared stake that can be permanently lost produces collective attention that no reward alone generates, so permadeath is the community mechanic

Asserted by [[don-t-kill-the-fish]] — *Don't Kill The Fish*  
<sub>Reddit and Kiro: Community Games Challenge</sub>

**Problem** community games have no consequence and attention fades

**Mechanism** a permadeath fish tank where all progress is lost if the community fails  
**Beneficiary** online communities seeking a shared object of care

**Recurs in** [[commons management]] [[fundraising]] [[public goods]]
