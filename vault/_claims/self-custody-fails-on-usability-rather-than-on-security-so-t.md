# self-custody fails on usability rather than on security, so the design problem is making the key disappear into a familiar gesture

Asserted by [[portal-oqpz8a]] — *Portal*  
<sub>Bitcoin 2025 Official Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** self custody is too difficult for ordinary users to adopt

**Mechanism** an NFC hardware signer tapped against the phone, providing login and payment without passwords  
**Beneficiary** people who want sovereign identity and will not manage keys

**Recurs in** [[passwordless login]] [[medical records]] [[voting]]
