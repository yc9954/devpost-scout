# until now we have looked at money as value-neutral — a bitcoin does not care whether it is donated to an orphanage or used to fund terrorism, as has all money in the history of civilisation

Asserted by [[eco-shift]] — *EcoShift*  
<sub>Chainlink Fall Hackathon 2021</sub>

**Problem** the medium of exchange carries no information about its use, which is why aligning spending with values requires effort at every transaction

**Mechanism** wallets with ethical rules built in, aligned to a community's shared values  
**Beneficiary** person whose spending contradicts their values without their knowing

**Recurs in** [[pension investment]] [[procurement]] [[charitable giving]]
