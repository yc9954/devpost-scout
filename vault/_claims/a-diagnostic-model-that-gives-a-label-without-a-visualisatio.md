# a diagnostic model that gives a label without a visualisation cannot be checked by the clinician, so explainability is what determines whether it is used

Asserted by [[deepx-chestxray]] — *DeepX-ChestXRay*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** chest X-ray analysis requires expertise and time and opaque models are not trusted

**Mechanism** pneumonia detection with explainable visual output  
**Beneficiary** clinicians and researchers needing a checkable result
