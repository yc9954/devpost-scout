# an AI answer's factual claims can be extracted and scored for how outdated each probably is

Asserted by [[temporal-blindspot]] — *Temporal Blindspot*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** AI models freeze knowledge at a training cutoff, so their facts silently go stale

**Mechanism** extract every factual claim from a response and score each by likely staleness  
**Beneficiary** AI users

**Recurs in** [[fact-checking]] [[content freshness auditing]]
