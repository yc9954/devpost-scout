# a ruin communicates almost nothing about what stood there, so reconstruction on the site is the only presentation that makes a historic place legible

Asserted by [[sutrobath-ar-tour]] — *Sutro Baths AR Tour*  
<sub>Google’s Immersive Geospatial Challenge</sub>

**Problem** visitors stand in ruins with no sense of the structure that was there

**Mechanism** an AR reconstruction anchored at the site with photographs and interaction  
**Beneficiary** visitors to heritage sites

**Recurs in** [[archaeology]] [[real estate]] [[urban planning]]
