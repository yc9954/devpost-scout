Asserted by [[da-derpy-derby]] — *Da Derpy Derby*  
<sub>Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** a spare closet and the wish to run Chainlink nodes with nothing to serve

**Mechanism** an arcade game whose control scripts live on IPFS and execute through an external adapter  
**Beneficiary** people with idle hardware looking for a workload
