# a missing person is lost twice, once when they disappear and again when the report is buried in a database, so the second loss is an information design failure

Asserted by [[second-alert]] — *Second Alert*  
<sub>AWS Artificial Intelligence Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** missing person reports pile up in databases and are never matched

**Mechanism** facial identification matching against the reported records
