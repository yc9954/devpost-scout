# an open mempool lets anyone replace a pending transaction, so a first-come mechanism becomes a fee auction and the design intent is defeated by the transport layer

Asserted by [[acai-gup0bm]] — *Acai*  
<sub>Bitcoin 2025 Official Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** minting systems suffer frontrunning and replace-by-fee attacks in the public mempool

**Mechanism** a contract construction resistant to fee replacement  
**Beneficiary** participants in open minting who lose to faster bidders

**Recurs in** [[auctions]] [[ticket sales]] [[limited releases]]
