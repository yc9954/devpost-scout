# trust between strangers is currently purchased by paying a middleman a percentage, so the cost of trust scales with transaction value even though the risk does not

Asserted by [[trustified-network]] — *Trustified Network*  
<sub>Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** freelancers pay heavy commissions to platforms that exist only to guarantee the other side

**Mechanism** escrowed delivery of digital services with crypto payment  
**Beneficiary** freelancers and their clients

**Recurs in** [[marketplaces]] [[cross-border trade]] [[rental deposits]]
