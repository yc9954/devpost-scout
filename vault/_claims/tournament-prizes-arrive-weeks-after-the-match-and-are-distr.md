# tournament prizes arrive weeks after the match and are distributed by an organiser, so the competitive experience and the reward are separated in time and trust

Asserted by [[rill-bzj0cx]] — *Rill*  
<sub>TRON Grand Hackathon 2022</sub>

**Problem** esports prize distribution is delayed and depends on the organiser

**Mechanism** instant or round-based reward distribution on a public ledger  
**Beneficiary** competitive players and small tournament organisers

**Recurs in** [[gig payouts]] [[bounty programmes]] [[sports prizes]]
