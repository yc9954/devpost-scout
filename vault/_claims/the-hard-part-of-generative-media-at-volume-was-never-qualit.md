# the hard part of generative media at volume was never quality, it was two unglamorous things: cost, and not knowing what made your asset

Asserted by [[sigmora-sigmora-org]] — *SIGMORA(sigmora.org)*  
<sub>Backblaze Generative Media Hackathon: Build with Genblaze on B2</sub>

**Problem** the provenance of a generated asset — which model, which settings, which inputs — is discarded at the moment of generation and needed at every moment after

**Mechanism** orchestrate every model with per-asset provenance and zero-egress storage  
**Beneficiary** studio running generation at production scale
