# an emergency alert tells people something is happening and not why it requires them to act, so the warning is received and discounted

Asserted by [[fire-tracker-d9uokc]] — *Anchor*  
<sub>UNIHACK 2026, funded by the European Union</sub>

**Problem** a family lost their house to a natural hazard and warnings did not produce action

**Mechanism** alerts that explain why action is necessary rather than only announcing the event  
**Beneficiary** people who receive warnings and do not act on them

**Recurs in** [[flood warnings]] [[evacuation orders]] [[health advisories]]
