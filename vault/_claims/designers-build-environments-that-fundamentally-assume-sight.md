# designers build environments that fundamentally assume sight, and accessibility is an afterthought if it is considered at all

Asserted by [[blindsight-virtual-eyes-through-haptic-feedback]] — *Blindsight - Virtual Eyes Through Haptic Feedback*

**Problem** for 285 million visually impaired people the built world encodes information in a channel they cannot receive
