# early Parkinson's signs are dismissed repeatedly before anyone tests for them, so the diagnostic delay is produced by the threshold for referral rather than by the tests

Asserted by [[neurosketch]] — *NeuroSketch*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** slight tremors and stiffness were repeatedly dismissed before diagnosis

**Mechanism** motor, voice and movement screening administered by paramedical workers anywhere  
**Beneficiary** people whose early symptoms are dismissed
