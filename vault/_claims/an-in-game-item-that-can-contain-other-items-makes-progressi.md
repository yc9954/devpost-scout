Asserted by [[unique-space]] — *Unique Space*

**Problem** tokens cannot hold other tokens so upgrades are recorded as attributes rather than as parts

**Mechanism** upgradable nested tokens where harvested parts are assembled into the ship  
**Beneficiary** players and developers modelling composite objects
