# a lecture has a part nobody says out loud — what is on the board, what the pointer is on — and that is exactly the part a blind student needs, so the fix is to speak it and stay silent when the teacher already did

Asserted by [[aster-sv8mi6]] — *Aster*

**Problem** video instruction encodes essential content visually and redundant narration makes it worse, not better

**Mechanism** generate only the unspoken visual content and suppress when narration already covers it  
**Beneficiary** blind or low-vision student on any lecture video
