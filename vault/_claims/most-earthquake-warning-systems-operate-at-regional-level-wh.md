Asserted by [[seismoarch-iot-model-bvp3gj]] — *SeismoArch IoT Model*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** the warning window is seconds and the detection architecture adds latency at exactly that scale

**Mechanism** building-level seismic detection triggering local alerts  
**Beneficiary** occupant of a building in the seconds before shaking
