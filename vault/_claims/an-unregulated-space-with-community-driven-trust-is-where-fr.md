# an unregulated space with community-driven trust is where fraud is cheapest, so the people least equipped to evaluate risk carry the most of it

Asserted by [[eagle-eye-6nh1oe]] — *Eagle Eye*  
<sub>TRON Grand Hackathon 2022</sub>

**Problem** web3 adoption brings in users who cannot assess whether something is a scam

**Mechanism** risk detection presented simply enough for a non-technical user  
**Beneficiary** newcomers and older users entering the space

**Recurs in** [[phone scams]] [[investment fraud]] [[charity fraud]]
