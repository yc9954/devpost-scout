# a privacy policy is a contract nobody reads because reading it costs more than the service is worth, so consent is given without comprehension by design

Asserted by [[good-site-org]] — *Good-Site.org*  
<sub>2024 TikTok TechJam</sub>

**Problem** agreements are accepted without being read in an era where data is the price

**Mechanism** finding the policy for any site and summarising its main points on demand  
**Beneficiary** users agreeing to terms they have not read

**Recurs in** [[terms of service]] [[loan agreements]] [[employment contracts]]
