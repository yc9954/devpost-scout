# exercise pays off on a horizon too long to motivate anyone, so the reward has to be moved forward to the day the effort was made

Asserted by [[burn2earn]] — *Burn2Earn*  
<sub>Chainlink Spring 2023 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** promoting physical activity to people for whom future health is not a sufficient incentive

**Mechanism** converting measured calorie burn into immediate discount value  
**Beneficiary** people who will not exercise for health reasons alone

**Recurs in** [[preventive screening]] [[savings]] [[study habits]]
