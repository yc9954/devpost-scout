# assistive tools are socially blind: they report that a person is present but not which person or what they are about to do, and the distinction is the whole content

Asserted by [[netra-empowering-the-visually-impaired]] — *Netra: Empowering the Visually Impaired*

**Problem** detection is delivered at a granularity below the one the decision requires

**Mechanism** scene description with face recognition and intent-relevant detail  
**Beneficiary** visually impaired person
