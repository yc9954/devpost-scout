# a four and a half star rating can coexist with a bad experience because nothing verifies that the reviewer was a customer, so the number measures review supply rather than quality

Asserted by [[appraisr]] — *Appraisr*  
<sub>Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** restaurant ratings are high and uninformative

**Mechanism** verified ratings with token incentives for reviewing and for voting on reviews  
**Beneficiary** people choosing from ratings they cannot trust
