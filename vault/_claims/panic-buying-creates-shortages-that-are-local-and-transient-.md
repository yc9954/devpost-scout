# panic buying creates shortages that are local and transient, so people travel to empty shelves because nobody publishes what is on them

Asserted by [[whatsinstock]] — *WhatsInStock*  
<sub>Pandemic Response Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** supermarkets face shortages and shoppers have no way to know where stock is

**Mechanism** a map of nearby stores and their inventories  
**Beneficiary** shoppers in shortage conditions and stores absorbing crowds

**Recurs in** [[fuel shortages]] [[pharmacy stock]] [[disaster supplies]]
