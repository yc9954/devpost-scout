# empty return trips are a large share of freight cost and they exist because matching happens per leg rather than per round trip, so the waste is a market design problem

Asserted by [[transsmart-smarter-routes-better-logistics]] — *TransSmart – Smarter Routes. Better Logistics*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** freight relies on manual coordination and trucks return empty

**Mechanism** route optimisation with load matching and live tracking  
**Beneficiary** carriers and shippers paying for empty miles
