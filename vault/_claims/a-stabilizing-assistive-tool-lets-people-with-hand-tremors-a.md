# a stabilizing assistive tool lets people with hand tremors apply their own makeup independently

Asserted by [[project-magic-spark]] — *Project Magic Spark*  
<sub>ELC Accessible Beauty Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** people with tremors from cerebral palsy cannot apply their own makeup and depend on caregivers

**Mechanism** an assistive device compensating for hand tremor during makeup application  
**Beneficiary** people with motor disabilities

**Recurs in** [[assistive eating utensils]] [[writing aids]] [[grooming aids]]
