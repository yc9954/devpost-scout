# an auction with a known end time invites bidding at the last second, so the mechanism produces sniping and a random ending removes the incentive

Asserted by [[meltdown-nft-candle-auctions]] — *Meltdown*  
<sub>Chainlink Fall Hackathon 2021</sub>

**Problem** timed auctions are dominated by last-moment bidding

**Mechanism** candle auctions with an unpredictable finalisation  
**Beneficiary** sellers and bidders without the fastest connection

**Recurs in** [[procurement]] [[spectrum allocation]] [[charity auctions]]
