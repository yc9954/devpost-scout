# caregiving technology monitors the recipient and strains the relationship, so the design goal is what it does to the pair rather than what it detects

Asserted by [[safesteps-9x4o7i]] — *SafeStep*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** monitoring tools reduce the older adult to a subject of surveillance

**Mechanism** fall and activity detection with simplified navigation for the recipient and logging for the caregiver  
**Beneficiary** older adults and the family members caring for them

**Recurs in** [[disability support]] [[childcare]] [[mental health]]
