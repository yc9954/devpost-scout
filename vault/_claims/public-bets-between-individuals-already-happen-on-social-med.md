# public bets between individuals already happen on social media with no settlement mechanism, so the demand for prediction markets is demonstrated before any market exists

Asserted by [[tpmarket-prediction-market-on-tron]] — *tpMarket - Prediction Market on Tron*  
<sub>TRON Grand Hackathon 2022</sub>

**Problem** high-profile wagers are made on Twitter and settled by honour

**Mechanism** a prediction market where users can be both maker and taker  
**Beneficiary** people who want their public positions settled
