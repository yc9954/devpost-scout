# captions serve hearing people who lost the audio, not Deaf people whose first language is a sign language, so the existing accommodation addresses a different need than the one claimed

Asserted by [[sign-cast]] — *Sign-Cast*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** over 70 million Deaf people face barriers to spoken content and captions are an imperfect substitute

**Mechanism** real-time privacy-preserving translation of speech into sign  
**Beneficiary** Deaf people whose first language is signed

**Recurs in** [[broadcast]] [[education]] [[public announcements]]
