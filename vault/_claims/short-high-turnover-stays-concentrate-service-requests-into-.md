# short high-turnover stays concentrate service requests into a narrow window, so a hotel without ordering infrastructure fails exactly during its busiest season

Asserted by [[roomerit-the-future-of-hotel-management]] — *Roomerit | The Future of Hotel Management*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** guests in Makkah during Umrah need services quickly and most hotels have no system

**Mechanism** QR-based in-room ordering with a management side for the hotel  
**Beneficiary** guests on short stays and small hotels

**Recurs in** [[hospitals]] [[student housing]] [[event venues]]
