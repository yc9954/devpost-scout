# assembly instructions are printed for an object that exists in space, so the reader performs a projection from a diagram to the parts in front of them

Asserted by [[spatial-guide]] — *Spatial Guide*  
<sub>XR Design Challenge</sub>

**Problem** furniture assembly from printed instructions is frustrating and error-prone

**Mechanism** step-by-step guidance shown at real-world scale in the user's own space  
**Beneficiary** people assembling things from diagrams
