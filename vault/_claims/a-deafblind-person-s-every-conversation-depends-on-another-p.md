# a DeafBlind person's every conversation depends on another person being present to relay it, so independence rather than communication speed is what an interface can return

Asserted by [[brailleai-a-two-way-voice-for-the-deafblind]] — *BrailleAI : A TwoWay Voice for the Deaf & Blind*  
<sub>UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2026</sub>

**Problem** saying something as simple as being hungry requires an intermediary

**Mechanism** typing by touch to speak aloud and receiving replies as Braille, including the speaker's emotion  
**Beneficiary** DeafBlind people

**Recurs in** [[locked-in syndrome]] [[aphasia]] [[non-verbal autism]]
