# a community has both experts and learners and they cannot be in the same place at the same time, so mentorship fails on synchronisation rather than on willingness

Asserted by [[live-coding-circle]] — *LiveCoding Circles*  
<sub>2018 Developer Circles Community Challenge</sub>

**Problem** people wanting to learn to code cannot always meet the experts who could teach them

**Mechanism** a tool connecting learners and mentors regardless of location  
**Beneficiary** learners in communities without local access

**Recurs in** [[language exchange]] [[trade skills]] [[professional mentoring]]
