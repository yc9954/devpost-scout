# most reference motions for humanoids come from clean lab data, which does not look like how a person actually moves

Asserted by [[robo-jab]] — *Robo Jab*  
<sub>UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2026</sub>

**Problem** the training distribution for embodied motion is collected in the one setting that excludes ordinary movement

**Mechanism** markerless motion capture from phone video, sim-trained for balance, deployed to real hardware  
**Beneficiary** anyone teaching a robot a physical skill
