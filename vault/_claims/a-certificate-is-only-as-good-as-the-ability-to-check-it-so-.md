# a certificate is only as good as the ability to check it, so a centralised issuer that cannot be queried produces credentials that are trivially faked

Asserted by [[certify-d6htlu]] — *Certify*  
<sub>Celo's Make Crypto Mobile Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** centralised certification systems are undermined by fakes and opaque status

**Mechanism** decentralised immutable certificates with privacy and revocable status  
**Beneficiary** holders of credentials and the people relying on them
