# the hardest part of starting an investment habit is the repeated decision, so automating small regular purchases removes the timing question that stops people

Asserted by [[daily-stacker]] — *Daily Stacker*  
<sub>Chainlink Fall Hackathon 2021</sub>

**Problem** getting newcomers past their first purchase and into a habit

**Mechanism** automatic daily small buys of a chosen asset  
**Beneficiary** first-time investors paralysed by timing

**Recurs in** [[retirement saving]] [[charitable giving]] [[debt repayment]]
