# in some countries staking rewards are taxed as income the moment they are received, even if never cashed out

Asserted by [[the-monitor]] — *The Monitor*  
<sub>Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** a tax liability is incurred in one asset and payable in another, so a price fall leaves the holder owing more than they hold

**Mechanism** automatically hedge the value of rewards at the moment they are received  
**Beneficiary** holder facing a tax bill on a fallen asset

**Recurs in** [[equity vesting]] [[commodity income]] [[foreign currency earnings]]
