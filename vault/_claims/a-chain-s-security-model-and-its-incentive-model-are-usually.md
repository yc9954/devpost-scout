# a chain's security model and its incentive model are usually designed separately, so validators are paid for uptime rather than for the property the network needs

Asserted by [[diora-network]] — *Diora Network*  
<sub>Polkadot Hackathon: North America Edition</sub>

**Problem** smart contract parachains need security guarantees and sustainable participation together

**Mechanism** proof of stake with double validation and randomization tied to incentives  
**Beneficiary** developers and users relying on the chain's guarantees

**Recurs in** [[consensus design]] [[auditing]] [[peer review]]
