# on-chain activity is fully visible and nobody is watching it in a structured way, so the transparency produces forensic evidence rather than prevention

Asserted by [[tron-security-dashkit]] — *Tron Security Dashcam*  
<sub>TRON Grand Hackathon 2022</sub>

**Problem** suspicious on-chain activity goes unmonitored until after an incident

**Mechanism** risk dashboards monitoring activity with alerting to security teams  
**Beneficiary** users and protocols exposed to attacks
