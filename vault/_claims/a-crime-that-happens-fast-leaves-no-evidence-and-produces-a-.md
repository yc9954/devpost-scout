# a crime that happens fast leaves no evidence and produces a slow response, so the decisive variable is whether anyone nearby could capture and broadcast the moment

Asserted by [[safenaija-community-led-security-through-voice-ai]] — *SafeNaija: Community-Led Security*  
<sub>Amazon Nova AI Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** the window in which evidence exists is shorter than the time it takes to reach an authority

**Mechanism** voice, photo and text capture dispatched to community and authorities at once  
**Beneficiary** bystander and victim

**Recurs in** [[road accidents]] [[wildlife poaching]] [[domestic violence]]
