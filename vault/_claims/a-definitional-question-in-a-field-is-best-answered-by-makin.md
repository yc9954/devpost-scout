# a definitional question in a field is best answered by making the reader construct their own answer, because every stated definition excludes something the reader already counts

Asserted by [[whatisagame-net]] — *WhatIsAGame.net*  
<sub>AWS Game Builder Challenge </sub>

**Problem** academic writing about what a game is produces definitions that nobody adopts

**Mechanism** a browser game whose play is the process of arriving at your own definition  
**Beneficiary** people thinking about the boundaries of a medium
