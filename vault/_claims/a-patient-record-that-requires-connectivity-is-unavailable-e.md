# a patient record that requires connectivity is unavailable exactly where care is hardest to deliver, so continuity of care is gated on infrastructure rather than on clinical skill

Asserted by [[carechain-decentralized-offline-first-patient-management]] — *CareChain2.0: Decentralized Offline-First Patient Management*  
<sub>FusionHacks 2</sub>

**Problem** frontline health workers in rural areas cannot reliably access or update patient data

**Mechanism** offline-first records carried as QR codes and reconciled when connectivity returns  
**Beneficiary** patients seen by community health workers

**Recurs in** [[disaster medicine]] [[refugee camps]] [[veterinary field work]]
