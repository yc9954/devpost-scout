# mutual aid fails on matching, not on generosity: the person who needs help most is the least likely to find the post offering it

Asserted by [[the-kindness-project]] — *The Kindness Project*  
<sub>COVID-19 Global Hackathon 1.0</sub>

**Problem** help is offered and requested in an unstructured feed where relevance is left to chance

**Mechanism** structured requests and offers matched by need and locality  
**Beneficiary** isolating vulnerable person

**Recurs in** [[disaster relief]] [[refugee support]] [[volunteer healthcare]]
