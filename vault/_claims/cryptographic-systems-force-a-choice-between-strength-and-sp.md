# cryptographic systems force a choice between strength and speed, so the deployed configuration is decided by performance budget rather than by threat model

Asserted by [[dms-4096-zoejya]] — *DMS 4096*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** strong security carries heavy computational cost and fast options are cryptographically shallower

**Mechanism** an encryption scheme increasing strength without a proportional speed cost  
**Beneficiary** systems that currently choose speed over strength

**Recurs in** [[embedded devices]] [[mobile]] [[high-volume services]]
