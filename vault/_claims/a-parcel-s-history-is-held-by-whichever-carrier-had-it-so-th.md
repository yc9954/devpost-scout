# a parcel's history is held by whichever carrier had it, so the sender and the receiver each see a partial record and disputes have no common ground

Asserted by [[blockfreight]] — *Blockfreight*  
<sub>Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** parcel tracking is fragmented across carriers

**Mechanism** an open tracking record for any parcel with stored evidence  
**Beneficiary** senders, receivers and carriers in disputes

**Recurs in** [[cold chain]] [[pharmaceutical distribution]] [[art shipping]]
