# a QR code hides its destination until the moment it has already been opened, so the one security decision a user could make is taken away from them

Asserted by [[qr-guard]] — *QR-Guard*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** QR codes are now used for menus and payment portals and carry no visible indication of where they lead

**Mechanism** inspecting and scoring the destination before the device follows it  
**Beneficiary** anyone scanning a code in a physical place

**Recurs in** [[shortened links]] [[NFC tags]] [[email redirects]]
