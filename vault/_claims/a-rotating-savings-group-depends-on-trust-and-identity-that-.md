# a rotating savings group depends on trust and identity that break at a distance, so the informal institution fails to scale for a verification reason rather than a financial one

Asserted by [[decentralized-kameti]] — *Decentralized Kameti*  
<sub>Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** rotating savings groups rely on personal trust that does not scale

**Mechanism** verified data and identity enabling a decentralised savings group  
**Beneficiary** communities running informal savings groups

**Recurs in** [[community lending]] [[mutual insurance]] [[cooperatives]]
