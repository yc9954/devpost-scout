# a certificate proves identity and skill and is trivially falsified, so the credential system depends on nobody bothering to check

Asserted by [[certidefi]] — *CertDeFi*  
<sub>Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** course and programme certificates can be falsified and identity stolen

**Mechanism** uniquely issued verifiable certificates  
**Beneficiary** holders of genuine credentials and the people relying on them

**Recurs in** [[professional licensing]] [[academic transcripts]] [[safety certification]]
