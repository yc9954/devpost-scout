# a supply chain's problem is that each participant can only see their own segment, so no party is lying and nobody knows the truth

Asserted by [[schain-wtyzxf]] — *Schain*  
<sub>Chainlink Spring 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** supply chain participants have no shared view of provenance

**Mechanism** recording supply chain events on chain so the record is common  
**Beneficiary** participants and regulators of a supply chain

**Recurs in** [[food safety]] [[pharmaceutical distribution]] [[conflict minerals]]
