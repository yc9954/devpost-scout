# a market can be live and invisible because nothing displays its state, so participation waits on an interface rather than on the venue

Asserted by [[spin-finance-dashboard]] — *Spin Finance Dashboard*  
<sub>NEAR MetaBUILD Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** spot markets were running on testnet with no way to see their metrics

**Mechanism** a dashboard exposing the market's own data  
**Beneficiary** participants who cannot evaluate a market they cannot see

**Recurs in** [[new exchanges]] [[public datasets]] [[internal systems]]
