# a caregiver watching several patients cannot tell which one is deteriorating, so the useful output is an ordering rather than a set of readings

Asserted by [[smartcare-fs8pkr]] — *SmartCare*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** caregivers monitor multiple patients and cannot quickly identify who is getting worse

**Mechanism** explainable risk scoring across vital signs to prioritise attention  
**Beneficiary** caregivers and the patient whose decline is missed

**Recurs in** [[neonatal units]] [[care homes]] [[field triage]]
