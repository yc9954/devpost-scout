# someone who cannot phonate still produces the full articulatory movement, so the speech exists and only the sound is missing

Asserted by [[silent-speech-recognition-for-the-voiceless]] — *Silent Speech Recognition for the Voiceless*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** people unable to speak audibly have no way to be understood in real time

**Mechanism** converting silent lip movement into text  
**Beneficiary** people with laryngeal loss or ventilation

**Recurs in** [[noisy environments]] [[covert communication]] [[underwater work]]
