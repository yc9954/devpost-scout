# discriminatory outcomes appear without anyone intending them, so fairness has to be expressible as a constraint in the training loop rather than as a review after it

Asserted by [[a-qeysp1]] — *FairTorch*  
<sub>PyTorch Summer Hackathon 2020</sub>

**Problem** machine learning systems produce unintended discriminatory decisions

**Mechanism** a fairness constraint added to the loss with one line  
**Beneficiary** practitioners who would not otherwise audit their models

**Recurs in** [[credit scoring]] [[hiring]] [[medical triage]]
