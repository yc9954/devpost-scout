# 911 call centres buckle under mass-casualty events when hundreds of calls arrive at once in languages the dispatchers do not speak

Asserted by [[aegis-jtde8o]] — *Aegis*  
<sub>UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2026</sub>

**Problem** the emergency channel's capacity is defined by human dispatchers and demand spikes precisely when the event is largest

**Mechanism** transcribe, translate and protocol-ground every call, spinning up agents to absorb the surge  
**Beneficiary** caller who cannot get through in an earthquake
