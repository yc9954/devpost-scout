# 15% of the global population lives with a disability and 70% of websites fail basic accessibility standards — students with dyslexia avoid online learning, professionals with ADHD cannot read long pages

Asserted by [[based_smiles]] — *Better Web*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** accessibility is the author's obligation and the author overwhelmingly does not meet it, so the burden lands on the reader

**Mechanism** a browser extension adapting pages at read time for the individual's needs  
**Beneficiary** person with ADHD, dyslexia or sensory sensitivity

**Recurs in** [[low literacy]] [[second-language readers]] [[aging]]
