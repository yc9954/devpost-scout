# a risk prediction a clinician cannot interrogate will not change a treatment decision, so explainability is a precondition for the prediction being used at all

Asserted by [[heartguard-ai-cardiovascular-risk-prediction-system]] — *HeartGuard AI: Cardiovascular Risk Prediction System*  
<sub>Frostbyte Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** patients discover heart conditions only after symptoms are severe and opaque models are not acted on

**Mechanism** cardiovascular risk from clinical data and ECG deep learning with explanations attached  
**Beneficiary** patients with undetected cardiovascular risk

**Recurs in** [[stroke]] [[kidney disease]] [[sepsis prediction]]
