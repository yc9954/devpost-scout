# a shared lending pool means every asset's risk is borne by every depositor, so isolating vaults by risk profile is what lets unusual collateral exist at all

Asserted by [[chedda-protocol]] — *Chedda Protocol*  
<sub>Oasis Bloom Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** lending protocols pool risk which excludes assets with unusual profiles

**Mechanism** isolated vaults separated by risk with support for non-standard collateral  
**Beneficiary** holders of assets no pooled protocol will accept

**Recurs in** [[insurance pools]] [[credit unions]] [[specialist lending]]
