# a token gift fails as a gift because the act of receiving it requires the recipient to already be a user, so the onboarding cost falls on the person being given something

Asserted by [[tokeniz]] — *Tokeniz :- Giving Value To NFTs*  
<sub>TRON Grand Hackathon 2022</sub>

**Problem** sending a friend crypto for her birthday required her to set up everything first

**Mechanism** NFTs that lock tokens inside them, giftable and fee-less to redeem  
**Beneficiary** people outside crypto receiving value from people inside it

**Recurs in** [[gift cards]] [[remittances]] [[loyalty points]]
