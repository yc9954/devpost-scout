# businesses collect feedback and stop before acting on it, so the failure is in the step between analysis and action rather than in collection

Asserted by [[getfeed]] — *GetFeed*  
<sub>Square + Google AI Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** collecting, analysing and acting on customer feedback are treated as one process and only the first happens

**Mechanism** turning feedback into specific actions  
**Beneficiary** businesses with unread feedback

**Recurs in** [[employee surveys]] [[patient experience]] [[public consultation]]
