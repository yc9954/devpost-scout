# a puzzle game where the player's progress advances the antagonist makes the mechanic and the narrative the same thing, so the tension comes from success rather than from failure

Asserted by [[n_0v1]] — *N_0V1*  
<sub>Reddit Games and Puzzles Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** puzzle games separate the story from the act of solving

**Mechanism** puzzles whose completion fuels the entity telling the story  
**Beneficiary** players and narrative designers

**Recurs in** [[training scenarios]] [[ethics teaching]] [[interactive fiction]]
