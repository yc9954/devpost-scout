# aid delivery through digital wallets requires both parties to have an app, which excludes precisely the recipients aid is for

Asserted by [[disposable-digital-wallets-pointcheckout]] — *Disposable digital wallets - PointCheckout*  
<sub>The European Commission's EUvsVirus Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** the distribution mechanism assumes the capability the recipient lacks

**Mechanism** single-use wallets needing no app or account on the receiving end  
**Beneficiary** unbanked aid recipient

**Recurs in** [[disaster payments]] [[refugee support]] [[rural subsidies]]
