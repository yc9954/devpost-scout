# a family cannot recover assets when the person who controlled them dies suddenly, so the failure is the absence of a discoverable record rather than the absence of a will

Asserted by [[2heavens]] — *2heavens*  
<sub>Celo's Make Crypto Mobile Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** a father died and nobody knew which bank held what

**Mechanism** an inheritance will recorded on chain that executes on death  
**Beneficiary** families of people who hold assets privately

**Recurs in** [[digital accounts]] [[insurance policies]] [[business succession]]
