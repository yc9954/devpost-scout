# a physics puzzle becomes durable when players author the levels and compete on cost and time, so the community supplies the content the designer cannot

Asserted by [[bridgedit-cosy-bridge-building]] — *BridgeDit! Cosy Bridge-Building*

**Problem** physics puzzle games exhaust their authored levels

**Mechanism** bridge building with player-created levels and competition on cost and time
