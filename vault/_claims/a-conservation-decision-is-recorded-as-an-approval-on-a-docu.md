# a conservation decision is recorded as an approval on a document that cannot hold the map it approves, so the record and the substance are separated

Asserted by [[chimp-auditor]] — *Chimp Auditor*  
<sub>DocuSign Good Code Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** reviewing and approving range maps requires the map and the signature in different systems

**Mechanism** embedding interactive maps into the approval envelope with decision routing  
**Beneficiary** conservation organisations approving spatial decisions

**Recurs in** [[planning permission]] [[land transactions]] [[environmental permits]]
