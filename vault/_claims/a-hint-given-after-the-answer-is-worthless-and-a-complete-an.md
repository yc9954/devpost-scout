# a hint given after the answer is worthless and a complete answer removes the learning, so the useful intervention is a partial nudge at the moment of the error

Asserted by [[frodo]] — *Frodo*  
<sub>UC Berkeley AI Hackathon 2024</sub>

**Problem** tutoring either arrives too late or replaces the student's thinking

**Mechanism** real-time mistake identification with guiding hints rather than solutions  
**Beneficiary** students who need direction without being given the answer

**Recurs in** [[clinical supervision]] [[apprenticeship]] [[code review]]
