# a non-custodial wallet gives ownership and removes every service a bank provided, so features like scheduled or recoverable transfers simply do not exist for self-custodied assets

Asserted by [[keeperc20]] — *KeepERC20*  
<sub>Chainlink Fall 2022 Hackathon</sub>

**Problem** decentralised wallets have no scheduling or recovery

**Mechanism** wrapping tokens so they support scheduled, recoverable and conditional transfers via automation  
**Beneficiary** self-custody users who lost banking features

**Recurs in** [[estate planning]] [[standing orders]] [[escrow]]
