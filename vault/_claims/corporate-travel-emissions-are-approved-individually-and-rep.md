# corporate travel emissions are approved individually and reported in aggregate, so nobody is accountable at the moment a trip is booked

Asserted by [[travelgreen-vxzf3g]] — *TravelGreen*  
<sub>Pega Community Hackathon 2022</sub>

**Problem** employees travel for work with no carbon accounting at the point of approval

**Mechanism** calculating per-trip footprint with limits enforced at booking  
**Beneficiary** companies with emissions targets and the atmosphere

**Recurs in** [[procurement]] [[energy use]] [[fleet management]]
