# tasks sent over messaging get lost among the conversations around them — either the sender forgets what they delegated or the receiver forgets what was assigned

Asserted by [[karo-trj4av]] — *Karo: Social Task Manager*  
<sub>RevenueCat Ship-a-ton</sub>

**Problem** delegation happens in the channel people actually use and that channel has no representation of a task

**Mechanism** delegate to anyone in your contacts and have the app follow up through the channels they already use  
**Beneficiary** person delegating to someone who will not install anything
